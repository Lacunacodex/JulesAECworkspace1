**CONSEQUENCE AND LEGACY**

Where the Cycle Completes and Compounds

Document 7A --- Vertebra 7: Feedback Loop

*The output of observation collapse becomes the input for the next
cycle*

**WHAT THE FEEDBACK LOOP IS**

Vertebra 7 is the vertebra that makes the spine a cycle rather than a
line. After observation collapse at Vertebra 6 produces consequences,
those consequences propagate backward through the spine. Relationships
shift. Clocks advance or retard. New pressure points emerge. The
character's accumulated record crystallizes into pattern. The modified
state feeds back into Vertebra 1 (Schema), and the cycle begins again.

Without the Feedback Loop, each adventure is isolated. Events happen,
resolve, and are forgotten. With the Feedback Loop, events compound.
Every cycle's output shapes the next cycle's input. The world after the
adventure is different from the world before it. The character after the
adventure is different from the character before it. The accumulation
across cycles IS the campaign.

This vertebra operates in two phases: the immediate cascade
(consequences propagating through the network within the same session or
between sessions) and the retrospective read (the pattern that emerges
from accumulated cascades over time). The cascade is mechanical. The
retrospective read is interpretive. Both are essential.

**CONSEQUENCE CASCADES**

When an observation collapse at Vertebra 6 resolves, it produces
consequences (Document 6A, collapse output table). Each consequence
enters the relationship map (Document 3B) and propagates along edges
according to the four propagation rules: consequences follow edges,
trust governs accuracy, tension governs amplification, state governs
direction.

resolutions, significant NPC state changes) require the full seven

The cascade is where emergent story lives. The party defeated the goblin
chief (direct consequence). The goblin tribe fractures (secondary tick).
The Redbrand front that was using the goblins as a buffer loses its
shield (propagated consequence). The Redbrands accelerate their timeline
(tertiary tick). A clock the party did not know about crosses its
threshold because of an action three links away in the network. The
party encounters the Redbrand escalation next session and does not
initially understand that THEY caused it.

**THE LEGACY SYSTEM**

The character-level feedback mechanism. The Legacy System reads
accumulated history in reverse and crystallizes identity from the
pattern that already exists.

**The Backward-Facing Read**

The Legacy System does not track forward-facing achievements. It does
not ask "what did you accomplish?" It asks "what pattern do your
accumulated choices reveal about who you are?" The answer is read
backward from the present, not forward from a starting point.

After every significant cycle (typically every 3-5 sessions, or at the
end of a campaign arc), the GM and player review the character's
accumulated actions. Not what they planned. Not what they intended. What
they actually DID. The pattern of choices reveals values the character
may not have consciously recognized.

A character who consistently chose to protect strangers over pursuing
objectives has a pattern. That pattern crystallizes into a Legacy trait
--- a permanent modifier that reflects who the character has become
through accumulated action. The trait was not planned. It was not
assigned at character creation. It EMERGED from the pattern of play. The
Selûnae read fate backward. The Legacy System is the Selûnae principle
expressed as character mechanics.

**Legacy Trait Crystallization**

When the backward read reveals a consistent pattern across multiple
cycles, the GM proposes a Legacy trait. The trait is discussed with the
player. If both agree the pattern is genuine (not forced or
coincidental), the trait is added to the character sheet.

**THE COMPOUNDING WORLD**

Nothing resets. Every cycle's consequences persist. The accumulation
across cycles IS the campaign's depth.

                     damage IS persistent consequence. A character
                     entering the next cycle with unhealed Mental track

  **XP and Mastery** Experience accumulated during the cycle produces

**Narrative Compounding**

The ledger grows. Session 1's notes are sparse. Session 30's notes
reference events, NPCs, promises, and consequences spanning the entire
campaign. The ledger's density IS the campaign's accumulated weight.

When the party returns to a location they visited twenty sessions ago,
the GM checks what has changed. The faction turns have been running. The
clocks have been ticking. The Default Timeline has advanced. The
location is different because the world moved. The party's earlier
actions in this location produced cascades that compounded across twenty
sessions of intervening play. Encountering those compounded consequences
IS the campaign's payoff --- the proof that the world remembers.

**CHEKHOV OBLIGATION TRACKING**

Outstanding seeds from previous cycles are tracked at this vertebra.
Every introduced element that has not yet resolved is a Chekhov
obligation --- a promise to the audience that this detail will matter.

**Between sessions:** Review the truth list from each previous session.
Which truths were encountered but not resolved? These are active
obligations. Which truths were not encountered at all? These are
available for future placement --- they remain in superposition.

**Between arcs:** Review all active obligations. Prioritize those
outstanding longest --- the audience's memory of the introduced element
makes the eventual payoff more satisfying the longer it waits (up to a
threshold of approximately 8-10 sessions, after which the audience
forgets and the payoff loses impact).

**Resolution or retirement:** Seeds outstanding beyond 8-10 sessions
should either be resolved in the next arc or integrated into a clock's
terminal state so their resolution is structurally guaranteed. The GM
can also decide that the unresolved seed WAS the payoff --- some
introduced elements are red herrings that teach the audience that not
every detail leads somewhere. But this should be deliberate, not
accidental.

**POSITIONAL DIVINITY CYCLING**

At the civilizational scale, the Feedback Loop includes the cycling of
Saints and Legends within the positional divinity system.

Divine aspects that resonated during this generation's cycles are
reinforced. The mortal narratives that supported them (the stories of
Saints and Legends) gain institutional weight through Mortis Ævum's
theological curation. Aspects that did not resonate are allowed to fade.
The divine portfolio adjusts to match the population's lived experience.

This is the Feedback Loop operating at the Zoetrope's temporal scale. A
single cycle (one session) does not shift divine relevance. Accumulated
cycles across a generation (20-30 years in-game) produce measurable
drift. The GM tracks this drift qualitatively rather than mechanically:
which divine aspects came up most frequently during play? Which gods'
portfolios were most relevant to the campaign's themes? Those aspects
are reinforced in the setting's institutional landscape.

The party does not control divine cycling. But their choices --- which
gods' values they enacted through their actions, which divine aspects
their Beliefs aligned with --- contribute to the aggregate signal that
determines which aspects persist and which fade. The party's influence
on the divine landscape is the Selûnae principle at its largest scale:
the pattern of accumulated mortal choices, read backward, determines
which faces of the gods remain relevant.

**THE RETROSPECTIVE READ**

The second phase of the Feedback Loop. After the cascade processes the
immediate consequences, the retrospective read asks: what does it all
MEAN?

The retrospective read is the Retrospective Lens from Document 0. It is
available only after the cycle completes. In the moment, events were
choices and consequences stacking. The retrospective read imposes
meaning on the accumulated stack. The campaign's theme was not planned.
It emerged from the weight of decisions and was named by the table
afterward.

**When to Perform the Retrospective Read**

  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------
  **After a major    When a clock completes its terminal state, when a
  arc**              front resolves, or when a campaign phase ends. The
                     arc's events are reviewed for pattern. What theme
                     emerged? What did the characters' choices reveal
                     about their values? What did the setting's response
                     reveal about its nature?

  **At Legacy        When the GM proposes a Legacy trait, the backward
  crystallization    read is the evidence. The pattern must be visible in
  moments**          the accumulated record, not inferred from a single
                     event.

  **At campaign      The final retrospective read is the campaign's
  end**              meaning. The table looks at everything that happened
                     and asks: what was this story about? The answer is
                     never what anyone planned. It is what the
                     accumulated choices, consequences, cascades, and
                     compounding produced.
  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------

The retrospective read is not the GM's interpretation imposed on the
table. It is a collaborative act. The table discusses what they
experienced. Each participant's reading is shaped by their own schema
(Bartlett) --- they notice the patterns that their experience prepared
them to recognize. Different participants may read different themes from
the same campaign. Both readings are valid. The retrospective read is
the campfire's third survival function (group binding) expressed through
shared interpretation of shared experience.

**EXPRESSION GUIDANCE**

Vertebra 7 has two strong expression bonds.

**Serial Packaging**

Where to cut the continuous cycle into discrete consumable units. The
Feedback Loop IS the cycle boundary --- the point where one iteration
ends and the next begins. Serial packaging decisions determine where on
the cycle the boundary falls, which shapes inter-session anticipation
and next-session energy.

  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------
  **Cliffhanger      Cut the boundary at the Clocks vertebra, just past a
  ending**           threshold crossing. The crisis has arrived. The
                     consequences are unknown. Maximum inter-session
                     anticipation. The table spends the week wondering.

  **Resolution       Cut the boundary at the Feedback Loop's completion.
  ending**           Consequences processed. New status quo established.
                     Minimal inter-session tension. Best when the next
                     session begins a new arc.

  **Revelation       Cut at the Symptoms vertebra of the NEXT cycle. The
  ending**           final piece of information arrives that
                     recontextualizes everything, but the party cannot
                     act until next session. The Chekhov callback landing
                     in the last five minutes.

  **Cold open next   Cut at the Adventures vertebra, mid-collapse. The
  session**          next session starts inside a situation already in
                     progress. No preamble. Action first, context emerges
                     during the Sequel.
  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------

**Persistence Encoding**

The Feedback Loop's second strong bond. How the cycle's outputs are
anchored in collective memory so they persist across iterations. The
ledger is the physical manifestation. The recap is the ritual
invocation. The callbacks to previous sessions ("remember when the
blacksmith's brother went missing in session 3?") are the mnemonic
structure that binds cycles together.

Persistence encoding is what transforms a sequence of sessions into a
campaign. Without it, each session is an isolated event. With it, each
session is a chapter in a continuous story whose meaning compounds with
every addition. The encoding IS the campfire's first survival function
(information encoding through experience) operating across sessions
rather than within them.
