# Contracts - Binding Companion Master Archetype

This path focuses on forging magically binding agreements with creatures, compelling them to service through oaths, contracts, `Geas`, or similar obligations.

## Foundational Ability

- **Magical Contract (Crux Talent):** Grants the ability to create magically binding contracts.
    - **Action:** Ritual (typically 1 hour, can be reduced by talents).
    - **Cost:** Stamina equal to the target creature's EML **AND** ÆP equal to the target creature's EML.
    - **Group Binding:** When binding multiple identical creatures simultaneously, every 10 creatures count as one creature of the next higher EML for calculating the ritual cost (e.g., binding 10 EML 2 creatures costs resources as if binding one EML 3 creature).
    - **Limitation:** You cannot bind a creature whose EML is higher than your own Mastery Rating.
    - A contracted creature counts towards the limit imposed by the **Mastery Capacity Pool**.
    - *This talent is the prerequisite for all talents within the Path of Binding.*

---

## Contract Enforcement Tree

*Focuses on strengthening the binding and ensuring compliance.*

### Novice Tier Talents (Mastery 1-4)

- **Ironclad Oath:** Increase the save DC against any negative effects imposed by your contract by `+ Half Mastery Rating` (rounded down).
- **Binding Mark:** Place a visible or invisible mark upon your contracted companion. You are always aware of the general direction and distance to the marked companion. *(Note: May overlap with a general Arcane Mark system).*
- **Contractual Sense:** Know the general emotional state (calm, agitated, afraid), current HP status (uninjured, injured, critical), and if the contract is **broken** or the companion is **dead**.

### Adept Tier Talents (Mastery 5-7)

- **Strengthened Chains:** The save DC penalty against negative effects imposed by your contract increases to `+ Half Mastery Rating` (total). Attempts to magically dispel or break your contract suffer a penalty equal to your `Full Mastery Rating`.
- **Binding Circle:** Designate a location (up to 100-ft radius per Mastery Rating) or object as an anchor for one contract. The bound creature cannot willingly move outside this area unless permitted.

### Master Tier Talents (Mastery 8-10)

- **Unbreakable Vow:** The save DC penalty against negative effects imposed by your contract increases to `+ Full Mastery Rating` (total). Attempts to magically dispel or break your contract suffer a penalty equal to `Mastery Rating x 2`. Creatures attempting to break the contract suffer significant backlash affecting all creatures within `Mastery Rating x 2 yards` of the violator.

---

## Contractual Enhancement Tree

*Focuses on empowering companions through the contract itself.*

### Novice Tier Talents (Mastery 1-4)

- **Contractual Vigor:** Creatures bound by your contracts gain temporary Hit Points equal to your Mastery Rating x 2 `after each long rest`. *(Can be purchased multiple times and selectively applied).*
- **Lesser Boon:** The companion gains bonus Feature Points equal to `Mastery Rating x 2` to spend on Basic Enhancement or Sensory Enhancement features.
- **Shared Resilience:** While a contracted companion is within 30 ft, gain a bonus equal to `Half Mastery Rating` (rounded down) to one chosen defense (Physical, Evasion, Mental, Social) or one type of Energy Resistance the companion possesses. *(Can be taken multiple times for different defenses/resistances).*

### Adept Tier Talents (Mastery 5-7)

- **Contractual Boon:** The companion gains bonus Feature Points equal to `Mastery Rating x 2` to spend on Movement, Combat, or Defensive features.
- **Master's Protection:** As a reaction when you would take damage, redirect half of that damage to one willing contracted companion within 60 ft.
- **Shared Wards:** *(Requires Shared Resilience)* While a contracted companion is within 30 ft, choose one: gain DR equal to half its DR, gain SR equal to half its SR, or gain immunity to one condition it is immune to. *(Can be taken multiple times).*

### Master Tier Talents (Mastery 8-10)

- **Greater Boon:** The companion gains bonus Feature Points equal to `Mastery Rating x 2` to spend freely on any features appropriate to its nature, potentially including minor Supernatural Abilities.
- **Greater Protection:** *(Requires Master's Protection)* As a reaction when you would take damage, redirect **all** of that damage to one willing contracted companion within 60 ft. That companion takes half the redirected damage.

---

## Contract Versatility Tree

*Focuses on the types and flexibility of contracts.*

### Novice Tier Talents (Mastery 1-4)

- **Swift Binding:** Reduce the time required for the Magical Contract ritual from 1 hour to 10 minutes for creatures whose `EML is equal to or less than` your Mastery Rating.
- **Task Specialization:** Create contracts focused on a single, non-combat task. Companions bound by such gain a bonus equal to your Mastery Rating on skill checks for that task.
- **Planar Acclimation Contract:** Create contracts granting immunity to penalties (not damage) of a specific hostile planar environment. Does not apply to Ætheric Saturation effects.
- **Contractual Link I:** Establish a one-way telepathic link with any contracted companion within `Mastery Rating miles`. Read surface thoughts and emotions.

### Adept Tier Talents (Mastery 5-7)

- **Transferable Contract:** With agreement or coercion, magically transfer ownership of one of your contracts (and control of the associated creature/effect) to another willing individual.
- **Multi-Binding:** The cost to maintain `[Contracted]` companions from your Mastery Capacity Pool is halved (rounded up).
- **Archetypal Mandate:** *(Requires Task Specialization)* Grant bonus Feature Points equal to `Half Mastery Rating` usable only for talents from a specific character Archetype relevant to the companion's specialized task.
- **Contractual Link II:** *(Requires Contractual Link I)* Link becomes two-way, regardless of distance (same plane). As a standard action, perceive through one companion's senses without being blind/deaf to self.

### Master Tier Talents (Mastery 8-10)

- **Adaptive Contract:** Alter an existing magical contract without needing full ritual components. Takes normal ritual time (potentially reduced by Swift Binding) and costs half the resources (Stamina/ÆP) of the original binding. Other party must still agree or be compelled.
- **Network Senses:** *(Requires Contractual Link II)* Simultaneously perceive through the senses of `half Mastery Rating` (min 1) contracted companions, regardless of distance (same plane), without being blind/deaf to self.

# Empathy - Bonded Companion Master Archetype

This path focuses on forging deep, innate connections with willing creatures, creating a true partnership based on loyalty and shared understanding.

## Foundational Ability

- **Empathetic Bond (Crux Talent):** Grants the ability to form a permanent, empathetic bond with a willing creature (typically `[Instinctive]` or higher Sapience). Allows silent communication of emotions and simple concepts. A bonded creature counts towards the limit imposed by the **Mastery Capacity Pool**. *This talent is the prerequisite for all talents within the Path of Bonding.*

---

## Bond Enhancement Tree

*Focuses on strengthening the connection and the companion's core capabilities.*

### Novice Tier Talents (Mastery 1-4)

- **Deepened Connection:** The bond strengthens, granting bonus Feature Points equal to `Mastery Rating x 2` usable for Basic Enhancements or Sensory Enhancements. *(Can be taken multiple times).*
- **Shared Vitality:** Your bonded companion gains temporary Hit Points equal to your Mastery Rating x 2 after each long rest. *(Can be purchased multiple times and selectively applied).*
- **Mutual Defense:** While you and a bonded companion are within 30 feet of each other, you both gain a bonus equal to `Half Mastery Rating` (rounded down) to your **Physical Defense** and **Magical Defense**.

### Adept Tier Talents (Mastery 5-7)

- **Resilient Bond:** Your companion gains a bonus equal to `Presence Modifier + Mastery Rating` on saves against **Mental or Social** effects. *(Can be taken multiple times, applying to either Mental or Social saves per purchase).*
- **Enhanced Training:** Bonus Feature Points granted = `Mastery Rating x 2` usable for Movement, Combat, or Defensive features. *(Can be taken multiple times).*
- **Bonded Warding:** *(Requires Mutual Defense)* While you and a bonded companion are within 30 feet of each other, you both gain a bonus equal to `Half Mastery Rating` (rounded down) to your **Mental Defense** and **Social Defense**. Additionally, you gain the ability to redirect damage as per the "Master's Protection" talent effect (redirect half damage to companion as reaction).

### Master Tier Talents (Mastery 8-10)

- **Perfect Synergy:** Bonus Feature Points granted = `Mastery Rating x 2` usable freely for any appropriate features, including minor Supernatural Abilities reflecting the bond's nature. *(Can be taken multiple times).*
- **Unbreakable Bond:** Your companion becomes immune to magical effects that would specifically sever, suppress, or hijack your bond. Additionally, the companion gains a bonus equal to your Mastery Rating on saves against commands issued by anyone other than you.

---

## Shared Abilities Tree

*Focuses on synergistic abilities that benefit both master and companion.*

### Novice Tier Talents (Mastery 1-4)

- **Share Senses (Basic):** Standard action to perceive through one bonded companion's senses.
- **Coordinated Movement:** When you and a bonded companion are adjacent, you can use the Aid Another action to boost each other's **Physical Defense** as a reaction.

### Adept Tier Talents (Mastery 5-7)

- **Share Senses (Advanced):** *(Requires Share Senses Basic)* Perceive through one companion's senses without penalty. You can establish a two-way telepathic link, allowing you to see through each other's senses simultaneously.
- **Guardian's Intervention:** When an enemy attacks you, an adjacent bonded companion (adjacent to you *or* the attacker) can use its reaction to impose a penalty equal to `Half Mastery Rating` (rounded down) on the attack roll.
- **Linked Reflexes:** You and your bonded companions within 30 feet gain a bonus to initiative rolls equal to half your Mastery Rating.
- **Symbiotic Healing:** When you receive magical healing, any bonded companion within 30 feet regains Hit Points equal to half the amount you received.

### Master Tier Talents (Mastery 8-10)

- **Share Senses (Master):** *(Requires Share Senses Advanced)* Perceive through multiple (`half Mastery Rating`, min 1) bonded companions' senses simultaneously without penalty.
- **Combined Attack:** Once per round, when you hit an enemy with an attack, *all* bonded companions within their own attack range of that enemy can make a single basic attack against it as a reaction.
- **Perfected Symbiosis:** *(Requires Symbiotic Healing)* When you receive magical healing, any bonded companion within 30 feet regains Hit Points equal to the **full** amount you received.

---

## Bond Flexibility Tree

*Focuses on managing the bond and its unique manifestations.*

### Novice Tier Talents (Mastery 1-4)

- **Empathic Communication:** Communicate non-verbally via feelings and intent with bonded companions. Conveys the equivalent of `25 + Mastery Rating` words per exchange. Meaning and intent are always clear between bonded individuals.
- **Pocket Companion (Form):** **Move action** to safely store one companion in a personal extradimensional space (suspended animation). Choose an alternate form while stored. Calling them forth is a **Move action**.
- **Silent Communication:** Your telepathic link allows for complex sentences and nuanced communication with bonded companions (provided intelligence). Communication includes clear intent and meaning and functions at **any distance on the same plane**.

### Adept Tier Talents (Mastery 5-7)

- **Swift Recall:** *(Requires Pocket Companion)* Calling forth a companion stored via Pocket Companion is now a **Swift action**.
- **Multi-Bond Management:** The cost to maintain `[Bonded]` companions from your Mastery Capacity Pool is halved (rounded up).

### Master Tier Talents (Mastery 8-10)

- **Bond Across Worlds:** *(Requires Silent Communication)* Your Silent Communication talent now functions across planar boundaries.

# Psychic Contagion - Seeded  Thrallherd Archetype

This path focuses on implanting a non-magical, psychic seed of the Thrallherd's consciousness into a host, creating a network of insidious influence.

## Foundational Ability

- **Implant Psychosometric Seed (Crux Talent):** Grants the ability to implant a psychic seed.
    - **Action:** Standard Action (requires touch). This attempt can be made as part of a casual, seemingly incidental touch.
    - **Cost:** 3 ÆP
    - **Save:** Target makes a Resolve save (DC 10 + Your Mastery Tier + Your Intellect Modifier). An unaware target is typically considered flat-footed against this attempt.
    - **Effect:** On a failure, a dormant psychic seed is implanted. The host is unaware. The seed is non-magical and difficult to detect.
    - A `[Seeded]` thrall counts towards the limit imposed by the **Mastery Capacity Pool**.
    - *This talent is the prerequisite for all talents within this Path.*

---

## Psychic Contagion Tree

*Focuses on the methods of implantation and spreading the psychic seeds.*

### Novice Tier Talents (Mastery 1-4)

- **Innate Contagion:** As a Standard Action, target one creature within `10 ft * Mastery Rating` and expend ÆP to implant a "Carrier Seed." (Resolve save negates).
    - **Cost & Duration:** Base cost is `(((Mastery * 2) - 1) * 2) ÆP`, making the target a carrier for `Mastery Rating * 1 day`. Duration can be increased by paying a proportionately higher ÆP cost.
    - **Carrier Effect:** For the duration, any creature (not already seeded by you) that comes within 10 ft of the carrier must make a Resolve save (once per 24 hours per carrier) or be implanted with a *normal* seed.
- **Psychic Static:** Your seeds are difficult to find. Any attempt to psychically or magically detect your seeds suffers a penalty to the check equal to your `Full Mastery Rating`.

### Adept Tier Talents (Mastery 5-7)

- **Ranged Implant:** You can attempt to implant a seed at range (`30 ft * Mastery Rating`) as a Standard Action. This is *not* a visible psychic assault; the target may be unaware an attempt was made, but they still get a Resolve save.
- **Vector Host:** As a Standard Action, target one *already seeded* thrall and expend ÆP (using the **Innate Contagion** cost/duration formula) to re-activate their "Carrier Seed" effect. Additionally, this Vector Host can be used as the origin point for *any* `[Seeded]` talent as if they were the Caster, using their location for range and line of sight.

### Master Tier Talents (Mastery 8-10)

- **Airborne Vector:** As a Standard Action, expend ÆP (using the **Innate Contagion** cost/duration formula) to create a `10 ft * Mastery Rating` burst at a range of `100 ft * Mastery Rating`.
    - **Effect:** All valid creatures in the burst must make a Resolve save or be implanted with a *normal* seed. Attended non-magical objects and unattended objects in the burst become "Infected Objects" for the duration.
    - **Infected Object Effect:** Anyone who touches the object must save or be implanted with a *normal* seed (once per creature per object).
- **Engineered Contagion:** When you successfully implant a seed using **Innate Contagion** or **Airborne Vector**, you can attach a generalized, **complex command or suggestion** (e.g., "Distrust all authority," "Gather and hide shiny objects"). The host will subconsciously rationalize and act upon this urge in their own unpredictable way.

---

## Network Command Tree

*Focuses on observing and controlling the network of thralls.*

### Novice Tier Talents (Mastery 1-4)

- **Sympathetic Scrying I:** As a standard action, perceive through one seeded thrall's senses. This does *not* make you blind/deaf to your own surroundings.
- **Puppeteer's Urge:** As a standard action, attempt to plant a simple, plausible suggestion in a thrall's mind. The thrall perceives it as their own thought. (Resolve save at a penalty equal to `Half Mastery Rating` negates).
- **Agonizing Burst:** As a standard action, psychically assault a seeded thrall, dealing `1d6` psychic damage per Mastery Tier. The seed is *not* destroyed. The host is unable to determine the cause, and any *unseeded* observer must succeed on a Resolve save (at this talent's original DC) to determine the true cause.

### Adept Tier Talents (Mastery 5-7)

- **Sympathetic Scrying II:** *(Requires Scrying I)* You can perceive through the senses of multiple (`Half Mastery Rating`, min 1) thralls simultaneously.
- **Dominant Command:** As a standard action, issue a direct, overt command to one thrall. The thrall gets a Resolve save (at a penalty equal to `Half Mastery Rating`) to resist. If they fail, they obey the command and rationalize it as their own (potentially flawed) decision, unaware of the direct control.

### Master Tier Talents (Mastery 8-10)

- **Dominant Network:** Issue a single command (via **Puppeteer's Urge** or **Dominant Command**) to *all* seeded thralls simultaneously, or to a specific subgroup. Each thrall saves individually (at a penalty equal to `Half Mastery Rating`).
- **Psychic Override:** As a standard action, attempt to seize direct control of one thrall's body within 100 ft. The target makes a Resolve save (at a penalty equal to `Half Mastery Rating`). On a failure, you dictate their actions. **This costs no Stamina to maintain.** The host is *not* aware of the control and rationalizes all actions.
- **Psychic Awareness:** You gain a supernatural awareness of psychic phenomena. You can sense the location and general nature of any active psychic effect or creature with psychic abilities within `Mastery Rating * 100` feet.

---

## Seed Enhancement Tree

*Focuses on strengthening the seed's influence and the host's capabilities.*

### Novice Tier Talents (Mastery 1-4)

- **Loyalty Reinforcement:** Your seeded thralls gain a bonus equal to your `Full Mastery Rating` on saves against mental effects *not* originating from you. They also gain an additional `Half Mastery Rating` bonus on saves against effects that would specifically turn them against you.
- **Subtle Conditioning:** Grant bonus Feature Points equal to your `Full Mastery Rating` usable *only* for mental/social enhancements (e.This.g., Skill Expertise [Deception, Guile], Mental/Social Defense).

### Adept Tier Talents (Mastery 5-7)

- **Zealous Devotion:** When you issue a command via **Puppeteer's Urge**, **Dominant Command**, **Dominant Network**, or any mundane/magical verbal command, your thralls (aware or not) execute it with unnatural zeal. They gain a morale bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls equal to `Half Mastery Rating` for 1 minute.
- **Enhanced Host:** Grant bonus Feature Points equal to `Mastery Rating x 2` usable for any Basic, Sensory, or Combat enhancements.

### Master Tier Talents (Mastery 8-10)

- **Perfected Host:** Grant bonus Feature Points equal to `Mastery Rating x 2` usable for any purpose, including Supernatural Abilities.
- **Persistent Will:** *(Replaces Psychic Bastion/Scrambler)* If one of your seeded thralls fails a saving throw against a Mental or Social effect *not* originating from you, the seed's influence grants them an immediate second saving throw to negate the effect. A thrall can only benefit from this talent once per 24 hours.
- **Collective Consciousness:** Your seeded thralls within `30 ft * M` of each other form a minor hive-mind. They gain a +1 bonus to all defenses and attack rolls for each *other* thrall within that range (max bonus equal to `Mastery Rating * 10`).

# Summons - Companion Master Archetype

This path focuses on creating temporary companions from raw magical energy using the `Summon` effect.

## Foundational Ability

- **Summon Creature (Crux Talent):** Grants the ability to use the `Summon` effect. Base duration for summoned creatures is **Mastery Rating x 10 rounds (1 minute per mastery level)** unless modified by Additives like `Tethered`. *This talent is the prerequisite for all talents within the Path of Creation.*

---

## Construct Enhancement Tree

*Focuses on improving the raw physical capabilities and resilience of summoned creatures.*

### Novice Tier Talents (Mastery 1-4)

- **Reinforced Summons:** Your created creatures are hardier. They gain temporary Hit Points equal to your Mastery Rating x 2. *(Can be taken multiple times; each purchase adds the bonus again).*
- **Empowered Strikes:** Your created creatures' natural attacks gain a bonus to damage rolls equal to your **full Mastery Rating**. *(Applies to all natural attacks. Can be taken multiple times; each purchase adds the bonus again).*
- **Elemental Adaptation:** Choose one Elemental Archetype. Your created creatures gain Energy Resistance equal to your **full Mastery Rating** against that element. *(Can be taken multiple times, choosing a different element or stacking on the same element).*
- **Augmented Element:** Choose one Elemental Archetype. When you summon a creature and grant it an Energy Attack feature of that type, its energy damage increases by an amount equal to your **full Mastery Rating**. *(Can be taken multiple times, choosing a different element or stacking on the same element).*
- **Form Enhancement:** When summoning, gain bonus Feature Points equal to **half your Mastery Rating** (rounded down, min 1) usable *only* for features from the Basic Enhancements or Sensory Enhancements list.

### Adept Tier Talents (Mastery 5-7)

- **Superior Augmentation:** Your created creatures gain a bonus to Might and Vigor scores equal to half your Mastery Rating (rounded down).
- **Combat Enhancement:** When summoning, gain bonus Feature Points equal to **half your Mastery Rating** (rounded down, min 1) usable *only* for features from the Movement Capabilities or Combat Abilities list.

### Master Tier Talents (Mastery 8-10)

- **Resilient Creation:** Your created creatures gain Damage Reduction equal to your Mastery Rating / - (no bypass).
- **Avatar Manifestation:** When you cast a `Summon` spell, pay double the ÆP cost. The resulting creature doubles all HP pools, multiplies base attack damage by 1.5, and gains bonus Feature Points equal to your Mastery Rating **in each category**. You may only have one Avatar Manifestation active at a time.

---

## Construct Utility Tree

*Focuses on increasing the versatility and non-combat functions of summoned creatures.*

### Novice Tier Talents (Mastery 1-4)

- **Skilled Creation:** When you summon a creature, grant it a bonus equal to your **full Mastery Rating** in one skill (chosen at summoning). *(Can be taken multiple times, choosing a different skill each time).*
- **Specialized Function:** When summoning, gain bonus Feature Points equal to **half your Mastery Rating** (rounded down, min 1). These points can *only* be spent to grant the creature **Talents from a single character Archetype** that the summon qualifies for. *(Can be taken multiple times, choosing a different Archetype each time).*

### Adept Tier Talents (Mastery 5-7)

- **Complex Programming:** Each time you take this talent, one `Summon` spell recipe you know gains a permanent **blank Talent Slot**. When casting, fill that slot with **any one General Talent you know** (or a pre-studied "rote" talent) that the creature qualifies for, granting it that talent for its duration. *(Can be taken multiple times).*
- **Lingering Presence:** Your created creatures persist for a number of rounds equal to your **Mastery Rating** after concentration breaks or duration ends (if not `Tethered`).
- **Share Senses I:** Standard action to perceive through one creature's senses (blind/deaf to self).
- **Share Senses II:** *(Requires Share Senses I)* Perceive through one creature's senses *without* being blind/deaf to self.
- **Share Senses III:** *(Requires Share Senses II)* Perceive through multiple (`half Mastery Rating`, min 1) creatures' senses simultaneously *without* being blind/deaf to self.
- **Master's Voice:** Communicate telepathically with created creatures within 100 feet. *(Can be taken multiple times; each purchase increases range by 100 feet, up to Mastery Tier times).*

### Master Tier Talents (Mastery 8-10)

- **Adaptive Design:** You can incorporate the **Variable Descriptor** Additive into your `Summon` spells at half the normal Complexity Pool cost.

---

## Construct Command Tree

*Focuses on efficiency, duration, and managing multiple summoned creatures.*

### Novice Tier Talents (Mastery 1-4)

- **Swift Conjuration I:** Reduce casting time of `Summon` spells (CML 4 or lower) from Standard Action to **Swift Action**.
- **Efficient Summoning I:** Reduce base ÆP cost of all `Summon` spells by **half your Mastery Rating** (rounded up, min 1 ÆP reduction).

### Adept Tier Talents (Mastery 5-7)

- **Extended Conjuration:** When using the `Tethered` Additive, the cost to maintain the summon from your Mastery Capacity Pool is halved (rounded up).
- **Multi-Summon:** Divide a single `Summon` spell's power. Summon multiple creatures (need not be identical) whose total CML equals the spell's CML. Each creature reserves points from Mastery Capacity Pool individually based on its own CML if `Tethered`.
- **Transposition:** Move action to swap places with one created creature within 30 feet. *(Can be taken multiple times; each purchase increases range by 30 feet).*
- **Swift Conjuration II:** *(Requires Swift Conjuration I)* Reduce casting time of `Summon` spells (CML 7 or lower) from Standard Action to **Swift Action**.
- **Efficient Summoning II:** *(Requires Efficient Summoning I)* Reduce base ÆP cost of all `Summon` spells by your **full Mastery Rating** (replaces Efficient Summoning I bonus).

### Master Tier Talents (Mastery 8-10)

- **Coordinated Assault:** Created creatures gain a flanking bonus equal to **half your Mastery Rating**. With **Master's Voice**, issue a single Swift/Move command to *all* or a designated *subgroup* of created creatures simultaneously.
- **Swift Conjuration III:** *(Requires Swift Conjuration II)* Reduce casting time of `Summon` spells (CML 10 or lower) from Standard Action to **Swift Action**.
- **Efficient Summoning III:** *(Requires Efficient Summoning II)* Reduce base ÆP cost of all `Summon` spells by **Mastery Rating x 2** (replaces Efficient Summoning II bonus).
- **Swarm Commander:** Cast a `Summon` spell to manifest multiple creatures. Summon a number of creatures equal to **half your Mastery Rating** (min 1). Each summoned creature has a CML that is 2 lower than the spell's normal CML. Creatures need not be identical. Each reserves points from Mastery Capacity Pool individually based on its reduced CML if `Tethered`.

- **Grave-Hardened Frame:** Your `[Returned]` companions gain temporary Hit Points equal to your `Mastery Rating x 2` after each long rest. *(Can be taken multiple times).*
- **Necrotic Resilience:** Your `[Returned]` companions gain a bonus to their Physical and Magical Defenses equal to `Half Mastery Rating` (min 1).
- **Undead Enhancement:** Grant bonus Feature Points equal to `Mastery Rating x 2` usable *only* for **Basic Enhancements** or **Defensive** features.
- **Aura of Dread:** Your `[Returned]` companions project a 30-ft aura of fear. Enemies within the aura suffer a penalty to Mental Defense and Resolve saves equal to `Half Mastery Rating` (min 1). Any enemy that starts its turn in the aura must make a Resolve save (DC 10 + Your Mastery Tier + Your Intellect Mod) or be **Frightened** for 1 round.

### Adept Tier Talents (Mastery 5-7)

- **Deadly Touch:** Grant bonus Feature Points equal to `Mastery Rating x 2` usable *only* for **Combat Abilities** (e.g., Paralysis, Disease, Incorporeal Touch).
- **Supernatural Resilience:** Your `[Returned]` companions gain Damage Reduction equal to `Half Mastery Rating` (bypassed by positive energy or magic). If the companion is `Incorporeal`, it gains a bonus to its Deflection bonus (vs. non-magical attacks) equal to `Full Mastery Rating` instead.
- **Energy Drain:** Grant bonus Feature Points equal to `Mastery Rating x 2` usable *only* to purchase or enhance **Energy Drain** or **Ability Drain** features.
- **Malevolence:** As a standard action, one of your `[Returned]` companions can attempt to dominate or possess an adjacent creature. The target must make a Resolve save (vs. the `Aura of Dread` DC). On a failure, the companion controls the target for `Half Mastery Rating` (min 1) rounds. This ability recharges in 5 rounds.
- **Ghostly Manifestation:** As a **Standard Action**, your `[Returned]` companions can toggle between a `Corporeal` and `Incorporeal` state. This has no duration and no recharge.
- **Deathless Master:** Your `[Returned]` companions are immune to any effect that would turn or destroy undead of a certain power level. Additionally, they become immune to positive energy damage.
- **Grave Chill Embodiment:** *(Prereq: Aura of Dread)* The companion's `Aura of Dread` now also functions as an aura of extreme cold. Enemies in the aura take `Half Mastery Rating` (min 1) cold damage at the start of their turn and are `Slowed` (no save).

### Master Tier Talents (Mastery 8-10)

- **Master's Fortitude:** Grant bonus Feature Points equal to `Mastery Rating x 2` usable for any **Combat** or **Supernatural** abilities.
- **Dominating Presence:** *(Prereq: Malevolence)* Your `Malevolence` ability can now target any creature within 30 ft (not just adjacent) and the duration is doubled to `Full Mastery Rating` rounds.
- **Perfected Drain:** *(Prereq: Energy Drain)* The save DC for the companion's `Energy Drain` or `Ability Drain` abilities increases by `+Half Mastery Rating`. Furthermore, any healing or temporary Hit Points gained by the companion from the drain is also granted to you (the master).

## Grave Lord Tree

*Focuses on the master's direct command and necromantic synergy.*

### Novice Tier Talents (Mastery 1-4)

- **Grave Command:** As a **Swift Action**, you can command up to `Half Mastery Rating` (min 1) `[Returned]` companions. Each commanded companion can immediately take one **Standard Action**.
- **Bolster Undead:** As a standard action, you can channel negative energy to heal one `[Returned]` companion within 60 ft for `Mastery Rating x 5` Hit Points. This ability recharges in `1d4+1` rounds.

### Adept Tier Talents (Mastery 5-7)

- **Grave Handler:** The cost to maintain `[Returned]` companions from your Mastery Capacity Pool is halved (rounded up).
- **Shared Immunities:** While within 30 ft of a `[Returned]` companion, you gain its **full immunity** to Poison, Sleep, Paralysis, and Stunning effects.
- **Animate Thralls:** As a standard action, you can animate `Half Mastery Rating` (min 1) humanoid corpses within 100 ft. These corpses rise as `[Returned]` companions two Tiers lower than your Mastery Tier and are `[Created]` companions. They last for `Mastery Rating` rounds before crumbling. This ability recharges in 5 rounds.
- **Negative Energy Flood:** *(Prereq: Bolster Undead)* Your `Bolster Undead` talent now affects **all** `[Returned]` companions within 60 ft. Furthermore, any *enemy* in the area (who is not undead) takes negative energy damage equal to the amount healed and is **Weakened** for `Half Mastery Rating` rounds (Vigor save for half damage and negates Weakened).
- **Undying Loyalty:** Your `[Returned]` companions within 100 ft of you gain `Fast Healing` equal to your `Half Mastery Rating` (min 1) as long as they are conscious and loyal.

### Master Tier Talents (Mastery 8-10)

- **Lord of the Undead:** *(Prereq: Grave Command)* Your `Grave Command` talent now affects **all** `[Returned]` companions under your control (no numerical limit) *and* grants them a **full turn's worth of actions** (Standard, Move, Swift) instead of just a Standard Action.
- **Grave Call:** If a `[Returned]` companion is destroyed, you can use this ability as a Standard Action. The companion's essence is pulled from its remains (regardless of distance) and reforms in an adjacent square to you at 50% of its maximum HP. A companion can only be affected by this ability once per long rest.
