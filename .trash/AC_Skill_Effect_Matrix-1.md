# THE ÆTHERIUM CODEX — SKILL × EFFECT COMPATIBILITY MATRIX
## Raw Bucket Assignment — Can This Skill Produce This Effect?
### Working Document — April 16, 2026

---

## METHOD

Each cell answers ONE question: can a trained practitioner using this skill produce this effect through technique alone (Tier 1-2), without constructing a Spell Sentence (Tier 3)?

**✓** = Yes, this skill can produce this effect through trained application.
**—** = No, this skill cannot produce this effect.

No hierarchy. No primary/secondary. Just compatibility.

## OBSERVATIONS

### Operate Is the Universal Interface

Operate scores 23 of 24 (only missing Feature). This is correct — Operate doesn't produce effects, it ACTIVATES DEVICES that produce effects. It's the mundane Variable. The skill's versatility IS the point. A character with high Operate and access to diverse Kan-tech can do almost anything — but they're always dependent on having the right device.

### Insight Is the Narrowest Skill

Insight scores 2 of 24 (Senses and Protection). It does ONE thing: perceive the metaphysical. Its narrowness is a feature — it's the preternatural sense that feeds data to Metaphysics for interpretation. Breadth would dilute its identity.

### Discovery Is Nearly As Narrow

Discovery scores 4 of 24 (Nullify, Weaken, Senses, and nothing else). Pure perception and analysis. Finding the linchpin. It's the Mental equivalent of Insight — narrow by design, powerful in its lane.

### Medicine and Artifice Are the Broadest Mental Skills

Both score 14 of 24. Medicine covers biological systems comprehensively (damage through anatomy, healing, conditions, immunity through vaccination, time manipulation through metabolic control, transformation through surgery). Artifice covers mechanical/structural systems equivalently. They're mirrors of each other — one for organisms, one for objects.

### Performance Is the Broadest Social Skill

Performance scores 13 of 24. It covers deception, crowd manipulation, morale, pacing, artistic creation, counter-narrative, and emotional resonance. This matches the setting — Unity Academy and Chiaroscuro Institute both weaponize Performance extensively.

### The Six Universal Effects Show Their Universality

| Effect | Skills That Can Produce It |

| Protection | 13 of 16 |

These six appear across nearly every skill. This matches the SRD v3's designation of Affliction, Damage, Movement, Protection, Senses, and Transform as "natural to all five planes." The skill system mirrors the planar universality at the mundane level.

|---|---|

| Immunity | 3 (Medicine, Operate, Praxis) |
| Immortality | 3 (Operate, Praxis, Conveyance via vehicle) |

The rarest effects are the most powerful and exotic ones — requiring either specialized equipment (Operate/Conveyance interfacing with devices) or deep metaphysical mastery (Praxis). This is correct — mortals don't casually achieve immortality or time travel through basic training.
