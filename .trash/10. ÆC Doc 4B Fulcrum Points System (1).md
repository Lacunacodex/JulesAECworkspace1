**FULCRUM POINTS**

The Distance-Based Metacurrency for Spine Manipulation

Document 4B --- Vertebra 4: Clocks (Companion)

*One currency. One mechanic. One spine. Different reach.*

**WHAT FULCRUM POINTS ARE**

Normal play operates through the spine's natural propagation sequence.
Actions at Vertebra 6 (Adventures) produce consequences that propagate
through the Feedback Loop (Vertebra 7), modify the Schema (Vertebra 1),
shift Pressure Points (Vertebra 2), reshape Fronts (Vertebra 3), advance
Clocks (Vertebra 4), and generate new Symptoms (Vertebra 5). This
natural propagation works. It takes time. Each vertebra processes in
sequence.

Fulcrum Points let the participant skip the natural sequence. A Fulcrum
Point spend reaches directly into a vertebra other than the one the
participant is currently operating at. The cost scales with distance ---
how many vertebrae are skipped between the participant's position and
the vertebra being manipulated. The further the reach, the greater the
cost and the larger the unintended side effects.

**POOL GENERATION**

Fulcrum Points are generated by internal complexity. The more
contradictory the entity's Schema, the more narrative energy it
produces, the more FP it generates per cycle.

**The Formula**

FP per session = (count of outlier scores) + (count of asymmetric
splits) + (count of pattern breaks).

This is the same pressure point count produced by the extraction
procedure at Vertebra 2 (Document 2A). The extraction procedure and the
FP generation procedure are the same operation. The pressure points that
generate the most interesting story are the same pressure points that
generate the most narrative energy. Character depth IS mechanical power.

**Generation Across Entity Types**

  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------
  **Player           FP from the player's own Schema pressure point
  Characters**       count. Typical range: 4-8 FP. Refreshes each
                     session.

  **Minor NPCs**     1-2 FP. Flat, do not track. Assume they can surprise
                     the party once per encounter.

  **Notable NPCs**   4-8 FP from personal Schema. Tracked on the
                     Assessment Card.

  **Major NPCs**     8-15+ FP from personal Schema + positional +
                     institutional pools. Tracked on front sheets.

  **Institutions**   FP from the institutional Schema's pressure points

                     institution.

  **Territories**    FP from the territory's Zoetrope tension, isotope
                     asymmetries, and factional conflicts. Determines
                     regional unpredictability.

  **Gods**           FP from divine aspect tension. Flows to resonant
                     mortals. Aíon's generation is infinite --- the
                     Smoothing Effect is continuous FP expenditure.
  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------

**Composite Pools**

An NPC operating within an institution within a territory under a god's
resonance draws from all applicable pools: Personal + Positional +
Institutional + Territorial + Divine. Each layer compounds. Headmasters
are the most narratively potent entities short of gods because every
layer generates energy. Adventurers participate in the same stacking
through their own college affiliation, territorial presence, and divine
resonance.

**MASTERY AS REACH LIMITER**

Mastery determines maximum distance, not pool size. A complex Schema
generates many FP. Mastery determines how far those FP can reach.

  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------
  **Low Mastery      Distance 0-1 maximum. Can manipulate personal
  (1-3)**            observation collapses and interact with adjacent
                     vertebrae. Cannot reach into the spine's deeper
                     structures.

  **Mid Mastery      Distance 0-2 maximum. Can manipulate clocks and
  (4-6)**            pressure points. Begins to perceive the spine's
                     intermediate architecture.

  **High Mastery     Distance 0-3 maximum. Can manipulate fronts and
  (7-9)**            relationship edges. Perceives the spine's full
                     structural depth. Political and institutional-scale
                     influence.

  **Setting-Scale    Distance 0-∞. Can interact with regulatory systems.
  (10+)**            Perceives and can manipulate the cosmological
                     architecture. Praxis Instruments, the Marked of
                     Aíon, Kyril-tier entities.
  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------

A low-Mastery character with a complex Schema generates many FP they can
only spend at short distances. This is intentional --- they have
internal energy (narrative complexity) but lack the experiential wisdom
to channel it at structural depths. They can boost rolls and place seeds
prolifically. They cannot manipulate relationship edges or override
regulatory systems. The energy is real. The reach is earned.

This may create a design tension: complex Schemas at low Mastery
generate FP that cannot be fully spent. Whether this feels like
frustrated potential (interesting) or wasted resources (punishing)
requires playtesting. The intentional reading is that a young,
contradictory character is a powder keg of narrative energy that they
cannot yet direct --- which is dramatically correct. The energy leaks
through their behavior rather than through deliberate spine
manipulation.

**THE BIDIRECTIONAL RESPONSE**

No explicit GM pool. The spine's natural consequence propagation handles
the response.

**How It Works**

When a participant spends FP and skips vertebrae, they create a
discontinuity. The vertebrae that were skipped still need to process the
change. They do not disappear --- they are deferred. The spine catches
up. The deferred processing produces consequences the participant did
not plan for because the natural sequence was bypassed.

A clock retarded by a Distance 2 spend: the front driving that clock
does not know its clock was manipulated. The front continues taking
faction turn actions as if the clock is where it expects. The mismatch
between expectation and reality produces a discontinuity that the spine
processes as an unintended side effect.

**Side Effect Scale**

  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------
  **Distance 0**     Minimal side effects. The spend operated within the
                     current vertebra. No vertebrae were skipped. The
                     resolution shifted slightly but the spine's sequence
                     was not disrupted.

  **Distance 1**     Minor side effects. One vertebra was skipped. The
                     change happened without normal symptom presentation
                     or without normal consequence propagation. The gap
                     is small but detectable.

  **Distance 2**     Moderate side effects. Two vertebrae skipped. The
                     change bypassed normal clock advancement or pressure
                     extraction. The affected front may act on outdated
                     information, producing inappropriate responses.

  **Distance 3**     Significant side effects. Three vertebrae skipped. A
                     relationship was altered or a Schema element
                     revealed without the intermediate steps. The network
                     around the altered edge rebalances, producing
                     cascading adjustments the participant did not
                     anticipate.

  **Distance ∞**     Maximum side effects. The regulatory system was
                     overridden. The Smoothing Effect was momentarily
                     punctured. Aíon's maintenance architecture noticed
                     the discontinuity and responded. The deferred
                     consequence is cosmological in character --- reality
                     seam disturbance, planar bleed amplification, or
                     Smoothing overcorrection.
  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------

**GM Processing**

When a participant spends FP, the GM notes which vertebrae were skipped.
Between sessions (or at the next dramatically appropriate moment), the
GM asks: "What would the skipped vertebrae produce if they processed
this change through normal sequence?" The answer is the side effect. The
oracle tables (Document 5B) can assist --- roll the deferred consequence
as an oracle prompt to generate the side effect when the GM needs
inspiration.

**PLANAR EXPRESSION**

The character's isotope profile determines the narrative expression of
the spend. Same mechanic, different description based on which plane's
isotopes the character channels.

  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------
  **Borderlands      The spend expresses as a physical feat. Impossible
  dominant**         strength, structural manipulation, material
                     transformation. The character reaches into reality's
                     physical substrate.

  **Verdant Font     The spend expresses as a biological event.
  dominant**         Accelerated healing, mutation, ecological
                     manipulation. The character channels life-force
                     directly.

  **Synod dominant** The spend expresses as consequence manifestation.
                     Debts called, entropy reversed or accelerated,
                     thermodynamic manipulation. The character channels
                     the plane of endings.

  **Hidden Forge     The spend expresses as catalytic process. Impossible
  dominant**         engineering, alchemical transformation, system
                     optimization. The character channels the universal
                     mediator.

  **Palimpsest       The spend expresses as probability manipulation. The
  dominant**         classic "luck" expression. Coincidences that aren't
                     coincidences. The character collapses superposition
                     states deliberately.
  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------

A Wildpath Warden retarding a clock does it by accelerating biological
growth that blocks the enemy's advance --- Verdant Font expression. A
Deepguard engineer retarding the same clock reinforces a structural
bottleneck --- Hidden Forge expression. Same mechanical effect (clock
retarded one segment). Different narrative description. Different
isotope channel. The unified system produces unified mechanics with
diverse expression automatically.

The character does not need to declare which isotope channel they use.
The character's dominant planar profile makes the selection automatic.
The GM describes the expression based on the character's established
isotope identity.

**THE COSMOLOGICAL CONNECTION**

Fulcrum Points are not an abstract game mechanic. They are the
Palimpsest made playable.

The Palimpsest holds superposition states. Every moment exists in
multiple potential configurations until observation collapses it into
one. Normal play collapses superpositions through standard resolution
(dice rolls, GM adjudication). Fulcrum Points are the participant's
ability to CHOOSE which state collapses rather than letting the dice
decide.

At Distance 0: choosing which state a single roll collapses to.
Manipulating the Palimpsest locally. At Distance 2: choosing which state
an entire clock's trajectory collapses to. Manipulating the Palimpsest
at organizational scale. At Distance ∞: choosing which physics govern
the probability space. Manipulating the Palimpsest at cosmological
scale.

The Smoothing Effect is Aíon continuously spending infinite FP to
maintain coherence. When a participant overrides Smoothing, they
momentarily outspend Aíon at one specific point. The Smoothing Effect
reasserts because Aíon's generation is functionally infinite. The
participant punched through momentarily. The maintenance resumes. The
deferred consequence is the architecture noticing.
