**ISOTOPE-TO-SYMPTOM**

Expression Guide

Document 5C --- Vertebra 5: Symptoms (Companion)

*How the 104 isotopes across five planes express as observable effects*

**THE EXPRESSION PRINCIPLE**

"Fire" is not a symptom. "Borderlands Fire-03 (volcanic mineral
combustion)" is a symptom. "Verdant Font Fire-07 (biological fever
cascade)" is a different symptom. The Spell Sentence provides the WHAT.
The isotope profile provides the HOW. This document bridges the two.

The full 104-isotope catalog with complete stat derivations is
maintained in the Dual Periodic Table companion volume. This guide
provides the five layer profiles and representative examples sufficient
for GM use at the table.

**LAYER 1: BORDERLANDS --- INORGANIC CHEMISTRY**

Isotopes express as mineral, metal, stone, crystal, and elemental
material. The physical world operating under unfamiliar but internally
consistent rules.

  ---------------- ---------------- -------------------------------------------
  **Spell Effect** **Expression**   **Observable Symptom Examples**

  **Damage**       *Structural      Walls cracking along mineral fault lines.
                   failure*         Metal fatiguing at stress points that glow
                                    faintly. Stone dissolving into powder.

  **Growth**       *Crystal         Mineral accretion on surfaces. Crystal
                   formation*       growths erupting from soil. Architecture
                                    slowly being encased in geological
                                    deposits.

  **Protection**   *Geological      Stone becoming unnaturally dense. Ground
                   hardening*       solidifying around defended perimeters.
                                    Mineral veins rearranging into
                                    reinforcement.

  **Transform**    *Material        Iron rusting to copper overnight. Stone
                   transmutation*   becoming glass-smooth. Material composition
                                    shifting without visible cause.

  **Healing**      *Mineral         Cracked stone reknitting. Corroded metal
                   restoration*     returning to polished state. The earth
                                    healing its own wounds.

  **Illusion**     *Geological      Ore veins appearing where none exist. Stone
                   mirage*          facades concealing different material
                                    beneath. The earth lying about its
                                    composition.
  ---------------- ---------------- -------------------------------------------

**Sensory profile:** Borderlands symptoms feel geological. They operate
on the timescale of the physical world. The observer's instinct is to
explain them through material science.

**LAYER 2: VERDANT FONT --- ORGANIC CHEMISTRY**

Isotopes express as vegetation, fauna behavior, biological mutation, and
ecological shift. Life itself operating under accelerated or modified
rules.

  ---------------- ----------------- -------------------------------------------
  **Spell Effect** **Expression**    **Observable Symptom Examples**

  **Damage**       *Biological       Rapid decomposition of living tissue.
                   degradation*      Plants wilting in accelerated time. Fauna
                                     dying without visible injury.

  **Growth**       *Vegetation       Overnight plant growth filling a room.
                   surge*            Roots breaking through stone. Fungal
                                     networks spreading visibly.

  **Transform**    *Mutation*        Creatures displaying unfamiliar
                                     adaptations. Plants producing unrecognized
                                     fruits. Biological forms matching no
                                     catalog.

  **Healing**      *Regeneration*    Wounds closing with visible tissue growth.
                                     Diseased tissue consumed and replaced by
                                     healthy growth. The body rewriting itself.

  **Affliction**   *Contamination*   Disease patterns following planar bleed
                                     zones. Parasitic organisms in sterile
                                     environments. Infections responding only to
                                     alchemical treatment.

  **Summon**       *Swarm            Creatures gathering without cause. Insect
                   convergence*      swarms forming geometric patterns.
                                     Biological forces concentrating at a
                                     specific location.
  ---------------- ----------------- -------------------------------------------

**Sensory profile:** Verdant Font symptoms feel organic. They involve
smell, texture, and movement. The explanations work for one symptom but
break down as the pattern accumulates.

**LAYER 3: SYNOD OF ENDINGS --- THERMODYNAMICS**

Isotopes express as decay, debt, accumulated cost, and temporal weight.
The past catching up. Consequences arriving that were deferred.

  ----------------- ---------------- -------------------------------------------
  **Spell Effect**  **Expression**   **Observable Symptom Examples**

  **Damage**        *Accelerated     Structures aging decades overnight. New
                    entropy*         construction showing years of weathering
                                     within days.

  **Chronos**       *Temporal        Clocks running at different speeds in
                    distortion*      adjacent rooms. Seasons progressing
                                     unevenly across a territory.

  **Senses**        *Consequence     Scars glowing. Old wounds aching. Places of
                    detection*       past violence radiating detectable residue.

  **Affliction**    *Karmic          Individuals experiencing consequences of
                    accumulation*    forgotten actions. Institutions paying for
                                     decisions made generations ago.

  **Healing**       *Entropy         Aged objects restored. Weathered stone
                    reversal*        returning to freshly quarried appearance.
                                     Time flowing backward in localized pockets.

  **Immortality**   *Undying         A fire burning without fuel. A wound
                    process*         neither healing nor killing. Something
                                     trapped between states, unable to complete
                                     its transition.
  ----------------- ---------------- -------------------------------------------

**Sensory profile:** Synod symptoms feel temporal. They involve
wrongness in time --- things too old, too new, frozen, or carrying
weight from events that happened elsewhere.

**CROSS-LAYER SYMPTOMS**

When multiple planes express simultaneously, symptoms blend
characteristics. Cross-layer symptoms defy categorization within a
single branch of natural philosophy.

  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------
  **Borderlands +    Living mineral. Crystal formations that grow
  Verdant Font**     biologically. Stone that bleeds. Metal that heals
                     wounds.

  **Verdant Font +   Ecological debt. An ecosystem paying for centuries
  Synod**            of abuse in one season. Species going extinct in
                     hours.

  **Synod + Hidden   Engineered entropy. Machines designed to fail at a
  Forge**            specific moment. Self-destructing infrastructure.

  **Hidden Forge +   Schematic contradictions. Machines whose blueprints
  Palimpsest**       describe a different device than the one built.

  **Palimpsest +     Reality seam. Setting A architecture embedded in
  Borderlands**      Setting B geology. Kan-tech tangled with ancient
                     artifacts. The Crash Site's signature.
  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------

Cross-layer symptoms are rare in stable territories but common in
transition zones and planar bleed areas. The City of Folly produces them
constantly.

**THE TRANSLATION PROCEDURE**

Five steps from oracle output to isotope-grounded description.

  -------- ---------------- --------------------------------------------------
  **\#**   **Step**         **Procedure**

  **1**    **Identify the   Which Spell Sentence effect? From the clock's
           effect**         symptoms or the oracle's Axis 2.

  **2**    **Identify the   Which alchemy layer? From the driving college's
           plane**          alignment or oracle's Axis 1.

  **3**    **Check the      What is the local isotope distribution? The
           territory**      territory's dominant layer shapes expression even
                            for non-native effects.

  **4**    **Select the     Consult the layer table. Effect + Layer = visual
           expression**     vocabulary. The specific form depends on local
                            ecology and current conditions.

  **5**    **Add            Not 'vegetation surge in sector 7.' Instead: 'The
           human-scale      baker's garden has grown through his kitchen wall
           detail**         overnight.'
  -------- ---------------- --------------------------------------------------

The translation takes seconds once familiar with the five layer
profiles. Effect + Layer + Territory + Human Scale = an engaging seed.

  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------

                     biological armor. Symbiotic organisms forming living
                     walls.

  **Torment's        The mesa's stone hardening. Mineral deposits
  Crown**            rearranging into structural reinforcement. The land
                     refusing to yield.

  **City of Folly**  All five expressions flickering simultaneously.
                     Ward-stones humming with Kan-tech resonance AND
                     growing biological crust AND showing temporal
                     stabilization.

  **Port Janus**     Trade contracts self-enforcing. Market regulations
                     gaining unusual compliance. Protection through
                     commercial systems.

Same effect. Five territories. Five completely different visual and
narrative experiences. The isotope profile IS the visual filter.
