## Document 4: Talent Trees as Transmission Specifications
### Canonical Draft — April 16, 2026

## PREAMBLE

Documents 1-3 established that every action is an effect from the 23+Variable canonical list, delivered through a transmission medium. The Spell Sentence grammar is universal — what changes between a spell, a sword strike, and a social argument is the cable, not the signal.

Document 4 specifies the combat petal: the talent trees that unlock physical transmission dialects. Each tree opens grammatical constructions the body can produce — effects, additives, Construction Pool parameters — that were inaccessible before training. A character without the Finesse Style Crux cannot compose precision expressions through their body. After training, the channel opens.

This document covers the 23 confirmed combat petal trees across four categories.

# PART I: THE TRANSMISSION FRAMEWORK

## Three Transmission Media

The Spell Sentence grammar produces identical effects regardless of medium. What differs is the cable:

| Medium | Scope | FP Source | Constraints |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Æther** (direct magical) | Micro (internal pattern) + Macro (environmental interface) | Metaphysical FP (ÆP) | Formal composition, isotope access, Mastery cap |
| **Body** (physical technique) | Middle — the body IS the cable | Physical FP (Stamina) | Body's physical properties, weapon material, trained reflexes |
| **Item** (material substrate) | Middle — the item IS the cable | Item's charge pool (drip recharge) | Material properties, Formation Stage, housing architecture |

Talents specify what the body-as-cable can transmit. Without training, the body can only produce the most basic expressions: raw Damage through an unrefined strike, basic Movement through walking. Training opens channels for complex compositions — linked effects, additive-modified expressions, expanded Construction Pool access.

## What a Talent Actually Does

A talent is NOT a pre-built technique. It is a TRANSMISSION PARAMETER UNLOCK.

| Talent Component | What It Unlocks |
|---|---|
| **Crux talent** | Opens the channel — you can now transmit on this frequency |
| **Novice progression** | Bandwidth increases — more effects composable through this channel |
| **Journeyman progression** | Signal fidelity increases — higher ESL, more CP access, cleaner output |
| **Master progression** | Full channel capacity — complex linked compositions, maximum ESL, full Additive access |

Players don't select from a menu of techniques. They compose physical expressions within the grammatical constraints their talents have unlocked, the same way a spellcaster composes rotes within their available grammar.

## The Physical Expression Formula

A physical technique decomposes identically to a spell:

**Physical Expression = Effect + Isotope (weapon/body material) + Construction Pool (geometry) + Additives/Subtractives**

The key differences from magical composition:

| Parameter | Magical (Tier 3) | Physical (Tier 1) |
|---|---|---|
| Effect selection | Any effect the caster has dots in | Only effects unlocked by talent trees |
| Isotope | External materials, foci | Weapon material or body composition |
| CP geometry | Full CP access based on formula | Restricted by talent tier — starts at melee/touch/instant, expands with training |
| ESL cap | min(Effect Tier, Isotope Tier, Mastery) | Skill ranks + relevant talent tier |
| FP source | Metaphysical FP | Physical FP |
| Composition method | Conscious, deliberate | Trained reflex, unconscious |

## Tempo Costs by Commitment

Physical expressions cost tempo based on complexity, not power:

| Commitment | Tempo | What It Means |
|---|---|---|
| Light (1) | Simple, single-effect expression | A basic strike, a quick parry, a short movement |
| Standard (2) | Moderate composition — linked effects or moderate CP | A Power Attack (Damage + Enhanced Trait linked), a precise shot, a combat maneuver |
| Heavy (3) | Complex composition — multiple links, high CP, or cross-pillar | A Stunning Fist (Damage + Affliction linked), a Whirlwind Attack (Damage + Burst geometry), a Spellstrike |

# PART II: THE CRUX & DROPLET MODEL

## Three-Tier Hierarchy

All combat talents are organized into three tiers representing progression from basic competency to specialized mastery:

**Tier 1 — Foundational Talents.** The basic building blocks. Not tied to any specific Precept. A character's initial selections almost always come from this group. These are the Crux talents that open channels.

**Tier 2 — Core Precept Talents (Droplets).** Specialized abilities within a specific tree that flow into and enhance the Crux. Each Droplet requires its parent Crux and often has additional prerequisites (Mastery rating, skill ranks, or prior Droplet mastery).

**Tier 3 — General Martial Talents.** Broad category of talents not tied to a specific Crux but may have Foundational prerequisites. Any character can purchase these to customize their build. Cross-tree talents live here.

## The Prerequisite Chaining Discount

The XP cost of a talent is reduced for each prerequisite in its direct chain. This rewards deep investment in a single tree:

- The Crux talent is "Level 0" — full cost
- First Droplet in the chain — full cost (no discount)
- Second Droplet — -1 XP discount
- Third Droplet — -2 XP discount
- And so on

This produces diminishing costs for deep specialization. A character who invests heavily in a single tree pays less per talent the deeper they go, incentivizing commitment over breadth.

## The Unique Talent Rule

Talents can only be taken once unless their description explicitly states otherwise. Talents marked "can be taken multiple times" (like Expanded Reserves) are explicitly noted.

## Stances as a Talent Subtype Tag

A Stance is not its own tree. It is a TAG applied to specific talents within other trees. A talent tagged [Stance] represents a sustained combat posture that modifies the character's expression profile while active. Only one Stance can be active at a time. Switching Stances costs 1 tempo (Light action).

Rage (within the Durability tree) is a [Stance]. Defensive Stance (within Agile Defense) is a [Stance]. Phalanx Formation (within Way of the Phalanx) is a [Stance].

## 1. Two-Handed Style

**Philosophy:** Overwhelming force through a single, heavy weapon. Maximum energy per strike. The embodiment of "I hit it harder."

**Crux Talent:** Power Attack
**Core Mechanic:** Take a scaling penalty to attack rolls to gain a bonus to damage. This IS a Damage + Weaken (self, accuracy) Subtractive composition — you're trading signal precision for signal amplitude.

**Transmission Specifications:**

| Tier | Effects Unlocked | Additives Unlocked | CP Access |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Novice** | Damage (amplified), Enhanced Trait (Might, self), Move Object (Shove) | Overwhelming (-4, all-or-nothing) | Melee, Individual, Instantaneous |
| **Journeyman** | Damage (area — Cleave geometry), Affliction (knockdown through impact) | Multiattack (Cleave — arcing strike hits adjacent), Split | Melee, Burst (small — weapon reach arc) |
| **Master** | Damage (maximized — Vital Strike), Nullify (Sunder — destroying equipment/expressions through force) | Penetrating (ignoring Soak partially) | Melee, Individual (single target, maximum force) |

**Representative Decompositions:**

| Technique | Spell Sentence Decomposition |
|---|---|
| Power Attack | Damage + Subtractive: Reduced Accuracy. Amplitude increases proportionally to accuracy penalty. |
| Cleave | Damage + Burst (small, weapon arc). Subtractive: Reduced Accuracy. Adjacent targets in arc take overflow damage. |
| Vital Strike | Damage at maximum ESL. Single target, single strike. No Multiattack — all energy into one expression. |
| Sunder | Nullify targeting item Pattern. Attack vs item's Physical Composite Defense. Success = item condition track damage. |
| Shove | Move Object (forced). Might-based attack vs target's Guard Defense. Success = target displaced. |

## 22. Martial Flexibility (Praxis Collegium Signature)

# PART VII: THE 18 ÆTHERFORGED PRECEPTS AS ASSEMBLY BLUEPRINTS

## Precepts Are Not Trees

The 18 Ætherforged Precepts (nine colleges, each with an Overt Way and a Covert Way) are NOT additional talent trees. They are ASSEMBLY BLUEPRINTS — recommended combinations of the trees from Parts III-VI that produce a specific martial philosophy.

A player doesn't buy the "Way of the Still Verse." They buy the specific Foundational Styles, Defensive Philosophies, and Tactical trees that the Way of the Still Verse prescribes. The Precept is the blueprint; the trees are the components.

## Assembly Structure

Each Precept specifies:

| Component | Description |
|---|---|
| **Crux Talents (1-2)** | The mandatory entry points. Without these, you're not practicing this Way. |
| **Supporting Trees (9-10)** | The recommended investment path. These trees compose the Precept's characteristic expression profile. |
| **Signature Compositions** | The linked expressions that define the Way's identity — the specific physical sentences this dialect produces. |

## Representative Precepts

### Mortis Ævum — Overt: The Way of the Still Verse

**Philosophy:** Methodical, surgical deconstruction. The anatomist's blade.

**Assembly:** Crux: Finesse Style, Precision Damage. Supporting: Two-Weapon Style (paired surgical instruments), Agile Defense, Combat Maneuvers (Disarm), Critical Specialization (Precision Branch), Medicine-informed anatomical targeting.

**Signature Composition:** Damage + Affliction linked, delivered through Finesse with Precision Damage conditional and Penetrating additive. The Still Verse practitioner's strike IS a surgical operation — they know exactly where to cut because Medicine informed their targeting, and Finesse routes the expression through precision rather than force.

### Stormpeak — Overt: The Way of the Phalanx

**Philosophy:** Disciplined, unyielding line-holding. Order as strategy.

**Assembly:** Crux: Shield Combat, Durability (Armor Training). Supporting: Way of the Phalanx, Commander (Aura of Command), Intimidation Style, Combat Maneuvers (Shove), Mounted Combat, Finesse Style (for defense).

**Signature Composition:** Protection (formation, Burst) + Enhanced Trait (allies) + Damage (coordinated strike). The Phalanx warrior's expression is SHARED — their Protection extends to adjacent allies, their Enhanced Trait aura buffs the formation, and their Damage expression synchronizes with the unit's collective strike.

### Stormpeak — Covert: The Way of the Vanguard

**Philosophy:** Brutal shock trooper. Break the line through overwhelming force.

**Assembly:** Crux: Two-Handed Style, Durability (Rage [Stance]). Supporting: Combat Maneuvers (Sunder), Critical Specialization (Brutal Branch), Intimidation Style, Finisher, Mounted Combat (Charge Focus).

**Signature Composition:** Damage (amplified) + Enhanced Trait (self, Rage) + Affliction (fear aura) linked. The Vanguard's expression is a WALL OF FORCE — maximum amplitude, intimidating presence, and the ability to break both equipment (Sunder) and morale (Intimidation) simultaneously.

### Deepguard — Overt: The Way of the Patient Snare

**Philosophy:** Master bodyguard. Defensive controller. Neutralize with minimal force.

**Assembly:** Crux: Combat Maneuvers (Control). Supporting: Finesse Style, Agile Defense, Shield Combat, Durability, Commander (Protector Auras).

**Signature Composition:** Affliction (Restrained/Disarmed/Prone) + Protection (ally-directed). The Patient Snare practitioner's expression is CONTROL — they impose conditions rather than deal damage, and their Protection expressions are directed outward to allies rather than inward to self.

### Praxis Collegium — Overt: The Way of the Scholar's Gambit

**Philosophy:** Universal adaptability. The right tool for every situation.

**Assembly:** Crux: Martial Flexibility, Ranged Style (Firearms). Supporting: Finesse Style, Two-Weapon Style (blade/firearm combo), Agile Defense, Durability, Commander (Tactician).

**Signature Composition:** Variable (bounded, through Martial Flexibility) + any Foundational Style's expressions as needed. The Scholar's Gambit practitioner's identity IS their flexibility — they don't have a signature strike because they can compose ANY strike the situation demands.
