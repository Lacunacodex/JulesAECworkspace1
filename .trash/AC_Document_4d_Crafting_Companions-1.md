## Document 4d: Specialized Petal Talents
### Canonical Draft — April 16, 2026

---

## PREAMBLE

Documents 4a-4c covered the combat petal (martial trees), general utility trees, and philosophy petal (magical trees). Document 4d covers the two remaining talent domains: the Five Artifice Paths (crafting) and the Companion system (creature acquisition and specialization).

Both domains use the same Spell Sentence grammar and transmission framework. Crafting talents unlock the ability to create items — composing expressions INTO material substrates. Companion talents unlock the ability to acquire, enhance, and command creatures — composing expressions THROUGH biological substrates.

# SECTION A: THE FIVE ARTIFICE PATHS

Source: SRD v3, Section: The Five Artifice Paths

All five paths use the Artifice skill as their primary skill. Path tiers align with proficiency bands: Novice (Artifice 1+, Formation Stage 1-4), Journeyman (Artifice 5+, Formation Stage 5-7), Master (Artifice 8+, Formation Stage 8-10).

**Artifice Check = d20 + (Artifice Rank × 2) + Relevant Attribute + Modifiers**

---

## Path 1: The Mundane Artisan

**Discipline:** Physical operations — shaping, cutting, grinding, joining, finishing, assembly.
**Primary Attributes:** Might (force operations), Finesse (precision operations), Wits (reactive decision-making).

**What it does:** Works with the physical properties of materials. Expertise in the real periodic table — hardness, tensile strength, density, elasticity. Transforms raw material into functional objects through physical technique.

**Transmission Framework:** The Artisan transmits through physical force applied to material substrates. They don't compose Spell Sentence expressions — they prepare the CABLE that other expressions will travel through. The sword the Artisan forges has no enchantment, but its material properties (Tensile Strength → damage die, Hardness → Soak contribution, Density → weight class) determine how effectively it transmits the wielder's physical expressions.

| Tier | Capability |
|---|---|
| **Novice** | Masterwork quality (×1.0 multiplier). Read material physical properties through examination. +1 Competence modifier on physical operations. |
| **Journeyman** | Complex multi-component assembly. Advanced material processing. Working with Formation Stage 5-7 materials. |
| **Master** | Pinnacle-tier materials. Legendary-quality items. Formation Stage 8-10 access. The item's physical properties are maximized. |

---

## Path 2: The Enchanter

**Discipline:** Component installation — expressions, attachments, insets, charge systems.
**Primary Attributes:** Dominion (channeling capacity), Intellect (compatibility analysis).

**What it does:** Populates the housing the Artisan built. Installs Active Expressions (internal rotes), Attachments (sub-items with their own rotes), and Insets (spell sentence modifiers). The Enchanter is NOT creating magic from nothing — they're installing pre-composed expressions into prepared material substrates.

**Transmission Framework:** The Enchanter installs transmission channels into items. A sword without enchantment transmits only the wielder's physical expressions. A sword WITH an installed Damage + Affliction (Burning) expression transmits both the wielder's strike AND the item's own rote simultaneously.

| Tier | Capability |
|---|---|
| **Novice** | Single expression installation. Basic charge system setup. Simple housing configurations. |
| **Journeyman** | Multiple expression layering. Attachment installation. Complex housing with selective routing (Forge Links managing which expression fires through which output). |
| **Master** | Full housing architecture. Inset installation. Multi-expression linked compositions within a single item. The item IS a spellcaster in material form. |

---

## Path 3: The Alchemist (Crafting Path)

**Discipline:** Chemical operations — reagent processing, compound synthesis, alchemical treatment, transmutation.
**Primary Attributes:** Intellect (formula design), Acuity (process monitoring), Wits (reactive adjustments).

**What it does:** Processes materials through chemical and alchemical operations. Where the Artisan shapes through force, the Alchemist shapes through chemistry. Potions, poisons, mutagens, reagent preparation, material purification, isotope extraction.

**Transmission Framework:** Alchemical processes ARE Spell Sentence compositions delivered through chemical medium. A potion IS a Create + Healing expression stored in liquid substrate. A poison IS an Affliction expression delivered through chemical transmission. The Alchemist composes the expression, the chemical substrate carries it, the target's biology receives it.

| Tier | Capability |
|---|---|
| **Novice** | Basic potions, poisons, alchemical reagents. Simple single-effect compounds. |
| **Journeyman** | Complex multi-component compounds. Mutagen synthesis. Isotope extraction from raw materials. Material purification processes. |
| **Master** | Philosopher's Stone project. Master-tier transmutations. Alchemical compounds that rival formal spellcasting in complexity. Magnum Opus capstone. |

---

## Path 4: The Appraiser

**Discipline:** Analysis — material identification, expression decomposition, quality assessment, compatibility verification.
**Primary Attributes:** Acuity (perception), Intellect (analysis), Wits (pattern recognition).

**What it does:** The Two-Table Read. Reads BOTH the real periodic table (physical properties) AND the planar periodic table (isotope identity, formation stage, concordance). Decomposes existing expressions to identify their Spell Sentence construction. Quality control for crafting operations.

**Transmission Framework:** The Appraiser doesn't transmit — they RECEIVE. Their talent opens Senses channels specifically for material and expression reading. They see what an item IS at every layer: base material, active expressions, attachments, insets, formation stage, charge state, condition track position.

| Tier | Capability |
|---|---|
| **Novice** | Read physical properties (Hardness, Tensile Strength, Density). Identify material origin. Basic quality assessment. |
| **Journeyman** | Two-Table Read — identify both physical AND planar properties simultaneously. Expression Decomposition — reverse-engineer an item's Spell Sentence construction. Compatibility assessment for installations. |
| **Master** | Full provenance reading — identify the crafter, the crafting method, the material source, and the history of modifications. Identify flaws invisible to lower-tier analysis. Certification authority for institutional quality standards. |

---

## Path 5: The Scrivener

**Discipline:** Documentation — schematics, formulas, process records, reproducibility.
**Primary Attributes:** Intellect (systematic documentation), Acuity (precise observation).

**What it does:** Records the crafting process so it can be REPRODUCED by others. Where the Artisan builds one sword, the Scrivener documents how to build a thousand. Institutional crafting IS Scrivener output — factory production requires schematics.

**Transmission Framework:** The Scrivener composes Senses (output) — Create (document) expressions. Their documentation IS a stored transmission — a blueprint that allows another crafter to reproduce an expression they didn't personally design. The Scrivener is the industrial printing press of the crafting world.

| Tier | Capability |
|---|---|
| **Novice** | Basic schematics — record physical operations and simple assemblies. |
| **Journeyman** | Full expression documentation — record Enchanter installations, Alchemist formulas, compatibility requirements. Cross-discipline schematics. |
| **Master** | Institutional-grade reproducibility documentation. Isolation of critical variables. Quality standards that allow production lines to operate without master-tier supervision. |

## Path Interactions

Common combinations from SRD v3:

| Combination | Application |
|---|---|
| Artisan + Enchanter | Smith who also installs components. Builds the housing AND populates it. Most common two-path combo. |
| Appraiser + Alchemist | Analyst who also processes. Reads what it IS, then changes it. Research alchemist. |
| Alchemist + Enchanter | Compound specialist who also installs. Creates treatments AND integrates them. Production enchanter. |
| Scrivener + Appraiser | Documentarian who also analyzes. Reads processes AND records them. Institutional researcher. |
| Artisan + Alchemist | Physical + chemical operations in one practitioner. Broadest single-practitioner capability. |
| **Full Workshop** | All five paths employed together — institutional-scale crafting. Artisans shape, Alchemists process, Enchanters install, Appraisers certify, Scriveners document. |

# SECTION B: THE COMPANION SYSTEM

Source: Combined Companion Archetype & Creature Types document

The companion system has two layers: MASTER ARCHETYPES (how you acquire companions) and CREATURE TYPE PATHS (what kind of companion you specialize in). A character selects one Master Archetype and one or more Creature Type Paths.

---

## Layer 1: Master Archetypes (Acquisition Method)

### Contracts (Path of Binding)

**Crux:** Magical Contract — create magically binding agreements with creatures through ritual.
**Cost:** Stamina equal to target's EML AND ÆP equal to target's EML.
**Limitation:** Cannot bind creature with EML higher than your Mastery.
**Group Binding:** Every 10 identical creatures count as one of next higher EML for cost calculation.

**Transmission Framework:** The contract IS a Mind Link + Affliction (Compelled) expression bound through ritual rather than combat. The creature is bound by the expression's terms, not by loyalty. Contract trees expand enforcement (Ironclad Oath, Binding Mark, Unbreakable Vow), enhancement (Contractual Vigor, Boons), and versatility (Swift Binding, Multi-Binding, Transferable Contract).

### Empathy (Path of Bonding)

**Crux:** Empathetic Bond — form a permanent empathetic bond with a willing creature. Silent communication of emotions and simple concepts.

**Transmission Framework:** The bond IS a Mind Link (sustained, mutual) expression. Unlike Contracts, Empathy requires willingness — no coercion. The bonded creature stays because it chooses to. Bond trees expand the connection (telepathic link, shared senses, shared defenses) and the companion's capabilities.

### Psychic Contagion (Seeded / Thrallherd)

**Crux:** Implant Psychosometric Seed — implant a psychic seed in a target.

**Transmission Framework:** The seed IS a Mind Link (invasive) + Transform (mental, subtle) expression. Unity Academy's signature acquisition method. The target doesn't know they've been seeded. This is the most ethically fraught archetype — the companion doesn't consent or even know they're a companion. The Thrallherd network IS crowd combat infrastructure at institutional scale.

### Summons (Path of Creation)

**Crux:** Summon Creature — use the Summon effect. Base duration = Mastery × 10 rounds.

**Transmission Framework:** The summon IS the Summon effect from the 23+Variable list. The creature is composed from æther using the same character engine as players (Spell = Item = Creature). ESL caps Creature Mastery Level. Evolution Pool (Feature Points) determines the construct's capabilities.

## Layer 2: Creature Type Paths (Specialization)

Each path specializes in a specific biological/metaphysical category of companion. The Crux for each is "[Type] Specialization" — unlocking the Companion Ascension system for that creature type.

### Beast — Path of the Wild

**Specialization:** Natural animals and bestial creatures. The ranger, the beast master, the cavalry officer.

**Enhancement Trees:**
- Primal Enhancement (raw physical power — Primal Vigor, Sharpened Fangs)
- Bond Synergy (shared benefits — Shared Resilience, Pack Tactics)

### Arcaniform — Path of the Arcanist

**Specialization:** Magical and elemental creatures. The familiar master, the elemental binder.

**Enhancement Trees:**
- Arcane Symbiosis (spell integration — Spell Conduit, Arcane Synthesis)
- Resonant Casting (companion as magical focus — reduced EML, ÆP cost reduction)

### Floraform — Path of the Verdant

**Specialization:** Plant and fungal creatures. The grove warden, the mycologist, the Verdant Font specialist.

**Enhancement Trees:**
- Verdant Growth (resilience, regeneration, adaptive biology)
- Spore Network (communication and area control through fungal transmission)

### Forged — Path of the Artificer

**Specialization:** Constructed creatures. Golems, automatons, Kan-tech drones. The engineer's companion.

**Enhancement Trees:**
- Structural Enhancement (physical upgrades — armor plating, weapon mounts)
- System Integration (Operate-linked control — remote operation, multiple construct management)

### Humanoid — Path of Command

**Specialization:** Humanoid followers, retainers, cohorts. The commander, the cult leader, the employer.

**Enhancement Trees:**
- Leadership (morale and capability enhancement — Commander tree integration)
- Organization (multiple follower management — squad mechanics, delegation)

### Interstitial — Path of the Binder

**Specialization:** Planar entities, outsiders, creatures from between the planes. The demonologist, the angel caller, the Palimpsest navigator's companion.

**Enhancement Trees:**
- Planar Attunement (resistance sharing, planar transit capabilities)
- Essence Binding (investing your own Pattern into the companion — power at personal risk)

### Returned — Path of the Gravemaster

**Specialization:** Undead creatures, revenants, spirits. The necromancer, the Mortis Ævum death-priest.

**Enhancement Trees:**
- Undead Enhancement (Synod-channeled resilience, fear aura, entropic touch)
- Spirit Binding (incorporeal companions, possession-based tactics, spectral scouts)

### Anomalous — Path of the Harbinger

**Specialization:** Aberrations, mutations, creatures that don't fit other categories. Vespidan symbiotes, Lacunae entities, beings born from Reality Seams.

**Enhancement Trees:**
- Mutagenic Symbiosis (shared mutation — the companion's biology feeds the master's adaptation)
- Harbinger Attunement (Planar Strain resistance through anomalous familiarity)

## The Companion Ascension System

All companion types use the same advancement framework. Companions don't earn XP — they advance through the Companion Ascension system powered by the master's investment.

| Resource | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Mastery Capacity Pool** | Each companion occupies capacity equal to its EML. Higher Mastery = room for more/stronger companions. |
| **Feature Points** | Budget for companion customization. Decompose into Spell Sentence expressions — claws (Damage + Transform), wings (Movement), breath weapon (Damage + Burst), etc. |
| **Companion Condition Tracks** | Same four-pillar track system as players. Companions can be Winded, Shaken, Flustered, or Frayed just like characters. |

The critical architectural point: companions use the SAME character engine as players. A companion's claw attack IS a Damage expression transmitted through biological medium. A companion's breath weapon IS a Damage + Burst expression using isotope geometry. The Feature Point budget determines how many of these expressions the companion has access to. The companion's "talent trees" are their Feature Point purchases.
