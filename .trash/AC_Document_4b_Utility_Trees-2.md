# THE ÆTHERIUM CODEX — GENERAL & UTILITY TALENT TREES
## Document 4b: Non-Combat Transmission Specifications
### Canonical Draft — April 16, 2026

---

## PREAMBLE

Document 4 specified the combat petal — 23 trees defining martial physical expression. Document 4b covers the general and utility trees that operate outside or alongside combat. These trees unlock transmission channels for investigation, crafting, social maneuvering, stealth, perception, performance, survival, and defensive resilience.

These trees use the same transmission framework. Each Crux opens a channel. Each Droplet expands bandwidth. The Spell Sentence grammar is unchanged — what differs is the application domain.

Organization follows the four skill pillars. Trees that span multiple skills are listed under their primary skill.

---

# PART I: PHYSICAL PILLAR UTILITY TREES

## Acrobatics (Athletics)

**Crux:** Graceful Motion — add Mastery Rating to jumping, balancing, and tumbling checks.

**Channel Opens:** Movement (enhanced — flips, rolls, tumbling), Protection (reactive — Fortuitous Contortion negates criticals through body positioning), Illusion (self — appearing to be elsewhere through acrobatic misdirection).

| Talent | Effect Decomposition |
|---|---|
| Body Shield Fall | Protection (reactive, Triggered) — using another creature to absorb fall damage. Damage + Split. |
| Careful Movement | Movement + Protection linked — move through threatened areas without triggering reactions. First 10 feet of threatened movement is safe. |
| Eel's Grace | Protection (substitute attribute) — use Finesse instead of Might against Grapple. Re-routes the defensive transmission through a different governing stat. |
| Fortuitous Contortion | Protection (reactive, costs Stamina) — negate critical hit. Athletics check vs attack roll. The body twists to convert a devastating hit into a normal one. |
| Unfettered Freedom | Protection (passive) — add Mastery to defense against entangle/paralyze/impede effects. Sustained resistance to Movement-denying Afflictions. |

---

## Mobility (Athletics)

**Crux:** Fleet of Foot — base land speed increased by 5 feet, scaling with Mastery.

**Channel Opens:** Movement (enhanced speed, terrain navigation), Protection (movement-based defense).

| Talent | Effect Decomposition |
|---|---|
| Fleet of Foot | Movement + Enhanced Trait (self, speed). Permanent transmission upgrade — base Movement expression is stronger. |
| Careful Movement | Movement through threatened areas without provoking. Protection through positioning rather than armor. |
| Nimble Moves | Movement ignoring first 5 feet of difficult terrain. Nullify (environmental, partial) — negating terrain-based Movement penalties. |

---

## Endurance (Athletics)

**Crux:** Resilient Body — add Mastery to checks against fatigue, exhaustion, and harsh environments.

**Channel Opens:** Protection (sustained — environmental resistance), Enhanced Trait (self — pushing physical limits over extended periods).

This tree is the physical stamina foundation. Where Durability (combat petal) handles hit absorption, Endurance handles prolonged exertion, forced marches, sleep deprivation, and environmental exposure. The transmission is sustained Protection against the world's passive damage rather than reactive Protection against active attacks.

---

## Brawler (General Combat)

**Crux:** Improvised Weaponry — no penalties for attacking with improvised weapons. Treat improvised weapons as simple weapons for prerequisites.

**Channel Opens:** Damage (through any available object), Movement (thrown improvised weapons via Deadeye Improviser).

The Brawler tree turns ANY physical object into a viable transmission cable. Where other styles require specific weapon types, the Brawler transmits Damage through whatever is at hand — chairs, bottles, tools, environmental debris. This is the Setting B street fighter's tree.

# PART II: MENTAL PILLAR UTILITY TREES

## Alchemy (Artifice)

**Crux:** Alchemical Training — unlocks ability to create alchemical items and potions.

**Channel Opens:** Create (alchemical compounds), Transform (material state changes), Damage (bombs/splash weapons), Affliction (poisons), Healing (potions), Enhanced Trait (mutagens — via Mutagenic Formula cross-tree).

| Talent | Effect Decomposition |
|---|---|
| Alchemical Training | Create (Crux — opens the alchemical channel). No recipes granted, just the transmission capability. |
| Efficient Brewer | Create + Chronos (self — halved crafting time). The alchemist's process accelerates through expertise. |
| Infused Concoctions | Create + Variable (bounded, CML 1) — adding a single magical "word" to an alchemical creation. Cross-medium transmission: item substrate carries a magical expression. |
| Poisoner's Craft | Affliction (Crux — opens the toxicology sub-channel). Foundation for all poison creation. |
| Master Toxicologist | Affliction (enhanced — increased TN to resist). The poison's transmission becomes harder to block. |
| Magnum Opus | Create (Master capstone). Auto-succeed identification. Unlocks Philosopher's Stone project. Mastery 10 + Artifice 10 prerequisite — the peak of alchemical transmission. |

**Setting B Application:** Kan-tech chemical engineering IS alchemy transmitted through industrial process rather than mystical tradition. The Sovereign Exchange and Stormpeak both operate alchemical manufacturing at industrial scale. The Alchemy tree's transmission specifications are identical whether the practitioner is a robed scholar or a factory chemist.

## Mechanics / Trapmaking (Artifice)

**Crux (Mechanics):** Intuitive Mechanic — add half Mastery to checks for understanding, repairing, or disabling mechanical devices. Full Mastery if the device originates from a familiar culture/tradition.

**Crux (Trapmaking):** Trapmaking — unlocks ability to craft and set mundane traps.

**Channel Opens:** Nullify (disabling devices/traps), Create (building traps/mechanisms), Senses (reading device architecture), Healing (Jury-Rig — temporary item repair).

| Talent | Effect Decomposition |
|---|---|
| Intuitive Mechanic | Senses (enhanced, Artifice-routed) — reading mechanical systems. Cultural familiarity doubles the bonus — the Setting A clockmaker and the Setting B machinist both understand mechanisms, but each reads their own tradition's devices more fluently. |
| Jury-Rig | Healing (item, temporary) — functional repair lasting Mastery rounds before the fix fails. Cheap, fast, unreliable. |
| Trapmaking | Create + Affliction linked — building devices that impose conditions on those who trigger them. The trap IS a stored expression waiting for activation. |
| Tech Savvy | Senses (enhanced, cross-skill Artifice + Lore) — identifying unfamiliar technology. Half Mastery to both skills when analyzing unknown devices. |

## Field Medic (Survival)

**Crux:** Healer's Hands — pool of healing power equal to derived formula. Touch to restore HP. Passive: all HP restoration doubled.

**Channel Opens:** Healing (primary — HP restoration through touch), Panacea (condition treatment), Protection (psychological anchoring — pre-treating against hazards).

| Talent | Effect Decomposition |
|---|---|
| Healer's Hands | Healing (Crux — supernatural touch healing) + Enhanced Trait (passive — all healing output doubled). The body becomes a Healing transmission cable. |
| Cauterizing Draught | Healing (alternative material — alcohol as medical supply) + Affliction (minor — Sickened 1 round). Trade-off composition: heal more but impose a temporary condition. |

---

## Beast Tamer (Survival)

**Crux:** Beast Empathy — add Mastery Rating to checks involving natural animals and beasts.

**Channel Opens:** Mind Link (animal — emotional resonance with beasts), Senses (reading animal behavior), Enhanced Trait (animal companion — improving beast capabilities).

| Talent | Effect Decomposition |
|---|---|
| Beast Empathy | Mind Link (limited — emotional communication with animals). Not telepathy, but trained empathic resonance. |
| Gentle Hand | Mind Link (expanded — influence attitudes of animals and magical beasts with Intelligence 2 or less). Extends the empathic channel to more exotic creatures. |

This tree feeds into the Companion system (Document 4d). Beast Tamer provides the foundational channel for companion interaction; the Companion trees (Path of the Wild, etc.) provide the specific evolution and enhancement mechanics.

---

## Forager (Survival)

**Crux:** Harvest Parts (Anatomical Salvage) — increase yield of harvested creature components by Mastery Rating. Can harvest from creatures that normally provide nothing (constructs).

**Channel Opens:** Senses (material reading — identifying what a creature's remains contain), Create (crude — processing natural materials into components), Transform (harvesting — converting creature remains into usable materials).

This tree connects directly to the Five Artifice Paths. Forager provides the RAW MATERIALS that the Artisan shapes, the Alchemist processes, and the Enchanter populates. Without Forager, crafters must purchase materials from vendors rather than harvesting from defeated creatures.

---

## Wilderness Attunement (Survival)

Not explicitly a single Crux tree but a cluster of Survival-routed talents covering environmental navigation, weather reading, and natural hazard management. These talents unlock the Survival skill's broadest transmission applications — the effects that make wilderness exploration mechanically meaningful rather than hand-waved.

# PART III: SOCIAL PILLAR UTILITY TREES

## Music (Performance)

**Crux:** Captivating Melody — Performance check vs Mental Composite Defense to fascinate creatures within 30 feet. Maximum targets = Presence score. Mind-affecting, language-independent.

**Channel Opens:** Mind Link (emotional — fascination, emotional resonance with audience), Enhanced Trait (allies — inspiration through music), Affliction (crowd conditions through sustained performance), Chronos (pacing — controlling the emotional tempo of a scene).

| Talent | Effect Decomposition |
|---|---|
| Captivating Melody | Mind Link (area, Burst 30ft) + Affliction (Fascinated). Language-independent — the transmission bypasses verbal processing. |
| Harmonious Performance | Enhanced Trait (allies, Teamwork) — coordinated performance amplifying the output. When two musicians play together, the combined transmission exceeds either alone. |
| Lingering Performance | Chronos (duration extension) — performance effects persist for half-Mastery rounds after cessation. The signal echoes after transmission stops. |

| Talent | Effect Decomposition |
|---|---|
| Master Orator | Enhanced Trait (self, conditional — bonus when addressing crowds of 10+). The orator's transmission amplifies with audience size. |
| Compelling Voice | Mind Link (area, 30ft) + Affliction (Compelled to listen, Will save). Verbal transmission forces attention — targets must listen for at least one round. |

# PART IV: METAPHYSICAL PILLAR UTILITY TREES

## Spellcraft (Metaphysics)

**Crux:** Knowledgeable Spellcaster (Rote Caster) — choose one magical "word" (Effect or Subtype). When composing a spell including that word, effective Mastery is +1. Can be taken multiple times.

**Channel Opens:** Enhanced Trait (self, conditional — amplified Metaphysical transmission for specific Effects/Subtypes). Each purchase deepens one channel's fidelity.

This tree represents magical SPECIALIZATION — the opposite of Variable's breadth. Where the Praxis Collegium values flexibility, Spellcraft values depth. A caster with Focus (Fire) + Rote Caster (Damage) + Rote Caster (Fire) transmits Fire Damage at effectively Mastery+3 compared to an unspecialized caster.

## Chameleon (General)

**Crux:** Aptitude Focus — gain a single "floating" talent slot. Once per 10 minutes, meditate for 1 minute to fill with any talent you meet prerequisites for. Supernatural.

**Channel Opens:** Variable (bounded — replicating any talent's transmission temporarily). The Chameleon IS Martial Flexibility applied beyond the combat petal.

| Talent | Effect Decomposition |
|---|---|
| Aptitude Focus | Variable (talent-level — borrow any talent's transmission parameters temporarily). Cost: 1.5× normal XP of highest-mimicable talent. |
| Adaptive Mind | Enhanced Trait (self, permanent — reduced XP costs for skills and skill-based talents by 2). The Chameleon's learning speed is permanently accelerated. |

# PART V: DEFENSIVE TREES (CROSS-PILLAR)

These trees provide pillar-specific passive defense, available to any character. Each opens a sustained Protection channel on its specific pillar.

**Crux:** Hardy Constitution — add half Mastery to Physical Dodge and Deflection against poisons, diseases, and Vigor-targeting effects.

| Talent | Effect |
|---|---|
| Hardy Constitution | Protection (Physical, passive — enhanced defense vs biological threats) |
| Increased Fortitude | Enhanced Trait (self, permanent — HP pool increased by Mastery × 2). Can be taken multiple times. |

## Mental Resilience

**Crux:** Fortified Mind — add half Mastery to Mental Dodge and Deflection against mind-affecting effects.

| Talent | Effect |
|---|---|
| Fortified Mind | Protection (Mental, passive — enhanced defense vs psychic/mental effects) |
| Vigilant Mind | Protection (Mental, passive — immune to magical sleep, no fatigue from 24-hour wakefulness). Supernatural. |

| Talent | Effect |
|---|---|
| Unshakeable Poise | Protection (Social, passive — enhanced defense vs humiliation, goading, composure attacks) |

| Talent | Effect |
