# PART I: THE SHARED RESOLUTION FRAMEWORK

## What Is Identical Across All Four Pillars

Every statement in this section applies to Physical, Mental, Social, AND Metaphysical combat without exception.

**Tempo:** Wits + Mastery = beats per phrase. Same pool, same commitment spectrum (Light 1 / Standard 2 / Heavy 3), same phrase structure.

**FP Cost:** (ESL × 2) − 1 drawn from the relevant pillar pool.

**Attack Check:** d20 + Attribute + Skill + Modifiers vs. target's Composite Defense.

**Composite Defense:** Dodge + Deflection for the relevant pillar (Player's Guide V4.1).

**Damage Application:** Aggregated per phrase. At phrase end, total compared against Damage Threshold. Threshold exceeded = condition track movement.

**Five Degrees of Success:**

| Result | Degree | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| ≥ TN+10 OR natural 20 that succeeds | Critical Success | Full effect + isotope expression + 1 FP refund |
| ≥ TN | Success | Full effect |
| 1-4 below TN | Success with Flaw | Full effect + target gains free reaction trigger |
| 5-9 below TN | Failure | No effect |
| ≤ TN-10 OR natural 1 | Critical Failure | No effect + all engaged actors gain 1 bonus tempo |

**Reactions:** Triggered by specific events, cost FP not tempo, one per trigger, appropriate reactive effect required.

**Condition Tracks:** 5 steps, penalties of -1/-2/-5/-10/Incapacitated. Steps replace within a track, stack across tracks. 0 HP = immediate Incapacitated.

# PART II: THE MENTAL PILLAR (S0-S3)

## What Mental Combat IS

Mental combat is the application of trained intellect against complex systems, cognitive hazards, and environmental challenges. The "enemy" is usually a non-sentient system with its own Resolve pool — a cipher, a pathogen, a Kan-tech lock, a forensic mystery — that must be worn down through sustained intellectual labor.

When the environment attacks back (memetic hazards, reality bleed, psychic intrusion, system counter-measures), the character defends with their Clarity track and Resolve pool.

Mental "damage" to non-sentient targets is PROGRESS THROUGH INTELLECTUAL LABOR, not psychic combat. Depleting a cipher's Resolve means you decoded it. Depleting a pathogen's Resolve means you synthesized the cure.

## Non-Sentient Systems as Targets

Puzzles, ciphers, locks, pathogens, investigations, and other complex systems use a simplified version of the character sheet:

| Stat | Derivation |
|---|---|
| Resolve Pool | Complexity rating (GM-assigned, typically 10-50) |
| Composite Defense | Difficulty TN (GM-assigned based on system sophistication) |
| Soak | Resistance to brute-force approaches (high for elegant ciphers, low for crude locks) |
| Threshold | How much progress triggers a breakthrough (system "gives" at threshold crossings) |

No condition track. When Resolve hits 0, the system is defeated/solved/cured. Progress is cumulative across sessions if the character returns to the work.

## The Four Mental Skills

### Lore (Academic Knowledge, History, Arcana)

**Attack formula:** d20 + Intellect + Lore + Mastery vs. system's Composite Defense

**What it does:** Cross-references retained knowledge against the problem. Brute-force academic assault — overwhelming a cipher with every linguistic precedent, identifying a ward's historical origin, recalling the architectural tells of a specific college's construction.

**Compatible effects (from matrix):** Damage, Affliction, Nullify, Weaken, Senses, Create, Illusion

**S0 example:** Translating a line of ancient text (Light, 1 Mental FP)
**S1 example:** Identifying the college origin and approximate age of a ward network (Standard, 3 Mental FP)
**S2 example:** Cross-referencing a complete dossier to map an enemy faction's doctrinal vulnerabilities (Standard, 3-5 Mental FP)
**S3 example:** Conducting a multi-day research operation analyzing institutional archives to identify a historical precedent that undermines an enemy's legal claim (Heavy across multiple S0 tempos filling an S3 clock)

### Medicine (Healing, Anatomy, Psychology, Diagnostics)

**Attack formula:** d20 + Intellect + Medicine + Mastery vs. system/target Composite Defense

**What it does:** Applied science of body and mind. Diagnostic assessment of biological state, pathogen deconstruction through laboratory work, surgical application of anatomical knowledge, psychological anchoring against cognitive hazards.

**Compatible effects (from matrix):** Damage, Affliction, Nullify, Weaken, Protection, Immunity, Healing, Panacea, Senses, Chronos, Create, Enhanced Trait, Transform

**S0 example:** Rapid triage assessment of an ally's condition (Light, 1 Mental FP)
**S1 example:** Performing field surgery on a wounded fireteam member (Standard, 3 Mental FP)
**S2 example:** Synthesizing a counter-agent for a Kan-tech pathogen at a field laboratory (Heavy across multiple S0 tempos, fills S2 clock)
**S3 example:** Developing a vaccination protocol for a regional plague (sustained S0 labor across days, fills S3 investigation clock)

### Artifice (Crafting, Engineering, System Interaction)

**Attack formula:** d20 + Relevant Attribute + Artifice + Mastery vs. system Composite Defense

**What it does:** Practical application of engineering and crafting knowledge. Building, repairing, disabling, analyzing mechanical and magical systems. The full SRD v3 crafting system runs through Artifice.

**Compatible effects (from matrix):** Damage, Affliction, Nullify, Weaken, Protection, Healing, Panacea, Senses, Create, Move Object, Enhanced Trait, Summon, Transform

**S0 example:** Picking a tumbler lock (Standard, 3 Mental FP)
**S1 example:** Disabling a Kan-tech alarm system protecting a facility (Standard, 3-5 Mental FP)
**S2 example:** Constructing field fortifications for a defensive position (Heavy, sustained across S2 clock)
**S3 example:** Engineering a Forge ward network for a district (sustained S0 labor across weeks, fills S3 project clock)

### Discovery (Deduction, Investigation, Precision Perception)

**Attack formula:** d20 + Wits + Discovery + Mastery vs. system/target Composite Defense

**What it does:** Finding the linchpin. The precision strike of intellectual labor — where Lore overwhelms with volume, Discovery finds the one detail that changes everything. Extremely narrow (4 compatible effects) but devastating when it lands.

**Compatible effects (from matrix):** Nullify, Weaken, Senses (primary), and nothing else

**S0 example:** Spotting the hidden variable in a rigged card game (Light, 1 Mental FP)
**S1 example:** Tracking a target through an urban environment by microscopic clues (Standard, 3 Mental FP)
**S2 example:** Noticing the structural flaw in a Chiaroscuro illusion during an extended investigation (Standard, 3-5 Mental FP)
**S3 example:** Identifying the single point of failure in an enemy faction's operational security through sustained surveillance (Heavy across multiple S0 tempos filling S3 clock)

## Mental Reactions

**Triggers:**
- Memetic hazard activating in your presence
- Psychic intrusion attempt targeting you
- Environmental cognitive pressure (reality bleed, forbidden knowledge)
- System counter-attack (trap triggers, cipher defenses activate)

**Appropriate reactive effects:**
- Protection (cognitive fortification — bracing mentally, maintaining coherence)
- Senses (pattern recognition — reflexively identifying the threat)
- Nullify (analytical counter — recognizing and dismissing the intrusion)

**Cost:** Mental FP at (ESL × 2) − 1. No tempo consumed.

## Mental Track Recovery

Mental track recovery occurs during downtime at rates determined by track state severity. Medicine (psychological anchoring, cognitive therapy) and rest accelerate recovery. Severe Mental track damage (-3 or below) often requires specific narrative intervention — therapy from a trained practitioner, confronting the source of the trauma, or Schema pressure point engagement.

# PART III: THE SOCIAL PILLAR (S0-S3)

## What Social Combat IS

Social combat is the battle for credibility, leverage, and control. You attack a target's Reputation pool (Social HP) through argument, deception, intimidation, and leverage. You defend your own Reputation through composure, wit, authority, and social positioning.

The Social HP pool (Reputation) represents internal social stamina — your confidence, composure, and rhetorical endurance in a specific encounter. It is NOT the world's perception of you (that's the Background system). A character at 0 Social HP has been outmaneuvered in THIS encounter — they haven't necessarily been ruined permanently.

Social HP regenerates at the same drip rate as all other pillar pools. Getting into back-to-back social conflicts before recovery is mechanically draining — the condition track reflects your internal state, not external perception.

## The Four Social Skills

### Influence (The Hammer — Direct Social Damage)

**Attack formula:** d20 + Presence + Influence + Mastery + Achieved ESL of active tool vs. target's Social Composite Defense

**What it does:** The ONLY social skill designed to consistently deplete Reputation. Direct argument, command, negotiation, logical inevitability. This is where the setup pays off — once Valuation has stripped Soak and Streetwise has applied conditions, Influence delivers the killing blow.

**Compatible effects (from matrix):** Damage (primary), Affliction, Nullify, Weaken, Protection, Healing, Panacea, Senses, Mind Link, Create, Enhanced Trait, Summon

**S0 example:** Demanding compliance from an individual (Standard, 3 Social FP)
**S1 example:** Issuing a command to a small group under your authority (Standard, 3 Social FP)
**S2 example:** Delivering a closing argument to a tribunal (Heavy, 5+ Social FP)
**S3 example:** Conducting a week-long diplomatic negotiation between factions (sustained S0 labor filling S3 clock)

### Performance (The Feint and Crowd Weapon)

**Attack formula:** d20 + Presence/Guile + Performance + Mastery vs. target's Social Composite Defense (1-on-1) or Crowd's Combined Defense (crowd targeting)

**What it does:** Two modes. One-on-one: the feint — projecting a false persona or intent, stripping the target's Social Dodge for the next attack. Crowd: weaponizing the audience as an environmental hazard against the target. Unity Academy and Chiaroscuro Institute's signature social tool.

**Compatible effects (from matrix):** Damage, Affliction, Nullify, Weaken, Protection, Healing, Panacea, Senses, Mind Link, Chronos, Create, Illusion (primary), Enhanced Trait

**S0 example:** Feinting in a one-on-one negotiation (Standard, 3 Social FP — target loses Social Dodge against next attack)
**S1 example:** Performing for a small audience to shift their attitude (Standard, 3 Social FP)
**S2 example:** Delivering a speech to a city square crowd (Heavy, 5-9 Social FP)
**S3 example:** Conducting a multi-day propaganda campaign through Kan-tech broadcast infrastructure (sustained S0 labor filling S3 clock)

### Valuation (The Armor Sunder — Defense Stripping)

**Attack formula:** d20 + Acuity + Valuation + Mastery vs. target's Social Composite Defense

**What it does:** Appraises what a person values, fears, or needs, then translates that knowledge into mechanical advantage. Does NOT deal damage — strips Social Soak by the Achieved ESL. Fed by investigation (Discovery, Lore, Medicine providing raw data). The cold read without prior data is hard; the informed appraisal with a dossier is devastating.

**Soak stripping is PROSPECTIVE.** It applies only to hits delivered AFTER the Valuation action, not retroactively to earlier damage in the phrase.

**Compatible effects (from matrix):** Nullify, Weaken (primary), Protection, Senses, Illusion, Enhanced Trait

**Five-degree resolution for Valuation:**

| Degree | Result |
|---|---|
| Critical Success | Social Soak stripped to 0 for remainder of encounter + target's Schema Anchor revealed |
| Success | Social Soak reduced by Achieved ESL |
| Success with Flaw | Social Soak reduced by half Achieved ESL, but you tip your hand — target knows what leverage you hold |
| Failure | Tool ineffective, Soak intact |
| Critical Failure | Target's Social Soak increases for the encounter as guard goes up |

### Streetwise (The Condition Applier — Targeted Suppression)

**Attack formula:** d20 + Guile + Streetwise + Mastery + Achieved ESL of tool vs. target's Social Composite Defense

**What it does:** Deploys criminal leverage, blackmail, or intimidation to functionally cripple the target's social maneuverability. The defacto method for inflicting social status conditions. Unlike other skills, Streetwise targets specific Background dots by name — you must have knowledge of the Background to suppress it.

**Compatible effects (from matrix):** Damage, Affliction (primary), Nullify, Weaken, Protection, Movement, Senses, Create, Illusion

**Targeted lockout mechanic:**
- Attacker declares which specific Background dots they're suppressing
- Attacker must have narrative knowledge of those Backgrounds
- Socially Restrained [Value] suppresses up to [Value] dots in the named Background
- Partial suppression: target retains access to remaining dots (5-dot Allies suppressed by 3 = 2 functional Allies dots)
- Wasted suppression: if you inflict Restrained 5 but only know about 3 dots, the extra 2 are lost

## Social Reactions

**Triggers:**
- Direct social attack targeting you (accusation, insult, demand)
- Social Tool deployed against your defenses
- Public revelation of damaging information about you
- Attempt to strip your Soak (Valuation) or suppress your Backgrounds (Streetwise)

**Appropriate reactive effects:**
- Protection (counter-argument — defending your position, maintaining composure)
- Damage (retort — immediate counter-accusation)
- Illusion (redirect — deflecting the attack, changing the subject)
- Nullify (dismissal — neutralizing the social tool through authority or indifference)

**Cost:** Social FP at (ESL × 2) − 1. No tempo consumed.

**Critical distinction for crowd combat:** Performance targeting a CROWD does not trigger a reaction from the ultimate target. The performer attacks the crowd's defense, not the target's. The target only gets to react when the crowd's condition APPLIES TO THEM on a subsequent round. This is what makes crowd weaponization powerful — the initial setup bypasses the target's reaction economy.

## The Investigation-to-Strike Pipeline

Social combat has a natural multi-round rhythm:

| Phase | Action | Skill | FP Pool | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Investigate** | Gather raw data about target | Discovery / Lore / Medicine | Mental FP | Senses |
| **Appraise** | Refine data into weaponized tool | Valuation | Social FP | Weaken (strip Soak) |
| **Condition** | Apply debilitating social state | Streetwise | Social FP | Affliction (target Backgrounds) |
| **Strike** | Deliver killing blow to exposed target | Influence | Social FP | Damage (to Reputation) |

Players who skip Investigation and Appraisal fight against full Soak every hit. Players who invest in setup strip defenses first, then strike cleanly. The pipeline rewards preparation and punishes brute force — the social equivalent of positioning before attacking.

The cross-pillar cost structure creates natural party roles: the analyst (Mental investment, Discovery/Lore) gathers the dossier, the face (Social investment, Influence/Valuation) weaponizes it. Neither can optimally do both unless they've invested deeply across pillars.

# PART IV: SOCIAL TOOLS AS SPELL SENTENCE CONSTRUCTS

## Core Principle

Information is mechanically built through the same dual-pool grammar as spells. A Social Tool (forged ledger, compromising photograph, sworn testimony, royal writ) is constructed with an Effect Pool and a Construction Pool, producing an Effective Social Level that determines its mechanical weight.

## The Effect Pool (What the Tool Does)

Like a spell, a piece of leverage has a primary effect:

| Tool Intent | Effect |
|---|---|
| Impose a social condition | Affliction |
| Strip target's defenses | Weaken |
| Deceive an audience | Illusion |
| Destroy target's standing | Damage |
| Suppress an active social effect | Nullify |

The tool's subtype targets a specific aspect of the target's identity — Commerce (financial ruin), Passion (secret lover), Alliance (proof of treason), Faith (heretical text). If the subtype directly threatens a target's Schema values, the impact is magnified.

## The Construction Pool (How the Tool Deploys)

| Parameter | Options |
|---|---|
| **Target/Area** | Individual (whispered blackmail) vs Spread/Burst (distributed pamphlets, broadcast) |
| **Range** | Close (gala whisper) vs Visual Sight (Kan-tech broadcast) |
| **Duration** | Hours (stolen patrol route) to Permanent (deep faction secret) |
| **Subtractives** | Sense-Dependent (must read document), Limited (only affects members of a specific College) |

When a Social Tool's Duration expires, its ESL drops to 0. Information has an expiration date.

## Achieved ESL Through Valuation

A Social Tool has a Base ESL determined by its quality and construction. Valuation determines the Achieved ESL — how effectively the tool is deployed in the moment.

The Achieved ESL determines the FP cost of the strike that uses it. A tool with Achieved ESL 5 makes the Influence strike cost (5 × 2) − 1 = 9 Social FP. The weight of what you're saying determines the social stamina required to say it. A character with a low Social FP pool literally cannot deliver a high-ESL revelation — they don't have the composure to swing that hammer.

## The Exposed Cooldown

Once a Social Tool is deployed against a target, it gains "Exposed" status against that specific individual or crowd for the remainder of the scene. The same tool cannot be used to repeatedly strip Soak or apply conditions round after round.

After a tool is Exposed, the attacker must either deploy a different tool or switch to Influence (the hammer) to exploit the opening the first tool created.

Exposed is a SCENE-LEVEL tracking mechanic, not a discrete condition. It does not interact with Panacea or Nullify. It simply reflects that the shock value of a specific piece of leverage is spent once deployed.

# PART V: CROWD COMBAT

## Core Principle

A crowd is not a collection of NPCs. It is an environmental hazard that can be seized, weaponized, or pacified. When Performance or Influence targets a crowd, you are attacking the CROWD's defenses to turn them into a weapon against your actual target. The crowd becomes a DoT that continuously damages the target's Reputation until they address it.

This is the Systemic Catalyst applied at Social scale. The crowd IS the natural system being leveraged.

## Crowd Statistics

**Combined Defense:** (Average Guile + Average Composure of demographic) / 2 + local faction's Mastery Level

**Attitude Modifiers:**

| Attitude | Defense Modifier |
|---|---|
| Hostile to your position | +5 |
| Neutral / Uninformed | +0 |
| Sympathetic / Aligned | -5 |

**Crowd Tier (Size determines DoT potency):**

| Tier | Size | Social Damage Per Round |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tavern, boardroom (10-30) | 1 per round |
| 2 | Gala, guild hall (50-100) | 3 per round |
| 3 | City square, stadium (500+) | 5 per round |

## The Attack

Roll Performance/Influence/Streetwise + relevant attribute + Mastery + Tool ESL (if applicable) vs Crowd's Combined Defense.

| Degree | Result |
|---|---|
| Critical Success | Crowd becomes weaponized mob. Severe condition AND Tier DoT applied. Target's Social Deflection overridden. |
| Success | Crowd turns on target. Standard condition OR Tier DoT applied. |
| Success with Flaw | Crowd turns on target and applies DoT, but you incur a debt to the crowd or the mob causes collateral Background damage. |
| Failure | Crowd unamused, remains neutral. Action wasted. |
| Critical Failure | Crowd turns on YOU. DoT applied to your Reputation instead. |

## Crowd-Applied Conditions

| Condition | Social Expression |
|---|---|
| Bleeding/Poisoned equivalent | Public Scrutiny — ambient DoT. Bypasses target's Dodge and Deflect; only Soak absorbs. |
| Deafened equivalent | Drowned Out — target suffers -5 to all social checks, constantly fighting the room's noise. |
| Restrained equivalent | Mobbed — target suffers -2 to Social Defenses, cannot leave without triggering physical conflict. |
| Frightened equivalent | Ostracized — target's only viable option is yield or flee entirely. |

## Defensive/Buff Conditions (Crowd Supporting You)

| Condition | Social Expression |
|---|---|
| Invisible equivalent | Echo Chamber — Advantage on your social attacks, Disadvantage on attacks against you. |
| Regeneration equivalent | Adulation — heal Social HP equal to Crowd Tier per round. |

## Clearing Crowd Conditions

The target has three options:

1. **Tank it:** Let Soak absorb the DoT, ignore the crowd, attack you directly
2. **Flee:** Leave the area to break the crowd's influence
3. **Pacify the crowd:** Spend their entire action rolling Performance or Influence against the Crowd's Combined Defense to calm, redirect, or intimidate them into silence

## Why the Target Can't React to the Initial Attack

Performance targeting a crowd attacks the CROWD's defense, not the ultimate target's. The target doesn't get a reaction trigger until the crowd's condition actually applies to them on a subsequent round. By then, the crowd is already turned. This is the power of indirect social warfare.

## Institutional Applications

**Unity Academy:** Their Thrallherd networks artificially lower Crowd Combined Defense in their territory, turning entire city blocks into pre-weaponized social infrastructure. The initiation teaches scope of influence through community building — proving yourself to move between social strata.

**Chiaroscuro Institute:** They use Illusion effects as Social Tools to trick crowds into perceiving fabricated events, instantly shifting Attitude Modifiers to Hostile against the target. The crowd doesn't know it was manipulated.

**Sim-stim technology (Unity-produced):** Kan-tech broadcast equipment that compresses Scope transitions for crowd manipulation. A sim-stim broadcast operates at S3 but is composed of accumulated S0 performances transmitted through infrastructure, reaching audiences across a region simultaneously.

# PART VI: THE METAPHYSICAL PILLAR (S0-S3)

## What Metaphysical "Combat" IS

The Metaphysical pillar is a laboratory, not a battlefield. Its four skills handle the identification, operation, perception, and survival of magical and planar phenomena. Combat applications exist but are secondary — these skills primarily function during investigation, downtime, crafting, and environmental navigation.

When Metaphysical combat DOES occur, it is slow, methodical, and consequence-heavy. Unweaving a ward takes minutes to hours, not a combat round. Operating a hostile Kan-tech device requires sustained concentration. Resisting planar mutation pressure is an endurance test, not a duel.

## The Four Metaphysical Skills

### Metaphysics (Spellcraft + Slow-Burn Unweaving)

**Attack formula:** d20 + Intellect + Metaphysics + Mastery vs. spell/ward Pattern (ESL + caster's Mastery)

**What it does:** The hard science of magical architecture. Identification of ongoing effects, reading ward structure, recognizing spell syntax during casting. When used offensively, slowly deconstructs magical phenomena by pulling apart their concordance structure. NOT a combat interrupt — requires focus and sustained time.

**Compatible effects (from matrix):** Damage, Affliction, Nullify (primary), Weaken, Protection, Healing, Panacea, Senses (primary), Create, Enhanced Trait

**S0 example:** Identifying a spell as it's being cast by reading somatic/verbal syntax (Light, 1 Metaphysical FP)
**S2 example:** Safely defusing a Kan-tech ward over ten minutes of careful unweaving (Heavy sustained, 5+ Metaphysical FP)
**S3 example:** Analyzing and deconstructing an institutional ward network over days of methodical work (sustained S0 labor filling S3 clock)

### Operate (Kan-tech Interface — The Mundane Variable)

**Attack formula:** d20 + relevant attribute + Operate + Mastery vs. device's activation TN

**What it does:** Use Magic Device applied to an industrialized magical setting. Activates Kan-tech terminals, triggers wands belonging to other colleges, boots ætheric engines, interfaces with any magical device regardless of prerequisites. The effect produced is determined by the DEVICE — Operate provides access, not capability.

**Compatible effects (from matrix):** 23 of 24 (everything except Feature). Operate IS the mundane Variable — any effect a device can produce, Operate can activate.

**S0 example:** Activating an unfamiliar wand (Standard, 3 Metaphysical FP)
**S1 example:** Operating a Kan-tech vehicle's ætheric engine while Conveyance handles steering (Standard, 3 Metaphysical FP)
**S2 example:** Hijacking an enemy facility's ward system through its control interface (Heavy sustained, 5-9 Metaphysical FP)
**S3 example:** Managing a Forge ward communication network across a district (sustained S0 labor, S3 operational task)

### Insight (Preternatural Wisdom Sense)

**What it does:** The gut feeling. The hair standing up. Insight "pings" the metaphysical environment — sensing the weight of active buffs/debuffs/mutations, detecting wrongness in reality, feeling danger before it manifests. Does NOT provide analytical data (that's Metaphysics interpreting what Insight detected).

**Compatible effects (from matrix):** Senses (primary), Protection (warning sense). Two effects total. Extremely narrow, extremely valuable.

**Soul Reading:** Requires a disconnected soul or a roundabout divinatory ritual (tarot, casting bones, reading entrails). Provides symbolic impressions of Schema values — "The Emperor inverted, shadowed by the Tower" — not raw numbers. Cannot directly soul-read a living person.

**Chiaroscuro Warning:** Using Insight to pierce hyper-real (>100% collapse) illusions is dangerous. Staring too deeply into a manufactured reality doesn't dispel it — it invites the cognitive dissonance of the Great Lie directly into your brain, risking Mental and Metaphysical track damage.

### Praxis (The Soul's Immune System)

**What it does:** The metaphysical muscle. Managing mutations, resisting planar warping, maintaining identity coherence under metaphysical stress. Not combat-first — this is the skill of SURVIVING in a world where the environment actively tries to rewrite you.

**Compatible effects (from matrix):** Damage, Affliction, Weaken, Protection (primary), Immunity, Immortality, Healing, Panacea, Enhanced Trait, Summon, Transform

**S0 example:** Suppressing an unwanted mutation manifestation in polite company (Light, 1 Metaphysical FP)
**S1 example:** Anchoring your pattern against a localized planar bleed zone during exploration (Standard, 3 Metaphysical FP)
**S2 example:** Acting as a human grounding wire for a damaged ley line while allies evacuate (Heavy sustained, 5-9 Metaphysical FP)
**S3 example:** Enduring a prolonged exposure to high-Formation-Stage isotope fields during an extended expedition (sustained S0 resistance across days, fills S3 endurance clock)

## Metaphysical Reactions

**Triggers:**
- Isotope expression contacting your body
- Planar bleed pressure exceeding Strain threshold
- Spell effect targeting your Pattern
- Nullify or Transform attempt on your active expressions

**Appropriate reactive effects:**
- Protection (ontological fortitude through Praxis — asserting your pattern)
- Senses (Insight ping — reflexively detecting the incoming threat)
- Nullify (Metaphysics counter — identifying and dismissing the effect through theory)

**Cost:** Metaphysical FP at (ESL × 2) − 1. No tempo consumed.

# PART VII: INSTITUTIONAL SCALE (S4-S5)

## The Dual Nature of Institutions

An institution is a large organism whose cell structure is visible. It uses BOTH the individual character sheet system AND the front/clock system from the Operating Philosophy:

**The character sheet** describes what the institution IS — aggregate HP pools across four pillars, condition tracks, Composite Defense, Soak, Threshold. When you attack an institution's Reputation directly (a propaganda campaign), you're targeting its Social HP pool.

**The front/clock system** describes what the institution DOES — operational clocks, methodology, representation chains, relationships. When you disrupt an institution's operations (sabotaging their communication network), you're advancing or retarding their operational clocks.

## Institutional Character Sheet

An institution's stats are derived from its leadership, infrastructure, and membership, scaled to its Scope:

| Stat | Derivation |
|---|---|
| HP Pools | Aggregate of leadership + institutional infrastructure + membership base |
| Composite Defense | Institutional Dodge (adaptability) + Deflection (structural resilience) |
| Soak | Institutional inertia — larger institutions absorb more damage before feeling it |
| Threshold | Scale-dependent — a guild's threshold differs from a college's |
| Condition Track | Same 5-step structure, mirrored at institutional scale |

## Institutional Condition Tracks

The same condition names apply but the EXPRESSION scales:

| Step | Individual Expression | Institutional Expression |
|---|---|---|
| -1 | Flustered | Minor operational disruption — delayed reports, confused messaging |
| -2 | Humbled | Public setback — rival gains ground, internal morale dips |
| -3 | Disgraced | Major institutional failure — public loss of confidence, membership questioning |
| -4 | Rapt | Institutional paralysis — leadership unable to coordinate, factions forming internally |
| -5 | Broken | Institutional collapse — dissolution, mass defection, restructuring required |

## The Two Attack Surfaces

**Attacking the Organism (HP/Track):**

Target the institution's aggregate stats through sustained S0-S3 actions that cascade upward. A propaganda campaign filling an S3 clock deals Social damage to the institution's Reputation pool. A sabotage campaign filling an S3 clock deals Physical damage to the institution's infrastructure.

When institutional HP is depleted or the condition track reaches Incapacitated, the institution ceases to function as a coherent entity. It may reform, restructure, or be absorbed — but its current incarnation is done.

**Attacking the Operations (Clocks):**

Target specific operational clocks through the front system. Advancing an enemy institution's "internal dissent" clock doesn't damage their HP — it moves their political situation toward a threshold crossing that produces specific consequences.

Clock attacks and HP attacks are independent but complementary. A propaganda campaign that deals Social HP damage AND advances the "public distrust" clock is attacking both surfaces simultaneously.

## How Individual Actions Cascade to Institutional Scale

Per the nested clock architecture (Document 2), individual S0-S3 actions accumulate to fill S4-S5 institutional tempos. The five validated attack vectors (Stealth Accumulation, Parallel Relay Exploitation, Clock Starvation, Single Point of Failure, Analytical Poisoning) all operate at this interface.

The Systemic Catalyst talent allows individual characters to target institutional leverage points, multiplying their reach from personal to institutional scale. A party member using Discovery to find the structural keystone of an institution's information network, then Artifice to sabotage it, then Performance to broadcast the resulting confusion — this is a multi-skill, multi-pillar, multi-Scope attack chain that cascades from S0 actions to S4-S5 institutional consequences.

# PART VIII: THE BACKGROUND SYSTEM

## Divine Umbrellas (XP Investment)

XP purchases Reputation capacity in a Divine Aspect (Lex, Rhapsodia, Agora, etc.). The Umbrella's rank determines how many Background dots can be slotted within that philosophical alignment. The XP investment is PERMANENT — the world cannot take away the fact that you are a person of consequence.

**Filling the Capacity:** Specific Backgrounds (Allies, Contacts, Influence, Rank, Resources, etc.) are slotted into the Umbrella through Schema alignment and narrative acquisition. The "payment" is philosophical loyalty, not additional XP.

**Fluidity:** Factions are not monolithic. Deepguard respects Lex. The Sovereign Exchange respects Lex. A local street gang respects Lex (the code). Your Umbrella is a passport — your Reputation travels with you and attaches to whoever in the new region recognizes your philosophical alignment.

## Cultural Dissonance (Replacing Binary Infamy)

When a character enters a region whose Dominant Divine Value is antithetical to their Reputation Umbrella, they experience Cultural Dissonance — not criminal status, but social friction.

**The Social Tax:** Penalty to Influence and Valuation checks equal to Umbrella Rank when dealing with aligned institutional NPCs. Your etiquette confuses them.

**The Novelty Bonus:** Bonus to Performance and Streetwise checks equal to Umbrella Rank. You are the fascinating outsider. Counter-culture, bored elite, and marginalized populations are drawn to you.

**Background Warping:** Your Contacts don't give you access to magistrates — they give you fascinated sociological scholars or underground art collectives. Your Influence doesn't pass laws — it starts cultural trends in the lower wards.

**Escalation to True Infamy:** Only triggered if the character WEAPONIZES their Cultural Dissonance to actively attack the region's dominant structure. Being the wrong alignment is Dissonance. Inciting a riot against the local Thrallherd network is Infamy.

## Background Fallout System

When Backgrounds are destroyed through social combat, the XP is NEVER dead.

**Phase 1 — Shock (1-3 sessions, GM discretion):** The destroyed Background dots enter the Pivot Pool. The character feels the mechanical sting of losing their safety net. The player marks the XP as safe but temporarily sequestered.

**Phase 2 — Grace Period (during Shock):** The GM drops narrative lifelines — factions offering aid with strings, opportunities arising from the fallout. The player is not expected to have an immediate answer. "I don't know" is valid.

**Phase 3 — Reallocation Mandate (end of 3rd session):** XP must be released from the Pivot Pool. Three resolution methods:
- **Proactive Pivot:** Player declares their plan — "I spent the last three sessions building a new network."
- **Taking the Lifeline:** Player accepts a GM-provided narrative hook.
- **Default Severance:** XP automatically converts to Resources or Favors as a fail-safe.

**The Law of Equivalent Exchange:** No objective has only one door. For every obvious solution, the GM must prepare or permit at least three alternative vectors utilizing different Pillars or Backgrounds.

# PART IX: INFORMATION INFRASTRUCTURE

## Dual Relay Systems

Two parallel relay systems exist in Tessera, one magical and one technological. Both compress Scope transitions. Both are attack surfaces.

**Magical Relay:**
- Senses effects for information gathering (Collection/Analysis/Prediction)
- Mind Link effects for bidirectional communication
- Forge ward networks for Scope compression
- Palimpsest links for unlimited-range cross-territorial communication

**Technological Relay (Kan-tech):**
- Radio networks (Forge-manufactured broadcast infrastructure)
- Photography and cameras (S0 evidence preserved for deployment at higher Scopes)
- Sim-stim technology (Unity-produced experiential broadcast — compressed sensory transmission)
- Print media (pamphlets, newspapers, institutional publications)
- Signal infrastructure (telegraph equivalent, semaphore networks, pneumatic tubes)

## Both Relay Systems Compress Scope

A Forge ward transmitting a report from S0 to S4 compresses five transitions to one. A Kan-tech radio broadcast transmitting news from S0 to S3 compresses three transitions to one. The mechanical effect is identical — faster information flow with less fidelity loss per transition.

## Both Relay Systems Are Attack Surfaces

Every infrastructure node is a point of vulnerability:

| Attack | Magical Infrastructure | Technological Infrastructure |
|---|---|---|
| **Destroy the node** | Nullify the Forge ward | Sabotage the radio tower |
| **Corrupt the signal** | Illusion injected into Mind Link | False broadcast, doctored photographs |
| **Intercept the traffic** | Senses tapping the ward network | Wiretapping, signal interception |
| **Flood with noise** | Multiple conflicting Senses effects | Propaganda saturation, competing broadcasts |
| **Steal the infrastructure** | Operate hijacking the ward control | Physical seizure of broadcast equipment |

## The Tet Offensive Vector in Tessera

Kan-tech broadcast infrastructure exists in Tessera because Setting B IS a dieselpunk/Kan-tech industrial civilization. A faction exploiting broadcast infrastructure to transmit damaging S0 evidence directly to an S3-S5 audience — bypassing the target institution's information relay chain — is using the Parallel Relay Exploitation vector through technological rather than magical means.

The infrastructure works for EVERYONE. The institution that built the broadcast network does not control what gets broadcast. A party with access to the physical equipment (Operate to activate, Performance to produce compelling content) can weaponize the institution's own communication infrastructure against it.

This is why the Unity Academy produces sim-stim technology but guards its broadcast infrastructure jealously. They understand that the system they built to manage public perception can be turned against them by anyone with technical access and a compelling narrative.
