## Compiled & Updated — April 15, 2026
### All Talents Organized by Tree, Tagged by Type, Sources Noted

---

**Tag Legend:**
- **[Mundane]** — No magical component. Physical/mental training only.
- **[Magical]** — Learned magical technique. Feeds Tₘ in ÆP pool formula.
- **[Infused]** — Mundane action enhanced by magical energy (ÆP cost).
- **[Supernatural]** — Innate magical ability tied to the body. Future mutation catalogue candidate.
- **[Active]** — Requires an action to use.
- **[Reactive]** — Uses reaction.
- **[Swift]** — Uses swift/minor action.
- **[Passive]** — Always on, no action required.
- **[Stance]** — Persistent state, one active at a time, switch as swift action.

**Source Legend:**
- **(F)** — Feat-derived (PF1e/3.5e feat conversion, April 10 pass)
- **(C)** — Class-derived (class ability extraction, April 15 session)
- **(B)** — Book of Shadows (existing magical talent document)
- **(S)** — SRD v2 Prima Materia (existing system document)
- **(N)** — New this session (novel extraction with no prior source)

**Status Legend:**
- **✅ PLACED** — Position confirmed, mechanics sketched
- **🔧 NEEDS WORK** — Placed but mechanics need development
- **⚠️ FLAGGED** — Placement or design conflict requiring resolution
- **📋 STUB** — Named and placed, no mechanics yet

# CATEGORY 1: FOUNDATIONAL COMBAT STYLES (8 Trees)

These define HOW a character fights. Each has a Crux talent as the entry point.

---

## TREE 1: TWO-HANDED STYLE

**Crux: Power Attack** [Mundane, Active] (F)
Trade accuracy for damage when wielding a two-handed weapon. Scaling penalty/bonus.

### Direct Droplets (enhance Power Attack):
- **Furious Focus** [Mundane, Passive] (F) — Negate the Power Attack penalty on the first attack of your turn.
- **Shattering Strike** [Mundane, Active] (F) — Spend Stamina when using Power Attack to deal bonus damage to objects, armor, and constructs.
- **Cornugon Smash** [Mundane, Active] (F) — When you hit with a Power Attack, attempt a free Intimidate check against the target. *(Cross-tags: [Intimidation])*
- **Vital Strike** [Mundane, Active] (F) — As a standard action, make one attack at your highest bonus dealing additional weapon damage dice. Scales in three tiers (Vital → Improved → Greater).
- **Devastating Strike** [Mundane, Active] (F) — Vital Strike upgrade. Add a flat bonus to damage equal to weapon damage dice × 2.

### Area Attack Sub-Tree (branch off Power Attack):
- **Cleave** [Mundane, Active] (F) — When you hit with a melee attack, make a second attack against an adjacent target at a penalty.
- **Great Cleave** [Mundane, Active] (F) — Cleave continues as long as you keep hitting adjacent targets.
- **Cleaving Finish** [Mundane, Active] (F) — When you drop a target, make a free Cleave attack.
- **Whirlwind Attack** [Mundane, Active] (F) — As a full action, make one melee attack against every opponent within your reach. Capstone of the Area Attack chain.

### Juggernaut Sub-Tree (branch off Power Attack):
- **Improved Shove** [Mundane, Active] (F) — Enhanced Shove (Bull Rush) combat maneuver checks when using Power Attack.
- **Wrecking Blow** [Mundane, Active] (F) — After a successful Shove, target provokes reaction attacks from your allies.
- **Awesome Blow** [Mundane, Active] (F) — Send an opponent flying backward on a successful power strike. *(Cross-tags: [Reposition])*

## TREE 2: FINESSE STYLE

**Crux: Weapon Finesse** [Mundane, Passive] (F)
Use Dexterity/Agility instead of Strength for attack rolls with light or finesse weapons.

### Dueling Style Sub-Tree:
- **Dueling Style (Adept)** [Mundane, Passive] (F) — Requires one-handed weapon, other hand free. Add Dexterity/Agility modifier to damage rolls.
- **Dueling Style (Expert)** [Mundane, Reactive] (F) — Significant bonus to Initiative. When an enemy misses you with a melee attack, use your reaction to counterattack.
- **Dueling Style (Master)** [Mundane, Active] (F) — When you successfully counterattack, you may forgo damage to automatically disarm the opponent.

### Parry & Riposte Sub-Tree:
- **Opportune Parry** [Mundane, Reactive] (F/C) — Spend Stamina to use your reaction to parry an incoming melee attack. Roll your attack; if it exceeds the enemy's, the attack misses.
- **Riposte** [Mundane, Reactive] (F/C) — When you successfully parry, make an immediate counterattack as part of the same reaction.

### Evasive Footwork Sub-Tree:
- **Sidestep** [Mundane, Reactive] (F) — When an enemy misses you, take a 5-foot step as a free action.
- **Canny Tumble** [Mundane, Active] (F) — When you successfully tumble past an enemy, gain a bonus to your next attack against them.

### Precise Strike:
- **Precise Strike** [Mundane, Passive] (F/C) — Add a scaling bonus to damage with finesse weapons while maintaining proper form (one hand free or dual-wielding light weapons). Core Daredevil damage engine. *(Cross-tags: [Deed])*

## TREE 3: RANGED STYLE

**Crux: Point-Blank Shot** [Mundane, Passive] (F)
Bonus to attack and damage with ranged weapons within 30 feet.

### Archery Sub-Tree:
- **Precise Shot** [Mundane, Passive] (F) — No penalty for shooting into melee.
- **Rapid Shot** [Mundane, Active] (F) — Make one additional ranged attack per round at a penalty to all attacks.
- **Manyshot** [Mundane, Active] (F) — Fire two arrows on your first attack of a full attack.
- **Improved Precise Shot** [Mundane, Passive] (F) — Ignore cover and concealment bonuses (except total cover/concealment).

### Firearm Sub-Tree:
- **Firearm Proficiency** [Mundane, Passive] (F) — Proficiency with Kan-tech firearms.
- **Rapid Reload** [Mundane, Passive] (F) — Reduce reload action by one step.
- **Leaping Shot** [Mundane, Active] (F) — Move and fire without penalty.

### Deadly Aim:
- **Deadly Aim** [Mundane, Active] (F) — Ranged equivalent of Power Attack. Trade accuracy for damage with ranged weapons.

### Overwatch Sub-Tree:
- **Overwatch Style** [Mundane, Active] (F) — As a full action, ready two ranged attacks with specific triggers.
- **Overwatch Tactician** [Mundane, Active] (F) — Ready three attacks instead of two.
- **Overwatch Vortex** [Mundane, Active] (F) — Ready four attacks. Can use Overwatch as a standard action to ready one attack without penalty.

### Close-Quarters Shooting:
- **Close-Quarters Shooting** [Mundane, Passive] (F) — No penalty for firing ranged weapons in melee. AoO provoked by shooting can be resolved as a melee attack with the ranged weapon.

### Arrow Deflection Sub-Tree:
- **Deflect Arrows** [Mundane, Reactive] (F) — Once per round, deflect one ranged attack with a free hand.
- **Snatch Arrows** [Mundane, Reactive] (F) — Catch deflected projectiles and throw them back.

## TREE 4: UNARMED STYLE

**Crux: Trained Fists** [Mundane, Passive] (F)
Unarmed strikes deal lethal damage and scale in damage dice. Can make unarmed attacks without provoking.

### Flurry Sub-Tree:
- **Flurry of Blows** [Mundane, Active] (F/C) — Make additional unarmed attacks at a penalty. Volume-of-attacks engine.

### Stunning Sub-Tree:
- **Stunning Fist** [Mundane, Active] (F/C) — Spend Stamina. On a successful unarmed hit, target must save or be stunned. Explicitly MUNDANE — represents anatomical precision, not magic. Stamina cost: 3.

### Elemental Fist:
- **Elemental Fist** [Infused, Active] (F) — Spend ÆP to add typed energy damage to unarmed strikes. *(Tags: [Infused] — this is where unarmed crosses into isotope expression)*

### Style Sub-Trees (each is a specific martial art):
- **Tiger Style chain** [Mundane] (F) — Bleed on crit, enhanced slashing damage, pounce.
- **Crane Style chain** [Mundane] (F) — Defensive fighting bonuses, counterattack on miss.
- **Janni Style chain** [Mundane] (F) — 🔧 Requires Trip and Shove systems. Mobile disruption.
- **Dragon Style chain** [Mundane] (F) — Ignore difficult terrain on charge, enhanced power strikes.
- **Snake Style chain** [Mundane] (F) — Sense Motive-based defense, piercing strikes.
- **Boar Style chain** [Mundane] (F) — Intimidation + bleed damage.

- **Shield Slam** [Mundane, Active] (F) — Free Shove attempt on a successful shield bash during a charge.
- **Bashing Finish** [Mundane, Active] (F) — Free shield bash after confirming a crit with primary weapon.
- **Stumbling Bash** [Mundane, Active] (F) — Shield bash makes target flat-footed for one round.
- **Missile Shield** [Mundane, Reactive] (F) — Deflect one ranged attack per round with shield.
- **Saving Shield** [Mundane, Reactive] (F) — As a reaction, grant shield bonus to an adjacent ally's defense.
- **Phalanx Style** [Mundane, Passive] (F) — Use a polearm one-handed when also wielding a shield. *(Cross-tags: [Teamwork])*
- **Shield Wall** [Mundane, Passive] (F) — Bonus when adjacent to ally with shield. *(Cross-tags: [Teamwork])*

## TREE 6: MOUNTED COMBAT

**Crux: Mounted Defender** [Mundane, Passive] (F)
Negate a hit on your mount with a successful Ride check once per round.

- **Ride-By Attack** [Mundane, Active] (F) — Move, attack, continue moving without provoking.
- **Spirited Charge** [Mundane, Active] (F) — Deal double damage on a mounted charge (triple with lance).
- **Mounted Archery** [Mundane, Passive] (F) — Reduce penalties for ranged attacks from horseback.
- **Trick Riding** [Mundane, Active] (F) — Enhanced mounted maneuverability.
- **Mounted Shield** [Mundane, Reactive] (F) — Redirect attacks targeting mount to your shield.

## TREE 7: BINDING STYLE

**Crux: Weapon Bind** [Mundane, Active] (F)
Use binding weapons (flails, chains, whips, nets) to perform combat maneuvers with enhanced effectiveness.

- **Flail Mastery** [Mundane, Passive] (F) — Bypass a portion of opponent's shield bonus with flail.
- **Whip Mastery** [Mundane, Passive] (F) — Increased reach with whip. Trip and Disarm from distance.
- **Net Mastery** [Mundane, Active] (F) — Throw and retrieve nets faster. Enhanced entangle.

*Note: This is the thinnest Foundational Style. Expected to remain small as a specialist tree.*

## TREE 8: REACH CONTROL

**Crux: Combat Reflexes** [Mundane, Passive] (F)
Make additional reactions per round equal to Dexterity/Agility modifier. This talent is load-bearing and referenced across multiple trees.

### Unrelenting Advance Sub-Tree:
- **Step Up** [Mundane, Reactive] (F) — When an adjacent enemy takes a 5-foot step away, take an immediate 5-foot step to follow.
- **Following Step** [Mundane, Reactive] (F) — Follow a withdrawing enemy up to 10 feet.
- **Step Up and Strike** [Mundane, Reactive] (F) — When following an enemy with Step Up, make a free attack.

### Reach Weapon Focus:
- **Lunge** [Mundane, Active] (F) — Increase reach by 5 feet at the cost of a defense penalty.
- **Pin Down** [Mundane, Reactive] (F) — AoO against enemies who attempt to move through your threatened area.

⚠️ **FLAG: Combat Reflexes is universal.** Multiple trees reference it as a prerequisite (Reach Control, Reactive Channel from Magus extraction, various Combat Maneuver reactions). It may need to be a General Pool talent rather than locked behind Reach Control.

# CATEGORY 2: DEFENSIVE PHILOSOPHIES (3 Trees)

These define HOW a character survives.

---

## TREE 9: ARMORED DEFENSE

**Crux: Armor Training** [Mundane, Passive] (F/C)
Proficiency with armor types. Reduce armor check penalties and speed reduction.

- **Armor Specialization** [Mundane, Passive] (F) — Choose one armor type. Ignore its speed penalty entirely.
- **Armor Mastery** [Mundane, Passive] (F) — Nearly immune to critical hits while wearing chosen armor. Spend Stamina to negate extra crit damage.
- **Stalwart Defense** [Mundane, Active] (F) — When fighting defensively, forgo dodge bonus to gain equal Soak (DR). *(Note: distinct from Fort/Will Evasion talent, see Durability tree)*

---

## TREE 10: AGILE DEFENSE

**Crux: Uncanny Dodge** [Mundane, Passive] (F/C)
Cannot be caught flat-footed. React to danger before senses would normally allow.

- **Evasion** [Mundane, Passive] (F/C) — On a successful Reflex save for half damage, take no damage instead. *(Requires light or no armor)*
- **Improved Uncanny Dodge** [Mundane, Passive] (F/C) — Cannot be flanked.
- **Improved Evasion** [Mundane, Passive] (F) — On a failed Reflex save for half damage, take half damage. On success, take none.

---

## TREE 11: DURABILITY

**Crux: Toughness** [Mundane, Passive] (F)
Bonus hit points per level.

### Unstoppable Sub-Tree:
- **Diehard** [Mundane, Passive] (F) — Automatically stabilize when reduced to negative HP. Continue acting while disabled.
- **Deathless Master** [Mundane, Passive] (F) — Continue fighting below 0 HP without penalty until truly dead.

### Endurance Sub-Tree:
- **Endurance** [Mundane, Passive] (F) — Bonus to saves against exhaustion, fatigue, environmental conditions.
- **Heroic Recovery** [Mundane, Active] (F) — Spend Stamina to immediately end one condition (fatigued, shaken, sickened).
- **Iron Resolve** [Mundane, Reactive] (C: Samurai) — As an immediate action, spend Stamina to negate one condition for 1 round or remove one minor condition entirely. ✅ PLACED

### Fortified Resolve (Fort/Will Evasion):
- **Fortified Resolve** [Mundane, Passive] (C: Inquisitor) — When you succeed on a Fortitude or Will save against an effect with reduced effect on success, you instead suffer no effect. *(Parallel to Evasion in Agile Defense, keyed to different saves. Distinct from Stalwart Defense in Armored tree.)* ✅ PLACED

### Rage Stance (Sub-Tree within Durability):
- **Primal Rage** [Mundane, Stance] (F/C) — Enter a rage state gaining bonuses to Strength-based attacks, damage, and Will saves, at the cost of defense penalties. Cannot use skills requiring concentration. Ends when combat ends or voluntarily ended as free action.
  - *Rage-Strain Interaction Rule:* While in Rage stance, effective Strain threshold for all isotopes is reduced by [Rage tier × modifier]. This makes the character more permeable to planar expression during rage. (C: Bloodrager)
- **Terrifying Rage** [Mundane, Passive] (F) — While Primal Rage is active, bonus to Intimidate checks.
- **Fearsome Howl** [Mundane, Active] (F) — While raging, spend Stamina to force all enemies in 30ft to save vs fear.
- **Tireless Rage** [Mundane, Passive] (F) — No fatigue when rage ends.
- **Superstitious** [Mundane, Passive] (F) — While raging, bonus to saves against spells and supernatural abilities.
- **Come and Get Me** [Mundane, Stance] (F) — While raging, enemies gain bonus to attack you, but each hit provokes a free counterattack from you.

### Totemic Path (Sub-Tree, Rage-dependent):
- **Beast Totem (Lesser)** [Supernatural, Passive] (F/C) — While raging, gain natural claw attacks. *(Verdant Font Fauna isotope expression activated by Rage Strain threshold reduction)*
- **Beast Totem** [Supernatural, Passive] (F/C) — While raging, gain natural armor bonus. *(Fauna isotope defensive expression)*
- **Beast Totem (Greater)** [Supernatural, Active] (F/C) — While raging, gain pounce (full attack on charge). *(Fauna isotope movement expression)*

⚠️ **FLAG: Totemic Path talents are tagged [Supernatural] — they are mutation candidates for the later separation pass. They remain here as scaffolding.**

- **Greater Sunder** [Mundane, Active] (F) — Excess damage carries through to wielder.

## TREE 14: COMMANDER / AURA

Three branches: Vow of Enmity, Battlefield Tactician, Aura of Command.

- **Inspiring Presence (Tier 3)** [Infused, Active] (F/C) — Spend Stamina per round. Allies gain scaling bonus to attack, damage, or skill checks.
- **Stance Projection (Capstone)** [Infused, Active] (C: Skald) — Project your current active Stance to willing allies within aura radius. Allies gain the stance's benefits and penalties. Can accept or decline each round. ✅ MERGED (replaces specific "Raging Song" capstone with generalized version)
- **Contingent Expression** [Magical, Active — Prep Phase] (C: Mesmerist) — During rest, spend ÆP to implant one triggered expression in a willing ally. Fires automatically when conditions are met. Uses YOUR stats. Maintain up to Dominion modifier simultaneously. ✅ PLACED

## TREE 19: CHANNELING DISCIPLINE (NEW — from class extractions)

Talents governing HOW magical expressions are delivered, recovered, and amplified. These are learned techniques, not innate abilities. Feed Tₘ.

### Conduit Channeling Path (C: Kineticist):
- **Conduit Channeling (Crux)** [Magical, Active] (N) — Accept voluntary Strain on a known isotope to reduce ÆP/Stamina cost of an expression using that isotope. Trade long-term mutation risk for short-term power.
- **Planar Reserve** [Magical, Passive] (N) — During rest, pre-process and store Strain points equal to (Integrity modifier). Stored points can be spent in place of accepting live Strain.
