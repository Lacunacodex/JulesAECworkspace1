Document 5B --- Vertebra 5: Symptoms (Companion)

**WHAT THE ORACLE IS**

The oracle is a constrained random prompt generator. It produces a seed
that the GM interprets within the current context. The oracle does not
generate situations --- it generates constraints. The GM provides the
interpretation. The same roll in different contexts produces completely
different results because the context supplies the meaning the oracle
withholds.

This is the Variable effect from the Spell Sentence system applied to
narrative generation. The oracle provides the isotope --- the GM
provides the expression. Same input, different output depending on
context.

  **Gap-Filling**     No active clock produces symptoms matching the
                      pacing tracker's needs (Document 4C). The GM needs a
                      seed from outside the active front network. The
                      oracle generates unexpected pressure that may reveal
                      a new front.

  Consequences**      the GM needs to generate the side effect. The oracle
                      provides the specific form of the deferred
                      consequence. Roll the oracle, interpret the result
                      as the spine's self-correction.

  **Session Start     The GM arrives at the table without a strong
  Prompt**            opening. Roll the oracle. The result provides the
                      session's first seed --- the cold open that starts
                      play mid-situation.

  **Improvisation     The party goes somewhere unexpected. No prepared
  Support**           truths apply. Roll the oracle. The result provides a

  **Between-Session   The GM wants the world to produce events independent
  Events**            of active fronts. Roll the oracle for the territory.

**INTERPRETATION PROCEDURE**

The three dice produce a prompt. The prompt is not a situation. It is a
constraint set that the GM interprets within the current context.

**The Four Steps**

**1. Read the prompt.** Combine the three results into a single
sentence: "Something \[Scale\] is happening involving \[Pressure Type\]
pressure expressed as \[Effect\]." This is the raw prompt.

**2. Apply the context.** What is currently happening in the territory?
Which fronts are active? Which clocks are near threshold? Which NPCs are
present? The context determines which specific interpretation of the
generic prompt makes sense. A "significant biological growth" event near
the Titanwood means something different than the same prompt in a desert
city.

**3. Generate the truth.** The raw prompt + context = a specific
situation. Write it as a one-sentence truth in the Lazy DM format. This
truth enters the seed inventory and is subject to the same five-tool
management (Document 5A) as any other seed.

**4. Check front connection.** Does the generated truth connect to an
existing front? If yes, the oracle has revealed a new clock or symptom
for that front. If no, the truth introduces new pressure from outside
the current front network. If the party investigates and finds no
connection to existing fronts, a NEW front may need to be created to
explain the event.

**The Interpretation Principle**

The oracle generates constraints. The GM generates meaning. The same
roll produces different situations depending on who interprets it and in
what context. This is correct by design. The oracle is the Ironsworn
principle applied to the Codex's five-plane architecture: constrained
random prompts calibrated to the setting's physics, interpreted through
the setting's current state, producing seeds that surprise the GM as
much as the party.

If a roll genuinely does not fit the current context, reroll any single
axis. If it still does not fit, reroll all three. If three full rolls
produce nothing usable, the current context may not need an
oracle-generated seed --- the active clocks are providing sufficient
symptoms and the oracle is unnecessary this session.

**WORKED EXAMPLES**

**Example 1: Gap-Filling**

The pacing tracker needs a lateral beat but no active clock's symptoms
fit. The GM rolls the oracle.

**Roll:** d10 = 6 (Entropic/Consequence, Synod). d20 = 12 (Teleport). d6
= 3 (Significant).

**Raw prompt:** *"Something significant has appeared or disappeared as a
consequence of accumulated past actions."*

**Context:** The party is in the City of Folly. Mortis Ævum's archive is
nearby. The Synod processes death and consequence.

**Generated truth:** A building that was warded against planar intrusion
has vanished overnight. The ward's maintenance was neglected for decades
and the accumulated entropy finally collapsed the protection. The party
encounters the empty lot where the building stood. Neighbors remember
the building but disagree about when it disappeared.

**Front connection:** Connects to Kyril's Institutional Maintenance
front --- the headmaster's attention is being diverted and routine
maintenance is slipping. The vanished building is a symptom of Front 1
degrading. The oracle generated a seed the GM did not plan that
retroactively connects to an existing front.

**Example 2: Deferred Consequence**

A player spent 3 FP (Distance 2) to retard a clock last session. The GM
needs to generate the side effect from the skipped vertebrae.

**Roll:** d10 = 4 (Biological/Growth, Verdant Font). d20 = 13
(Illusion). d6 = 2 (Noticeable).

**Raw prompt:** *"Something noticeable is not what it seems, involving
biological processes."*

**Context:** The retarded clock was a Stormpeak military operation near
the Titanwood. The player spent FP through Borderlands expression
(physical feat) to reinforce a collapsing defensive position.

**Generated truth:** The Verdant Font responded to the sudden
reinforcement by routing growth around it. The reinforced position looks
stable, but the biological growth on either side is accelerating --- the
position is now a bottleneck that redirected the forest's expansion
rather than stopping it. The communities on either side of the defensive
line are now under increased pressure they were not expecting. The
party's successful intervention at one point created an unforeseen
problem at two adjacent points.

**Spine logic:** This is the spine self-correcting. The Distance 2 spend
skipped the Symptoms vertebra --- the change happened without the normal
observable warning signs. The deferred consequence arrives as the
Verdant Font's response to the discontinuity, producing symptoms the
party did not anticipate from their action.

**Example 3: Session Cold Open**

The GM arrives without a strong opening. Rolls the oracle for the
session's first beat.

**Roll:** d10 = 9 (Probabilistic/Contested Truth, Palimpsest). d20 = 14
(Mind Link). d6 = 1 (Minor).

**Raw prompt:** *"Something minor is happening involving contested truth
and new communication."*

**Context:** The party is staying at an inn in a crossroads town.

**Generated truth:** Two travelers at the bar are having a heated
argument about whether a particular road is safe. One insists the road
was cleared of bandits last month. The other insists the bandits have
returned and are worse than before. Both are sincere. Both cite
firsthand experience. The contested truth is a Palimpsest seed ---
contradictory accounts that the party can investigate. The minor
intensity means this is background texture, a lateral beat that opens
the session with a question rather than a crisis.

**ORACLE MATHEMATICS**

The three axes produce 10 × 20 × 6 = 1,200 unique combinations. The
distribution is not uniform across all three dimensions.

  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------
  **Pressure Type**  Each plane has a 20% probability (2 results on a
                     d10). All planes are equally likely. No plane is
                     privileged over any other.

  **Expression**     Each effect has a 5% probability (1 result on a
                     d20). All effects are equally likely. The Variable
                     result (roll 20) gives the GM complete discretion 5%
                     of the time.

  **Intensity**      Each level has a 16.7% probability (1 result on a
                     d6). Minor and Noticeable events are as likely as
                     Severe and Critical ones. This is intentional ---
                     the world produces as many minor anomalies as it
                     does major crises. The GM should not reroll
                     low-intensity results. Background texture IS the
                     world's density.
  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------

The frequency of oracle use determines how many unique combinations the
campaign explores. At one roll per session across a 50-session campaign,
50 of the 1,200 combinations are used --- less than 5%. At three rolls
per session, 150 combinations --- still only 12.5%. The oracle's
combination space vastly exceeds any single campaign's consumption.
Repetition is mathematically improbable. No two campaigns using the
oracle will produce the same seed pattern.

**CONTEXTUAL MODIFIERS**

Optional rules for adjusting the oracle's output based on current
conditions.

  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------
  **Territorial      In a territory with a dominant planar alignment, the
  Bias**             GM may replace Axis 1's roll with the territory's
                     dominant plane. A seed generated in the Titanwood
                     (Verdant Font dominant) automatically uses Verdant
                     Font pressure. The d10 is not rolled. This produces
                     seeds consistent with the territory's isotope
                     character.

  **College Bias**   When generating a deferred consequence for an FP
                     spend by a college-affiliated character, the GM may
                     replace Axis 2's roll with one of the driving
                     college's dominant Spell Sentence effects (see
                     Document 3A, College Methodology Table). The
                     consequence expresses through the college's native
                     vocabulary.

  **Intensity        Late in a campaign arc, when multiple clocks are
  Escalation**       approaching threshold, the GM may add +1 to the d6
                     result (minimum 2, maximum 6). This increases the
                     proportion of higher-intensity seeds, reflecting the
                     setting's accumulated pressure producing
                     larger-scale events.

  **Intensity        During a recovery arc or between major campaign
  Dampening**        events, the GM may subtract 1 from the d6 result
                     (minimum 1). This produces more Minor and Noticeable
                     seeds --- the background texture of a world between
                     crises.
  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------

Contextual modifiers are optional. The unmodified oracle produces valid
seeds in any context. The modifiers exist for GMs who want the oracle's
output to reflect specific territorial or narrative conditions more
consistently. Overusing modifiers reduces the oracle's surprise value
--- the unexpected combinations are its greatest strength.

**THE ORACLE AS DUAL-FUNCTION TOOL**

The oracle serves two distinct functions in the unified architecture.
Both use the same tables. The interpretation context differs.

**Function 1: Gap-Filling at Vertebra 5**

The oracle generates seeds from outside the active front network. The
result is a new piece of the world that the GM had not prepped. It may
connect to existing fronts retroactively (the oracle revealed a hidden
consequence of an active clock's advancement), or it may introduce
entirely new pressure (a front the GM had not considered, a territorial
event independent of institutional action, a cosmic anomaly the colleges
have not yet detected).

The gap-filling function maintains the world's density beyond the GM's
prepared content. When the party goes somewhere unexpected or the pacing
tracker demands a beat type not available from active clocks, the oracle
fills the gap with content that is consistent with the setting's physics
(the three axes ensure planar coherence) while surprising the GM (the
random combination prevents authorial prediction).

**Function 2: Deferred Consequence Generation at Vertebra 4**

When a Fulcrum Point spend skips vertebrae, the deferred processing must
produce a consequence. The oracle generates the form of that
consequence. The skipped vertebra determines the context; the oracle
determines the specific expression.

A Distance 2 spend skipped the Symptoms vertebra. The oracle generates
the symptom that SHOULD have appeared but was bypassed. A Distance 3
spend skipped Symptoms, Clocks, AND Pressure Points. The oracle
generates a more complex consequence reflecting the multiple skipped
vertebrae --- potentially rolling multiple times and combining the
results into a compound side effect.

The deferred consequence function ensures that FP spends have meaningful
side effects without the GM bearing the full creative burden of
inventing each one. The oracle distributes that burden between GM
judgment (interpreting the roll) and system-generated randomness (the
roll itself). The GM cannot predict the side effect. The system ensures
the side effect is planar-coherent and scale-appropriate. The surprise
IS the mechanism's value.
