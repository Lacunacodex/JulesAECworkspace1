### Kintsugi Cosmology — Unique Content Summary

**Section 1: The Smoothing Effect as Kintsugi**
- Aíon transformed catastrophe into survival, not averted it
- The Smoothing Effect makes seams aesthetic and comprehensible, not invisible
- Four comprehension tiers: Everyman (can't parse), Scholar (partial), College of Illusion (most advanced but incomplete), Aíon (full picture)
- College of Illusion's function: ongoing narrative maintenance of the patchwork through story
- Ongoing maintenance requires constant narrative reinforcement from five sources: College of Illusion, priests/clerics, bards/historians/scholars, every parent, and Aíon itself

**Section 2: Accords and Lacunae**
- Five forms of Accords: Item-Housed (weakest), Worship-Based (moderate), Named Pact (high), Symbiotic/Possession (deepest), Hybrid/Circumstantial
- Each form has a four-phase taint progression (cosmetic → social → compulsion → mutation)
- Calcified mutations persist even if the Accord is broken; non-calcified taint fades
- Four motivations for forming Accords: Belief (rejection of canonical narrative), Desperation, Proximity/Osmosis, Transactional
- Cult formation: four concentric circles (Core/Inner/Outer/Sympathetic)
- The establishment's dilemma: violent suppression strengthens Lacunae cults; only narrative reframing works

### Divine Comedies v3 — Deep Dive Extraction Summary

Full extraction document produced: `Divine_Comedies_v3_Concept_Extraction.md` (delivered to outputs this session).

**18 concept clusters extracted. Unique-to-this-document systems (found nowhere else in the project):**

1. **The Decision Pipeline:** Advisory Gods (set cage) → Chorus (deliberates) → Selûnae (encode action) → Alloy (judges). The four-stage mechanical sequence for all dramatic decisions in Tessera.

2. **16 Neutral Circumstances:** Universal morally-neutral events in four categories (Disruptions of Presence, Time, Value, Truth) that serve as engine inputs before any narrative framing is applied.

3. **Six Elements of Impossible Choice:** The formula for constructing genuinely impossible choices: Prior Commitment Chain, Asymmetric Consequence Shape, Scale Conflict, Irreversibility Type, Epistemic Condition, Witness Register. Derived from FDR/Truman atomic bomb analysis.

4. **Five-Layer Perspective Stack:** Aíon → Selûnae → Divine → Collegiate → Guild → Adventurer → NPC. Each layer sees more than the one below it. Genre is a function of which layer is narrating.

5. **Genre Lens Table:** 10 genre definitions mapping perspective layer + thematic engine + narrative mechanism. Horror = adventurer + theodicy + dramatic irony. Comedy = Aíon + gift-curse + structural irony. Etc.

6. **Seven-Layer Narrative Overlay:** Structural, Mechanism, Character Dynamic, Thematic Engine, Perspective, Tonal/Emotional Register, and Textural Background layers — simultaneous, not exclusive. Different layers foregrounded without altering base events.

7. **Nomos Silence Taxonomy:** Three types of divine silence (resentful, anticipatory, complicit) — all carrying content. Can be present simultaneously.

8. **Selûnae as Retrospective Evaluator:** The Fates read the thread backward — the choice doesn't create character, it discloses it. This is the Legacy/Palimpsest principle at the divine level. Confirmed as key structural pillar.

9. **In-Setting Media as Real Product:** Every in-setting media object (Unity radio dramas, Daedalon shop tales, etc.) has dominant and suppressed narrative layers and is simultaneously a world artifact, a positional divinity mechanism, and a packageable real-world product.

10. **Storytelling Structure Taxonomy:** Organized by what they process — what can't be said directly (Monomyth, Frame Narrative), what can't be seen from inside (Dramatic Irony, Structural Irony), what can't be resolved (Theodicy, Loyalty Conflict, Inheritance of Sin).
