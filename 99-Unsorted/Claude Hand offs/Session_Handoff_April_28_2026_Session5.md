### Hidden Forge (W4) — Deep Read Summary

16 isotopes, all ◆ Natural (unique — no antithetical states). Phase wheel: Crystal(Solid)/Chem(Liquid)/Steam(Gas)/Volt(Plasma).

The Forge has a **dual role**: direct Effect producer AND universal catalytic mediator for cross-planar combinations. Without Forge mediation, antithetical cross-planar combinations (Borderlands↔Palimpsest, Verdant Font↔Synod) produce Forbidden or Paradox states.

Key isotopes with unique mechanical significance:
- **F-01 Spire** — Psionic resonance, information processing. Deepguard relay network backbone.
- **F-02 Serum** — Injectable magic crystals. Subdermal augmentation. Foundation of Kan-tech implants.
- **F-07 Mana-Punk** — Liquid magic in crystal pipelines. Battery efficiency, charge sharing. Kan-tech infrastructure backbone.
- **F-08 Galvanism** — Electricity through fluid. Reanimation science. Three distinct healing mechanisms. Soul-drive technology foundation. Harkness Engine theoretical basis.
- **F-11 Diesel** — Sustained operation. Self-sustaining loop. Setting B industrial backbone.
- **F-14 Cyberpunk** — Hard-light projection. THE Forge's illusion (construction, not perception).
- **F-15 Clockwork** — Precision timing + mechanical determinism. Luck Control through CALCULATION, not fate. Chronosynclastic Institute's philosophical foundation.
- **F-16 Battery** — Energy storage. Foundation of ALL Powered items. Explosive failure risk on full depletion.

Forge Healing exists in FIVE distinct mechanisms: Galvanism (threshold maintenance), Vat (pharmaceutical correction), Serum (diagnostic repair), Diesel (biological maintenance), Mana-Punk (life support). None are biological acceleration — that's Font territory.
