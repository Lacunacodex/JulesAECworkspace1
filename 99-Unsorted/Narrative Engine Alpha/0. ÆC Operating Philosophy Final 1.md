*The narrative engine, the game system, and the expression layer are one
architecture in three vocabularies*

**THE CORE INSIGHT**

There are not three systems. There is one system described three ways.

The narrative engine generates situations through profiling, pressure
extraction, front organization, clock tracking, symptom placement,
observation collapse, and feedback compounding. The game system resolves
situations through Condition Tracks, Composite Defense, Soak, the Spell
Sentence, talent trees, Fulcrum Points, and the four-pillar resource
model. The expression layer presents situations through attention
direction, temporal texture, information sequencing, evidentiary
presentation, embodied expression, serial packaging, and persistence
encoding.

These are not three layers stacked on top of each other. They are three
readings of the same architecture. The Spell Sentence IS the symptom
vocabulary. Condition Tracks ARE clocks. The Legacy System IS the
feedback loop. The Psyche Schema IS the profiling instrument. The
Political Zoetrope IS the civilizational front system. The isotope map
IS the resource and expression layer simultaneously. There are no
analogies in the preceding sentences. Each IS statement is an identity
statement.

**THE THREE LENSES**

The same events are legible through three perspectives. Each perspective
is correct. Each is incomplete without the other two.

  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------
  **The GM Lens**    The prep-side view. What the operator built, what
                     clocks are running, what fronts are acting, what
                     truths are available. This lens sees the machinery.
                     It is the perspective from which the system is
                     operated. Documents 1A through 5C primarily serve
                     this lens.

  **The Player       The experience-side view. What the participant
  Lens**             encounters, investigates, debates, and chooses. This
                     lens sees the fiction. It is the perspective from
                     which the system is experienced. Document 6A
                     primarily serves this lens. The player never sees
                     the machinery. They see the output.

  **The              The meaning-side view. What emerged from the
  Retrospective      collision between preparation and observation. This
  Lens**             lens sees the pattern. It is available only after
                     the cycle completes. Documents 7A and 7B serve this
                     lens. The meaning was not planned. It was
                     discovered.
  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------

The unified engine is not optimized for one lens. It produces output
legible through all three simultaneously. A session that works only from
the GM perspective (mechanically functional but experientially flat) is
incomplete. A session that works only from the player perspective
(exciting but structurally arbitrary) is incomplete. A session that
produces no retrospective pattern (fun in the moment but forgotten
afterward) is incomplete. The complete session is legible through all
three.

**THE UNIVERSAL CHARACTER SHEET**

Everything uses the same character sheet. The same Schema instrument
profiles player characters, NPCs, institutions, territories, gods, and
the Overdeity. The same rules apply. The same build rules apply. The
same pressure extraction procedure applies. The same front generation
procedure applies. The same clock tracking applies. The same feedback
loop applies.

The difference between an NPC and an institution is not the system. It
is the SCALE. The same Spell Sentence effect describes a character
casting a spell and an institution deploying a program. The same clock
template tracks a character's wound recovery and a civilization's
governmental transition. The same Legacy System reads pattern in a
character's accumulated choices and in a territory's accumulated
factional resolutions.

The perception of three separate game modes (character-scale,
institutional-scale, civilizational-scale) is a filter across the same
medium. The Fulcrum Point distance model makes this explicit: the
participant extends their reach from Distance 0 (personal resolution)
through Distance 3 (institutional manipulation) to Distance ∞
(cosmological intervention) on a single continuum. No game mode changes.
The reach changes. The content changes. The system is the same.

**THE COSMOLOGICAL GROUNDING**

The engine's architecture is not a game designer's abstraction imposed
on a setting. It is the setting's cosmology expressed as playable
mechanics.

The Palimpsest holds superposition states. Fulcrum Points are the
participant's ability to choose which state collapses. The Smoothing
Effect is Aíon continuously spending infinite FP to maintain coherence
across two merged realities. The five alchemy layers are five planes'
isotopes expressing through observable effects. The Spell Sentence is
the grammar of those effects at every scale. The Legacy System is the
Selûnae reading fate backward. The Zoetrope is the civilizational clock
operating at the longest timescale.

When the participant spends Fulcrum Points, they are performing a
cosmological act. They are manipulating the Palimpsest. When the GM
holds seeds in superposition, they are performing Aíon's function at the
table scale. When the table reads the campaign's meaning
retrospectively, they are performing the Selûnae's function at the human
scale. The game's cosmology and the game's mechanics are the same
system. The setting's gods and the table's participants are performing
the same operations at different scales. This is not metaphor. It is
architecture.

**THE CAMPFIRE PRINCIPLE**

The unified engine is a campfire. The operator lights it and steps back.
The participants gather around it and co-create. The fire's fuel is
prepared density --- truths held in superposition. The fire's heat is
emotional engagement produced when Collins' conditions are met. The
fire's light is meaning --- the retrospective pattern that emerges when
the table looks at the path they've walked together.

The campfire is not a metaphor for the engine. The campfire IS what the
engine produces. The three survival functions (information encoding,
social norm calibration, group binding) are not aspirational goals. They
are the outputs of a properly operating spine cycle. A cycle that
produces all three is a complete session. A cycle that produces fewer is
mechanically functional but humanly incomplete.

The engine's ultimate product is not a story. It is a shared experience
that the participants remember, reference, and use to understand
themselves and each other. The story is the residue. The experience is
the product. The meaning is the gift the participants give each other by
tending the fire together.

**PSYCHE SCHEMA**

System Reference --- Version 4

Document 1A --- Vertebra 1: Schema

*The profiling instrument of the Ætherium Codex unified architecture*

**WHAT THE SCHEMA IS**

The Psyche Schema is the profiling instrument at Vertebra 1 of the
Codex's seven-vertebra spine. It answers three questions about any
entity: who are they, how do they process events, and what do they seek.
Every subsequent vertebra reads the Schema's output. Pressure Points
(Vertebra 2) are extracted from Schema anomalies. Fronts (Vertebra 3)
are built from Schema-driven motivations. Clocks (Vertebra 4) tick at
rates determined by Schema-derived methodology. The Schema is not a
character sheet supplement. It is the foundation the entire architecture
builds from.

The Schema applies to everything that has internal complexity. Player
characters, NPCs, institutions, territories, and gods all have Schemas
or Schema-equivalents (see Document 1B: Profiling Companions Guide).
Everything uses the same profiling architecture because everything
interacts with the same spine. The rules do not change between scales.
The content changes. The instrument is universal.

**The Schema as Narrative Energy Source**

The Schema generates Fulcrum Points --- the metacurrency for spine
manipulation. Internal tensions (outlier scores, asymmetric splits
between paired dimensions, pattern breaks) are pressure points. Each
pressure point generates one Fulcrum Point per session. A flat, simple
profile generates minimal narrative energy. A complex, contradictory
profile generates enormous narrative energy. Character depth IS
mechanical power in the Codex. Not because deeper characters have higher
stats. Because their internal contradictions produce more Fulcrum
Points.

**The Schema as a Genuine Instrument**

The Psyche Schema is a measurement tool, not a personality quiz. It does
not sort characters into types. It maps the internal landscape of
values, tensions, and emotional architecture with enough resolution to
predict behavior under pressure and identify where the interesting story
lives. The scores are coordinates in a dimensional space defined by the
gods' portfolios. The gods are the axis labels. The character is the
point plotted in that space.

**THE THREE SECTIONS**

The Schema is organized into three sections, each capturing a different
category of data. The sections are not hierarchical. They are three
lenses on one identity, each revealing information the others cannot.

  ------------------- ---------------------------------------------------
  **I. Core           11 paired dimensions mapping the character's
  Philosophy**        worldview. Each pair is anchored to a god's
                      dual-aspect portfolio. Scored 1-100 per aspect.
                      These are the character's philosophical convictions
                      --- how they believe the world works and how they
                      believe it should work. The most stable section.
                      Changes slowly through accumulated experience.

  **II. The Selûnae** 3 paired dimensions mapping the character's
                      relationship to fate, time, and consequence. Scored
                      1-100 per aspect. These are the character's
                      temporal orientation --- how they relate to their
                      own past, present trajectory, and future. More
                      fluid than Core Philosophy. Face drift between
                      paired aspects IS the character's personal clock.

  **III. Heart's      9 dimensions mapping the character's emotional
  Compass**           architecture. Each dimension has three faces:
                      Balanced (healthy expression), Unrestrained
                      (excess), and Trauma (wounded expression). The face
                      the character currently operates from determines
                      how the dimension manifests. The most volatile
                      section. Can shift within a single session through
                      emotionally significant events.

Sections I and II are derivable from the character's philosophical
position and institutional role. A GM generating an NPC can produce
reasonable scores for these sections from a brief character concept.
Section III (Heart's Compass) requires knowledge of the character's
personal emotional life and is the section that demands the most
authorial investment. The GM shortcut (see Section 9) acknowledges this
asymmetry.

**Positional Divinity as Coordinate System**

The gods' portfolios are not flavor text. They are the dimensional axes
of the Schema's measurement space. Each score is the character's
coordinate on that god's axis. The full Schema plots the character in a
22-dimensional space (11 pairs × 2 aspects) anchored by divine
principles. This is the Positional Divinity system expressed as
character architecture --- the gods define the space, the character
occupies a position within it, and the position determines which divine
aspects resonate with the character's soul.

Veneration occurs through performing a role within a god's portfolio,
not through formal worship. A character with Kaelen 85 venerates Bellum
Hegemon's productive-conflict aspect every time they engage in
purposeful struggle. The character's scores are simultaneously a
personality profile AND a map of which divine aspects they embody
through their actions.

**Face Drift as Personal Clock**

The Selûnae scores are not static. They drift over the course of a
campaign as the character accumulates experience. The direction and rate
of drift IS the character's personal clock. A character drifting from
Maiden toward Wench is claiming more agency. A character drifting from
Crone toward Hag is accumulating burdens faster than they can integrate
them. A character whose Selûnae scores are stable has reached
equilibrium with their fate. A character whose scores are in rapid drift
is in crisis.

Face drift is the only Schema section that changes without explicit
player or GM intervention. The drift happens naturally as the Legacy
System (Vertebra 7) reads the character's accumulated choices and
adjusts the temporal orientation accordingly. The player does not update
their Selûnae scores --- the GM updates them based on the pattern the
Legacy System reveals.

Face transitions are the Heart's Compass's equivalent of Selûnae drift.
A character whose Silas (Friendship) transitions from Balanced to Trauma
during the campaign has experienced a betrayal significant enough to
restructure their emotional architecture. The transition is a dramatic
event --- it should emerge from play, not be assigned arbitrarily.

**THE COMPANION ELEMENTS**

The Schema's three sections profile the character's internal landscape.
Five companion elements provide the external context that shapes how the
internal landscape expresses.

**College Affiliation**

Which college shaped the character determines their methodology, their
persistent test, their failure state, and their operational vocabulary.
Two characters with identical Schema scores but different college
affiliations produce completely different fronts because the college
determines HOW the profile expresses. The Schema says what you value.
The college says how you pursue it.

College affiliation also determines the character's planar alignment
(which of the five planes their methodology channels), their operational
tempo (how quickly they act), and their institutional vocabulary (how
they describe what they do). See the College Initiations Canon for the
full initiation, persistent test, and failure state architecture of each
college.

**Archetype**

The mechanical chassis the profile operates through. The Schema is the
personality. The Archetype is the mechanical toolkit. A high-Kaelen
Wildpath character playing through a martial Archetype produces a
different set of mechanical expressions than the same Schema through a
caster Archetype. The Archetype constrains which mechanical expressions
of the Schema are available at Vertebra 6 (Adventures / Observation
Collapse).

**Legacy System**

The backward-facing identity that accumulates at Vertebra 7 (Feedback
Loop). At Vertebra 1, the Legacy is the character's starting state ---
what the Palimpsest has already recorded about this soul. The Legacy
system does not read forward from milestones. It reads BACKWARD from
accumulated pattern. The character's identity at session 1 is the Legacy
reading everything that came before. Over a campaign, the Legacy
accumulates new pattern from the character's choices, which in turn
modifies how the Schema is read --- not the scores themselves, but their
weight and significance.

**Soul Stone**

The physical container of the Schema data. In-setting, the soul stone
carries the god-name weights, aspect values, and full experiential
record. The Chirurgeons' query reads it. The Legacy system is stored in
it. The soul stone is the MEDIUM in which the Schema exists. This
matters because it means the Schema is a THING in the world --- it can
be read, queried, stolen, damaged, or transferred. The profile has a
physical instantiation that can be interacted with through the game's
mechanics.

**Positional Divinity**

The Schema's coordinate system (described in Section I) is
simultaneously a map of which divine aspects the character embodies. The
character's scores determine which gods' faces resonate with their soul,
which in turn determines which divine portfolio the character's actions
support. This is not worship. It is structural resonance. The character
venerates a god by being the kind of person whose actions fall within
that god's portfolio.

**THE ACTIVE PROPERTIES**

Three properties transform the Schema from a static profile into a
generative instrument that reaches into the spine and shapes play.

**Goffman: The Two-Faced Profile**

Every profiled entity has a front stage (what they present to the world)
and a back stage (what they actually think, feel, and plan). The gap
between front stage and back stage IS data. The Schema's surface scores
are the front stage. The outliers, asymmetric splits, and pattern breaks
are where the back stage leaks through.

At Vertebra 2 (Pressure Points), the Goffman gap is a primary source of
extractable pressure. When you identify that an NPC's presented behavior
contradicts their Schema scores, that contradiction IS a pressure point.
The NPC Assessment Card's Contradictions Log is literally a Goffman gap
tracker --- recording the observable discrepancies between what the NPC
presents and what their profile predicts.

For player characters, the Goffman gap is chosen by the player. They
decide what their character presents versus what they actually feel. For
GM-generated NPCs, the gap is derived from the Schema --- the front
stage is what the NPC's institutional role demands, and the back stage
is what the Schema scores reveal about who they actually are underneath
the role.

**Beliefs and Instincts**

Beliefs are Schema elements stated as active convictions: "I will
protect the innocent." "The law exists to serve power, not justice." "No
one should die alone." Each belief is anchored to one or more Schema
dimensions and represents the character's commitment to acting on that
dimension's values. Beliefs CREATE mandatory engagement --- certain
seeds at Vertebra 5 (Symptoms) automatically trigger observation
collapse at Vertebra 6 (Adventures) because the participant's belief
demands response. The character CANNOT walk away from situations that
challenge their beliefs. This is the bonding mechanism between Vertebra
1 (who you are) and Vertebra 6 (how you engage).

Instincts are automatic behavioral rules: "I always check for exits." "I
never turn my back on someone I don't trust." "I share food before
eating." Instincts are Seeds that collapse automatically without
deliberation. They produce consistent observable behavior that other
characters and the audience can track. Instincts are the leitmotifs of
the expression layer --- the recurring behaviors that encode the
character's identity in the group's collective memory.

Players write 2-3 beliefs and 2-3 instincts during character creation.
Beliefs can change through play when the character's convictions are
tested and found wanting. Instincts are deeper --- they change only
through transformative experiences (initiations, traumatic events,
Legacy system crystallization).

**Narrative Identity Type (McAdams)**

How the character processes events --- the interpretive template they
apply to experience. Four types:

  ------------------- ---------------------------------------------------
  **Redemption**      Bad events are processed as setups for eventual
                      positive outcomes. The character finds meaning in
                      suffering by believing it leads somewhere better.
                      The suffering was worth it because of what came
                      after.

  **Contamination**   Good events are processed as precursors to loss.
                      The character anticipates that positive experiences
                      will be corrupted or destroyed. Joy is temporary
                      because pain follows.

  **Agency**          Events are processed through the lens of personal
                      control. The character believes their choices
                      determine outcomes. Success is earned. Failure is
                      the character's responsibility.

  **Communion**       Events are processed through relationships. The
                      character's experience is defined by connection to
                      others. Success is shared. Loss is relational.
  ------------------- ---------------------------------------------------

The Narrative Identity type is not a Schema score. It is a processing
template that determines how the character INTERPRETS the events the
spine produces. The same clock advancement produces different character
reactions depending on which type processes it. A Redemption character
whose clock crosses the intervention threshold sees it as the darkest
moment before the turning point. A Contamination character sees it as
confirmation that the positive trajectory was always going to fail.

The type is derived from the character's backstory and their current
Heart's Compass face distribution. Characters with predominantly
Balanced faces tend toward Redemption or Agency. Characters with
predominantly Trauma faces tend toward Contamination. Characters with
high Silas/Aethel/Zeniel scores tend toward Communion. The type can
shift through play as the character's Heart's Compass faces change.

**FULCRUM POINT GENERATION**

The Schema generates Fulcrum Points from its internal tensions. The
generation mechanic connects Vertebra 1 (Schema) to Vertebra 4 (Clocks)
where Fulcrum Points are spent. See Document 4B (Fulcrum Points System)
for the complete spend mechanic, distance model, and bidirectional
response architecture.

**Pressure Point Identification**

Three types of pressure points generate Fulcrum Points:

**Outlier Scores:** Any single aspect score above 80 or below 20 in any
section. Extreme scores represent convictions so deep they are
load-bearing --- the character's identity would collapse without them.
Convictions this deep create pressure because reality constantly tests
them. Each outlier generates 1 FP.

**Asymmetric Splits:** Any paired dimension where the gap between the
two aspects exceeds 50 points. A 75-point gap between Veritas 90 and
Gnosis 15 is not a preference --- it is an emotional wound disguised as
a philosophical position. The world will inevitably present situations
where the suppressed aspect is the correct response, and the character
cannot access it. Each asymmetric split generates 1 FP.

**Pattern Breaks:** Scores that contradict the expected profile for the
character's college, role, or presented identity. An immortal warden
with a moderate-high Veyra (Unrequited) score breaks the pattern.
Pattern breaks indicate hidden pressures the character themselves may
not be aware of. Each pattern break generates 1 FP.

**Generation Formula**

Fulcrum Points per session = (count of outlier scores) + (count of
asymmetric splits) + (count of pattern breaks).

A Tier 0 Everyman with a mostly centered Schema might generate 1-2 FP. A
headmaster with massive outliers, enormous splits, and multiple pattern
breaks might generate 12-15 FP. The system rewards character complexity
with narrative energy. This is the same formula applied to NPCs,
institutions, territories, and gods --- the GM's Fulcrum Point budget
for any entity is generated by the same instrument from the same source.

**THE PLAYER QUESTIONNAIRE**

For player characters, the Schema is generated through a structured
intake process. The player answers questions about their character's
values, history, and emotional life. The GM derives scores from the
answers. The derivation uses the questions as anchors --- the specific
answers map to specific dimensional ranges.

**Intake Sections**

  ------------------- ---------------------------------------------------
  **1. The Concept**  Name, alias, core concept in one sentence. What is
                      this character about?

  **2. The            Questions mapping to each Core Philosophy pair.
  Philosophy**        "When rules conflict with justice, which do you
                      follow?" (Nomos: Themis vs. Lex). "Is struggle the
                      point of living or the obstacle to living?" (Moros:
                      Silas vs. Dolor). One question per divine pair.

  **3. The Timeline** Questions mapping to the Selûnae pairs. "What came
                      to you without seeking it?" (Maiden). "What did you
                      take by force?" (Wench). "What bond has lasted
                      longest?" (Mother). "What relationship did you
                      leave behind?" (Strumpet). "What lesson cost you
                      the most?" (Crone). "What consequence do you still
                      carry?" (Hag).

  **4. The Heart**    Questions mapping to the Heart's Compass
                      dimensions. "Who do you love and how?" (Isolde).
                      "Who would you die for?" (Eselle). "When did you
                      last laugh so hard you couldn't breathe?" (Corin).
                      "What obligation do you carry that you didn't
                      choose?" (Constantine). For each: which face is
                      active (Balanced, Unrestrained, Trauma)?

  **5. The Toolkit**  Defining moment, signature skill, greatest
                      weakness, method of operation. These are not scored
                      --- they contextualize the scores. The defining
                      moment often reveals the Heart's Compass face
                      transitions. The weakness often maps to the
                      lowest-scoring dimension.
  ------------------- ---------------------------------------------------

The GM reviews the answers and assigns scores using the 10-band overlay
as a guide. A strong, specific answer about a dimension suggests a high
score (61-100). A vague or uncertain answer suggests a mid-range score
(41-60). A deflection or avoidance suggests either a very low score (the
dimension is absent) or a Trauma face (the dimension is present but
painful). The questionnaire produces an initial Schema that the first
few sessions of play will refine.

After the questionnaire, the player writes 2-3 Beliefs (active
convictions anchored to Schema dimensions) and 2-3 Instincts (automatic
behavioral rules). These are the Schema's active components that reach
into the spine during play.

**THE GM SHORTCUT**

The full questionnaire is player-facing. GMs generating NPCs need a
compressed procedure that produces a functional Schema from minimal
inputs. The shortcut acknowledges that GM-generated scores are authored
estimates rather than derived measurements --- the precision is
approximate, but the diagnostic function (pressure point identification)
still works.

**The Five-Question NPC Generator**

  ------------------- ---------------------------------------------------
  **1. College and    Which college trained them? What role do they
  Position**          occupy? These two facts constrain the Core
                      Philosophy scores --- a Stormpeak officer will have
                      high Bellum Hegemon scores, and the specific
                      balance between Kaelen and Bellax depends on
                      whether they're trending toward mastery or failure.

  **2. The Want**     What do they want right now? The want maps to the
                      highest Core Philosophy scores. A character who
                      wants justice has high Nomos. A character who wants
                      knowledge has high Vestus. The specific face
                      (Themis vs. Lex, Veritas vs. Gnosis) reveals their
                      methodology.

  **3. The Fear**     What are they afraid of? The fear maps to the
                      lowest scores or the Trauma faces in the Heart's
                      Compass. A character afraid of abandonment has
                      Silas or Aethel in Trauma face. A character afraid
                      of losing control has Rhapsodia tension.

  **4. The            What's one thing about them that doesn't match
  Contradiction**     their role? This is the deliberate pattern break
                      --- the score that generates the mystery front. The
                      military commander with a secret artistic passion.
                      The healer who enjoys the power that proximity to
                      death provides.

  **5. Clock          Where are they on their college's personal clock?
  Position**          Early (still learning), middle (competent but
                      tested), late (approaching mastery or failure).
                      Which direction are they trending? This determines
                      which Selûnae face is dominant.
  ------------------- ---------------------------------------------------

From these five answers, the GM assigns approximate scores to the 3-5
most relevant Core Philosophy dimensions, the dominant Selûnae pair, and
the 2-3 most active Heart's Compass dimensions. Everything else is left
at 50 (neutral). The pressure points are identifiable from the extremes
and the contradiction. The NPC is functional for engine derivation.

Section III (Heart's Compass) is the section that requires the most
creative authorship for GM-generated NPCs. The engine acknowledges this:
Sections I and II are derivable from position and philosophy. Section
III requires the GM to make character choices that the engine cannot
generate. The five-question shortcut minimizes this burden by asking for
only one Heart's Compass data point (the fear) and one deliberate
pattern break (the contradiction).

**THE COMPOSITE SCORES**

Two composite scores sit outside the three sections. They are derived
from the full Schema but are not dimensions themselves --- they are
emergent properties of the overall profile.

**Aíon (Cosmic Stability)**

A measure of how well the character's identity integrates into the
cosmic architecture. High Aíon stability indicates a profile that is
coherent, internally consistent, and aligned with the setting's
structural logic. The character makes sense within the world. Their
actions are predictable from their profile. The spine models them
accurately.

The Aíon score is relevant to the Smoothing Effect --- characters with
high cosmic stability encounter less resistance from the setting's
coherence-maintenance architecture. Their actions are already within the
parameters the Smoothing Effect permits. Characters with low cosmic
stability encounter more friction because their existence is itself a
discontinuity the Smoothing Effect wants to dampen.

**The Lacuna (Cosmic Instability)**

A measure of how much the character's identity disrupts the cosmic
architecture. High Lacuna instability indicates a profile that contains
contradictions the setting's structural logic cannot fully resolve. The
character is a discontinuity --- a point where the Smoothing Effect
cannot fully suppress the gap between the two merged settings, or
between what the character's profile predicts and what they actually do.

The relationship between Aíon and Lacuna is not inversely proportional.
A character can have high stability AND high instability simultaneously
--- they are deeply integrated into the cosmic architecture AND they are
a source of disruption within it. These characters are the most
narratively interesting because they are simultaneously essential to the
system and threatening to it.

The derivation formula for both composite scores is pending calibration
and will be finalized in the game system's mathematical framework. The
conceptual definitions are established. The specific numerical
derivation requires playtesting.

**STORY STACK VALIDATION**

Every completed Schema should be tested against the Story Stack's four
layers to ensure the profile is rich enough to sustain play across all
dimensions of the game.

  ------------------- ---------------------------------------------------
  **Fantasy**         Who does the player want to BE through this
                      character? The Schema should support the player's
                      fantasy --- the version of themselves they want to
                      explore. If the player wants to be a cunning
                      strategist, the Schema should have high Kanon and
                      Vestus scores. If the fantasy is a passionate
                      defender, the Schema needs high Bellum Hegemon and
                      Constantine.

  **Actions**         What does the player want to DO? The Schema's
                      active components (Beliefs and Instincts) should
                      generate engagement opportunities. If there are no
                      Beliefs that force interaction with the world, the
                      character will be passive. Every Schema should
                      produce at least 2-3 situations per session where a
                      Belief demands response.

  **Economy**         How does the character interact with the game's
                      systems? The 10-band overlay should produce a
                      distinctive mechanical signature. Characters whose
                      dice pools are all d8 (everything at 41-60) are
                      mechanically bland. Characters with d12s and d4s
                      (strong outliers) have mechanical texture that
                      creates meaningful choices.

  **World**           How does the character relate to the setting?
                      College affiliation, Positional Divinity resonance,
                      and the Selûnae's temporal orientation should
                      connect the character to the setting's cosmology. A
                      character with no connection to any college, no
                      divine resonance, and no temporal orientation is
                      floating free of the world rather than embedded in
                      it.
  ------------------- ---------------------------------------------------

If a Schema fails one or more layers, the solution is not to add scores.
It is to add specificity. A character who fails the Actions layer needs
sharper Beliefs. A character who fails the World layer needs clearer
college affiliation or divine resonance. A character who fails the
Fantasy layer needs a conversation between the player and the GM about
what the player actually wants to experience.

**EXPRESSION GUIDANCE: PERSISTENCE ENCODING**

The Schema's strongest expression bond is Persistence Encoding --- the
techniques that anchor a profiled entity in the group's collective
memory across sessions. Without encoding, the profile is forgotten
between encounters. With encoding, the character becomes a recognizable
presence that the group tracks and anticipates.

**Leitmotifs**

A recurring verbal phrase, physical action, or behavioral pattern
associated with the character. The phrase or action appears whenever the
character is present and the associated Schema dimension is active. Over
time, the leitmotif carries the character's full emotional weight in a
few words or gestures.

**Design principle:** One per character maximum. More than one dilutes
the signal. The leitmotif should be derived from the character's highest
or most distinctive Schema dimension.

**Behavioral Signatures**

The NPC personality technique: one behavior (repeated physical action
when not addressed), one opinion (editorial perspective nobody asked
for), one refusal (what they won't do). Each maps to a Schema dimension.
The behavior maps to the dominant Core Philosophy face. The opinion maps
to the strongest Belief. The refusal maps to the lowest score or a
Trauma-face dimension.

**Third-Person Narration**

Report instead of perform. "The Docent goes quiet and then says the
symbol is familiar but won't elaborate" communicates mystery without
performing it. The emotional cue is the narrator's contribution. The
audience's imagination provides the performance. Each participant
generates a different character in their imagination from the same cue.
The underspecification is a feature --- Schema Theory (Bartlett) says
the audience's schema fills the gap with personally meaningful content.

**DOCUMENT 1A --- ADDENDUM**

Diagnostic Findings: Corrected Resolutions

*Supersedes Section 15 of Document 1A (Psyche Schema System Reference
v4)*

The original diagnostic findings in Section 15 were identified during
proof-of-concept testing under the pre-unified architecture. The unified
spine, the universal character sheet principle, and the regulatory
systems resolve or reframe each finding. This addendum replaces Section
15 with corrected resolutions.

**1. FALSE PRECISION**

**Original finding:** GM-authored NPC scores feel authoritative but are
estimates. Why 85 and not 80? There is no derivation formula for
GM-generated scores.

**Status:** Resolved.

The system never reads individual scores as absolute values. It reads
three relative properties: outlier status (above 80 or below 20),
asymmetric splits (gap exceeding 50 points between paired aspects), and
pattern breaks (scores contradicting the expected profile). All three
are relative readings. A score of 82 and a score of 88 produce identical
diagnostic output --- both are outliers, both generate 1 Fulcrum Point,
both signal a load-bearing conviction.

The 10-band overlay compounds this resolution. The GM does not need the
exact number. They need the correct band. Any score within 81-100 maps
to d12 and produces the same mechanical expression. The difference
between 82 and 88 is invisible to the resolution system.

**Corrected Procedure**

For GM-generated NPCs, assign bands rather than specific numbers. The
six bands are: Absent (1-10, no die), Minimal (11-20, d4), Developing
(21-40, d6), Established (41-60, d8), Dominant (61-80, d10),
Load-bearing (81-100, d12). Band assignment is honest about its
resolution. It does not claim decimal precision it cannot support.

Specific numerical scores are a player-facing refinement for characters
who complete the full questionnaire. The questionnaire's structured
intake produces answers specific enough to justify placement within a
band. NPCs generated through the five-question shortcut produce band
assignments. Both produce the same diagnostic output --- the pressure
point identification reads bands, not points.

**2. HEART'S COMPASS AUTHORSHIP**

**Original finding:** Sections I-II are derivable from position and
philosophy. Section III requires GM authorship. This was framed as a gap
in the engine.

**Status:** Reframed as correct design.

Schema Theory (Bartlett), one of the spine's regulatory systems,
establishes that participants co-complete underspecified elements with
personally meaningful content. The GM is a participant in the spine. The
Heart's Compass is the designated entry point for the GM's creative
investment. The "authorship burden" is the same mechanism as the
player's contribution at Vertebra 6 (Adventures) --- it is the
participation that makes the output meaningful rather than mechanical.

A fully derivable NPC would be a mechanical output with no soul. The
Heart's Compass is where the GM invests enough personal creative energy
that the NPC stops being a structural position and starts being a
person. The Corin 35 score derived from "military people have dark
humor" IS the GM's schema filling the gap with their knowledge of
military fiction. That is not a failure of the engine. That is the
engine working as designed --- the underspecification invited the GM's
contribution, and the GM contributed from their own experience.

**Corrected Framing**

Section III is not a derivation gap. It is the designated entry point
for GM authorship. The procedure should acknowledge this explicitly:

*Sections I and II are derivable from position and philosophy. Section
III is where you make the character a person. The engine provides the
structure. You provide the heart. This is not a limitation. This is your
contribution to the campfire.*

The five-question shortcut handles this correctly by asking for the fear
(one Heart's Compass data point) and the contradiction (one pattern
break). Those two inputs are sufficient for the GM's schema to fill the
remaining Heart's Compass dimensions through natural co-completion
during play. The NPC does not need a complete Heart's Compass at
generation. They need enough for the first encounter. Play reveals the
rest.

**3. EXTERNAL PRESSURE GAP**

**Original finding:** The derivation handles NPC-internal pressure (what
the Schema reveals) but not NPC-external pressure (what the environment
does TO the person regardless of their Schema). This was identified as a
missing step in the derivation procedure.

**Status:** Resolved by the universal character sheet principle.

The unified architecture establishes that everything uses the same
character sheet. The POSITION has its own pressure profile. A headmaster
position generates specific pressures regardless of who occupies it ---
institutional maintenance demands, inter-college diplomacy requirements,
faculty management obligations, resource allocation decisions. These are
not derived from the person's Schema. They are derived from the
position's Schema.

**Corrected Procedure**

Add one step to the NPC-to-Adventure derivation procedure. After reading
the NPC's personal Schema for internal pressure points, read the
POSITION's pressure profile for external pressure points. Two Schema
readings --- one for the person, one for the role --- producing two sets
of fronts that interact.

**Internal fronts:** Internal fronts are driven by the NPC's personal
tensions. They exist because of who the person is. Kyril's Vestus
Containment front (from the Veyra pattern break) and The Aíon Silence
front (from the Constantine/Mother tension) are internal fronts.

**External fronts:** External fronts are driven by the position's
structural demands. They exist because of what the role requires
regardless of who occupies it. Kyril's Institutional Maintenance front
and The Political Leverage Problem are external fronts. Any headmaster
of Mortis Ævum would face these pressures.

**The intersection:** The intersection between internal and external
fronts is where the richest story lives. How does this specific person's
internal tensions interact with this specific position's structural
demands? Kyril's personal Vestus containment pulls attention away from
the position's institutional maintenance demand. That interaction ---
internal front competing with external front for the NPC's finite
attention --- is the pressure that produces observable symptoms the
party can detect.

This also retroactively explains the Colonel proof-of-concept's Front 4
(Succession Question). That front was invented by the GM because the
personal Schema could not generate it. With the corrected procedure,
Front 4 would have been derived from the positional reading --- "what
does the senior officer position generate?" produces "other officers see
this person as a leadership candidate" as a structural demand of the
role, not as a property of the person occupying it.

**4. CREATIVE INTERPRETATION STEP**

**Original finding:** The mapping from pressure point to front requires
creative interpretation informed by setting knowledge. This step was
hidden in the derivation procedure.

**Status:** Named and acknowledged. The step is now explicit.

The step is named: Contextual Interpretation. It sits between pressure
point identification (analysis) and front mapping (construction). The
procedure should mark the transition clearly:

*Steps 1-2 are analysis. Step 3 is interpretation. You are now
contributing, not calculating.*

**The Three Sub-Operations**

**1. Operational Context:** For each pressure point, ask: where in this
NPC's operational life does this tension manifest? The answer requires
knowing the NPC's college methodology, their position's daily demands,
and the current state of the setting around them. Kyril's Veritas/Gnosis
split manifests as the Vestus Containment front because of the specific
setting fact that Vestus IS the Gnosis aspect made divine. A different
NPC with the same split in a different setting context would produce a
completely different front.

**2. Personal Shaping:** For each external pressure point (from the
positional reading), ask: how does this NPC's personal Schema shape
their response to the positional demand? The headmaster position demands
institutional maintenance. Kyril's Constantine 95 means he handles this
demand through absolute duty. A different headmaster with Constantine 40
would delegate, producing a completely different institutional culture.

**3. Intersection Check:** Check whether any internal and external
fronts interact. Do the personal tensions amplify or dampen the
positional demands? Where does the NPC's attention divide between
personal and positional fronts? The allocation of finite attention
between competing fronts IS the dramatic engine. An NPC whose internal
fronts consume all attention neglects their external obligations. An NPC
whose external obligations consume all attention suppresses their
internal tensions until they erupt.

The creative interpretation step is the same mechanism operating at
every level of the campfire --- the engine provides the framework, the
GM interprets it through their own experience and setting knowledge. The
interpretation is the GM's contribution. It cannot and should not be
automated. The procedure's job is to make the invitation explicit so the
GM knows when they are calculating and when they are creating.

**5. CLOCK PARAMETERIZATION TIMING**

**Original finding:** Schema derivation produces fronts with potential
clocks, not parameterized clocks. Specific clock values (segments,
thresholds, terminal states) need play context. This was framed as a
limitation of the derivation procedure.

**Status:** Confirmed as correct architecture.

The spine has distinct prep-phase and play-phase operations. Vertebrae
1-3 (Schema, Pressure Points, Fronts) are prep-phase --- they establish
what exists before play begins. Vertebrae 4-7 (Clocks, Symptoms,
Adventures, Feedback Loop) are play-phase --- they operate during and
between sessions. The boundary between Fronts and Clocks is the boundary
between prep and play.

**Prep-Phase Output**

The Schema derivation produces the front's clock POTENTIAL --- a list of
what this front could be tracking, what the tick triggers might be, what
the terminal states would look like. This is the menu. The GM creates
these during prep without committing to specific mechanical values.

A well-prepped front has 2-4 potential clocks described in narrative
terms: "This front could track how aggressively Vestus's agents probe
the archives, or how quickly the containment protocols are being
circumvented, or how much of Kyril's attention is being diverted from
institutional maintenance." Each potential clock is one sentence. No
segments. No thresholds. No terminal state mechanics. Just what it would
track.

**Play-Phase Output**

When a front enters active play --- when the party engages with the area
of the setting where the front operates --- the GM instantiates specific
clocks from the potential menu. The clock template's nine fields are
filled in at this point using the play context to set appropriate
values.

Segments are set based on how long the situation should run at the
table. A 4-segment clock resolves in one session. A 6-segment clock runs
across two or three sessions. An 8-segment clock is a multi-session arc.
The GM calibrates to desired pacing, not to abstract correctness.

Tick triggers are set based on what the party is doing. An
event-triggered clock ticks when specific party actions occur. A
session-triggered clock ticks automatically between sessions, creating
pressure regardless of party behavior. A hybrid uses both. The trigger
type is chosen based on whether the GM wants the clock to respond to the
party (event) or pressure the party (session).

The intervention threshold is set based on dramatic structure. Place it
where the transition from "preventable" to "mitigable" produces the most
dramatic decision point. Usually halfway to two-thirds through the
segments.

**The Advantage of Separation**

The GM can prep extensively --- many fronts, many potential clocks ---
without overcommitting to specifics that play might invalidate. A front
whose potential clocks include "tracking Vestus's probe escalation"
becomes an instantiated clock with specific segments only when the party
enters the City of Folly and the front becomes relevant. If the party
never goes to the City of Folly, the clock potential enriched the GM's
understanding of the setting without requiring mechanical
parameterization.

Prep that does not reach the table is not wasted. It is density that
informs the GM's improvisation, colors NPC behavior, and creates the
sense of a world in motion beyond the party's immediate field of view.
But density does not need mechanical specifics until it reaches the
table. The separation between prep-phase potential and play-phase
parameterization is the engine's efficiency mechanism.

**PROFILING COMPANIONS GUIDE**

Extending the Schema to Every Entity

Document 1B --- Vertebra 1: Schema (Companion)

*The universal character sheet applied beyond individual characters*

**THE PRINCIPLE**

Everything uses the same character sheet. The same profiling instrument.
The same pressure extraction. The same Fulcrum Point generation. The
same spine. Player characters, NPCs, institutions, territories, and gods
are all entities with internal complexity that generates narrative
energy proportional to their contradictions.

This document does not define new instruments. It applies the Psyche
Schema (Document 1A) to entity types other than individual characters.
The architecture is identical at every scale. The content changes. The
instrument is universal. The perception filter determines which lens the
operator views it through.

Each entity type maps onto the Schema differently --- an institution
does not have a Heart's Compass in the same way a person does. But each
entity type produces the same diagnostic outputs: outlier values,
asymmetric splits, and pattern breaks that generate Fulcrum Points and
drive the derivation procedure at Vertebra 2 (Pressure Points) and
Vertebra 3 (Fronts).

**PROFILING PLAYERS**

The player is not their character. The player is a participant in the
campfire with their own engagement patterns, preferences, and needs.
Profiling the player tells the GM what kind of fuel to add to the fire
so this participant stays engaged.

**Player Types (Robin Laws)**

Six engagement profiles describing what each player seeks from the game.
Most players are composites of two or three types with one dominant. The
profile is read from observed behavior across 3-6 sessions, not from
asking the player directly. What they engage with reveals their type
more reliably than what they say they want.

  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------
  **Butt-Kicker**    Wants decisive physical action. Engages most during
                     combat, chases, and confrontations. Disengages
                     during extended planning, investigation, or social
                     negotiation. Feed with: situations where hitting
                     things is the obvious AND correct solution. Create
                     encounter designs that let them shine physically.

  **Tactician**      Wants puzzles, plans, and optimization. Engages most
                     when analyzing options, designing approaches, and
                     executing strategies. Disengages during
                     roleplay-heavy scenes or railroaded outcomes. Feed
                     with: tactical information, multiple valid
                     approaches, and respect for their plans.

  **Method Actor**   Wants character moments and emotional scenes.
                     Engages most during NPC interactions, moral
                     dilemmas, and situations that test their character's
                     personality. Disengages during mechanical combat or
                     content that doesn't touch their character concept.
                     Feed with: situations that press on their
                     character's beliefs and instincts.

  **Storyteller**    Wants dramatic narrative. Engages most during plot
                     revelations, climactic moments, and scenes with
                     thematic weight. Disengages during routine
                     encounters or mechanics-heavy sessions. Feed with:
                     consequences that matter, revelations that
                     recontextualize, and dramatic structure.

  **Power Gamer**    Wants mechanical mastery and character growth.
                     Engages most during character advancement, loot
                     acquisition, and situations that reward system
                     knowledge. Disengages during scenes where mechanical
                     skill is irrelevant. Feed with: mechanical
                     challenges that reward optimization and character
                     investment.

  **Casual Player**  Wants to participate without pressure. Engages most
                     when included in the group's activity without being
                     forced to lead. Disengages when put on the spot or
                     required to drive the session. Feed with: a role in
                     the group's plans without the burden of inventing
                     the plans.
  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------

**Player Profiling as Pressure Points**

Each player's type IS a pressure point in the table's collective Schema.
A table with three Butt-Kickers and one Method Actor has an asymmetric
split --- the Method Actor's needs are suppressed by the dominant
engagement mode. That suppression generates pressure that, if
unaddressed, produces disengagement (the Method Actor stops attending)
or conflict (the Method Actor disrupts combat-heavy sessions seeking
character moments).

The GM's job is to feed all types every session. Not equally ---
proportionally. The dominant type gets the most fuel. The minority types
get enough to sustain engagement. A session that feeds only one type is
a session that loses everyone else. The player profile determines the
fuel mix, not the session structure.

**Fulcrum Point Analog**

Players do not generate Fulcrum Points. Their characters do. But the
player's type determines HOW they spend. A Tactician spends FP on
Distance 0 (boosting tactical outcomes). A Method Actor spends FP on
Distance 1-2 (revealing pressure points or placing character-relevant
seeds). A Storyteller spends FP on Distance 2-3 (manipulating clocks and
relationship edges for dramatic effect). The player's type shapes the
spend pattern, not the generation.

**PROFILING TABLES**

The table is a group with its own creative agenda, distinct from the
individuals composing it. GNS Theory (Ron Edwards) profiles the GROUP's
collective optimization target.

**GNS Theory: The Three Creative Agendas**

  ------------------- ----------------------------------------------------
  **Gamism**          The table optimizes for challenge and triumph.
                      Encounters are tests of player skill. Character
                      builds matter. Tactical decisions matter. The
                      satisfaction comes from overcoming difficulty
                      through competent play. The system's mechanical
                      depth is the product.

  **Narrativism**     The table optimizes for meaningful choice and
                      thematic weight. Moral dilemmas matter. Character
                      arcs matter. The satisfaction comes from making
                      decisions that reveal what the characters (and by
                      extension the players) believe. The story's
                      emotional impact is the product.

  **Simulationism**   The table optimizes for internal consistency and
                      world logic. The setting's rules must be coherent.
                      Consequences must follow logically. The satisfaction
                      comes from a world that behaves predictably
                      according to its own established physics. The
                      setting's believability is the product.
  ------------------- ----------------------------------------------------

Most tables are composites with one dominant agenda. The dominant agenda
determines which regulatory systems the table prioritizes:

  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------
  **Gamist tables**  Weight the Information Economy heavily ---
                     information is a tactical resource. The Smoothing
                     Effect is an obstacle to overcome. Transportation is
                     secondary to mechanical engagement.

  **Narrativist      Weight Transportation heavily --- the fictional
  tables**           dream must be protected. The Smoothing Effect
                     supports verisimilitude. Information Economy serves
                     dramatic pacing rather than tactical advantage.

  **Simulationist    Weight Schema Theory (Bartlett) heavily --- internal
  tables**           consistency is paramount. The Smoothing Effect IS

**Table Profile as Meta-Schema**

The table's GNS profile is a meta-Schema that governs how the spine's
regulatory systems are prioritized. It does not determine WHAT the spine
produces --- the same fronts, clocks, and symptoms operate regardless of
creative agenda. It determines HOW the table RECEIVES those outputs. A
Gamist table encountering the same symptom as a Narrativist table will
investigate it differently, engage with it through different mechanics,
and judge the session's success by different criteria.

The GM reads the table's GNS profile the same way they read an NPC's
Schema --- through observed behavior, not through asking. Which sessions
produced the most engagement? Which moments did the table remember
afterward? Which moments fell flat? The engagement pattern reveals the
creative agenda more reliably than any conversation about preferences.

**PROFILING INSTITUTIONS**

An institution has a Schema. Not metaphorically. The institution's
operational philosophy produces outlier values, asymmetric splits, and
pattern breaks that generate Fulcrum Points and drive fronts exactly as
a character's Schema does.

**The Institutional Schema**

An institution's Schema is constructed from three sources:

**Founding Philosophy:** The institution's official charter, values
statement, or founding philosophy maps onto the Core Philosophy
dimensions. A college founded on abjuration principles (Deepguard) has
high Nomos scores. Its Themis/Lex balance reveals whether the
institution prioritizes the spirit or the letter of its mandate. A
mercantile guild (Sovereign Exchange) has high Valorin scores. Its
Mercatus/Credo balance reveals whether the institution prioritizes fair
exchange or predatory extraction.

**Operational Reality:** How the institution actually behaves ---
distinct from what it claims to value. The gap between charter and
practice IS the institutional Goffman gap. Every institution has a front
stage (the public mission) and a back stage (the covert operations, the
institutional self-interest, the compromises that keep it functioning).
Praxis Collegium's front stage is humanitarian coordination. Its back
stage is comprehensive surveillance disguised as interrelated jobs.

**Internal Factions:** Which of the institution's internal factions are
in tension. A monolithic institution with total internal alignment is
rare and generates minimal FP. An institution riven by competing
philosophies --- the Sovereign Exchange's Mercatus faction against its
Credo faction --- generates enormous institutional FP from the
irreconcilable tension at its core.

**Reading the Institutional Schema**

The same three diagnostic reads apply:

**Outliers:** Institutional values that are extreme produce outliers.
Stormpeak's Bellum Hegemon scores are load-bearing --- the institution
would cease to exist without its martial identity. That extremity
generates FP because the world constantly presents situations where
martial identity is insufficient (diplomatic crises, civilian
governance, peacetime malaise).

**Asymmetric Splits:** The gap between the institution's two strongest
values. Mortis Ævum's tension between its overt medical mission and its
covert truth-keeping function produces an asymmetric split --- the
institution simultaneously serves life AND manages the architecture of
death. The split generates FP because every operational decision must
balance both mandates.

**Pattern Breaks:** Behaviors that contradict the institutional charter.
A humanitarian institution conducting surveillance (Praxis). A
commercial institution enforcing justice (Sovereign Exchange under
certain headmasters). Each break generates FP because the contradiction
demands energy to maintain.

**Institutional Fulcrum Points**

The institution's FP pool is generated by its Schema's pressure points,
the same formula as individual characters. The FP are spent by whoever
is OPERATING the institution at the moment --- the headmaster, the
faction taking its turn, the front's driving NPC. When Kyril takes a
faction turn action on behalf of Mortis Ævum, he draws from BOTH his
personal FP pool (generated by his personal Schema) AND the
institutional FP pool (generated by the college's institutional
tensions). He is powerful because he is a complex person running a
complex institution. Both generate energy. Both fuel his actions.

A simple guild with a straightforward mission and no internal
contradictions generates minimal institutional FP. It can take basic
faction turn actions but cannot do anything surprising. An institution
riven by internal politics, competing factions, and ideological splits
generates enormous institutional FP. The internal chaos is mechanically
dangerous because it is narratively energetic.

**The Nine Colleges as Institutional Profiles**

Each college's initiation, persistent test, master state, and failure
state (see College Initiations Canon) constitute its institutional
Schema. The initiation defines the founding philosophy. The persistent
test defines the operational reality. The master state defines the ideal
expression. The failure state defines the institutional back stage ---
what happens when the institution's process fails and produces slag
instead of masters. The gap between master and slag IS the institutional
Goffman gap.

**PROFILING TERRITORIES**

A territory has a Schema-equivalent constructed from three systems: its
Zoetrope position, its isotope profile, and its institutional landscape.

**Zoetrope Position as Core Philosophy**

The Political Zoetrope's 16-position civilizational lifecycle IS the
territory's Core Philosophy equivalent. Each position describes the
territory's current governance philosophy, economic structure, and
social contract. The directional pressures pulling the territory toward
adjacent positions are the territorial equivalent of paired-dimension
tension.

A territory at Zoetrope Position 8 (commercial ascendancy) has high
Valorin scores by definition --- the society's organizing principle is
trade. The Mercatus/Credo balance at Position 8 depends on whether the
commercial ascendancy is producing shared prosperity (Mercatus-dominant)
or concentrated wealth (Credo-dominant). The transition pressure toward
Position 9 or back toward Position 7 IS the territory's asymmetric split
--- the forces pulling the society in competing directions.

**Isotope Profile as Resource Landscape**

The 104 isotopes across five planes distributed across the territory's
geography determine what resources exist, who can access them, and which
types of action the territory supports. A territory rich in Verdant Font
isotopes supports biological operations (Wildpath methodology). A
territory rich in Palimpsest isotopes supports information operations
(Chronosynclastic methodology). The isotope distribution constrains what
fronts CAN do by determining what materials they have access to.

Isotope distribution asymmetries ARE territorial pressure points. A
territory with abundant Borderlands isotopes but depleted Verdant Font
isotopes has a resource split --- physical infrastructure is abundant
but biological resources are scarce. That scarcity generates fronts (who
controls the scarce resource?) and clocks (how quickly is the scarcity
worsening?).

**Institutional Landscape as Operational Context**

Which colleges, guilds, and governments operate in the territory
determines the institutional dimension of the territorial Schema. A
territory with three competing colleges has a richer institutional
Schema (more pressure points, more FP) than one dominated by a single
institution. The inter-institutional tensions within the territory are
the territorial equivalent of the Heart's Compass --- the emotional
architecture is replaced by the relational architecture of competing
institutional interests.

**Territorial Fulcrum Points**

A stable territory at a comfortable Zoetrope position with no major
factional conflicts generates few FP. Events are predictable. The spine
models this territory easily. A territory in Zoetrope transition with
competing institutional interests and asymmetric isotope distribution
generates many FP. Surprising things happen constantly because the
territory's internal tensions create too many possible states for the
spine to predict.

Territorial FP are spent by whatever entity is interacting with the
territory at that moment. The GM introduces the territory's
unpredictability through its FP --- more FP means more unexpected
events, more complications, more emergent situations. When the party
enters a high-FP territory, the GM has a larger budget for injecting
surprise. When they enter a low-FP territory, events unfold predictably.
The territory's complexity IS its narrative energy.

**PROFILING GODS**

The gods have Schemas. The god documents (Aurelion, Moros, Daedalon,
Nomos, Bellum Hegemon, Valorin, Agora, Verdena, Rhapsodia, Vestus,
Kanon/Anima, the Selûnae, Thalassus, the Chorus, Aíon) each define a
divine Schema through the god's dual-aspect portfolio, institutional
expressions, and internal tensions.

**Divine Schema Architecture**

A god's Schema is defined by its paired aspects. Each god owns one
dimension of the Core Philosophy. The god's relationship to its own two
faces IS its Schema:

**Harmonious Faces:** A god whose two faces are in harmony generates
minimal FP. The god is stable. Its portfolio is coherent. Its mortal
agents operate without internal conflict from the divine source.

**Tense Faces:** A god whose two faces are in tension generates enormous
FP. Valorin's Mercatus (fair trade) versus Credo (predatory extraction)
are irreconcilable. The god IS the tension. Bellum Hegemon's Kaelen
(productive conflict) versus Bellax (suffering) are in permanent
competition. Kanon versus Anima are two separate gods in structural
opposition. Each tension generates divine FP that flows through the
god's mortal agents and institutional expressions.

**Divine Fulcrum Points**

Divine FP flow DOWNWARD through the institutional hierarchy. The god's
internal tension generates FP. Those FP are available to the god's
mortal agents (Saints, Legends, clergy, initiated practitioners) through
their divine resonance. A character whose Schema scores align strongly
with a tense god has access to more divine FP than a character aligned
with a harmonious god --- not because the tense god is more powerful,
but because its internal contradictions generate more narrative energy
that its resonant mortals can channel.

This means the gods who are most interesting narratively are also most
mechanically potent through their mortal agents. The dramatic gods ---
the ones whose portfolios contain irreconcilable tensions --- produce
more FP than the stable gods. This is correct by design. The campfire
burns brightest with fuel that contains internal energy.

**Aíon as Special Case**

Aíon's FP generation is theoretically infinite because Aíon's Schema
encompasses the tension between TWO MERGED SETTINGS. The contradiction
at the foundation of reality itself. The Smoothing Effect is Aíon
continuously spending FP to maintain coherence --- infinite narrative
energy allocated to preventing the world from tearing apart at the
seams.

When a participant spends FP to override the Smoothing Effect (Distance
∞), they are momentarily outspending Aíon's continuous maintenance
expenditure at one specific point. The Smoothing Effect reasserts
because Aíon's generation is functionally infinite. The participant
punched through momentarily, and the maintenance resumes. The deferred
consequence of overriding Smoothing is Aíon's architecture noticing the
discontinuity and responding.

entity's narrative potency is the sum of all layers' internal tensions.

Bellum Hegemon's divine FP (moderate to high depending on the

adventurer is not separate from the system. They are a participant in
the same architecture that governs everything else.

**Using the Questions at the Table**

The ten questions are not a checklist to work through sequentially. They
are a diagnostic reflex. When a scene begins and the player or GM senses
uncertainty about what is happening, the questions identify which
specific uncertainty is generating the discomfort. Usually one or two
questions produce the critical insight. The rest confirm what is already
visible.

**For players:** When the table stalls: run the ten questions
internally. The question whose answer you cannot produce is the question
that identifies the stall's source. If you cannot identify what everyone
wants (Q1), the motivational landscape is unclear --- the situation
lacks defined actors. If you cannot identify what happens if you do
nothing (Q6), the default trajectory is unclear --- the situation lacks
urgency. If you cannot identify the gap (Q9), the informational
landscape is opaque --- the situation lacks investigable leads.

**For GMs:** The ten questions generate the raw material for the NPC
Assessment Card. Each NPC encountered during play should produce answers
to at least Q1 (want), Q7 (performing or genuine), and Q4 (what's
confirmed versus assumed about them). These three answers populate the
Card's core fields.

**THE SCHEMA DIAGNOSTIC READS**

The prep-phase extraction method. Applied to completed or partially
completed Schemas before play begins. Three reads, each identifying a
different type of pressure point.

**Read 1: Outlier Detection**

**Procedure:** Scan every Schema score for values above 80 or below 20
(or Load-bearing / Absent bands for GM-generated NPCs). Each outlier is
a pressure point.

**Why outliers generate pressure:** High outliers (80+) are load-bearing
convictions. The entity's identity would collapse without this value.
Reality constantly tests load-bearing convictions because the world does
not accommodate extremes without friction. A character with Constantine
95 (Duty) is under permanent pressure because every situation presents
competing claims on their attention that duty alone cannot resolve.

Low outliers (below 20) are absent capacities. The entity cannot access
this dimension when the situation demands it. A character with Corin 5
(Playfulness) has no mechanism for levity, stress relief, or perspective
shift through humor. The absence is structural brittleness --- the
entity functions, but the missing dimension is a fault line that
sufficient stress will expose.

**Read 2: Asymmetric Split Identification**

**Procedure:** Scan every paired dimension for gaps exceeding 50 points
between the two aspects. Each split is a pressure point.

**Why splits generate pressure:** A split this large is not a
preference. It is an emotional wound disguised as a philosophical
position. Kyril's Veritas 90 / Gnosis 15 (a 75-point gap) does not mean
he merely prefers contextual knowledge over raw data. It means his
relationship to decontextualized knowledge is so aversive that it
distorts his judgment. He cannot engage with Gnosis-aligned situations
rationally because the suppressed aspect is too far below access
threshold.

The world will inevitably present situations where the suppressed aspect
is the correct response. When it does, the entity either fails (cannot
access the needed capacity) or breaks (the suppressed aspect erupts
uncontrolled). Both outcomes generate story. The asymmetric split is the
most reliable front generator in the Schema because it guarantees that
the world and the entity will eventually collide on the dimension the
entity cannot handle.

**Read 3: Pattern Break Recognition**

**Procedure:** Compare each score against the expected profile for the
entity's college, role, and presented identity. Any score that
contradicts the expected profile is a pattern break. Each break is a
pressure point.

**Why breaks generate pressure:** Pattern breaks indicate hidden
pressures the entity themselves may not be aware of. Kyril's Veyra 55
(Unrequited) breaks the expected profile of an immortal warden whose
emotional range has compressed to institutional service. An entity in
his position should not have a moderate-high unrequited connection
score. The fact that he does means something is happening beneath the
surface that the rest of the profile cannot explain.

Pattern breaks generate the richest fronts because they produce mystery
fronts --- situations where the entity's own behavior surprises them.
The entity acts in ways their conscious self-model cannot account for.
These are the adventures that lead toward the deepest revelations.

**THE DUAL READING**

Every NPC occupies a role. The role has its own pressure profile
independent of who fills it. Pressure extraction requires TWO readings:
the personal Schema (who the entity is) and the positional profile (what
the role demands).

**The Personal Reading**

The three diagnostic reads above applied to the entity's individual
Schema. This produces internal pressure points --- tensions that exist
because of who the person IS, regardless of what role they occupy.
Kyril's Vestus Containment front and Aíon Silence front are internal.
They would follow him into any role.

**The Positional Reading**

Ask: what pressures does this POSITION generate independent of who
occupies it? A headmaster position demands institutional maintenance,
inter-college diplomacy, faculty management, resource allocation, and
succession planning regardless of the headmaster's personal Schema. A
military officer position demands tactical decisions, personnel
management, morale maintenance, and chain-of-command navigation
regardless of the officer's personality.

The positional reading produces external pressure points --- tensions
that exist because of the ROLE, not the person. Kyril's Institutional
Maintenance front and Political Leverage Problem are external. Any
headmaster of Mortis Ævum would face these pressures.

**The Intersection**

The richest story lives where internal and external fronts compete for
the entity's finite attention. How does this specific person's internal
tensions interact with this specific role's structural demands?

Kyril's personal Vestus containment (internal front) pulls attention
away from institutional maintenance (external front). The institutional
decay that results is not caused by Kyril's incompetence --- it is
caused by a complex person whose internal pressures divert attention
from positional demands. The party detects the institutional decay (a
symptom at Vertebra 5) and investigates. The investigation leads to the
front. The front leads to the intersection. The intersection reveals
both who Kyril is AND what the position demands of him. Both are
necessary to understand the situation.

**THE CONTEXTUAL INTERPRETATION STEP**

Between pressure point identification (analysis) and front mapping
(construction at Vertebra 3), a creative interpretation step is
required. This step is explicitly named because it represents the
transition from calculation to contribution.

*Steps 1-2 of the derivation are analysis. This step is interpretation.
You are now contributing, not calculating.*

**The Three Sub-Operations**

**1. Operational Context:** For each pressure point, ask: where in this
entity's operational life does this tension manifest? The answer
requires knowing the entity's college methodology, their position's
daily demands, and the current state of the setting around them. The
same pressure point in different operational contexts produces
completely different fronts. A Veritas/Gnosis split in a
Chronosynclastic analyst produces an intelligence front. The same split
in a Wildpath Warden produces a survival methodology front. Context
determines expression.

**2. Personal Shaping:** For each external pressure point (from the
positional reading), ask: how does this entity's personal Schema shape
their response to the positional demand? The position demands
institutional maintenance. A headmaster with Constantine 95 handles this
through absolute duty. A headmaster with Constantine 40 delegates and
produces a completely different institutional culture. The personal
Schema is the lens through which positional demands are processed.

**3. Intersection Check:** Check whether any internal and external
fronts interact. Where does the entity's attention divide between
personal and positional fronts? An entity whose internal fronts consume
all attention neglects their external obligations --- producing
observable symptoms of institutional decay that the party can detect. An
entity whose external obligations consume all attention suppresses their
internal tensions until they erupt --- producing sudden behavioral
anomalies that break the expected pattern.

The creative interpretation step cannot and should not be automated. It
is the GM's contribution to the campfire at this vertebra. The
procedure's job is to make the invitation explicit so the GM knows when
they are calculating (pressure point identification) and when they are
creating (contextual interpretation).

**THE REGULATORY DIAGNOSTICS**

Two regulatory systems are most active at this vertebra.

**McDowall Diagnostic (Information / Choice / Consequence)**

When you cannot find pressure points, one of three elements is absent
from the situation:

  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------
  **Information      You cannot extract pressure because you do not have
  missing**          enough data about the entity. The Schema is
                     incomplete. The ten questions are unanswered. The
                     solution is not deeper analysis --- it is more data.
                     Ask more questions. Observe more behavior. Read more
                     of the profile.

  **Choice missing** You can see the pressure but it is not generating
                     fronts because the entity has no meaningful options.
                     They are locked into a single course of action. The
                     solution is introducing competing demands --- give
                     them something else they want that conflicts with
                     what they are already pursuing.

  **Consequence      The pressure points exist but nothing happens when
  missing**          they are stressed. The entity's tensions have no
                     stakes attached. The solution is connecting the
                     pressure points to things the entity (or the party,
                     or the setting) cares about. What happens if this
                     tension breaks?
  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------

The McDowall diagnostic is the fastest tool for unsticking extraction.
When the ten questions and the Schema reads are not producing useful
output, check which of the three elements is absent. Supply it. The
extraction resumes.

**Red Team Method --- First Application**

Adopt the adversary's perspective on the pressure landscape. After
identifying the entity's pressure points through the personal and
positional readings, ask: if I were an adversary targeting this entity,
which pressure points would I exploit?

The adversary's perspective reveals which pressure points are VULNERABLE
--- not just where tension exists, but where tension can be weaponized.
The pressure point that the entity has managed for decades is stable
tension. The pressure point that the entity does not know exists is
exploitable tension. Red Teaming the pressure landscape identifies which
points are managed, which are unmanaged, and which are invisible to the
entity.

This is the method's first of three applications. At Vertebra 3
(Fronts), Red Team validates whether a front's behavior is strategically
coherent. At the Catalysts layer, Red Team checks whether the operator
(the GM or the campfire-lighter) has become visible.

**EXPRESSION GUIDANCE: EVIDENTIARY PRESENTATION**

Vertebra 2's strongest expression bond is Evidentiary Presentation ---
presenting raw observational data rather than interpreted conclusions.
This bond is strong because pressure extraction is fundamentally an
analytical process that operates on evidence, not inference.

**The Journalism Principle**

Show the primary source. Let the audience interpret. At the table, this
means the GM (or the investigating player) presents what was OBSERVED,
not what it MEANS.

*The merchant touched his collar twice while saying the shipment was
delayed. He looked toward the warehouse when he mentioned his supplier.
His voice dropped when he said the word 'bandits.'*

These are three pieces of raw behavioral evidence. The GM does not say
"the merchant is lying." The GM presents the behaviors. The party draws
the conclusion. When the party draws its own conclusion, the conclusion
is more convincing because they reached it themselves. The evidence was
the call. The interpretation is the response. The participation makes
the discovery feel earned rather than delivered.

**Applying Evidentiary Presentation to Schema Reads**

When the GM identifies a pressure point through the Schema diagnostic
reads, the pressure point is not presented as "this NPC has an
asymmetric split between Veritas and Gnosis." That is an analytical
conclusion. The pressure point is presented through its observable
symptoms --- the NPC's specific behaviors that result from the split.
The chirurgeon who flinches when asked about research methodology. The
officer who changes the subject when philosophical questions arise. The
merchant who maintains meticulous records but never reads them.

Each observable behavior is one piece of evidence that the party (or the
reader, or the community member) collects and assembles into a picture.
The picture they assemble is shaped by their own schema (Bartlett) ---
they see the pattern that their experience prepares them to recognize.
Different participants may assemble different pictures from the same
evidence. Both pictures are valid. The evidence is the same. The
interpretation is the contribution.

**FULCRUM POINT CALCULATION**

The extraction procedure produces the FP generation count as a
byproduct.

**For individual entities:** Count all pressure points identified in
Steps 1-3 of the extraction procedure. This count IS the entity's FP
generation per session. No additional formula is needed. The extraction
procedure and the FP generation procedure are the same operation.

**For institutions:** Count the institutional pressure points identified
in Step 4's positional reading applied to the institution's own Schema
(Document 1B). This count is the institutional FP pool, available to
whoever operates the institution.

**For composite entities:** Sum the personal FP (Step 1-3) and the
applicable positional, institutional, territorial, and divine FP pools
(Document 1B). The composite FP budget determines the GM's total
narrative energy budget for this entity in this context.

The pressure extraction procedure at Vertebra 2 is simultaneously the
diagnostic tool (finding where the story lives), the front generator
input (each pressure point maps to a front at Vertebra 3), and the
economy tool (each pressure point generates one FP). These three
functions are not separate operations. They are three readings of the
same output.

the contextual interpretation step, how fronts connect through
relationship maps (Document 3B), and how fronts take action through
faction turns (Document 3C). The NPC Assessment Card (Document 2B) is

**USAGE INSTRUCTIONS**

**When to Create a Card**

Create a card when an NPC transitions from minor to notable --- when the
party interacts with them a second time, when they become relevant to an
active front, or when the GM identifies them as a potential front driver
during prep. Do not create cards preemptively for NPCs the party may
never encounter. Create them when they matter.

**Progressive Fill**

The card is not filled in all at once. It accumulates across sessions.

**First encounter:** Name, college, position. Want if known. Leave
everything else blank.

**Second encounter:** Fear if revealed. Performing or genuine
assessment. First contradictions log entry if anything surprised you.

**Sessions 3-5:** Goffman gap observations. Relationship edges to other
NPCs. Pressure points becoming visible through accumulated behavior.

**When the NPC becomes a front driver:** Front potential section. By now
the card has enough data to identify which pressure points map to
potential fronts and what clocks those fronts could drive. This is the
prep-phase output described in Document 2A.

**The Contradictions Log**

The most important section on the card. Date each entry by session
number. Record what the NPC said or did that contradicted their previous
behavior, their stated position, or their expected profile. Each
contradiction is a live Goffman gap detection --- a moment where the
back stage leaked through the front stage.

Over time, the contradictions log reveals the NPC's actual character
more reliably than their stated position. A log with zero entries after
five sessions means the NPC is either exactly what they present (rare)
or the observer has not been looking closely enough (common). A log with
multiple entries is a goldmine --- each contradiction is a potential
mystery front waiting to be mapped.

**The Relationship Edges Section**

Records the NPC's connections to other NPCs with three properties per
edge:

**Trust:** How freely information flows between them. High trust =
honest communication. Low trust = guarded, filtered, or deceptive
communication.

**Debt:** Latent obligations. Who owes whom. Debts create leverage.
Unresolved debts accumulate tension.

**Tension:** Current stress on the connection. High tension = the
relationship is approaching a state change. When tension exceeds
threshold, the state changes (Ally → Neutral → Rival → Enemy or the
reverse).

This section is the raw data for the Relationship Map (Document 3B). The
cards store individual edges. The map visualizes the network.

**The Front Potential Section**

Filled during prep, not during play. After the card has accumulated
enough data (typically 3-5 sessions of observation), the GM reviews the
pressure points and maps each to a potential front:

**Internal:** Fronts driven by the NPC's personal Schema tensions.
Outlier scores generate maintenance fronts. Asymmetric splits generate
conflict fronts. Pattern breaks generate mystery fronts.

**External:** Fronts driven by the position's structural demands. What
the role requires regardless of who occupies it.

**Mystery:** Fronts emerging from pattern breaks where the NPC's own
behavior surprises them. These are the richest adventure generators.

Each front potential entry includes a one-sentence description of what
clock that front could drive. These are narrative descriptions, not
parameterized clocks --- the clock is instantiated at play-phase when
the front becomes active (see Document 4A).

**When to Retire a Card**

A card is retired when the NPC is no longer relevant to the campaign ---
deceased, departed from the region, or all associated fronts resolved.
Retired cards are not discarded. They become the Feedback Loop's raw
material (Vertebra 7) --- the record of past NPCs whose consequences may
still propagate through the relationship map. A retired NPC whose debt
edges are still live on other NPCs' cards is not truly retired. Their
influence persists through the network.

**FRONT ARCHITECTURE**

Organized Actors, Plans, and Connections

Document 3A --- Vertebra 3: Fronts

*The organizational layer of the spine --- where pressure becomes
action*

**WHAT A FRONT IS**

A front is a motivated actor pursuing a goal through a specific
methodology with specific resources along a trajectory that will produce
a specific outcome if no one intervenes. It is the organizing structure
that converts pressure points (Vertebra 2) into clocks (Vertebra 4).
Without fronts, pressure points are static observations. With fronts,
pressure points become stories in motion.

A front is not a villain. A front is not a plot. A front is a situation
with momentum. The Wildpath Wardens running containment operations in
the Titanwood is a front. The Sovereign Exchange expanding trade routes
into contested territory is a front. A chirurgeon trending toward
numbness while managing a hospital wing is a front. Any entity pursuing
an objective through sustained action over time is running a front,
whether they intend to or not.

The spine produces fronts from pressure points through the Contextual
Interpretation step (Document 2A). Each pressure point identified at
Vertebra 2 maps to at least one front. The front's character is
determined by which type of pressure point generated it.

**FRONT GENERATION: FROM PRESSURE TO ACTION**

The Contextual Interpretation step (Document 2A, Step 5) maps each
pressure point to a front. The mapping follows the pressure point's
type:

**Maintenance Fronts --- from Outlier Scores**

An outlier score (above 80 or below 20) represents a load-bearing
conviction or an absent capacity. The entity is actively investing
energy in sustaining or suppressing the extreme value. That investment
IS a front --- the entity's ongoing effort to maintain the extreme.
Kyril's Constantine 95 generates the Institutional Maintenance front
because the duty IS the institution's operation. The front exists
because the outlier demands continuous energy.

Maintenance fronts are the background hum. They run constantly. They
rarely produce dramatic symptoms on their own. They become dramatic when
OTHER fronts divert the entity's attention and the maintenance front
begins to degrade. The first sign that something is wrong is usually the
maintenance front faltering --- the institution's routine slipping, the
commitment wavering, the suppressed dimension leaking through.

**Conflict Fronts --- from Asymmetric Splits**

An asymmetric split (paired gap exceeding 50 points) means the world
will inevitably present situations that require the suppressed aspect.
The entity either fails or breaks. The conflict front is the story of
that collision --- the entity's dominant aspect encountering a situation
that demands the aspect they cannot access.

Kyril's Veritas 90 / Gnosis 15 split generates the Vestus Containment
front because Vestus IS the Gnosis aspect made divine. The conflict is
structural --- Kyril cannot engage with decontextualized
knowledge-seeking without distortion, and the world keeps presenting him
with exactly that demand through Vestus's institutional probing.

Conflict fronts produce the most dramatic clock moments because they
force the entity into encounters they are structurally unable to handle
well. The intervention threshold on a conflict front's clock is the
point where the entity's coping mechanism fails and the suppressed
aspect erupts.

**Mystery Fronts --- from Pattern Breaks**

A pattern break (score contradicting expected profile) means the entity
is doing something their conscious self-model cannot explain. The
mystery front is the story of that hidden pressure surfacing --- the
entity's behavior generating observable anomalies that neither they nor
the observer can immediately account for.

Kyril's Veyra 55 break generates the double-blind dynamic with Vestus.
The mystery front is the richest adventure generator because it leads
toward revelations that reshape the campaign's understanding of the
setting. Mystery fronts are the fronts the party investigates --- the
symptoms are anomalous behavior that doesn't match the entity's known
profile, and the investigation leads toward the hidden truth.

**Operational Fronts --- from Positional Demands**

The positional reading (Document 2A, Step 4) produces external pressure
points --- demands the role generates regardless of who occupies it.
Each positional demand maps to an operational front. These are the
fronts that any entity in this position would run: institutional
obligations, structural dependencies, inter-entity diplomacy.

Operational fronts are not derived from the Schema. They are derived
from the position. Any headmaster of Mortis Ævum runs the Institutional
Maintenance front because the position demands it. Any senior Stormpeak
officer runs a personnel management front because the role requires it.
The personal Schema shapes HOW the entity handles the operational front,
not WHETHER the front exists.

**FRONT TYPES**

Four types of fronts, distinguished by what kind of entity drives them.
The template is identical. The content differs.

**Institutional Fronts**

Driven by colleges, guilds, governments, or other formal organizations.
The institution's charter defines the methodology. The headmaster or
leader's Schema shapes the expression. The representation chain is long
--- multiple layers of distortion between institutional intent and
ground-level execution.

The nine colleges are nine permanent institutional fronts in the
setting. Their methodologies are defined by their initiations (College
Initiations Canon). Their interactions drive the world's political,
social, and metaphysical dynamics. Every inter-college relationship is a
front connection with trust, debt, and tension properties.

**Individual Fronts**

Driven by a single NPC pursuing personal objectives. The representation
chain is short or absent --- the NPC acts directly. Individual fronts
are generated from the NPC's personal Schema pressure points
(maintenance, conflict, and mystery fronts). They are the most common
front type for adventure-scale play because the party encounters
individuals more frequently than institutions.

An individual front operating within an institutional context inherits
the institution's methodology but applies it through the NPC's personal
lens. A rogue Chronosynclastic analyst running a personal intelligence
operation is an individual front using institutional methodology without
institutional authorization. The gap between the personal and
institutional is itself a pressure point.

**Location Fronts (Project Slaughterhouse)**

Driven by a physical environment that behaves as a reactive organism.
The dungeon, the contested zone, the living ecosystem. The location has
assets (monsters, traps, environmental hazards), methodology (how it
responds to intrusion), and a representation chain (the location's
inhabitants at different depths represent different levels of the
environment's defensive response).

The Titanwood IS a location front. It has motivation (biological
expansion), methodology (Verdant Font isotope expression), assets
(fauna, flora, failed Wardens), and a failure trajectory (if
uncontained, the ecosystem consumes adjacent civilization). The Crash
Site beneath Mortis Ævum is a location front. The Pythia Gauntlet
between Stormpeak and the Chronosynclastic Institute is a location
front.

Location fronts respond to the party's actions as an organism responds
to disturbance. The goblins in room 4 hear the fight in room 3 and
react. The ecosystem shifts when resources are extracted. The
environment has its own logic that produces consequences independent of
any NPC's intentions. The location front's clocks track the
environment's state rather than an actor's progress.

**Territorial Fronts**

Driven by the pressures inherent to a territory's Zoetrope position,
isotope distribution, and institutional landscape. Territorial fronts
are not run by any single actor --- they are the emergent properties of
the territory's internal tensions. The transition pressure between
Zoetrope positions IS a territorial front. The resource competition for
scarce isotopes IS a territorial front.

Territorial fronts operate at the slowest timescale. Their clocks
measure decades, not sessions. They are tracked between campaign arcs
rather than between sessions. The accumulated weight of resolved
institutional and individual fronts within the territory IS the tick
trigger for territorial clocks. When enough fronts resolve in a
consistent direction, the territorial clock advances and the Zoetrope
position shifts.

**THE REPRESENTATION CHAIN**

Field 5 on the front template. The mechanism by which intent distorts
through human layers. The most important field for adventure generation
because the party never interacts with the top of the chain --- they
interact with the bottom, where the original intent has been filtered,
interpreted, and transformed by every intermediary.

**How Distortion Works**

At each link in the chain, three things happen:

**Information is added:** The intermediary adds their own understanding,
biases, and institutional vocabulary to the message. A headmaster's
strategic directive becomes a faculty member's operational order becomes
a regional representative's local interpretation.

**Information is lost:** Nuance, context, and conditional qualifiers are
dropped at each link. "Exercise caution in the eastern district, there
may be Verdant Font bleed activity" becomes "Watch the east side"
becomes "Stay out of the east."

**Information is distorted:** The intermediary's own Schema shapes how
they pass the message. A cautious intermediary amplifies warnings. An
ambitious intermediary softens restrictions. A burned-out intermediary
transmits the letter without the spirit.

The party encounters the bottom-of-chain version of the top-of-chain
intent. They see the local chirurgeon implementing a policy that makes
no sense at their level. Investigation leads upward through the chain.
At each link, the party discovers how the message changed and why. The
chain IS the adventure --- tracing distorted information backward to its
source.

  **Institutional    Long chain (3-5 links typical). Maximum distortion.
  fronts**           The party sees heavily filtered output and must

  fronts**           Surface encounters represent the location's outer

**THE DUAL-TRACK SYSTEM: GREAT AND LITTLE TRADITION**

Every front system operates on two parallel tracks. The gap between
tracks is automatically a seed at Vertebra 5 (Symptoms).

**The Great Tradition**

The institutional narrative. Official histories, college curricula,
sanctioned knowledge, formal doctrine. Information that flows through
the representation chain of authorized institutions. The Great Tradition
is curated, controlled, and deliberately shaped. It tells the story the
institutions want told.

In the Ætherium Codex, the Great Tradition is maintained primarily by
Praxis Collegium's foundational curriculum --- the shared conceptual
vocabulary that every Tier 1+ practitioner learns. The curriculum is
accurate but deliberately incomplete. The omissions ARE the Great Lie's
architecture. What the Great Tradition does not say is as significant as
what it says.

**The Little Tradition**

The folk narrative. Nursery rhymes, drinking songs, local stories,
tradesman's wisdom, old wives' tales. Information that exists outside
institutional control. The Little Tradition is not curated --- it
accumulates through oral transmission, practical experience, and
communal memory. It encodes information the institutions do not control
and sometimes do not know about.

In the Ætherium Codex, the Little Tradition includes songs that encode
planar hazard data ("don't sleep where the moss glows blue"), trade
secrets that describe isotope behaviors without the formal alchemical
vocabulary, and folk remedies that work for reasons the colleges have
not formalized. Tier 0 (Everyman) characters live entirely in the Little
Tradition. They may know things the colleges do not --- local knowledge
that never entered the institutional record.

**The Gap as Seed Source**

The discrepancy between the Great and Little Traditions IS a symptom at
Vertebra 5. When the institutional narrative says one thing and the folk
narrative says another, the gap is a seed the party can investigate. The
investigation leads to one of three outcomes:

**1.** The institution omitted information deliberately (the gap reveals
institutional control).

**2.** The folk tradition preserved information the institution lost
(the gap reveals institutional limitation).

**3.** Both traditions are wrong in different ways (the gap reveals that
the truth is more complex than either account).

Each outcome produces a different kind of adventure. The GM does not
need to decide which outcome is correct during prep. They prepare the
gap. The party's investigation determines which outcome emerges. This is
Schrödinger's Gun applied to institutional knowledge --- the truth is in
superposition until the party collapses it through observation.

**THE DEFAULT TIMELINE**

The sum of all active fronts' terminal states reached without player
intervention. Not a separate document. A derived forecast.

**Derivation Procedure**

List every active front. For each front, read the terminal state (Field
8 on the clock template, or the narrative description from the front's
clock potential if clocks are not yet parameterized). The terminal state
describes what happens when the front achieves its goal without
interference.

Now read all terminal states simultaneously. What does the world look
like when ALL of them resolve? Some terminal states are compatible ---
they can coexist. Some are contradictory --- one front's success
prevents another's. The contradictions between terminal states ARE the
setting's central tensions. The compatible resolutions are the
background changes. The contradictory resolutions are the dramatic
collisions.

The Default Timeline is the answer to Ten Questions Q6 applied to the
entire setting: "What happens if nobody intervenes?" The answer is the
gravitational trajectory that the party's actions bend. The further the
actual outcome diverges from the default, the more narrative energy was
required and the more dramatic the story becomes.

**Using the Default Timeline**

The GM does not read the Default Timeline to the party. The GM uses it
as the hidden benchmark against which the party's actions are measured.
When the party disrupts a front, the default shifts. When the party
ignores a front, the default advances toward its terminal state. The
party does not need to know the default --- they need to encounter
symptoms that hint at what will happen if they do nothing.

Between sessions, the GM advances all active fronts whose tick triggers
fired. The Default Timeline updates automatically. The world moves. The
party enters the next session into a world that is slightly different
from the one they left --- not because the GM authored a change, but
because the fronts advanced on schedule. The party's sense that the
world moves without them IS the Default Timeline operating.

**FRONT METHODOLOGY AND THE GAME SYSTEM**

The front's methodology (Field 3) is not narrative flavor. It is the
game system expressed at the institutional level.

**The Spell Sentence as Institutional Action**

When a front takes action, the action is describable as a Spell Sentence
operation. A Deepguard front expanding its ward network is performing
Protection and Nullify effects at institutional scale. A Wildpath front
reclaiming territory is performing Growth and Transform effects through
the Verdant Font's biological processes. The 19 canonical effects are
not just combat mechanics --- they are the vocabulary of institutional
action.

This connection means that the same grammar describes individual
spellcasting (Vertebra 6: Adventures) and institutional operations
(Vertebra 3: Fronts). A character casting Protection on themselves and a
college deploying a ward network across a territory are performing the
same effect at different scales. The rules do not change. The scale
changes.

**The Dual Periodic Table as Front Resources**

A front's assets (Field 4) include access to specific isotopes. The 104
isotopes across five planes determine what a front can build, deploy,
and sustain. Territorial control of isotope-rich zones IS the resource
game that fronts compete over.

A front operating in a Borderlands-dominant territory has access to
inorganic chemistry resources --- metals, minerals, structural
materials. A front in a Verdant Font territory has biological resources
--- living materials, mutation catalysts, ecological leverage. The
isotope distribution shapes what each front CAN do, which constrains how
its clocks advance and what symptoms its operations produce.

**THE SMOOTHING EFFECT AS FRONT RESISTANCE**

The Smoothing Effect (regulatory system) governs how much resistance
fronts encounter when they try to change the world. It is referenced
here because it most directly shapes front behavior.

Small front actions pass through unnoticed. A local Deepguard operative
installing a ward on one building does not trigger Smoothing resistance.
The change is small enough to be absorbed by the setting's natural
coherence.

Large front actions trigger dampening. A Deepguard initiative to
restructure an entire city's ward network encounters resistance ---
institutional inertia, political opposition, resource constraints,
unexpected complications. These are not authored obstacles. They are the
Smoothing Effect expressing through the setting's social physics. Large
changes require accumulated pressure over multiple clock cycles to
overcome the threshold.

This is why clocks have multiple segments rather than binary states. The
front does not achieve its goal in one action. It advances incrementally
through a resistant medium. Each segment represents one step of
accumulated pressure overcoming one layer of Smoothing resistance. The
front that fills its clock has pushed through enough resistance to
produce irreversible change. The Smoothing Effect ensures that only
sustained, committed action produces lasting results --- which ensures
that only fronts driven by genuine pressure (deep Schema tensions) have
the energy to complete their clocks.

**EXPRESSION GUIDANCE: INFORMATION SEQUENCING**

Vertebra 3's strongest expression bond is Information Sequencing --- the
order in which information reaches the audience. This bond is strong
because the representation chain IS an information sequencing structure.

**Dramatic Irony**

The audience (or some participants) know something the characters do
not. The representation chain creates dramatic irony naturally: the GM
knows the headmaster's intent at the top of the chain. The party knows
only the local operative's distorted version at the bottom. The gap
between the two IS dramatic irony. The tension sustains as long as the
party has not yet traced the chain to its source.

When one player character knows something the others do not (Kismet's
scouting report delivered only to the operator), intra-party dramatic
irony exists. The player who knows can choose when and how to share. The
delay IS the tension. The reveal IS the payoff.

**The Unreliable Chain**

Every link in the representation chain is an unreliable narrator. They
are telling the truth as they understand it, but their understanding was
shaped by their own Schema, filtered through their institutional
vocabulary, and constrained by what their link above them chose to
share. When the party receives contradictory information from two NPCs
who are both telling the truth, the contradiction is not a lie --- it is
the representation chain distorting the same source through different
filters.

Framing NPC contradictions as chain distortion rather than deception
teaches the table that perspective shapes reality. Both NPCs are right
from their position. The truth is at the top of the chain, visible only
through investigation. This is the novelistic technique of the
unreliable narrator applied structurally rather than stylistically.

**Sequencing Decisions**

The GM chooses which front's symptoms to present first. This is an
information sequencing decision. Presenting the Vestus containment
symptoms before the institutional maintenance symptoms creates a mystery
(why is the archive being locked down?). Presenting institutional
maintenance symptoms first creates a different mystery (why is the
institution faltering?). Same fronts, same clocks, different
presentation order, different audience experience.

The order is not arbitrary. The pacing tracker (Document 4C) guides the
selection based on the session's current beat pattern. But within the
pacing constraints, the GM's sequencing choice shapes what the party
investigates first, which shapes their understanding of the situation,
which shapes their response. The sequencing IS a creative act with
narrative consequences.

pressure points. Document 3B (Relationship Map System) defines how
fronts CONNECT to each other. Document 3C (Faction Turn Procedures)
defines how fronts ACT between sessions. Together, the three documents
constitute the complete Vertebra 3 architecture.*

**RELATIONSHIP MAP SYSTEM**

The Topology of Connection and Consequence

Document 3B --- Vertebra 3: Fronts (Companion)

*How fronts connect to each other and how consequences propagate through
the network*

**WHAT THE RELATIONSHIP MAP IS**

The relationship map is the wiring diagram for consequence propagation.
Every front exists in a network of connections to other fronts. When a
clock advances on one front, the advancement produces pressure that
travels along the connections to other fronts. Which fronts feel the
pressure, how much pressure arrives, and what form it takes are all
determined by the properties of the connections between them.

Without the map, fronts are isolated countdowns. Each clock ticks toward
its own terminal state in a vacuum. With the map, fronts are a system.
Each clock's advancement affects other clocks through the network of
connections. The emergent complexity of the setting comes from these
interactions --- outcomes that no single front would produce on its own
but that the network produces through propagation.

The map is simultaneously a prep tool (the GM draws it to understand the
setting's power structure), a play tool (the GM reads it to determine
how consequences cascade), and a diagnostic tool (the GM examines it to
find where the pressure is accumulating and which connections are
approaching state changes).

**NODES**

Every entity that can drive a front or be affected by one is a node on
the map. Nodes are not limited to NPCs. Any entity with a Schema or
Schema-equivalent (Document 1B) can be a node.

  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------
  **NPC Nodes**      Individual characters with personal Schemas. Each
                     NPC's Assessment Card (Document 2B) feeds the node's
                     data. The NPC's front potential section on the card
                     lists which fronts this node drives.

  **Institutional    Colleges, guilds, governments, military
  Nodes**            organizations. Each institution has an institutional
                     Schema (Document 1B) and drives institutional
                     fronts. Institutional nodes are larger than NPC
                     nodes --- they contain multiple NPCs within them,
                     each of whom may also be an individual node.

  **Location Nodes** Reactive environments that function as fronts
                     (Project Slaughterhouse). The Titanwood, the Crash
                     Site, the Pythia Gauntlet. Location nodes connect to
                     institutional and NPC nodes through operational
                     relationships.

  **Territorial      Regions defined by Zoetrope position and isotope
  Nodes**            profile. Territorial nodes contain institutional,
                     NPC, and location nodes within them. They operate at
                     the longest timescale and connect to other
                     territorial nodes through trade, diplomacy, and
                     conflict.

  **The Party**      The party is a node. It has connections to NPCs,
                     institutions, and locations. Its edges track the
                     same three properties (trust, debt, tension) as
                     every other connection. The party's position on the
                     map reveals which parts of the network they can
                     influence and which are beyond their current reach.
  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------

**Node Hierarchy**

Nodes nest. A territorial node contains institutional nodes. An
institutional node contains NPC nodes. The map can be drawn at different
zoom levels depending on what the GM needs to see:

**Adventure zoom:** Shows only the party, the NPCs they've met, and the
connections between them. The most common view during play. Fits on one
page.

**Campaign zoom:** Shows institutions, their inter-institutional
connections, and the key NPCs who drive each institution's fronts. Used
during prep to understand the political landscape.

**Setting zoom:** Shows territories, their inter-territorial
connections, and the institutional forces operating within each. Used
for long-arc planning and Zoetrope tracking.

The same map at three resolutions. The edges between nodes carry the
same three properties at every zoom level. Trust between two NPCs and
trust between two nations describe the same thing at different scales.

**EDGES**

Every connection between two nodes is an edge with three properties. The
properties are rated on a qualitative scale (Low / Medium / High) rather
than numerical values. The qualitative rating is sufficient for the GM
to make propagation decisions during play.

**Trust**

How freely information flows between the two nodes. Trust governs the
quality of communication --- not whether nodes communicate (most do),
but how honestly and completely.

  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------
  **High Trust**     Honest, complete communication. Each node shares
                     relevant information willingly. Intelligence flows
                     freely in both directions. Information passed along
                     this edge is reliable --- the receiving node can act
                     on it without verification.

  **Medium Trust**   Filtered communication. Each node shares some
                     information but withholds what they consider
                     sensitive. Intelligence is partial. Information
                     passed along this edge should be verified before
                     critical decisions.

  **Low Trust**      Guarded or deceptive communication. Each node
                     assumes the other is withholding or distorting.
                     Intelligence is unreliable. Information passed along
                     this edge may be deliberately misleading --- the
                     receiving node must treat it as unverified at best,
                     disinformation at worst.
  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------

Trust determines how QUICKLY and ACCURATELY consequences propagate. When
a clock advances on a front, the symptoms travel to connected fronts
along edges. High-trust edges transmit accurate symptoms rapidly --- the
connected front knows what happened and responds appropriately.
Low-trust edges transmit distorted symptoms slowly --- the connected
front learns something happened but misunderstands what, and responds
based on the misunderstanding.

Distorted propagation along low-trust edges IS a story generator. Front
A's clock advances. Front B learns about it through a low-trust edge and
receives a garbled version. Front B responds to the garbled version,
producing consequences that make no sense to Front A. The
miscommunication cascades. Neither front understands why the other is
behaving strangely. The party, encountering both fronts' symptoms, sees
two sets of anomalous behavior and must investigate to discover that the
source is a miscommunication, not a conspiracy.

**Debt**

Latent obligations between two nodes. Who owes whom, and what. Debt is
the stored energy in the relationship --- it creates leverage and shapes
behavior even when not actively invoked.

  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------
  **Owes**           This node owes the connected node. The connected
                     node has leverage --- the ability to call in the
                     debt at a moment of their choosing. The indebted
                     node's behavior is partially constrained by the
                     awareness that the debt could be called.

  **Owed**           The connected node owes this one. This node has
                     leverage. The debt is an asset that can be deployed
                     strategically --- called in during a crisis, traded
                     to a third party, or held as insurance.

  **Mutual**         Both nodes owe each other. Neither has clear
                     leverage. The debts may be of different kinds (one
                     owes money, the other owes loyalty) creating a
                     complex interdependency that neither can cleanly
                     resolve.

  **None**           No outstanding obligations. The cleanest possible
                     relationship state. Also the least interesting ---
                     no stored energy means no leverage and no
                     constraint.
  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------

Debt interacts with trust. A high-trust edge with debt is a genuine
obligation that both parties acknowledge --- the debt will be honored
because the relationship supports it. A low-trust edge with debt is a
weapon --- the debt will be called at the worst possible moment because
the debtor does not trust the creditor to be reasonable. Debt on a
low-trust edge is a bomb waiting for a trigger.

Unresolved debt accumulates tension (see below). Every session that
passes with an outstanding debt increases the tension on the edge by one
step. The longer a debt goes uncalled, the more weight it carries when
it finally arrives. This is Chekhov's Gun applied to relationships ---
every placed debt must eventually resolve, and the delay between
placement and resolution increases the dramatic impact.

**Tension**

Current stress on the connection. The pressure that accumulates from
unresolved conflicts, competing interests, and outstanding debts.
Tension is the measure of how close the relationship is to a state
change.

  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------
  **Low Tension**    The relationship is stable. No active conflicts. No
                     pressing unresolved issues. The edge is quiet.
                     Consequences propagate along it without producing
                     secondary effects.

  **Medium Tension** Active stress. Competing interests, unresolved
                     disagreements, or accumulated small frictions. The
                     edge is loaded. Consequences propagating along it
                     may amplify --- a small clock advancement on one
                     front could trigger a disproportionate response from
                     the connected front because the tension converts
                     neutral information into threatening information.

  **High Tension**   The relationship is approaching a state change. One
                     more significant event will push it past the
                     threshold. The edge is a fault line. Any consequence
                     propagating along it will be amplified dramatically
                     --- even minor events become crises because the
                     relationship has no remaining capacity to absorb
                     stress.
  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------

**Threshold Crossings**

When tension on an edge reaches High and a triggering event occurs, the
state changes one step in the direction of the tension's source:

**Negative tension:** Tension from competing interests or unresolved
conflict pushes the state toward hostility: Ally → Neutral → Rival →
Enemy.

**Positive tension:** Tension from an act of sacrifice, trust-building,
or debt resolution pushes the state toward cooperation: Enemy → Rival →
Neutral → Ally.

After a state change, tension resets to Low. The relationship has
reorganized into its new state. Tension begins accumulating again from
the new baseline.

State changes are dramatic events. They should emerge from play, not be
imposed arbitrarily. The GM tracks tension qualitatively through
accumulated events. When a specific in-game event pushes the
relationship past the threshold, the state change happens in response to
that event, producing a visible moment the party (and the affected NPCs)
can react to.

**State Changes as Bangs**

Every state change is a bang at Vertebra 4 (Clocks) --- a situation that
demands an immediate response with no predetermined right answer. When
two allied institutions become rivals, every NPC connected to both must
choose a side, renegotiate their position, or find a way to maintain
both relationships. The state change produces forced-choice situations
that cascade through the network. The more edges connected to the
changed relationship, the more NPCs and fronts are affected, and the
more bangs are generated.

**CONSEQUENCE PROPAGATION**

When a clock advances on a front, the consequences travel along the
edges to connected fronts. The propagation follows four rules.

**Rule 1: Consequences Follow Edges**

Consequences do not jump between unconnected nodes. If Front A's clock
advances and Front A has no edge to Front C, then Front C does not feel
the consequences directly. Front C may feel them indirectly if Front B
is connected to both A and C --- the consequences propagate from A to B,
then from B to C. Each intermediary edge filters and potentially
distorts the consequence.

**Rule 2: Trust Governs Accuracy**

A consequence propagating along a high-trust edge arrives accurately.
The receiving front knows what happened and can respond appropriately. A
consequence propagating along a low-trust edge arrives distorted. The
receiving front may misunderstand the cause, the severity, or the intent
behind the event. The distortion IS a story seed --- the
misunderstanding produces responses that create new complications.

**Rule 3: Tension Governs Amplification**

A consequence propagating along a low-tension edge arrives at its actual
magnitude. A consequence propagating along a high-tension edge is
AMPLIFIED. The receiving front interprets the event as more threatening,
more significant, or more urgent than it actually is because the loaded
relationship converts neutral information into alarming information. A
routine clock advancement on one front, propagating through a
high-tension edge, can trigger a crisis-level response on the connected
front --- not because the event warranted it but because the
relationship's stored stress amplified it.

**Rule 4: State Governs Direction**

The edge's state determines WHETHER the consequence helps or hinders the
receiving front. An ally edge converts consequences into support. An
enemy edge converts consequences into threat. A neutral edge converts
consequences into data. A rival edge converts consequences into
competitive pressure. The same clock advancement produces four different
effects depending on which state the connecting edge occupies.

These four rules combined produce emergent complexity. A single clock
advancement propagates through multiple edges, each with different
trust, tension, and state values, producing different effects at each
receiving front. The network of edges transforms one event into multiple
consequences, each filtered and shaped by the connection it travels
through. The GM does not need to author each consequence individually.
The map's properties determine them.

**BUILDING AND MAINTAINING THE MAP**

**Initial Construction**

Draw the map during prep, after the fronts are defined (Document 3A).
Place every active front as a node. Draw an edge between every pair of
fronts that have a relationship --- institutional, personal, geographic,
or economic. Assign the three properties (trust, debt, tension) and the
state (ally/neutral/rival/enemy) to each edge based on the setting's
established situation.

Not every pair of fronts needs an edge. Only fronts that interact,
compete, cooperate, or share resources need connections. A Stormpeak
front and a Sovereign Exchange front operating in the same territory are
connected. A Stormpeak front in the far north and a Sovereign Exchange
front on the southern coast probably are not, unless a specific
relationship links them.

**Adding Nodes During Play**

When a new NPC becomes notable (triggers an Assessment Card) or a new
front emerges from play, add them to the map. Draw edges to existing
nodes based on the relationships that have been established. New nodes
start with few edges. Over the campaign, they accumulate connections as
relationships form.

The party is a node. As they form relationships with NPCs, make
agreements with institutions, and establish presence in territories,
their edges accumulate. The party's map of connections IS their
political influence. A party with many ally-state edges to powerful
nodes has significant structural leverage. A party with few edges is
isolated and vulnerable to being acted upon without allies.

**Updating Properties**

After each session, review the map for changes:

**Trust changes:** Did any event increase or decrease trust between two
nodes? Successful cooperation increases trust. Betrayal or deception
decreases it. Adjust accordingly.

**Debt changes:** Were any debts created, called in, or traded? New
favors, unmet obligations, or transfers of leverage change the debt
landscape.

**Tension changes:** Did any event increase tension between nodes?
Competing over the same resource, conflicting clock objectives, or
propagated consequences from other edges all increase tension.
Resolution of conflicts or acts of reconciliation decrease tension.

**State changes:** Did any edge's tension cross the threshold? If yes,
process the state change and reset tension to Low.

**Physical Format**

The map is whatever the GM can maintain. Practical options:

  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------
  **Index cards on a One card per node (doubling as the Assessment Card).
  wall**             String or drawn lines between cards for edges.
                     Properties written on the strings or noted on the
                     cards. Maximum visibility. Requires wall space.

  **Digital graph    Any diagramming tool that supports labeled edges.
  tool**             Scalable, searchable, shareable. Requires screen and
                     software comfort.

  **Notebook         A hand-drawn diagram in the campaign notebook.
  sketch**           Compact and portable. Harder to update cleanly. Best
                     for small networks (under 15 nodes).

  **Spreadsheet      Nodes on rows and columns. Each cell contains the
  matrix**           edge properties for that pair. Compact, easy to
                     update, but loses the spatial intuition of a visual
                     map.
  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------

The format does not matter. What matters is that the GM can look at the
map and immediately answer: "When Front A's clock advances, which other
fronts feel it, through what quality of connection, and how will they
respond?" If the format answers that question at a glance, it is
sufficient.

**THE MAP AND THE PARTY**

The party sees the NPC Assessment Cards. They do not see the map. The
map is a GM tool. But the party BUILDS the map through play without
knowing it.

**Player-Visible Outputs**

When the party encounters two NPCs who react to each other, they are
seeing an edge. When an NPC's behavior changes because of something that
happened to a different NPC three sessions ago, the party is seeing
consequence propagation along an edge they may not have mapped. When an
NPC who was helpful becomes hostile after the party allied with their
rival, the party is seeing a state change triggered by their own actions
modifying the network.

The party's investigative instinct --- "why did the merchant suddenly
become hostile?" --- IS the party detecting a state change and tracing
the edge that caused it. The investigation leads to the rival
relationship they disrupted. The map becomes visible to the party
through its EFFECTS, not through its structure.

**The Party as Network Actor**

Every party action that affects an NPC's relationship modifies an edge.
Helping an NPC increases trust and potentially shifts the state toward
ally. Betraying an NPC increases tension and shifts toward rival or
enemy. Making a promise creates debt. Fulfilling a promise resolves
debt. The party is not outside the network --- they are a node within
it, subject to the same propagation rules as every other node.

This means the party's actions have NETWORK consequences, not just
individual ones. Allying with the Wardens is not just a relationship
with one institution --- it modifies the Wardens' allies' trust in the
party (positive propagation), and the Wardens' rivals' tension with the
party (negative propagation). The party's alliance choice cascades
through the network the same way any front's clock advancement does.

Fulcrum Point spends at Distance 3 (reaching into Fronts from
Adventures) are the party directly manipulating an edge. Spending FP to
alter a relationship's trust, debt, or tension is the player reaching
into the map and changing its wiring. The deferred consequences of that
spend (spine self-correction through skipped vertebrae) are the network
rebalancing around the altered edge.

**MAP PATTERNS**

Certain configurations of nodes and edges produce recognizable story
structures. The GM can read the map for these patterns to identify where
the most dramatic potential lives.

  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------
  **The Triangle**   Three nodes where A is allied with both B and C, but
                     B and C are rivals. A is forced to choose, mediate,
                     or manage competing loyalties. The party in a
                     triangle is under maximum social pressure. Classic
                     dramatic geometry.

  **The Bridge**     Two clusters of nodes connected by a single edge.
                     The bridge node or edge is a chokepoint --- if it
                     breaks, the two clusters are isolated from each
                     other. The party occupying the bridge position has
                     enormous structural leverage but is also the most
                     vulnerable to being cut.

  **The Web**        One central node connected to many peripheral nodes.
                     The central node is a hub --- consequences propagate
                     through it to the entire network. If the hub is
                     disrupted, all connected nodes lose their primary
                     communication channel. Headmasters and institutional
                     leaders are often hubs.

  **The Chain**      Nodes connected in a line: A→B→C→D. Consequences
                     propagate sequentially with distortion at each link.
                     By the time an event travels from A to D, it may be
                     unrecognizable. This is the representation chain
                     expressed as network topology.

  **The Island**     A node with no edges or only one edge. Isolated from
                     the network. Cannot receive propagated consequences
                     and cannot influence the network. An island NPC is
                     narratively inert unless the party creates
                     connections to them. An island NPC who SHOULD be
                     connected but isn't is a red flag --- someone
                     severed the edges deliberately.

  **The Pressure     Multiple nodes all connected to each other with high
  Cooker**           tension on every edge. Any event triggers cascading
                     state changes across the entire cluster. Maximum
                     dramatic potential. The GM should place the party's
                     most important adventure inside or adjacent to a
                     pressure cooker.
  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------

**FACTION TURN PROCEDURES**

How the World Moves Between Sessions

Document 3C --- Vertebra 3: Fronts (Companion)

*The movement mechanism that makes the setting a living system*

**WHAT A FACTION TURN IS**

Between sessions, the world moves. Fronts pursue their goals. Clocks
advance. Relationships shift. The party returns to a setting that is
different from the one they left --- not because the GM authored a
change, but because the factions took their turns.

A faction turn is one action taken by one front between sessions. The
action advances or retards a clock, creates or modifies an edge on the
relationship map, produces observable symptoms the party will encounter,
and potentially triggers consequence propagation to connected fronts.
The faction turn is the engine that makes the Default Timeline advance
toward its terminal states when the party is not intervening.

The purpose is not to simulate a political strategy game between
sessions. The purpose is to produce a world that feels alive --- one
where the party's sense that things happened while they were away is
grounded in actual events that the GM can trace, explain, and connect to
the network of fronts and relationships. The world is not static between
sessions. The faction turn is the minimum mechanism that prevents
stasis.

**THE FOUR ACTION TYPES**

Every faction turn is one of four action types. The type determines what
kind of clock the action advances, what kind of symptom it produces, and
how the consequence propagates through the relationship map.

**Expand**

The front extends its reach. It acquires new territory, recruits new
members, establishes new operations, or increases its resource base.
Expansion is the offensive action that grows the front's footprint in
the setting.

**Clock effect:** Advances clocks tracking the front's geographic or
institutional growth. Symptoms: new personnel appearing in areas the
front didn't previously operate, new infrastructure under construction,
recruitment efforts visible to the public.

**Map effect:** Increases tension on edges with rival and enemy fronts
whose territory or resources are being encroached upon. May increase
trust on edges with ally fronts who benefit from the expansion. May
create new edges where the expanding front enters a new node's sphere of
influence.

**Defend**

The front consolidates its position. It fortifies existing operations,
secures resources, hardens against anticipated threats, or conducts
internal maintenance. Defense is the reactive action that protects what
the front already has.

**Clock effect:** Retards clocks tracking threats to the front. Advances
clocks tracking the front's internal stability. Symptoms: increased
security presence, access restrictions, fortification activity, internal
audits.

**Map effect:** Decreases tension on edges with the front's allies
(reassurance). May increase tension with rivals who interpret defensive
posture as preparation for aggression. No new edges created.

**Attack**

The front actively undermines, disrupts, or destroys a specific target.
Attack is the aggressive action that directly opposes another front's
interests. Not limited to military action --- economic sabotage,
political maneuvering, social manipulation, and intelligence operations
are all attack actions.

**Clock effect:** Advances clocks tracking the target front's
degradation or threat level. May retard the target's operational clocks.
Symptoms: disruptions to the target's operations, damage to their
assets, interference with their personnel, visible conflict between the
two fronts.

**Map effect:** Dramatically increases tension on the edge with the
target. May trigger a state change (Neutral → Rival, Rival → Enemy).
Increases tension on edges with the target's allies. May decrease
tension on edges with the target's enemies (the enemy of my enemy).

**Influence**

The front shapes perception, builds alliances, or maneuvers politically
without direct confrontation. Influence is the indirect action that
modifies relationships rather than resources. The most subtle action
type and the one whose consequences are hardest for the party to trace.

**Clock effect:** Advances clocks tracking social or political shifts.
May advance clocks on other fronts whose behavior is being shaped.
Symptoms: shifts in public opinion, changed NPC attitudes, new alliances
or partnerships, subtle behavioral changes in connected NPCs.

**Map effect:** Directly modifies edge properties. May increase trust
between the front and its targets. May increase or decrease tension on
edges the front is trying to manipulate. May create new debt
relationships. The influence action is the primary mechanism for
modifying the map's wiring.

**THE RESOLUTION PROCEDURE**

How the GM determines whether a faction turn action succeeds, partially
succeeds, or fails.

**The Three Factors**

Every faction turn resolution considers three factors:

**1. Asset Match:** The front's available resources for this action
type. A Stormpeak front with depleted military assets attempting an
Expand action has fewer resources than one at full strength. The front's
Asset field (Document 3A, Field 4) determines what the front can deploy.
Depleted assets reduce the action's effectiveness.

**2. Opposition:** Active opposition from connected fronts. If the
target of an Attack action has taken a Defend action this turn, the
defense opposes the attack. If a front is Expanding into territory a
rival front is also Expanding into, the two actions oppose each other.
Unopposed actions succeed more completely than opposed ones.

**3. Smoothing Resistance:** The Smoothing Effect's resistance to
change. Small actions in stable territories encounter minimal
resistance. Large actions in stable territories or any action in
unstable territories encounter more resistance. The territory's Zoetrope
position stability and the action's magnitude determine how much
Smoothing dampening applies.

**Resolution Method**

The GM has two options for resolution:

**Option A: Adjudicated Resolution**

The GM considers the three factors and determines the outcome through
judgment. This is the default for most campaigns. The GM asks: does the
front have the resources, is anyone opposing them, and how much does the
setting resist this change? The answer produces one of three outcomes:

  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------
  **Full Success**   The action achieves its intended effect. The
                     relevant clock advances one segment. Observable
                     symptoms are produced. Map effects apply at full
                     strength.

  **Partial          The action achieves part of its intended effect, or
  Success**          achieves the full effect with an unintended side
                     effect. The clock advances half a segment (rounded
                     down) or advances fully but produces a complication
                     on a connected edge. The most common outcome --- the
                     world resists clean resolution.

  **Failure**        The action does not achieve its intended effect. The
                     clock does not advance. The attempt itself may
                     produce symptoms (failed operations are visible) and
                     may increase tension on edges with fronts who
                     detected the attempt.
  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------

**Option B: Mechanical Resolution**

For GMs who prefer dice-driven outcomes, a simple mechanical procedure:

The front rolls against a Target Number (TN) set by the GM based on the
three factors. Strong assets and weak opposition produce a low TN
(easy). Depleted assets, strong opposition, and high Smoothing
resistance produce a high TN (difficult).

The roll uses the front's driving NPC's relevant Schema dimension die
(from the 10-band overlay, Document 1A). An Expand action uses the
dimension most relevant to the expansion methodology. A Stormpeak
military expansion rolls the Kaelen die. A Unity social expansion rolls
the Agora Architect die. A Chronosynclastic intelligence expansion rolls
the Vestus Gnosis die.

Meet or exceed the TN: full success. Miss by 1-3: partial success. Miss
by 4+: failure. The Schema's die type IS the front's competence level
for this type of action. A front driven by an NPC with a d12 in the
relevant dimension is significantly more effective than one driven by an
NPC with a d6.

**SCALING THE FACTION TURN**

Not every front needs to take an action every between-session turn. The
GM manages the number of active turns based on campaign complexity and
available prep time.

  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------
  **Early Campaign** 1-3 active fronts taking turns. The party has
                     limited awareness of the setting's power structure.
                     Only the fronts directly relevant to their current
                     adventure are active. The world moves subtly.

  **Mid Campaign**   3-6 active fronts. The party has established
                     relationships with multiple NPCs and institutions.
                     More fronts are visible. The world moves noticeably
                     --- the party returns to find things changed.

  **Late Campaign**  6-10 active fronts. The party is embedded in the
                     network. Multiple fronts interact, producing
                     cascading consequences. The world moves dramatically
                     --- major shifts happen between sessions.

  **Climax**         All active fronts converge. The faction turn
                     procedure runs at maximum density. Multiple opposed
                     actions resolve simultaneously. The setting's
                     pressure cooker regions (Document 3B) erupt. This is
                     the phase where the Default Timeline and the party's
                     actual timeline diverge most dramatically.
  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------

The GM should never run more faction turns than they can resolve
meaningfully in available prep time. A rushed faction turn produces
incoherent consequences. A thoughtful faction turn for three fronts
produces more useful play material than a hasty faction turn for ten.
Quality over quantity. If the GM has twenty minutes, run three fronts
thoroughly. If they have an hour, run six.

**THE PLAYER TRIGGER**

Faction turns do not only happen between sessions. Player actions during
a session can trigger immediate faction responses.

When the party takes an action that directly affects a front ---
disrupting an operation, killing a key NPC, exposing a secret, making an
alliance --- the affected front may take an immediate response action.
This is not a full faction turn. It is a reactive action triggered by
the party's intervention.

  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------
  **Disrupted        The front Defends: secures remaining assets, locks
  Operation**        down compromised operations, assesses damage.
                     Symptoms appear within the same session or the next.

  **Key NPC          The front either Defends (consolidates around the
  Removed**          next-in-command) or Attacks (retaliates against
                     whoever removed the NPC). The response depends on
                     the institutional culture. Stormpeak retaliates.
                     Deepguard consolidates. Unity manages the narrative.

  **Secret Exposed** The front Influences: damage control,
                     counter-narrative deployment, witness management.
                     The Chiaroscuro methodology is particularly active
                     here. Unity's methodology overlaps.

  **Alliance         Connected rival fronts may Attack (preemptive
  Formed**           disruption of the new alliance) or Influence

  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------

Player-triggered reactions happen within the session's normal flow. The
GM processes them in real time using the same resolution procedure
(three factors, adjudicated outcome). The triggered reactions produce
immediate symptoms the party encounters during the same session. This
creates the feeling that the setting is responsive --- the party's
actions produce visible reactions from the power structure.

**THE ZOETROPE COUPLING**

Accumulated faction turn resolutions within a territory push the
Zoetrope position toward transition. This is the mechanism by which
individual and institutional actions aggregate into civilizational
change.

**How Accumulation Works**

Every resolved faction turn action within a territory adds weight to the
Zoetrope's directional pressure. The weight is determined by the college
methodology behind the action. When enough actions from the same
methodological family accumulate, the Zoetrope position begins to slide
toward the position associated with that methodology.

A territory where Deepguard fronts dominate the faction turn landscape
accumulates abjuration-methodology weight. If sustained, the territory
drifts toward Zoetrope positions associated with institutional
governance, legal infrastructure, and engineered stability. A territory
where Wildpath fronts dominate accumulates transmutation-methodology
weight, drifting toward positions associated with ecological integration
and biological governance.

**The Threshold**

Zoetrope positions do not change from individual faction turns. They
change when the accumulated weight of faction turn resolutions overcomes
the Smoothing Effect's resistance at the civilizational scale. This
requires sustained, consistent pressure over multiple campaign arcs ---
not sessions, but arcs. The Zoetrope operates at the longest timescale
in the system.

The GM tracks Zoetrope pressure qualitatively: no pressure, building
pressure, critical pressure. When critical pressure is reached, the next
arc's events will include the Zoetrope transition as a background event.
The transition itself is the source of new territorial fronts --- the
old power structure giving way to the new, producing new institutional
conflicts, new resource competitions, and new adventure material.

**The Party's Role**

The party does not control Zoetrope transitions. But their actions
influence which faction turns succeed and which fail. A party that
consistently supports Stormpeak operations and disrupts Wildpath
operations is, over time, adding military-methodology weight to their
territory's Zoetrope pressure. They may not realize they are shaping
civilizational drift. They are making choices about individual fronts.
The aggregate consequence emerges from the pattern of choices read
backward --- the Selûnae principle applied at the setting scale.

**FACTION TURNS AND FULCRUM POINTS**

Institutional FP (Document 1B) fuel faction turn actions. The
institutional FP pool generated by the institution's Schema tensions
represents the narrative energy available for faction-level operations.

**NPC FP Spends:** The driving NPC may spend personal FP during faction
turn resolution to improve outcomes. A Distance 0 spend adds to the
resolution roll or tips an adjudicated judgment. A Distance 1-2 spend
shapes the consequence propagation --- directing which edges carry the
effect, or damping an unintended side effect. The NPC's Mastery
determines how far they can reach.

**Institutional FP Spends:** The institution's FP pool funds ambitious,
large-scale, or surprising faction turn actions. A front taking an
action that exceeds its normal operational scope (a Deepguard front
attempting a social influence operation rather than a legal one)
requires institutional FP to cover the methodology stretch. Operating
outside your college's native methodology is possible but costs
narrative energy.

**Deferred Consequences:** Each FP spent during a faction turn produces
deferred consequences through the normal spine propagation. The skipped
vertebrae process the discontinuity. The GM notes which vertebrae were
skipped and generates side effects between sessions. This means
ambitious faction turn actions --- the ones that produce the most
dramatic outcomes --- also produce the most unintended consequences. The
system self-balances.

**Step 1: List Active Fronts**

**Step 3: Check Opposition**

**Steps 4-5: Resolve and Advance**

Front A vs. B: Kyril's Defend is well-resourced (archive security is

information, but they identified a vulnerability in the archive's outer

clock does not advance (defense held). But Kyril's attention was
diverted --- note for Step 9.

Front C: Unopposed Expand with strong assets (the Exchange has money).

**WHAT FULCRUM POINTS ARE**

Normal play operates through the spine's natural propagation sequence.
Actions at Vertebra 6 (Adventures) produce consequences that propagate

shift Pressure Points (Vertebra 2), reshape Fronts (Vertebra 3), advance

sequence.

Fulcrum Points let the participant skip the natural sequence. A Fulcrum
Point spend reaches directly into a vertebra other than the one the
participant is currently operating at. The cost scales with distance ---
how many vertebrae are skipped between the participant's position and
the vertebra being manipulated. The further the reach, the greater the
cost and the larger the unintended side effects.

splits) + (count of pattern breaks).

This is the same pressure point count produced by the extraction
procedure at Vertebra 2 (Document 2A). The extraction procedure and the

generate the most interesting story are the same pressure points that

**THE BIDIRECTIONAL RESPONSE**

No explicit GM pool. The spine's natural consequence propagation handles
the response.

**How It Works**

When a participant spends FP and skips vertebrae, they create a
discontinuity. The vertebrae that were skipped still need to process the
change. They do not disappear --- they are deferred. The spine catches
up. The deferred processing produces consequences the participant did
not plan for because the natural sequence was bypassed.

A clock retarded by a Distance 2 spend: the front driving that clock
does not know its clock was manipulated. The front continues taking
faction turn actions as if the clock is where it expects. The mismatch
between expectation and reality produces a discontinuity that the spine
processes as an unintended side effect.

**Side Effect Scale**

  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------
  **Distance 0**     Minimal side effects. The spend operated within the
                     current vertebra. No vertebrae were skipped. The
                     resolution shifted slightly but the spine's sequence
                     was not disrupted.

  **Distance 1**     Minor side effects. One vertebra was skipped. The
                     change happened without normal symptom presentation
                     or without normal consequence propagation. The gap
                     is small but detectable.

  **Distance 2**     Moderate side effects. Two vertebrae skipped. The
                     change bypassed normal clock advancement or pressure
                     extraction. The affected front may act on outdated
                     information, producing inappropriate responses.

  **Distance 3**     Significant side effects. Three vertebrae skipped. A
                     relationship was altered or a Schema element
                     revealed without the intermediate steps. The network
                     around the altered edge rebalances, producing
                     cascading adjustments the participant did not
                     anticipate.

  **Distance ∞**     Maximum side effects. The regulatory system was
                     overridden. The Smoothing Effect was momentarily
                     punctured. Aíon's maintenance architecture noticed
                     the discontinuity and responded. The deferred
                     consequence is cosmological in character --- reality
                     seam disturbance, planar bleed amplification, or
                     Smoothing overcorrection.
  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------

**GM Processing**

When a participant spends FP, the GM notes which vertebrae were skipped.
Between sessions (or at the next dramatically appropriate moment), the
GM asks: "What would the skipped vertebrae produce if they processed
this change through normal sequence?" The answer is the side effect. The
oracle tables (Document 5B) can assist --- roll the deferred consequence
as an oracle prompt to generate the side effect when the GM needs
inspiration.

**SEED MANAGEMENT**

Generating, Placing, and Resolving Observable Effects

Document 5A --- Vertebra 5: Symptoms

**WHAT A SEED IS**

A seed is an observable detail, event, anomaly, or NPC behavior that the
participants can detect. Seeds are the party's window into the spine's
activity. The party never sees clocks directly. They never see fronts'
internal mechanics. They never see the relationship map's edge
properties. They see symptoms --- the effects that clocks, fronts, and
edges produce in the observable world.

Every seed has a source (which clock or front produced it), a form (what
it looks like in the world), and an obligation (it must eventually
resolve). The source is the GM's information. The form is the party's
experience. The obligation is the engine's quality constraint --- seeds
that never pay off are worse than seeds never placed.

Seed management is the discipline of generating seeds from clock
advancement, placing them where participants can encounter them, and
tracking them to ensure resolution. Five tools govern this discipline.
Together they form a complete apparatus: quantity constraint, placement
method, quality constraint, prep format, and distribution topology.

**THE FIVE TOOLS**

**1. The Three Clue Rule --- Quantity Constraint**

For any conclusion the participants need to reach, at least three
independent expressions of the seed must exist. Participants will miss
at least two. If you place one expression, the plot stalls when they
miss it. If you place two, there is a coin flip. Three guarantees they
find at least one and feel clever for connecting it.

The three expressions must be independently discoverable. Three clues in
the same room are one expression encountered three times. Three clues in
three different locations, discoverable through three different methods
(investigation, social interaction, scouting), are three independent
expressions. Each expression should be accessible through a different
party member's competency so that different character builds contribute
to the discovery.

This is a MINIMUM. Critical seeds --- the ones that determine whether
the party engages with a major clock's threshold --- should have four or
five expressions. The more important the seed, the more redundancy it
needs. The Three Clue Rule is the floor, not the ceiling.

**2. Schrödinger's Gun --- Placement Method**

The expressions are not pre-placed. They exist in superposition until
the participants look somewhere, at which point the expression was
always there. The GM writes the seed. The GM does not assign it to a
specific location, NPC, or object. When the party searches a room, the
GM checks the seed list and places the most appropriate expression in
what the party is searching.

This is not cheating. This is the Palimpsest operating through the GM.
The world exists in multiple potential states until observation
collapses it. The seed was always in the desk drawer or always in the
merchant's ledger or always in the guard captain's report --- whichever
one the party investigates first. The other two locations still contain
their expressions for future discovery.

Schrödinger's Gun IS the superposition principle given practical
expression at the table. The GM is performing the same function as the
Palimpsest --- holding multiple states simultaneously and letting
observation determine which one becomes real. The setting's cosmology
and the GM's methodology are the same process at different scales.

**3. Chekhov's Gun --- Quality Constraint**

Every placed seed must eventually resolve. If the merchant's nervous
collar-touching was introduced in session 3, it must pay off. The payoff
may be immediate (the merchant was hiding contraband), delayed (the
collar-touching becomes relevant when the party discovers the merchant's
connection to the Redbrands in session 7), or inverted (the
collar-touching was a meaningless habit that the party over-interpreted,
revealing their own paranoia rather than the merchant's guilt). Each
outcome is a valid resolution. No resolution is not valid.

**Cross-reference to Feedback Loop:** Chekhov's Gun creates a temporal
obligation ACROSS spine cycles. The seed placed at Vertebra 5 in cycle 3
pays off at Vertebra 7 (Feedback Loop) in cycle 7. The Feedback Loop is
where outstanding Chekhov obligations are tracked --- the ledger of
introduced elements that have not yet resolved. See Document 7A.

The practical implication: do not introduce details casually. Every
detail the participants notice becomes a Chekhov obligation. If the GM
mentions that the inn has a cellar, the cellar must matter --- or the GM
must accept that the party will investigate the cellar expecting it to
matter. Seed placement is a commitment, not a decoration.

**4. Lazy DM Secrets --- Prep Format**

Before the session, write a list of truths. Not locations. Not NPCs. Not
objects. TRUTHS --- things that are true about the current state of the
world. The truths are seeds in their pre-placed state. They exist. They
do not have locations yet.

Write five to ten truths per session. Each truth is one sentence
describing a fact about the current situation that the party does not
yet know. Examples:

  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------
  **Truth**          "The mine has been sealed by the Redbrands."

  **Truth**          "The blacksmith's brother was taken alive, not
                     killed."

  **Truth**          "The merchant is paying protection money to two
                     different groups simultaneously."

  **Truth**          "The ward on the eastern gate has been failing for
                     three days and nobody has reported it."

  **Truth**          "A Chronosynclastic operative has been in town for a
                     week, posing as a traveling scholar."
  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------

Each truth becomes a seed when the party encounters it. The GM does not
decide WHERE the party learns each truth. The GM decides THAT the truth
exists. Schrödinger's Gun handles the placement. The Three Clue Rule
ensures each truth has multiple discoverable expressions. Chekhov's Gun
ensures each truth eventually resolves.

This is the prep format that eliminates railroading. The GM prepares
density (many truths available) rather than direction (a predetermined
sequence of discoveries). The party's path through the truths is their
own. Every path produces a coherent story because the truths are
internally consistent --- they all describe the same situation from
different angles.

**5. Node-Based Design --- Distribution Topology**

Seeds are distributed across an interconnected network of locations.
Each location contains at least one seed expression. Each seed
expression connects to at least two other locations. There are no dead
ends.

A dead-end location (one that contains no seed expressions connecting to
other locations) kills momentum. The party investigates, finds nothing,
and stalls. Node-based design prevents this by ensuring every location
the party visits contains something that points somewhere else. The
network is navigable from any entry point.

The topology is not a map of physical locations. It is a map of
information connections. Location A contains a clue pointing to Location
B and Location C. Location B contains a clue pointing to A and D. The
lines between locations are the clue connections, not the roads. The
party may physically travel from A to D directly, but the information
path goes A → B → D. The investigation follows the information topology,
not the geography.

The Palimpsest IS the mega-dungeon expression of this principle. Every
extradimensional fold is a node. Every portal is a connection. The
Palimpsest's node network is infinite in principle. The GM instantiates
the portion of the network the party can access based on their current
narrative reach.

**SEED DESIGN: THE "THIS COULD BE YOU" PRINCIPLE**

Seeds must have personal relevance hooks --- the participant must be
able to see themselves in the situation. This is the oral tradition's
parable function applied to seed design.

A seed that is too alien to identify with does not trigger engagement
regardless of structural quality. "The dimensional rift is expanding at
0.3 meters per hour" is a fact. "The baker's children are afraid to
sleep because their bedroom wall has started humming" is a seed. Both
describe the same event (a rift advancing toward inhabited structures).
The second one triggers "this could be you" because the participant can
project themselves into the situation. The children's fear is legible.
The dimensional mathematics are not.

The design principle: every seed should be expressible in terms of
human-scale experience. Institutional operations become visible through
their effects on individual lives. Clock advancements become tangible
through the specific people they affect. The party encounters the
baker's fear, not the front's operational metrics. The investigation
works backward from human-scale experience to institutional-scale cause.

At Vertebra 6 (Adventures), "this could be you" is the FELT EXPERIENCE
--- the moment the participant's transportation into the fictional space
activates and they engage with the seed as though it were real. At
Vertebra 5, it is a DESIGN PRINCIPLE --- the seeds must be constructed
with hooks that activate that transportation. The design produces the
experience. The experience produces the engagement.

**SEEDS AND THE GAME SYSTEM**

The Spell Sentence's 19 canonical effects provide the vocabulary for
what seeds LOOK LIKE in the world. The isotope map provides the
specificity for HOW they look.

**The Spell Sentence as Symptom Vocabulary**

When a clock advances, the symptom is describable as one or more Spell
Sentence effects manifesting at observable scale. A Wildpath front's
expansion clock advancing produces Growth and Transform effects visible
in the landscape. A Deepguard front's ward network clock advancing
produces Protection and Nullify effects detectable by Detect Magic or
simple observation (new rune-marked stones, restricted access zones,
faintly humming walls).

The same grammar that describes individual spellcasting describes
institutional symptoms. A character casting Growth and a territory
experiencing ecological expansion are the same effect at different
scales. The Spell Sentence IS the symptom vocabulary for every scale of
clock advancement.

**Isotope Specificity**

The 104 isotopes across five planes replace generalized descriptions
with specific ones. "Fire" is not a symptom. "Borderlands Fire-03
(volcanic mineral combustion)" is a symptom. "Verdant Font Fire-07
(biological fever cascade)" is a different symptom. The same Spell
Sentence effect in different territories produces visibly different
seeds because the local isotope profile shapes the expression.

A Growth symptom in a Borderlands territory looks like crystal formation
or mineral accretion. A Growth symptom in a Verdant Font territory looks
like vegetation surge or biological proliferation. A Growth symptom in a
Palimpsest territory looks like probability condensation --- the same
unlikely event happening repeatedly, accumulating into pattern. Same
effect. Different isotope. Different observable seed. The territory's
isotope profile IS the symptom's visual vocabulary.

**EXPRESSION GUIDANCE: ATTENTION DIRECTION**

Vertebra 5's strongest expression bond is Attention Direction ---
controlling which seed the audience perceives and in what order. Three
media techniques govern this bond.

**Film: The Camera**

Film directs attention through camera placement. Close-up means this
detail matters. Wide shot means take in the whole scene. The cut to the
reaction shot tells the audience how to feel about what they just saw.

At the table, the GM's description IS the camera. What the GM describes
first is the close-up --- the detail the party should notice. What the
GM describes last is the establishing context. The order of description
determines the order of attention. Leading with the blood on the wall
(close-up) before describing the room (wide shot) creates a different
experience than describing the room first and mentioning the blood last.

The player who describes their character noticing something specific is
also directing the camera. "Lacuna watches the fighter's face when the
prisoner begs." That is a reaction shot. The table is now looking at the
fighter's player. The scene became about the fighter's moral choice
because the camera was pointed there.

**Journalism: Show the Primary Source**

Present raw observational data rather than interpreted conclusions. The
merchant touched his collar twice while saying the shipment was delayed.
He looked toward the warehouse when he mentioned his supplier. His voice
dropped on the word 'bandits.' Three pieces of behavioral evidence. The
party draws the conclusion. The conclusion is more convincing because
they reached it themselves.

Seeds should be presented as primary sources --- what the party can
observe, not what it means. The GM's job is pointing the camera. The
party's job is interpreting the image. When the party interprets, they
engage. When the GM interprets for them, they consume. The participation
IS the engagement. The evidence IS the call. The interpretation IS the
response.

**Comics: Density Controls Time**

Information density controls perceived pace. Sparse description
accelerates time. Dense description decelerates it. A single vivid image
STOPS time --- the audience lingers on the moment.

For fast scenes (chases, urgent situations, rapid transitions): sparse
detail. "Four guards, two doors, one torch, go left." The scene moves
quickly because the information is minimal.

For important scenes (revelations, threshold crossings, emotionally
significant moments): one vivid image. "The room is entirely empty
except for a single chair in the center, facing the wall." Time stops.
The table lingers. The emptiness is louder than any description of a
crowded room.

The density of description is a deliberate choice. The GM controls
pacing at the seed-presentation level by choosing how much detail to
include. This is the finest-grain pacing tool available --- finer than
beat selection (Document 4C) because it operates within individual beats
rather than between them.

**INTERPRETATION PROCEDURE**

The three dice produce a prompt. The prompt is not a situation. It is a
constraint set that the GM interprets within the current context.

**The Four Steps**

**1. Read the prompt.** Combine the three results into a single
sentence: "Something \[Scale\] is happening involving \[Pressure Type\]
pressure expressed as \[Effect\]." This is the raw prompt.

**2. Apply the context.** What is currently happening in the territory?
Which fronts are active? Which clocks are near threshold? Which NPCs are
present? The context determines which specific interpretation of the
generic prompt makes sense. A "significant biological growth" event near
the Titanwood means something different than the same prompt in a desert
city.

**3. Generate the truth.** The raw prompt + context = a specific
situation. Write it as a one-sentence truth in the Lazy DM format. This
truth enters the seed inventory and is subject to the same five-tool
management (Document 5A) as any other seed.

**4. Check front connection.** Does the generated truth connect to an
existing front? If yes, the oracle has revealed a new clock or symptom
for that front. If no, the truth introduces new pressure from outside
the current front network. If the party investigates and finds no
connection to existing fronts, a NEW front may need to be created to
explain the event.

**The Interpretation Principle**

The oracle generates constraints. The GM generates meaning. The same
roll produces different situations depending on who interprets it and in
what context. This is correct by design. The oracle is the Ironsworn
principle applied to the Codex's five-plane architecture: constrained
random prompts calibrated to the setting's physics, interpreted through
the setting's current state, producing seeds that surprise the GM as
much as the party.

If a roll genuinely does not fit the current context, reroll any single
axis. If it still does not fit, reroll all three. If three full rolls
produce nothing usable, the current context may not need an
oracle-generated seed --- the active clocks are providing sufficient
symptoms and the oracle is unnecessary this session.

**ORACLE MATHEMATICS**

The three axes produce 10 × 20 × 6 = 1,200 unique combinations. The
distribution is not uniform across all three dimensions.

  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------
  **Pressure Type**  Each plane has a 20% probability (2 results on a
                     d10). All planes are equally likely. No plane is
                     privileged over any other.

  **Expression**     Each effect has a 5% probability (1 result on a
                     d20). All effects are equally likely. The Variable
                     result (roll 20) gives the GM complete discretion 5%
                     of the time.

  **Intensity**      Each level has a 16.7% probability (1 result on a
                     d6). Minor and Noticeable events are as likely as
                     Severe and Critical ones. This is intentional ---
                     the world produces as many minor anomalies as it
                     does major crises. The GM should not reroll
                     low-intensity results. Background texture IS the
                     world's density.
  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------

The frequency of oracle use determines how many unique combinations the
campaign explores. At one roll per session across a 50-session campaign,
50 of the 1,200 combinations are used --- less than 5%. At three rolls
per session, 150 combinations --- still only 12.5%. The oracle's
combination space vastly exceeds any single campaign's consumption.
Repetition is mathematically improbable. No two campaigns using the
oracle will produce the same seed pattern.

**THE ORACLE AS DUAL-FUNCTION TOOL**

The oracle serves two distinct functions in the unified architecture.
Both use the same tables. The interpretation context differs.

**Function 1: Gap-Filling at Vertebra 5**

The oracle generates seeds from outside the active front network. The
result is a new piece of the world that the GM had not prepped. It may
connect to existing fronts retroactively (the oracle revealed a hidden
consequence of an active clock's advancement), or it may introduce
entirely new pressure (a front the GM had not considered, a territorial
event independent of institutional action, a cosmic anomaly the colleges
have not yet detected).

The gap-filling function maintains the world's density beyond the GM's
prepared content. When the party goes somewhere unexpected or the pacing
tracker demands a beat type not available from active clocks, the oracle
fills the gap with content that is consistent with the setting's physics
(the three axes ensure planar coherence) while surprising the GM (the
random combination prevents authorial prediction).

**Function 2: Deferred Consequence Generation at Vertebra 4**

When a Fulcrum Point spend skips vertebrae, the deferred processing must
produce a consequence. The oracle generates the form of that
consequence. The skipped vertebra determines the context; the oracle
determines the specific expression.

A Distance 2 spend skipped the Symptoms vertebra. The oracle generates
the symptom that SHOULD have appeared but was bypassed. A Distance 3
spend skipped Symptoms, Clocks, AND Pressure Points. The oracle
generates a more complex consequence reflecting the multiple skipped
vertebrae --- potentially rolling multiple times and combining the
results into a compound side effect.

The deferred consequence function ensures that FP spends have meaningful
side effects without the GM bearing the full creative burden of
inventing each one. The oracle distributes that burden between GM
judgment (interpreting the roll) and system-generated randomness (the
roll itself). The GM cannot predict the side effect. The system ensures
the side effect is planar-coherent and scale-appropriate. The surprise
IS the mechanism's value.

**RESOLUTION AND COLLAPSE**

Where Participants Meet Seeds

Document 6A --- Vertebra 6: Adventures

*The moment of observation collapse --- where the superposition resolves
into one actual state*

**WHAT OBSERVATION COLLAPSE IS**

Every seed placed at Vertebra 5 exists as a situation with multiple
possible resolutions. The party encounters the seed. The engagement
resolves through the game's mechanical systems. One resolution becomes
real. The others collapse. The story that emerges is the record of which
resolutions became real and why.

This is the Palimpsest principle expressed as gameplay. The world holds
superposition states until participants observe them. The observation is
not passive --- it is the participant's engagement with the seed through
action, investigation, combat, negotiation, or deliberate inaction. The
engagement collapses the superposition. The game's resolution mechanics
determine WHICH state becomes real. The participant's choices determine
HOW they engage with the superposition.

Vertebra 6 is where the player lens dominates. The other vertebrae are
primarily GM-facing (prep, management, tracking). This vertebra is
participant-facing. It is the moment of play --- the experience of
encountering the world and changing it through engagement.

**THE RESOLUTION SYSTEMS**

Three resolution systems handle observation collapse across different
domains. All three are Distance 0 operations on the spine --- the
participant is at Adventures and manipulating Adventures. Fulcrum Point
spends at this distance (1 FP) enhance the resolution. Spends at greater
distances reach into other vertebrae from this position.

**Physical Resolution (Combat)**

The participant encounters a physical threat or obstacle. The resolution
uses the combat system: initiative, attack rolls against Composite
Defense, damage against Soak, Condition Track (Physical) advancement.
Each attack is a micro-collapse --- the superposition of "hit or miss"
resolving into one state through dice and modifiers.

The Spell Sentence is the participant's action vocabulary during
physical resolution. A character casting a Spell Sentence construction
(Effect + Primary Subtype + Construction Pool purchases) is performing
the same operation as the institutional fronts at Vertebra 3 ---
deploying the setting's magical physics toward an objective. The scale
differs. The grammar is identical.

The talent trees (24 top-level across Foundational Styles, Defensive
Philosophies, Tactical/General, Esoteric/Resource) determine which
physical resolution options the participant can access. The talent
selection IS the participant's mechanical vocabulary --- what they can
do during collapse is defined by what they invested in during character
creation and advancement.

**Social Resolution**

The participant encounters a social situation --- negotiation,
persuasion, intimidation, deception, or social combat. The resolution
uses the Poise/Clout system: social attacks against social defenses,
Condition Track (Social) advancement, relationship edge modification.

Social resolution is not a separate game mode. It is the same Distance 0
operation using social mechanics instead of physical ones. The
participant deploys social "attacks" (arguments, persuasion, leverage,
charm) against the target's social "defenses" (Poise = social HP, Clout
= social background influence). When Poise depletes, the target's social
position changes --- they concede, flee, or counterattack. The
mechanical structure mirrors combat because both are observation
collapse through contested resolution.

Fulcrum Points at Distance 1-2 during social resolution reach into
adjacent vertebrae: revealing a hidden pressure point about the NPC
(Distance 2, reaching into Pressure Points), or placing a social seed
("I happen to know the mayor" --- Distance 1, reaching into Symptoms).
Social play naturally extends across multiple distances because social
situations involve information, relationship, and history beyond the
immediate moment.

**Skill Resolution**

The participant encounters a task requiring specific competency. The
resolution uses ability checks against a Target Number (TN). The TN
reflects the difficulty of the task as shaped by the current conditions.
The check resolves the superposition of "succeed or fail" into one
state.

Skill resolution is the most frequent collapse type --- investigation
checks to discover seeds, perception checks to notice symptoms,
knowledge checks to interpret evidence. Each successful check reveals
information that shifts the participant's understanding of the
situation. Each failed check maintains the current superposition state
and may produce alternative information (partial success) or
complications (critical failure).

**ANALYSIS OF COMPETING HYPOTHESES**

When the participant encounters multiple seeds that could explain the
same situation, ACH provides the analytical framework for processing
them. Derived from CIA tradecraft methodology, ACH is the intelligence
analysis tool for determining which explanation best fits the available
evidence.

**1. Generate hypotheses:** List every possible explanation for the
observed symptoms. Do not evaluate yet. The list should be comprehensive
--- include explanations you think are unlikely. The unlikely ones
discipline the analysis.

**2. Evaluate evidence against each hypothesis:** For each piece of
evidence (each seed encountered, each clue discovered, each NPC
testimony), evaluate whether it supports, contradicts, or is neutral
toward each hypothesis. Build the matrix.

**3. Identify the diagnostic gap:** The hypothesis with the most
supporting evidence and fewest contradictions is the strongest. But the
critical output is identifying the DIAGNOSTIC evidence --- the single
piece of information that would definitively distinguish between the top
two hypotheses. That diagnostic gap IS the next investigative objective.

ACH converts a plot debate into a mission briefing. The party stops
arguing about what they THINK is happening and starts identifying what
they NEED TO KNOW to distinguish between explanations. The gap becomes
the next adventure's objective. The investigation is directed by the
analysis rather than by intuition.

**BELIEF ACTIVATION**

Beliefs (Document 1A) are Schema elements that create mandatory
engagement. Certain seeds automatically trigger observation collapse
because the participant's profile demands response. The character CANNOT
walk away.

A character with the belief "I will protect the innocent" encounters a
seed involving a threatened innocent. The belief activates. The player
does not choose whether to engage --- the belief commits them. The
choice is HOW to engage, not WHETHER. This is the bonding mechanism
between Vertebra 1 (who you are) and Vertebra 6 (how you engage). The
Schema reaches across the spine and grabs the situation.

Belief activation produces the campaign's most character-defining
moments because the engagement is compulsory and the method is chosen.
The gap between "I must act" and "how do I act" is where the character's
identity crystallizes. The choice under compulsion reveals more than any
elective decision.

When a belief is tested and found wanting --- when the character acts
against their stated belief because the situation demanded it --- the
belief may change. Belief modification is a significant character event.
It should emerge from play through the pressure of a specific situation,
not be revised arbitrarily between sessions. The moment of belief change
IS the character's turning point.

**FATE-STYLE COMPELS**

Character traits as sources of complication, not just bonuses. A compel
occurs when the character's own profile interacts with a seed to produce
an unexpected difficulty.

The Redemption-narrative character (Document 1A, Narrative Identity)
encounters the same seed differently than the Contamination-narrative
character. The Redemption character sees the crisis as a setup for
eventual positive outcome and engages with hope. The Contamination
character sees it as confirmation that positive developments always
corrupt and engages with defensive pessimism. Same seed. Different
profile. Different engagement. Different collapse.

Compels do not require mechanical enforcement. They emerge from honest
character portrayal. When a player says "My character wouldn't trust
this NPC because of what happened in session 4," that is a self-compel
--- the character's accumulated experience (the Legacy at Vertebra 7
feeding back into Schema at Vertebra 1) distorting their engagement at
Vertebra 6. The player is operating the full spine cycle in a single
decision.

The GM can offer explicit compels: "Your Constantine 95 means you feel
obligated to help even though this is not your problem. Do you resist
the obligation or accept it?" The question makes the Schema's influence
visible to the player. Accepting the compel produces complications (the
obligation diverts resources from the party's current objective).
Resisting the compel produces internal pressure (the Schema's tension
increases because the character acted against a load-bearing value).
Both outcomes generate story.

**FLASHBACKS: REVERSE-DIRECTION COLLAPSE**

Normal collapse is forward-facing: the participant encounters a seed and
responds in the present moment. Flashback collapse is backward-facing:
the participant declares a past action that retroactively placed a seed
or created a condition that changes the present situation.

"Of course I bribed the guard yesterday." The participant spends FP at
Distance 1 (reaching into Symptoms from Adventures) and declares that a
past action produced a current condition. The bribe was always there.
The guard was always amenable. The Palimpsest collapsed backward ---
observation from the present determining what was true in the past.

This is Schrödinger's Gun given to the participants. The GM uses
Schrödinger's Gun to place seeds forward in time (the truth was always
in the location the party searches). The participant uses flashbacks to
place seeds backward in time (the preparation was always completed
before the operation began).

**EXPRESSION GUIDANCE: EMBODIED EXPRESSION**

**The Dramatic Pause**

Silence before a critical decision. Not a performative pause --- a
genuine beat of consideration. "Lacuna looks at the prisoner for a long
time. Then he says: let him go." The pause did the work. The decision
landed because there was space before it. At the table, the pause
signals that this moment matters. The other participants stop and
attend. Collins' focused attention condition is met through the silence
itself.

**Blocking**

alliance. The participant cannot physically move at the table, but they

prisoner and the door." The positioning IS the statement. No dialogue

declaring it. Describe where the character is, who they are near, what
they face. The spatial information communicates relationships that
dialogue cannot.

state without dialogue. Writing in the ledger. Cleaning a weapon.
Touching a scar. Each action communicates something the character is not
saying. The table reads it unconsciously. The character's instincts

behaviors that reveal identity through action rather than declaration.

another character's response --- "Lacuna notices the cleric has been

awareness. The table starts noticing each other. The DM starts thinking
about how NPCs relate to the whole party, not just the speaker.

**WHAT THE FEEDBACK LOOP IS**

Vertebra 7 is the vertebra that makes the spine a cycle rather than a
line. After observation collapse at Vertebra 6 produces consequences,
those consequences propagate backward through the spine. Relationships
shift. Clocks advance or retard. New pressure points emerge. The
character's accumulated record crystallizes into pattern. The modified
state feeds back into Vertebra 1 (Schema), and the cycle begins again.

Without the Feedback Loop, each adventure is isolated. Events happen,
resolve, and are forgotten. With the Feedback Loop, events compound.
Every cycle's output shapes the next cycle's input. The world after the
adventure is different from the world before it. The character after the
adventure is different from the character before it. The accumulation
across cycles IS the campaign.

This vertebra operates in two phases: the immediate cascade
(consequences propagating through the network within the same session or
between sessions) and the retrospective read (the pattern that emerges
from accumulated cascades over time). The cascade is mechanical. The
retrospective read is interpretive. Both are essential.

**THE LEGACY SYSTEM**

The character-level feedback mechanism. The Legacy System reads
accumulated history in reverse and crystallizes identity from the
pattern that already exists.

**The Backward-Facing Read**

The Legacy System does not track forward-facing achievements. It does
not ask "what did you accomplish?" It asks "what pattern do your
accumulated choices reveal about who you are?" The answer is read
backward from the present, not forward from a starting point.

After every significant cycle (typically every 3-5 sessions, or at the
end of a campaign arc), the GM and player review the character's
accumulated actions. Not what they planned. Not what they intended. What
they actually DID. The pattern of choices reveals values the character
may not have consciously recognized.

A character who consistently chose to protect strangers over pursuing
objectives has a pattern. That pattern crystallizes into a Legacy trait
--- a permanent modifier that reflects who the character has become
through accumulated action. The trait was not planned. It was not
assigned at character creation. It EMERGED from the pattern of play. The
Selûnae read fate backward. The Legacy System is the Selûnae principle
expressed as character mechanics.

**Legacy Trait Crystallization**

When the backward read reveals a consistent pattern across multiple
cycles, the GM proposes a Legacy trait. The trait is discussed with the
player. If both agree the pattern is genuine (not forced or
coincidental), the trait is added to the character sheet.

**CHEKHOV OBLIGATION TRACKING**

Outstanding seeds from previous cycles are tracked at this vertebra.
Every introduced element that has not yet resolved is a Chekhov
obligation --- a promise to the audience that this detail will matter.

**Between sessions:** Review the truth list from each previous session.
Which truths were encountered but not resolved? These are active
obligations. Which truths were not encountered at all? These are
available for future placement --- they remain in superposition.

**Between arcs:** Review all active obligations. Prioritize those
outstanding longest --- the audience's memory of the introduced element
makes the eventual payoff more satisfying the longer it waits (up to a
threshold of approximately 8-10 sessions, after which the audience
forgets and the payoff loses impact).

**Resolution or retirement:** Seeds outstanding beyond 8-10 sessions
should either be resolved in the next arc or integrated into a clock's
terminal state so their resolution is structurally guaranteed. The GM
can also decide that the unresolved seed WAS the payoff --- some
introduced elements are red herrings that teach the audience that not
every detail leads somewhere. But this should be deliberate, not
accidental.

This is the Feedback Loop operating at the Zoetrope's temporal scale. A

drift. The GM tracks this drift qualitatively rather than mechanically:
which divine aspects came up most frequently during play? Which gods'
portfolios were most relevant to the campaign's themes? Those aspects
are reinforced in the setting's institutional landscape.

The party does not control divine cycling. But their choices --- which
gods' values they enacted through their actions, which divine aspects
their Beliefs aligned with --- contribute to the aggregate signal that
determines which aspects persist and which fade. The party's influence
on the divine landscape is the Selûnae principle at its largest scale:
the pattern of accumulated mortal choices, read backward, determines
which faces of the gods remain relevant.

The ledger performs three functions: it encodes information (the first
survival function --- knowledge embedded in experience), it enables
participatory correction (the oral tradition's error-checking

memories (the third survival function --- group binding through

**How to Maintain the Ledger**

One person writes during the session. This person is the scribe --- the
role can rotate or be permanent. The scribe captures in real time:
names, facts, promises, contradictions. The rest of the table
contributes corrections and additions during the session's Sequel
moments (Document 4C) when the pace allows.

Between sessions, the scribe reviews and cleans the notes. Entries are
organized by session number. Outstanding threads from previous sessions
are flagged. The ledger is available to all players at the start of the
next session.

The GM does NOT write the ledger. The GM has their own tools (front
sheets, clock trackers, Assessment Cards, relationship map). The ledger
is the PLAYER-SIDE record. Its accuracy reflects what the party actually
paid attention to, not what the GM intended them to notice. The gap
between the GM's notes and the ledger IS diagnostic data --- it reveals
what the party tracked and what they missed.

**The Ledger as Recap Tool**

At the start of each session, the scribe (or another player) delivers
the recap from the ledger. Not the complete record --- the selective
recap. Two to three threads that are most relevant to THIS session. This
is the "Previously On" from television (Document 4C) delivered in the
player's voice rather than the GM's.

The selective recap IS attention direction (Vertebra 5 expression).
Which threads the recap emphasizes primes the table's collective memory
for the content that will recur. The choice of what to include and
exclude shapes the session's starting frame. A recap that emphasizes the
missing blacksmith's brother primes investigation. A recap that
emphasizes the Redbrand threat primes confrontation. Same ledger,
different selections, different session energy.

**CULTURAL TRAUMA**

Not every event in the campaign carries equal weight. Some events become
defining --- they reshape the group's understanding of the setting,
permanently alter the campaign's tone, and become reference points that
subsequent events are measured against. These are cultural traumas.

**What Constitutes Cultural Trauma**

An event becomes a cultural trauma when it meets three conditions:

**1. Collective experience:** The event must be experienced by the group
collectively. An individual character's private tragedy does not produce
group-level cultural trauma unless the group witnesses it or experiences
its consequences.

**2. Framework rupture:** The event cannot be absorbed by the group's
existing narrative framework. It BREAKS the framework. What the group
believed about the setting, an NPC, or themselves before the event is no
longer viable after it. The rupture forces a new framework to be
constructed.

**3. Narrative claiming:** The group consciously identifies the event as
significant. They talk about it after the session. They reference it in
future sessions. They use it as a benchmark ("not since the Folly
Incident has anything been this bad"). The narrative claiming IS the
trauma becoming cultural.

**Mechanical Effects of Cultural Trauma**

  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------
  **Amplified weight The trauma event is referenced more frequently than
  in collective      events of comparable objective magnitude. It appears
  memory**           in recaps disproportionately. It shapes how the
                     party interprets new situations --- new threats are
                     compared to the trauma. New NPCs are evaluated
                     through the lens of the trauma's lessons.

  **Pressure map     The trauma reshapes the party's pressure landscape
  distortion**       for all subsequent cycles. Situations resembling the
                     trauma trigger heightened engagement. Belief
                     activations related to the trauma's theme become
                     more frequent. The party's Schema is modified by the
                     shared experience --- new outliers or pattern breaks
                     emerge from the collective processing of the event.

  **Selûnae drift    Characters involved in the trauma experience
  acceleration**     accelerated face drift. The Crone/Hag axis
                     (understood consequence versus unending consequence)

**PARTICIPATORY CORRECTION**

The oral tradition's error-checking mechanism applied to the campaign.
When the accumulated record contains contradictions, the group corrects
them.

**How Correction Works**

The DM says something that contradicts established continuity. The
scribe checks the ledger. "The guards were wearing blue, not red ---
session 3." The correction surfaces. Two outcomes are possible:

**Outcome 1: Error correction.** The DM made a genuine mistake. The
continuity is corrected. The guards are blue. Play continues. The error
does not enter the canon. This is the simplest and most common outcome.

**Outcome 2: Mystery generation.** The DM did not make a mistake --- the
guards changed their uniforms. The contradiction is a SEED. Why did the
uniforms change? Who ordered it? What does it signify? The contradiction
that was flagged as an error becomes an investigative lead. The
correction produced a mystery. This is the most narratively productive
outcome because it rewards the table for paying attention.

The GM can decide in the moment which outcome to use. If the
contradiction was genuinely a mistake and there is no interesting
explanation for it, accept the correction and move on. If the
contradiction could produce an interesting thread, adopt it as
deliberate and let the party investigate. Schrödinger's Gun operates
here --- the reason for the contradiction was always the one the GM
selects, whichever it is.

**Participatory Correction as Cultural Practice**

Over time, the table develops the habit of cross-referencing the ledger.
New information is checked against established facts. NPC behavior is
compared to previous encounters. The table becomes its own continuity
department. This is the oral tradition's participatory correction
elevated from instinctive to deliberate.

The practice compounds. A table that corrects continuity errors in
session 5 has a more coherent campaign by session 20 than one that never
checks. The corrections prevent the setting from drifting into
incoherence. The corrections-turned-mysteries add depth. The table's
investment in accuracy IS engagement --- they care enough about the
setting to track its details. The caring IS the campfire's third
survival function operating.

**THE SELÛNAE PRINCIPLE**

The story only has structure when viewed in retrospect. In the moment,
it is choices and consequences stacking. The meaning is applied after
the fact by the participants who lived through it.

**Applied to Sessions**

The session's theme was not planned. The GM selected symptoms from the
truth list based on pacing needs (Document 4C). The party engaged with
them based on their beliefs and instincts. The consequences cascaded.
After the session, the table looks back and says "that session was about
trust" or "that session was about the cost of ambition." The theme
emerged from the intersection of prepared density and participant
choice. The Selûnae read it backward.

**Applied to Campaign Arcs**

An arc's narrative structure is invisible while it is happening. The
party is making choices, encountering consequences, and navigating
situations. After the arc resolves --- when a clock reaches terminal
state, when a front completes or is defeated --- the arc's structure
becomes visible. The rising action, the climax, the falling action. The
theme. The character development. None of it was authored in advance.
All of it is legible in retrospect.

**Applied to the Campaign**

The campaign's meaning is the final retrospective read. What was this
story about? The answer is never what anyone planned. It is what the
accumulated choices, consequences, cascades, and compounding produced.
The GM's prepared density provided the fuel. The players' observations
collapsed the possibilities. The meaning was retrospective. The story
was always there. The participants walked through it and recognized its
shape when they turned around to look at the path they'd taken.

This is the Selûnae reading fate backward. This is the Legacy System
reading pattern from accumulated action. This is the Palimpsest's
superposition collapsing into one coherent history. The same mechanism
operates at every scale: character identity (Legacy traits), session
theme (retrospective discussion), arc structure (narrative recognition),
and campaign meaning (the final reading). The mechanism is universal.
The content changes. The process is the same.

**MICROSCOPE: NON-LINEAR HISTORY**

History does not have to be built chronologically. Coherence emerges
retroactively even when the elements were created out of sequence.

During campaign play, the party generates history fragments in the order
they encounter them. They discover a ruin in session 4 and learn its
backstory in session 12. They meet an NPC's descendant before they meet
the NPC's legend. They see the consequence before the cause. The
Palimpsest superposition collapses in whatever order the party's
investigation determines.

Microscope's insight is that this non-linear discovery IS the natural
way history builds in a participatory medium. The table does not need
the chronological sequence to understand the setting. They need the
connections --- the edges between fragments that allow the fragments to
be arranged retroactively into a coherent timeline. Each discovered
fragment is a node. Each connection between fragments is an edge. The
relationship map (Document 3B) applied to history rather than to active
fronts.

**The Generative Application**

The GM can use non-linear history construction as a worldbuilding tool.
When the setting needs history for a region the party is about to enter,
the GM does not need to build the complete chronological record. They
need to generate 3-5 historical fragments and the connections between
them. The party's investigation fills in the gaps. The gaps filled by
the party's schema (Bartlett) produce personally meaningful history that
the GM could not have authored alone.

The technique: generate one consequence (what the world looks like NOW),
then build backward to discover 2-3 causes (what must have happened for
this consequence to exist). The causes do not need to be chronologically
ordered. They need to be connected by edges that the party can trace.
Each cause, when discovered, retroactively enriches the consequence's
meaning. The history was always there. The party observed it into
existence by investigating the present state.

This IS the Selûnae reading fate backward applied to worldbuilding. The
GM does not build history forward from a beginning. They build it
backward from the present. The present state is the observation point.
The historical fragments are the superposition states that collapse into
coherent history through the party's investigation. The history is as
real as any forward-built timeline --- more real, perhaps, because it
was built to explain a present that the participants have already
experienced.

**CATALYSTS AND ACTIVATION**

Initiating and Sustaining the Spine's Reaction

Document C1 --- Phase 5: Meta Layer

*How to light the campfire, step back, and keep it burning*

**WHAT CATALYSTS ARE**

In chemistry, a catalyst initiates a reaction without being consumed by
it. The reaction runs on its own fuel once started. The catalyst made it
possible but is not part of the product.

The spine's catalysts operate the same way. They are mechanisms that get
the engine running, establish the conditions for self-sustaining
operation, and then recede. The campfire-lighter is not the fire. The
fire belongs to everyone who tends it. The lighter's job is to ignite
the fuel, establish the draft, and step back before their presence
becomes the thing the participants are attending to.

This document is for the operator --- the person who runs the campfire.
The GM, the community builder, the novelist, the facilitator. The person
who knows the spine exists and uses it deliberately. The participants do
not need this document. They experience the spine's output without
seeing its architecture. That invisibility is the catalyst's essential
property.

**THE FOUR-PHASE ACTIVATION PLAN**

The temporal deployment schedule for bringing the spine's vertebrae
online. Not all vertebrae activate simultaneously. The system comes
alive in phases, each building on the previous.

**Phase 1: SERE (Observe Before Acting)**

*Survival, Evasion, Resistance, Escape --- adapted from military field
craft to social intelligence.*

The operator enters the environment and does nothing except observe. At
a table, this means the first 1-3 sessions. At a community launch, this
means the first weeks. In a novel's planning phase, this means reading
the setting's existing material without imposing structure.

Only Vertebra 1 (Schema) is active during Phase 1. The operator profiles
the system --- the players, the DM, the table's creative agenda (GNS),
the individual engagement patterns (player types). No fronts are built.
No clocks are set. No seeds are placed. The operator is gathering
intelligence, not running operations.

  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------
  **Observe**        Profile every participant. What engages them? When
                     do they lean forward? When do they check their
                     phone? What kind of moments do they remember
                     afterward?

  **Do not direct**  Resist the impulse to improve things immediately.
                     The observation must be clean --- uncontaminated by
                     the operator's interference. Phase 1 data is the
                     baseline against which all future changes are
                     measured.

  **Establish        If the operator is a player (co-DM model), establish
  cover**            the character's routine behaviors. The ledger. The
                     scouting. The questions. These become the operator's
                     instruments but they must be established as
                     character behavior first, not as operational tools.
  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------

**Phase 2: Tradecraft (Begin Profiling)**

Vertebrae 1-2 active. Schema profiling produces pressure point
extraction.

The operator begins running the Ten Questions (Document 2A) during play.
Not visibly --- internally. Each encounter produces Assessment Card
data. Each NPC's want, fear, and contradictions are noted. Each player's
type is confirmed through accumulated observation.

The operator begins asking questions that prompt the DM or facilitator
to provide information they are sitting on. "Kismet scouts ahead. What
does she see?" is not a tactical action --- it is a structured prompt
that invites the facilitator to describe content they prepared but have
not yet delivered. The operator is teaching the facilitator to deliver
content by creating structured opportunities for delivery.

  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------
  **Profile          Move from passive observation to structured
  actively**         information gathering. The Assessment Cards begin
                     filling. The player type profiles are confirmed.

  **Establish        The operator's information-gathering behaviors
  routines**         become expected. The table expects the ledger recap.
                     They expect the scouting report. They expect the
                     investigative questions. The routines are now
                     background --- invisible infrastructure.

  **Begin seeding**  Small seeds only. Questions that prompt the
                     facilitator to elaborate. Observations that invite
                     the table to investigate. The operator is not yet
                     placing truths --- they are creating the conditions
                     under which truths will be received naturally.
  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------

**Phase 3: SADRAT (Full Operations)**

Vertebrae 1-5 active. The full spine up to Adventures is operational.

The operator begins actively seeding. Fronts are identified (even if
only the operator tracks them). Clocks are mentally tracked. Symptoms
are surfaced through the operator's character actions. The spine is
producing output that the participants encounter as natural play.

SADRAT --- the relationship catalyst --- drives this phase:

  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------
  **Spotting**       Identify who in the environment is actively engaged.
                     Which participants are contributing? Which are
                     consuming? Which are disengaged? The engagement
                     pattern IS the targeting data for the operator's
                     next action.

  **Assessing**      What does each engaged participant care about? What
                     do they contribute? What is their domain? The
                     assessment refines the player type profile into a
                     specific engagement signature.

  **Developing**     Build the relationship through consistent engagement
                     that rewards participation. When a participant
                     contributes, reinforce it. "That moment where the
                     rogue bluffed the guard captain was incredible."
                     Name what worked. They repeat what was reinforced.

  **Recruiting**     Invite deeper participation. Not explicitly ---
                     through structure. Create spotlight moments for each
                     participant. Ask questions that only they can
                     answer. Give them domain ownership. The invitation
                     is the gap in the structure that their contribution
                     fills.

  **Agent Handling** Maintain the relationship through ongoing
                     engagement. The cycle does not end because the
                     community IS the product. Each session is a
                     maintenance cycle. Each community interaction
                     reinforces the participation pattern. The handling
                     IS the tending of the fire.
  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------

SADRAT is not manipulation. It is hospitality. The operator identifies
who is present, learns what they need, provides opportunities for
contribution, invites deeper engagement, and maintains the relationship
across time. This is what a good host does at a dinner party. The
campfire analogy is literal --- the host lights the fire, arranges the
seating, prompts the conversation, and tends the coals so everyone else
can enjoy the warmth.

**Phase 4: Red Team / Gray Man (Full Cycle with Oversight)**

All seven vertebrae active. The spine cycles autonomously. The operator
shifts from active operation to regulatory oversight.

The spine is self-sustaining. Participants are co-creating without
prompting. The DM or facilitator is running fronts, advancing clocks,
producing symptoms. The table is investigating, engaging, producing
consequences. The feedback loop compounds. The campfire tends itself.

The operator's role shifts:

  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------
  **Gray Man**       Step back. Do less. The operator's contributions
                     become proportionally smaller as the table's
                     contributions grow. The fire is burning. Adding more
                     fuel is counterproductive. The operator's visibility
                     decreases until they are one participant among
                     equals.

  **Red Team         Am I still directing rather than participating? Am I
  self-check**       holding onto control the table should have? Can the
                     table sustain the session without my prompting? If
                     the answer to the last question is no, the operator
                     has not yet reached Phase 4. Return to Phase 3.

  **Regulatory       Watch for the conditions that degrade the spine.
  monitoring**       Transportation breaks (Document R1). Collins
                     conditions failing. Information economy imbalances.
                     Pacing monotony. The operator's Phase 4 function is
                     the regulatory systems expressed through a
                     participant's natural behavior.

**THE GRAY MAN PRINCIPLE**

The person operating the spine must remain invisible for the spine to
function as a participation framework. If the operator becomes visible,
the participants stop co-creating and start consuming. The campfire
becomes a lecture. The participatory architecture collapses into
broadcast.

**What Visibility Looks Like**

  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------
  **The fixer**      The operator solves every problem. The table waits
                     for the operator to unstick situations rather than
                     engaging themselves. The operator's competence has
                     become a dependency.

  **The director**   The operator's suggestions are always followed. The
                     table has stopped generating options and started
                     executing the operator's options. The co-creative
                     dynamic has become hierarchical.

  **The star**       The operator's character or contributions dominate
                     every session. Other participants' moments are
                     overshadowed. The operator is performing rather than
                     facilitating.

  **The teacher**    The operator explains techniques, names frameworks,
                     or corrects play in terms that reveal the system's
                     architecture. The participants see the scaffolding.
                     Transportation breaks.
  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------

  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------

  character**        action. Recapping is reviewing the ledger. Prompting

  tell**             elaborate. Directions take control away. "Didn't the

  then retreat**     stop. Let silence sit. Someone fills it. When they

  others**           where the fighter held the corridor was incredible."

teller selects, reorders, and emphasizes based on the audience. The
modules are scale-independent --- a story module works whether the
audience is five people around a fire or five thousand at a festival.
The content is the same. The delivery adapts.

The spine's modules are its vertebrae. Each vertebra's content (the
specific Schemas, fronts, clocks, seeds) is scale-dependent. Each
vertebra's PROCESS (profiling, extraction, organization, tracking,
placement, collapse, feedback) is scale-independent. The same seven-step
process runs at the table, in the novel, in the community, and in the
marketing campaign. The content changes. The process is portable.

  campaign**         operates through the campfire principle --- creating

The modularity catalyst enables this cross-scale deployment by ensuring

**REGULATORY SYSTEMS**

The Physics the Spine Operates Under

Document R1 --- Phase 5: Meta Layer

*Eight systems that shape how every vertebra operates without occupying
positions on the spine*

**WHAT REGULATORY SYSTEMS ARE**

Regulatory systems run alongside the spine. They do not occupy positions
on it. They do not produce output the way vertebrae do. They SHAPE how
every vertebra processes its content --- governing speed, quality,
density, amplitude, immersion, completeness, and the social conditions
under which the spine operates effectively.

Each regulatory system is stated once in this document. The
vertebra-specific documents reference these systems where they are most
active. The regulatory systems are not optional extensions. They are the
physics the engine runs on. A spine operating without regulatory
oversight produces output that is mechanically functional but
experientially flat, thematically hollow, or socially disconnected.

The eight systems fall into three categories: two diagnostic tools
(applied when something is wrong), one universal method (applied for
validation at any vertebra), and five governors (continuously shaping
the spine's operation).

**1. INFORMATION / CHOICE / CONSEQUENCE**

*Chris McDowall --- Into the Odd / Electric Bastionland*

**The universal diagnostic. At any point where the engine stalls, one of
three elements is absent from the current situation. Identify which
element is missing. Supply it. The engine resumes.**

  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------
  **Information**    What the participant perceives about the situation.
                     Without information, the participant cannot make a
                     meaningful choice --- they are guessing in the dark.
                     The stall manifests as confusion, paralysis, or
                     random action.

  **Choice**         A decision the situation demands. Without choice,
                     the situation is a cutscene --- the participant
                     observes but does not participate. The stall
                     manifests as passivity or the sense that nothing the
                     participant does matters.

  **Consequence**    What happens as a result of the choice. Without
                     consequence, the choice is meaningless --- the
                     participant decides but the world does not respond.
                     The stall manifests as disengagement or the sense
                     that the game is not real.
  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------

**Application Across the Spine**

  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------
  **At Pressure      Cannot find pressure? Check which element the
  Points**           situation lacks. No information about the entity
                     means the Schema is incomplete. No choice means the
                     entity has no agency. No consequence means the
                     pressure has no stakes.

  **At Fronts**      Front not advancing? The front lacks resources
                     (information about what tools it has), options
                     (choices about methodology), or impact (consequences
                     from its actions).

  **At Clocks**      Clock stalled? The tick trigger is not firing
                     because there is no information triggering it, no
                     choice activating it, or no consequence following
                     activation.

  **At Symptoms**    Seeds not engaging the party? The seed lacks
                     perceptible information, does not demand a choice,
                     or the party does not believe it will produce
                     consequences.

  **At Adventures**  Encounter not working? The participant lacks
                     information to act, has no meaningful choice, or the
                     resolution does not produce visible consequences.
                     Supply the missing element.

  **At Feedback      Consequences not propagating? The cascade lacks
  Loop**             information about which edges carry the effect,
                     choice about how receiving fronts respond, or
                     consequential impact at the receiving end.
  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------

McDowall's diagnostic is the fastest tool for fixing any stall at any
vertebra. It does not tell you WHAT is wrong in detail. It tells you
WHERE to look. The three elements are the minimum viable situation.
Every situation needs all three. Every stall is missing at least one.

**2. RED TEAM METHOD**

*Intelligence tradecraft --- adversarial perspective adoption*

**Adopt the opposing perspective to validate your work. The method is
universal. The target changes at each application.**

**Three Applications**

  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------
  **At Pressure      Red Team the pressure landscape. If you were an
  Points**           adversary targeting this entity, which pressure
                     points would you exploit? The adversary's
                     perspective reveals which points are managed,
                     unmanaged, and invisible to the entity. The
                     unmanaged and invisible points generate the most
                     dangerous fronts.

  **At Fronts**      Red Team the front. If you were running this front,
                     would you do what the front is doing? Is the
                     methodology strategically coherent given the assets
                     and opposition? A front whose behavior does not
                     survive Red Team scrutiny is either incompetent (the
                     driving NPC is trending toward slag) or deceptive
                     (the apparent methodology conceals the real one).

  **At Catalysts**   Red Team yourself. Am I manipulating rather than
                     facilitating? Am I directing rather than seeding? Am
                     I visible? The operator's self-check. The person
                     running the campfire must remain invisible (Gray Man
                     principle, Document C1) for the campfire to function
                     as participatory. If you have become visible as the
                     operator, the participants stop co-creating and
                     start consuming.
  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------

The Red Team method is not a procedure. It is a cognitive operation:
temporarily adopt the adversary's priorities, information set, and
constraints, then evaluate the target from that position. The operation
takes thirty seconds of focused thought. It does not require worksheets,
dice, or formal process. It requires the discipline to genuinely adopt a
perspective that opposes your own preferences.

**3. SCHEMA CO-COMPLETION**

*Frederic Bartlett --- Schema Theory (Remembering, 1932)*

**At every vertebra, optimal specification leaves room for participant
co-completion. Underspecified elements are features when the
participant's imagination fills the gaps with personally meaningful
content.**

People do not remember stories accurately. They remember them
coherently. Unfamiliar elements are dropped. Familiar structures are
imposed. The participant's schema --- their accumulated mental model of
how stories work, how people behave, how the world operates --- fills
every gap the creator leaves open.

This means underspecification is not laziness. It is an invitation.
Every gap the GM leaves in an NPC's description, every detail the
novelist omits from a scene, every unexplained element in the setting's
history --- each one is filled by the participant's schema with content
that is personally meaningful because it came from their own experience.
The participant's contribution makes the fiction feel more real than any
amount of author-provided detail because the participant OWNS what they
contributed.

**Application Across the Spine**

  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------
  **At Schema**      The GM shortcut's compressed NPC generation works
                     because the participant's schema fills the
                     unspecified Heart's Compass dimensions with
                     genre-appropriate content. Section III IS the
                     designated underspecification zone.

  **At Fronts**      Front methodology described in one sentence. The
                     GM's schema fills the operational details during
                     play. The sentence provides the constraint. The GM
                     provides the specifics.

  **At Symptoms**    Seeds described as primary source evidence rather
                     than interpreted conclusions. The party's schema
                     provides the interpretation. Different participants
                     interpret the same evidence differently. Both
                     interpretations are valid.

  **At Adventures**  The "this could be you" principle. The seed is
                     specified enough to constrain engagement but
                     underspecified enough for the participant to project
                     themselves into the situation. The projection IS the
                     engagement.

  **At Feedback      Microscope's non-linear history construction. The GM
  Loop**             specifies consequences and a few causes. The party's
                     schema fills the historical gaps with coherent
                     content.
  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------

The calibration question: how much specification is optimal? Too much
removes the invitation. Too little produces confusion rather than
co-completion. The threshold is whether the participant has enough
constraint to produce coherent content from the gap. If the gap is so
large that every participant produces a different, contradictory
filling, more specification is needed. If the gap is small enough that
every participant fills it the same way, less specification would invite
more personally meaningful content.

**4. TRANSPORTATION**

*Green & Brock --- Transportation Theory (2000)*

**At every vertebra, structural choices must protect the fictional
dream. Structural brilliance that breaks immersion is worse than
simplicity that sustains it.**

Transported readers change beliefs to match the story even knowing it is
fiction. The emotional engagement, the sense of "being there," the
temporary suspension of the outside world --- these are the conditions
under which the campfire's survival functions operate. Information
encoding requires engagement. Social calibration requires emotional
investment. Group binding requires shared immersion. If transportation
breaks, all three functions degrade.

**What Breaks Transportation**

  ------------------- ----------------------------------------------------
  **Visible           The participant sees the engine rather than the
  scaffolding**       fiction. A rule dispute that lasts longer than the
                      scene it interrupted. A seed that is obviously
                      authored rather than discovered. A consequence that
                      serves the plot rather than the world's logic. Each
                      one momentarily lifts the curtain.

  **Inconsistency**   The world contradicts its own established rules. An
                      NPC behaves in a way that cannot be explained by
                      their Schema. A front takes an action that
                      contradicts its methodology. A clock produces a
                      symptom inconsistent with its driving college. Each
                      inconsistency erodes the world's credibility.

  **Tonal mismatch**  The expression layer's delivery contradicts the
                      content's weight. A threshold crossing described
                      casually. A character moment interrupted by
                      mechanical procedure. A dramatic revelation buried
                      in a rules discussion. The content was significant.
                      The delivery said otherwise.

  **Pace disruption** The rhythm established by the clock system (Document
                      4A) breaks without purpose. Two rests in a row when
                      the session needs a crescendo. A Sequel eliminated
                      when the table needs processing time. The dissonance
                      between felt rhythm and structural rhythm pulls the
                      participant out.
  ------------------- ----------------------------------------------------

The protective instinct: when in doubt, choose the simpler option. A
simple NPC description that sustains the fictional dream is superior to
a complex Schema-derived profile that requires mid-scene explanation.
The Schema informs the GM's portrayal. The participant sees the
portrayal, not the Schema. The system serves the fiction. The fiction
does not serve the system.

**5. THE SMOOTHING EFFECT**

*Setting cosmology as regulatory system*

**The spine's resistance to dramatic change. Small outputs pass through
unnoticed. Large or rapid outputs trigger dampening. Only sustained
pressure from genuine Schema tensions produces lasting change.**

The Smoothing Effect is simultaneously a setting element (Aíon's
continuous Fulcrum Point expenditure maintaining coherence across two
merged settings), a game mechanic (the resistance that gives clocks
multiple segments rather than binary states), and a regulatory system
(the amplitude governor that prevents any single cycle from producing
disproportionate world change).

**How Smoothing Operates**

  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------
  **At Fronts**      Front actions encounter proportional resistance.
                     Small operations pass unnoticed. Large operations
                     trigger institutional inertia, political opposition,
                     resource constraints, and unexpected complications.
                     The resistance is not authored --- it is the
                     setting's social physics expressing through natural
                     obstacles.

  **At Clocks**      Clocks have segments rather than binary states
                     because the Smoothing Effect ensures gradual
                     transition. Each segment represents accumulated
                     pressure overcoming one layer of resistance. Only
                     sustained, committed action completes a clock.
                     Casual or half-hearted pressure is dampened before
                     it reaches threshold.

  **At Fulcrum       Distance ∞ spends (regulatory overrides) momentarily
  Points**           punch through the Smoothing Effect. The Effect
                     reasserts because Aíon's generation is infinite. The
                     deferred consequence is the architecture noticing
                     and responding. The greater the override, the
                     stronger the correction.

  **At the           Civilizational change requires accumulated factional
  Zoetrope**         pressure sustained across multiple arcs to overcome
                     the Smoothing Effect's civilizational-scale
                     resistance. This ensures that civilizational
                     transitions are earned through genuine, sustained
                     effort rather than produced by single dramatic
                     events.
  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------

The Smoothing Effect is the reason the setting has not torn apart
despite containing two forcibly merged realities. It is Aíon's
architectural solution. It is also the reason the game produces
satisfying dramatic arcs --- change must be earned through sustained
pressure, which means the change carries weight when it finally arrives.
Easy change is meaningless. Resisted change is dramatic.

**6. THREE SURVIVAL FUNCTIONS**

*Oral tradition theory --- the campfire's purpose*

**Every cycle should produce information encoding, social norm
calibration, and group binding. If a cycle produces fewer than three, it
is incomplete.**

  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------
  **1. Information   The participants learned something through
  Encoding**         experience. Not through exposition --- through
                     engagement. The chirurgeon's story taught them
                     something about the cost of medical practice. The
                     investigation taught them how to trace a
                     representation chain. The combat taught them that
                     the forest's creatures were once people. Knowledge
                     embedded in experience persists longer than
                     knowledge delivered as fact.

  **2. Social Norm   The participants debated values through choice. The
  Calibration**      prisoner dilemma. The competing loyalties. The
                     ticking clock forcing triage. Every moral choice the
                     party makes calibrates the group's shared values.
                     They discover what they collectively believe by
                     discovering what they collectively choose. The
                     calibration IS the campfire's cautionary tale
                     function --- what would we do if this were real?

  **3. Group         The participants shared an emotional peak. Collins'
  Binding**          interaction ritual conditions were met (focused
                     attention, shared mood, mutual awareness, boundary).
                     The clock threshold crossing produced collective
                     effervescence. The table remembers the moment
                     afterward. The shared memory becomes a sacred symbol
                     --- a reference point that binds the group's
                     identity. "Remember when the ward collapsed?" That
                     shared reference IS the binding.
  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------

A session that delivers only combat (information encoding through
tactical experience but no social calibration or group binding) is
mechanically functional but narratively incomplete. A session that
delivers only moral dilemmas (social calibration without tactical
engagement or emotional peaks) is thematically rich but experientially
thin. A session that delivers only emotional peaks (group binding
without information or calibration) is memorable but shallow. The
complete session touches all three. The Three Survival Functions are the
output completeness validator.

**7. INFORMATION ECONOMY**

*Intelligence theory applied to narrative flow*

**Information moves between participants as currency with supply,
demand, and exchange value. The rate and direction of flow shapes how
quickly observation collapses occur and how completely consequences
propagate.**

  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------
  **Supply**         How much information is available. High supply (many
                     seeds visible, many truths placed) produces rapid
                     collapse --- the participants have enough data to
                     act quickly. Low supply (few visible seeds, opaque
                     situation) produces slow collapse --- the
                     participants must investigate before acting.

  **Demand**         How urgently the participants need information. A
                     ticking clock creates demand. A comfortable status
                     quo eliminates it. The pacing tracker (Document 4C)
                     manages demand through beat selection --- presenting
                     threatening symptoms creates demand; presenting
                     discovery symptoms supplies information.

  **Exchange Value** What information costs to obtain. Some information
                     is free (the seed is visible in the environment).
                     Some costs resources (the NPC demands payment for
                     what they know). Some costs risk (investigating the
                     archive means entering contested territory). The
                     cost shapes how the party prioritizes --- cheap
                     information is gathered first, expensive information
                     only when necessary.

  **Flow Direction** Who knows what. Information asymmetry IS dramatic
                     tension. When the party knows something the NPC does
                     not, they have leverage. When the NPC knows
                     something the party does not, the NPC has power. The
                     Goffman gap (Document 1A) is an information
                     asymmetry between an entity's front stage and back
                     stage. The representation chain (Document 3A) is an
                     information degradation pipeline.
  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------

The GM controls the information economy through seed placement (supply),
clock management (demand), and NPC behavior (exchange value and flow
direction). A session with too much information feels rushed --- the
party acts before they understand. A session with too little feels
stalled --- the party cannot act because they have nothing to act on.
The information economy is the flow speed governor that determines the
spine's operational pace.

**8. INTERACTION RITUAL**

*Randall Collins --- Interaction Ritual Chains (2004)*

**Four conditions must be met for the spine's output to produce maximum
emotional impact. These are the social prerequisites for the campfire to
function.**

  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------
  **Focused          Everyone at the table is attending to the same
  Attention**        moment. No side conversations, no phone checks, no
                     rule lookups during a dramatic beat. Focused
                     attention is the container that holds the collective
                     experience. Without it, the experience scatters.

  **Shared Mood**    The table's emotional state is aligned. The
                     crescendo built the mood collaboratively across
                     multiple beats. By the threshold crossing, everyone
                     is feeling the same tension. Shared mood is not
                     enforced --- it emerges from the rhythm of play when
                     the pacing is working.

  **Mutual           Each participant knows the others are also engaged.
  Awareness**        The awareness amplifies individual emotion into
                     collective experience. I know you are feeling this.
                     You know I am feeling this. We know we are feeling
                     this together. The amplification IS the social
                     magic.

  **Boundary**       The group has a sense of insiders and outsiders. The
                     table is a unit. The shared experience distinguishes
                     the group from people who were not there. "You had
                     to be there" is the boundary articulated. The sacred
                     symbols produced by the ritual ("Remember when the
                     ward collapsed?") reinforce the boundary across
                     sessions.
  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------

When all four conditions are met, the clock threshold crossing, the
belief activation, the state change --- any structurally significant
moment --- produces collective effervescence. The peak emotional
experience that the table remembers. The campfire's binding function at
maximum intensity.

When any condition breaks, the same structural moment falls flat. The
GM's job at the spine's climactic moments is not narrating brilliantly.
It is ensuring these four conditions exist. The narration can be simple
if the conditions are met. The narration can be brilliant and still fail
if the conditions are not met. The conditions are the prerequisites. The
content is secondary.

  **Adventures       Belief activations, forced choices, and dramatic
  (Vertebra 6)**     collapses produce the moments that generate sacred
