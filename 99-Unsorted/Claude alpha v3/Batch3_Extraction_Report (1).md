### 1A. ESL Calculation as Actually Practiced

The header states ESL = "lesser of Effect Tier and Subtype Tier." The worked examples do NOT use this formula. Every spell instead uses:

**Final Rank = Effect Cost + Subtype Cost + Duration CP + Range CP + Target CP + Additives − Subtractives**

Then:
- Architect recipes apply band reduction: ESL 1–4 = −4, ESL 5–7 = −3, ESL 8–10 = −2
- Improviser recipes take Final Rank as ESL directly (no reduction)
- Minimum ESL = 1 (floor)

**CONFLICT FLAG:** The "lesser of Effect Tier and Subtype Tier" rule from the header is never applied in practice. The 90 spells consistently use the Final Rank summation method. Either the header is outdated or the "lesser of" is a cap rule that never triggers in these examples. Needs resolution against SRD v3.

The Forge is the universal mediator. It has no native products and no inherent expression. What it has is the ability to make other planes' processes do things they would not do unassisted. The Forge is a catalyst in the chemical sense: it lowers activation energy without being consumed. It is the only plane that can interface with all others without being captured by any of them.

- Instability: Shatters if information load exceeds geometric capacity.

- Sub-variants: F-13 (maximum precision, most brittle), F-14 (directed flow through Channeled infrastructure), F-15 (actively cycling through electrical chain), F-16 (deliverable through liquid carrier — injectable form)

- Sub-variants: F-01 (pure pressure, most consistent, highest maintenance), F-02 (electrified boundary — active), F-03 (self-fueling through combustion — lowest ongoing maintenance), F-04 (geometric structure — visible traversable interface zones)

- Instability: Bridge overload — explosive discharge rather than conversion.

- Sub-variants: F-05 (maximum conversion speed, maximum arc risk), F-06 (precision-timed by mechanical structure — converts on schedule), F-07 (stores charge from chemical cycling — highest single-use power), F-08 (geometric structure — visible emulsion boundary)

**Step 2 — The Nascent State (Burden in Escrow):**

**Step 3 — Player's Choice (Calcification):**

- **Calcification:** Mutation becomes permanent the moment player spends the XP to pay off its Escrow cost. Character's body and soul fully accept and integrate the change.
- **Consequences of Calcification:** Permanent (cannot be removed short of world-altering event). Stable (no more unreliable quirks). Alterable (can be reshaped by alchemical Alter effect — form and function refined, but not removed).

**Post-Calcification Rule:** Once calcified, alchemy cannot affect it. Magic CAN still alter or remove it permanently. This preserves player options without being too restrictive.

**Summary: The Life Cycle of a Mutation:**
1. Infliction → Nascent Mutation (via Strain or Mutagen)
2. Escrow → XP Cost added to Escrow
3. Player Choice: (A) Purge while Nascent, clearing XP cost; (B) Keep unstable, carrying Escrow burden; (C) Pay XP cost — Calcified, permanent, stable, customizable
