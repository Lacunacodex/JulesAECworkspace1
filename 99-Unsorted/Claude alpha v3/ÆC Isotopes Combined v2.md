# ÆTHERIUM CODEX — Complete Isotope Effect Reference
## The Borderlands (W1): The Physical Register

*Profile derivation: Solid=Static, Liquid=Cycling, Gas=Projecting, Plasma=Absorbing*
*Compound isotopes: dominant axis sets primary profile, recessive adds behavioral character*

---

### B-01 — Inferno (Plasma Pure / Fire)

**Phase:** Plasma | **Type:** ◆ Natural | **Profile:** Absorbing
*Consuming fire. Draws fuel inward and converts it to heat. Self-sustaining as long as fuel exists.*

**Natural Effects:**
- **Damage** — Direct thermal destruction. The most immediate expression. Combustion on contact.
- **Environment** — Area denial through sustained heat. Temperature alteration. Atmospheric change.
- **Affliction** — Burning condition (ongoing). Ignition of flammable materials. Thermal shock.
- **Weaken** — Prolonged exposure degrades material integrity. Melting, warping, structural softening.
- **Transform** — Fire as processing agent at controlled application. Smelting, calcination, charcoal production. The basis of metallurgical processing.
- **Senses** — Light production. Fire illuminates. At low Stage this is the most common practical application — a torch.
- **Feature** — Persistent warmth. Heat source. Comfort in cold. Cooking.

**Hard Exclusions:** Healing, Protection (physical), Concealment (fire is maximally visible), Luck Control, Flight, Teleport, Communication, Mind Reading.

**Formation Stage Shift:**
- Stage 1–2: Warmth. Ignites tinder. Light source. Comfort heat. The campfire.
- Stage 3: Active combustion. Sustained burning. Area fire damage. Structural threat.
- Stage 4–5: Self-sustaining consumption field. Consumes all available fuel without cessation. Melts stone. Plasma threshold.

**Median Expression:** Damage (fire). A B-01 compound installed without further specification produces fire damage on contact or activation.

### B-02 — Steam (Plasma + Liquid / Fire + Water)

**Phase:** Plasma dominant, Liquid recessive | **Type:** ⚙ Catalyzed Diagonal | **Profile:** Absorbing primary, Cycling character
*Scalding high-pressure vapor. Expansion under containment. The diagonal means this state requires Forge mediation to hold — fire and water naturally annihilate toward one or the other.*

**Natural Effects:**
- **Damage** — Scalding thermal damage. Pressure damage. Burns that penetrate sealed protection because vapor permeates.
- **Environment** — Fog/vapor creation. Pressure alteration. Humidity. Atmospheric saturation.
- **Affliction** — Scalding burns (ongoing, penetrating). Disorientation from steam blindness. Respiratory distress from superheated air.
- **Concealment** — Steam as visual obscurant. Reduces both physical and magical line of sight.
- **Move Object** — Pressure as motive force. The steam engine principle — contained pressure pushing mass. The historical basis of Setting B industrial power.
- **Weaken** — Pressure degradation of sealed containers, joints, and armor. Steam finds and exploits every gap.
- **Feature** — Persistent heat and moisture. Anti-freeze. Humidity maintenance.

**Hard Exclusions:** Healing, Luck Control, Illusion, Teleport, Mind Reading, Growth, Create (physical — steam disperses rather than builds).

**Formation Stage Shift:**
- Stage 1–2: Warmth and dampness. Prevents freezing. Mild obscurement. Comfortable humidity.
- Stage 3: High-pressure emission. Scalding damage on contact. Active obscurement. Armor joint degradation begins.
- Stage 4–5: Violent decompression. Explosive pressure release. Structural destruction of containment. Boiler-burst equivalent.

**Median Expression:** Damage (scalding) + Environment (obscurement). Steam naturally does both simultaneously — it hurts and it hides.

### B-03 — Slag (Plasma + Solid / Fire + Earth)

**Phase:** Plasma dominant, Solid recessive | **Type:** ◆ Natural | **Profile:** Absorbing primary, Static character
*Superheated semi-liquid heavy matter. Adhesive, slow-moving, retains heat long after source withdraws. The Solid recessive gives the fire mass and persistence — it doesn't flash and fade, it sticks and burns.*

**Natural Effects:**
- **Damage** — Fire damage with mass behind it. Persistent adhesive burn. Both thermal and bludgeoning simultaneously.
- **Affliction** — Adhesion condition. Molten material sticks to target, ongoing burn that can't be brushed off. Requires active removal.
- **Environment** — Terrain hazard creation. Molten pools as difficult terrain with ongoing fire damage. Persistent zone control.
- **Protection** — Self-sealing property. Fills its own fractures with molten material. Resists condition track degradation downward. Slag heals itself.
- **Create** — Crude structural material from cooled flow. Volcanic stone, basalt. Rough but effective.
- **Weaken** — Thermal degradation through sustained contact. Melts through lower-tier materials over time.
- **Feature** — Persistent heat long after removal from source. Thermal mass. Cold resistance.

**Hard Exclusions:** Concealment (magma glows), Healing, Flight, Speed, Luck Control, Teleport, Communication.

**Formation Stage Shift:**
- Stage 1–2: Retains heat for hours. Minor cold resistance. Warm to touch.
- Stage 3: Active adhesive burn on contact. Ongoing damage. Self-sealing begins.
- Stage 4–5: Flowing destruction. Self-reshaping under its own heat. Area terrain creation on knockback. Cannot be destroyed by fire or cold extremes.

**Median Expression:** Damage (adhesive fire). Slag hits, sticks, and keeps burning with weight behind it.

### B-04 — Scorch (Plasma + Gas / Fire + Air)

**Phase:** Plasma dominant, Gas recessive | **Type:** ◆ Natural | **Profile:** Absorbing primary, Projecting character
*Solar wind. Radiant heat delivered at distance without combustion. No fuel consumed, no smoke, no residue. The Gas recessive gives the fire range and penetration — it reaches across distance rather than burning locally.*

**Natural Effects:**
- **Damage** — Ranged fire damage without combustion. Flash-burn at distance. Radiant energy. Bypasses cover that would stop flame because it doesn't need a fuel path.
- **Senses** — Light production at range. Radiance. Illumination as a ranged field rather than a point source. The basis of all "light" spells that aren't biological.
- **Affliction** — Charring without ignition. Desiccation. Sunburn at intensity. Dehydration through radiant exposure.
- **Environment** — Radiant heat field. Temperature increase in an area without physical fire. Heat shimmer affecting visibility.
- **Weaken** — Material degradation through sustained radiant exposure. UV-equivalent damage. Bleaching. Brittling of organic materials.
- **Feature** — Persistent radiance. Warmth at distance. The "ever-warm" property at low Stage.

**Hard Exclusions:** Concealment (Scorch is the opposite — maximum illumination and visibility), Protection (physical), Healing, Communication, Teleport, Luck Control.

**Formation Stage Shift:**
- Stage 1–2: Radiant warmth from a distance. Melts soft materials on extended contact. Light source.
- Stage 3: Flash-burn delivery. Ranged fire damage without combustion. Chars rather than ignites.
- Stage 4–5: Solar intensity field. Area radiant aura dealing ongoing fire damage. Melts stone. Sustained exposure destroys organic material.

**Median Expression:** Damage (radiant fire at range). Scorch is the sniper rifle of fire — it reaches out and burns without needing to be there.

### B-05 — The Abyss (Liquid Pure / Water)

**Phase:** Liquid | **Type:** ◆ Natural | **Profile:** Cycling
*Endless crushing ocean. Pressure without limit. Sensory deprivation. Weight of infinite depth made tangible. Cycling because water transforms what passes through it — dissolves, carries, redistributes, converts.*

**Natural Effects:**
- **Protection** — Hydrostatic pressure as defense. Compression resistance. The deeper the Abyss expression, the harder it is to penetrate.
- **Environment** — Deep water conditions. Pressure increase. Temperature drop. Darkness. Atmospheric replacement with liquid medium.
- **Affliction** — Crushing pressure. Suffocation. Disorientation. Sensory deprivation. The Abyss imposes isolation.
- **Move Object** — Hydraulic force. Current and flow. Water as motive medium carrying or dragging mass.
- **Concealment** — Depth obscurement. Sound dampening. Light absorption. The deep ocean hides everything.
- **Speed (Swimming)** — Enhanced movement through liquid medium specifically. The Abyss facilitates travel through water.
- **Weaken** — Pressure fatigue. Sustained compression weakening structural integrity. Water seepage undermining foundations.
- **Feature** — Weight increase. Density enhancement. Pressure resistance for the wielder.
- **Damage** — At high Stage, crushing/bludgeoning through overwhelming hydraulic pressure. The ocean floor expression.

**Hard Exclusions:** Flight (the Abyss pulls down, not up), Fire damage (water extinguishes), Illusion, Luck Control, Growth.

**Formation Stage Shift:**
- Stage 1–2: Heavy. Item feels denser than expected. Minor resistance to displacement. Pressure comfort.
- Stage 3: Pressure field. Area movement impeded. Sensory suppression begins. Sound and light dampened.
- Stage 4–5: Abyssal depth. Total sensory deprivation zone. Crushing force. Area control through overwhelming pressure.

**Median Expression:** Protection (pressure). The Abyss makes things harder to reach, harder to move, harder to affect.

### B-06 — Vapor (Liquid + Plasma / Water + Fire)

**Phase:** Liquid dominant, Plasma recessive | **Type:** ⚙ Catalyzed Diagonal | **Profile:** Cycling primary, Absorbing character
*Choking white fog. Humidity that drowns on land. Distinct from Steam — Vapor is the fog state, not the pressure state. Requires Forge mediation to hold the intermediate state between boiling off and condensing.*

**Natural Effects:**
- **Concealment** — The primary expression. Obscurement of both physical sight and magical detection simultaneously. The fog jams scrying as effectively as it blinds eyes.
- **Environment** — Fog creation. Humidity saturation. Atmospheric alteration. Visibility reduction.
- **Affliction** — Choking. Respiratory distress. Disorientation in dense fog. Spatial confusion.
- **Nullify** — Magical detection interference. Scrambles targeting systems and divination. The "jamming" expression — Vapor doesn't hide the target, it corrupts the detection method.
- **Senses (negative)** — Suppresses existing senses and magical detection. Anti-perception field.
- **Feature** — Persistent dampness. Fire resistance through moisture saturation. Humidity maintenance.

**Hard Exclusions:** Damage (direct — Vapor diffuses energy rather than concentrating it), Healing, Luck Control, Teleport, Growth, Create, Protection (physical — Vapor has no structural integrity).

**Formation Stage Shift:**
- Stage 1–2: Persistent dampness. Fire resistance. Mild obscurement in enclosed spaces.
- Stage 3: Dense fog field. Significant visual and magical obscurement. Ranged attacks at penalty.
- Stage 4–5: Blinding fog. Total obscurement in radius. Ranged attacks auto-miss. Magical detection severely penalized. Items within become difficult to locate magically.

**Median Expression:** Concealment. Vapor hides things from both eyes and magic.

### B-07 — Mire (Liquid + Solid / Water + Earth)

**Phase:** Liquid dominant, Solid recessive | **Type:** ◆ Natural | **Profile:** Cycling primary, Static character
*The bog. Mud, quicksand, sucking earth. Neither fully liquid nor solid. The Solid recessive gives the water drag, mass, and cohesion — it doesn't flow freely, it pulls.*

**Natural Effects:**
- **Affliction** — Restrain, entangle, slow. The cohesive pull that holds targets. The primary combat expression — Mire grabs and doesn't let go.
- **Protection** — Cohesive resistance. Surfaces coated in Mire resist penetration. The mud absorbs impact by deforming around it.
- **Environment** — Terrain alteration. Difficult terrain creation. Movement impediment over area.
- **Feature** — Grip. Non-slip surfaces. Advantage on grapple/hold maintenance. The craftsman's use.
- **Create** — Mud and clay constructs. Physical objects formed from the cohesive medium. Adobe, brick, ceramic when dried/fired.
- **Concealment** — Submersion. Targets pulled beneath Mire surface are hidden from sight and most detection.
- **Move Object** — Slow inexorable pull. Drag. Patient cohesive force. Mire doesn't throw — it sinks.
- **Weaken** — Structural undermining. Foundation loosening. Material saturation weakening structural integrity over time.
- **Enhanced Trait** — Grapple/grip enhancement at specialized application. The wrestler's mud.

**Hard Exclusions:** Flight, Teleport, Illusion, Speed (Mire is definitionally anti-speed), Communication, Luck Control, Damage (direct — Mire doesn't strike, it holds).

**Formation Stage Shift:**
- Stage 1–2: Tacky surface. Grip advantage. Non-slip. Minor adhesion.
- Stage 3: Active cohesive pull. Movement halved for opponents. Restrained on failed check.
- Stage 4–5: Immobilizing field. Automatic difficult terrain in radius. Foundations undermined. Structures sink.

**Median Expression:** Affliction (restrain/slow). Mire grabs things and holds them.

### B-08 — Hail (Liquid + Gas / Water + Air)

**Phase:** Liquid dominant, Gas recessive | **Type:** ◆ Natural | **Profile:** Cycling primary, Projecting character
*Hardened water. Glaciers. Persistent cold that endures. The Gas recessive gives the cold penetration and reach — it spreads through the air rather than requiring contact.*

**Natural Effects:**
- **Damage** — Cold damage. Thermal drain on contact. Flash-freeze. Ice impact.
- **Affliction** — Slowed (joint crystallization). Restrained (full freeze, especially on wet/water-adjacent targets). Numbness. Frostbite.
- **Environment** — Temperature reduction in area. Ice formation on surfaces. Slippery terrain. Frost conditions.
- **Protection** — Ice as ablative barrier. Shield that shatters on impact but absorbs the hit. Temporary structural defense.
- **Create** — Ice constructs. Walls, bridges, barriers, weapons from crystallized moisture. Temporary but immediately available.
- **Weaken** — Material embrittlement through thermal stress. Cold makes ductile materials brittle (direct Brittle Flag interaction — cold can temporarily IMPOSE the Brittle condition on materials that don't normally carry it).
- **Feature** — Preservation through cold. Organic material doesn't decay at low temperature. The earliest food preservation technology.
- **Concealment** — Ice refraction/reflection creating visual distortion at specialized application. Glare.

**Hard Exclusions:** Healing (cold arrests biological processes rather than accelerating them — that's Synod territory), Fire damage, Growth, Luck Control, Teleport.

**Formation Stage Shift:**
- Stage 1–2: Persistent chill. Cold immunity for wielder. Liquids freeze on contact. Organic preservation.
- Stage 3: Flash-freeze field. Slowed condition on hit. Wet targets Restrained. Area temperature drop.
- Stage 4–5: Cryogenic. Shatters most metals through thermal shock. Area ice field. Sustained exposure Restrains without saving throw.

**Median Expression:** Damage (cold) + Affliction (Slowed). Hail freezes what it hits and impedes what it doesn't fully freeze.

### B-09 — The Bedrock (Solid Pure / Earth)

**Phase:** Solid | **Type:** ◆ Natural | **Profile:** Static
*Infinite stone. Geology without time limit. Maximum structural density. THE baseline structural expression of the Borderlands — everything else is modifications of this foundation.*

**Natural Effects:**
- **Protection** — Physical defense. Structural reinforcement. Soak increase. The single most direct physical defense expression in the entire system.
- **Create** — Physical objects. Stone, earth, structural elements. Walls, barriers, foundations, buildings. The basis of all construction.
- **Environment** — Terrain creation and alteration. Stable ground. Structural modification of space. Earthworks.
- **Feature** — Weight increase. Density. Knockback resistance. Structural permanence. The "immovable" property.
- **Enhanced Trait (Might)** — Density enhancing physical force at specialized application. Stone-fist. Earth's weight behind a blow.
- **Affliction** — Entombment at high Stage. Burial. Encasement in stone. The earth swallowing targets.
- **Move Object** — Earthquake. Ground displacement. Shifting earth masses. Landslide. Not telekinesis — the earth itself moving.

**Hard Exclusions:** Flight (Bedrock is weight — the opposite of lift), Speed (mass resists acceleration), Concealment (stone is maximally present and detectable), Healing, Illusion, Luck Control, Teleport, Communication.

**Formation Stage Shift:**
- Stage 1–2: Dense material. +1 Soak contribution. Minor knockback resistance. Heavy.
- Stage 3: Structural permanence. Condition track resists downward movement. Full knockback immunity for wielder.
- Stage 4–5: Immovable object. Resists all displacement, magical and physical. Only Master-tier Sunder at advantage can threaten condition track.

**Median Expression:** Protection. Bedrock makes things harder to break and harder to move.

### B-11 — Acid (Solid + Liquid / Earth + Water)

**Phase:** Solid dominant, Liquid recessive | **Type:** ◆ Natural | **Profile:** Static primary, Cycling character
*Solvents and sludge. Rock dissolved into corrosive fluid. The Static primary means the dissolution is persistent and structural — it doesn't splash and fade, it eats through. The Cycling character means it converts what it contacts into dissolved product.*

**Natural Effects:**
- **Damage** — Corrosive damage. Dissolution on contact. Penetrates by eating through barriers rather than overpowering them.
- **Weaken** — Structural degradation. Item condition track damage on struck items. Soak reduction through material dissolution. THE anti-equipment expression.
- **Affliction** — Ongoing corrosion condition. Continues dissolving after initial contact until neutralized or expended. Tissue damage that worsens over rounds.
- **Environment** — Corrosive area. Pools of dissolving material. Hazardous terrain that damages anything resting in it.
- **Transform** — Dissolution as processing step. Acid as tool for breaking compounds into constituent parts. The basis of refining and separation chemistry.
- **Feature** — Natural Sunder advantage against organic and low-tier materials. Corrosive surface treatment.
- **Nullify** — At specialized application: dissolving the physical substrate of magical constructs or enchantments. If the item carrying the enchantment dissolves, the enchantment has nothing to inhabit.

**Hard Exclusions:** Healing (acid destroys tissue, doesn't repair it), Protection (acid weakens structural integrity), Concealment, Luck Control, Flight, Teleport, Communication.

**Formation Stage Shift:**
- Stage 1–2: Mildly corrosive surface. Slow degradation on prolonged contact. Sunder advantage vs organic materials.
- Stage 3: Active dissolution. Struck items move down condition track. Armor degradation over sustained combat. Ongoing damage condition.
- Stage 4–5: Deep corrosion. Armor-Piercing (bypasses Soak points). Tissue degradation on critical hits. Structural failure of contacted materials.

**Median Expression:** Damage (corrosive) + Weaken (structural). Acid damages the target AND makes the target more vulnerable to subsequent damage.

**Phase:** Gas | **Type:** ◆ Natural | **Profile:** Projecting

## BORDERLANDS SUMMARY — Effect Coverage

**Effects the Borderlands EXCELS at:** Damage (physical/elemental — the widest damage type coverage of any plane), Protection (physical — B-09 Bedrock is THE structural defense), Environment (terrain and atmospheric — the Borderlands IS the physical environment), Affliction (physical conditions — burning, freezing, restraining, blinding), Create (physical objects), Move Object (physical force), Speed/Movement (physical enhancement), Weaken (structural degradation)

**Effects the Borderlands CAN produce with effort:** Concealment (through Vapor, Dust, Frost — physical obscurement), Senses (through Scorch light, Current detection — physical perception), Transform (through Acid dissolution, fire processing — physical conversion), Flight (through Void Sky at high Stage), Nullify (through Vapor jamming, Acid substrate dissolution — indirect)

**Effects the Borderlands CANNOT produce:** Healing (biological repair is Font territory), Regeneration, Panacea, Illusion (false perception is Palimpsest), Luck Control (probability is Palimpsest), Mind Reading (cognitive access crosses planes), Communication (information transmission is Forge), Teleport (location selection is Palimpsest), Dimensional Travel, Time Travel, Immortality (soul management is Synod), Summon (calling through infrastructure is Forge), Comprehend (pattern recognition is Forge)

## The Palimpsest (W5): The Arbiter

*Phase Wheel: Echo/Resolved (Solid) / Veil/Handed (Liquid) / Probability/Contested (Gas) / Glitch/Suspended (Plasma)*
*Profile derivation: Echo=Static, Veil=Cycling, Probability=Projecting, Glitch=Absorbing*
*Diagonal pairs: Echo↔Probability (Resolved↔Contested), Veil↔Glitch (Handed↔Suspended)*

*The Palimpsest produces no physical effects on its own. Every physically impactful Palimpsest expression requires a secondary plane to give the probability a body. What the Palimpsest does is determine WHICH of the possible outcomes actually occurs.*

### P-01 — The Loop (Solid Pure / Echo)

**Phase:** Solid | **Type:** ◆ Natural | **Profile:** Static
*A moment determined to repeat. The fixed outcome that cycles back to itself. Not a time loop — a probability state that has Resolved to "this outcome will occur again." Static because the Loop doesn't adapt, respond, or negotiate. It simply IS and will be again. The most absolute expression of Resolved fate.*

**Natural Effects:**
- **Immunity** — THE fate-lock expression. A specified outcome is guaranteed for the next triggering event. No variance roll. The result is predetermined. At low Stage, a single guaranteed hit or save. At high Stage, an entire event sequence fixed in advance.
- **Affliction** — Forced repetition. The target is compelled to repeat an action, relive an experience, return to a location. The curse of the Loop. The Groundhog Day expressed as a condition.
- **Nullify** — Counter-probability manipulation. The outcome is already Resolved — attempts to shift probability bounce off because there's nothing left to shift. Anti-Luck Control.
- **Feature** — Absolute reliability. The item performs exactly as specified every time. No variance. No critical success, no critical failure. Take average on all dice. Perfectly predictable output.
- **Protection** — At specialized application, fated defense. The Loop has Resolved that the target is not harmed by the next triggering event — so they aren't. Not physical resistance; narrative immunity.
- **Senses** — At specialized application, reading a Resolved outcome. The Loop already knows what happens next because it's already happened. Precognition through repetition rather than prediction.

**Hard Exclusions:** Healing (the Loop doesn't repair — it repeats), Damage (the Loop doesn't cause harm — it fixes outcomes), Growth, Speed, Teleport, Create, Move Object, Transform, Concealment (the Loop asserts itself — it demands to be recognized).

**Formation Stage Shift:**
- Stage 1–2: Minor fate-fixing. A single die roll predetermined. Reliability enhancement on one specific function.
- Stage 3: Active outcome guarantee. A full action guaranteed to succeed or fail as specified. Curse-level repetition compulsion on targets.
- Stage 4–5: Sequence-level fate lock. An entire chain of events fixed. The target lives the same moment over and over. Local reality repeating.

**Median Expression:** Immunity (fate-lock) + Feature (reliability). The Loop guarantees specific outcomes.

### P-02 — The Haunt (Solid + Liquid / Echo + Veil)

**Phase:** Solid dominant, Liquid recessive | **Type:** ◆ Natural | **Profile:** Static primary, Cycling character
*The historical overlay. The past visible in the present. Two descriptions of the same space — what it is now and what it was then — simultaneously true and perceivable. NOT the same as S-09 Haunt (Synod Spectral persistence). The Palimpsest Haunt is about perceiving a fixed past through the lens of the present. Static (the historical record is Resolved, permanent, unchangeable) with Cycling (the Veil converting between the two descriptions, allowing the observer to perceive either or both).*

**Natural Effects:**
- **Illusion** — Historical overlay. The space shows what it was alongside what it is. Not a false image — a TRUE image from a different time visible in the current moment. THE "see the past" expression.
- **Senses** — Perceiving historical events in a location. Psychic archaeology. Reading what happened here by witnessing it replayed in spectral overlay. Investigation through temporal perception.
- **Affliction** — Temporal disorientation. The target cannot distinguish past from present. They interact with furniture that no longer exists. They dodge attacks from wars that ended centuries ago. They respond to people who died generations back.
- **Feature** — Dual-timeline perception. The ability to see both the current and historical version of a space simultaneously. Invaluable for investigation, archaeology, and navigation through changed terrain.
- **Communication** — At specialized application, the historical figures in the overlay respond to contextual prompts from their original event. Not conscious — they're recordings. But the recording responds as if the original event were still occurring.
- **Environment** — At high Stage, the historical overlay becomes partially tangible. The old building's walls provide actual cover. The historical fire produces actual heat. The past becomes locally real alongside the present.

**Hard Exclusions:** Damage (direct), Healing, Growth, Speed, Teleport, Luck Control, Protection (physical), Create (new objects — the Haunt only shows what was), Move Object.

**Formation Stage Shift:**
- Stage 1–2: Faint impressions. Emotional residue of past events. Vague visual shimmer of historical conditions. "Something happened here."
- Stage 3: Full historical overlay. Past events visible in detail. Temporal disorientation becomes a combat hazard. Investigation-grade historical perception.
- Stage 4–5: The past reasserts physically. Historical objects are tangible. Historical conditions (temperature, weather, light) manifest locally. Two timelines coexist as equally real.

**Median Expression:** Illusion (historical overlay) + Senses (past-reading). The Haunt shows you what happened here.
