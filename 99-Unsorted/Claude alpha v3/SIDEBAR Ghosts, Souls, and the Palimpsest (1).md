SIDEBAR: Ghosts, Souls, and the Palimpsest

METAPHYSICAL INCAPACITATION & SOUL DISSOLUTION
When a character's Metaphysical HP reaches 0, one of three outcomes occurs:

1. Planar Dissolution: Your body dissolves into a slurry of unstable planar energy. You can be raised or resurrected normally. This only occurs if an individual has more than half of their XP debt filled with mutations

2. Supernatural Coma: You remain visible but comatose in a magical sleep. This happens when an individual only has no mutations or mutations within their escrow.

3. Undeath/NPC Transformation: You become an NPC, possibly bestial-minded, magically suppressed, or corrupted by runaway mutations. You can theoretically be restored, but becoming yourself again is a quest. This happens if the individual has less than half of total number of mutations for their XP debt but is debt past their escrow limit. 

THE CYCLE: Death, Soul Recovery, and Reincarnation
The gods know what mortals do not: souls are never destroyed—only damaged and 
degraded. Death is not the end but a transition into the Palimpsest, the plane 
where souls are processed, repaired, and recycled by the Fates.

- Soul Window: A soul remains recoverable for Mastery Rating ×2 days after death, 
  allowing for powerful resurrection rituals.
- Soul Cycle Duration: After ~2,000 years in the Palimpsest, a soul reincarnates 
  or is cycled into a new entity. Even captured or well-guarded souls eventually 
  pass through the Cycle again.
- Soul Trade: Soul gems have recently become a commodity, allowing field capture 
  and storage of souls. Colleges distribute binding charms widely, enabling 
  the first steps toward lichdom and other undead states. The "avoid death" 
  market is exploding.

GHOSTS & PERSISTENT SOULS
Some souls refuse the Cycle or become stuck in the material plane, manifesting 
as ghosts. Ghosts remain conscious but powerless unless given an anchor: a 
beloved location, unfinished business, or a powerful emotional wound.

- Known to Few: Only the Palimpsest-dwelling gods and Aon truly understand this 
  system. Most NPCs treat ghosts as curiosities or curses.
- Narrative Weight: A ghost's presence is a sign of something deeply wrong—either 
  divine interest or cosmic neglect.

WHEN TO USE THIS SIDEBAR
This information is best revealed slowly during the campaign, as PCs encounter:
- Powerful undead or lich-lords attempting to circumvent the Cycle
- Divine agents (priests, paladins) sent to prevent soul theft
- Ghosts bound to specific locations with narrative significance
- College intrigue around soul commerce and Ascension magic
