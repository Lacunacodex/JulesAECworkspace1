### V-01 — The World Tree (Solid Pure / Flora)

**Phase:** Solid | **Type:** ◆ Natural | **Profile:** Static
*Permanent structural foundation of life. Builds and maintains biological complexity. Static because Flora constructs and holds — it doesn't consume or transform, it grows and persists. The canopy that never falls.*

**Natural Effects:**
- **Healing** — Tissue construction. Wound closure. Structural biological repair. THE primary healing expression in the entire system. What Generative Font expression IS when expressed as an Effect.
- **Regeneration** — Sustained healing over time. The tree that regrows what was cut. Continuous biological repair without repeated application.
- **Protection** — Biological armor. Bark-skin. Structural reinforcement of living tissue. Living shields that grow back.
- **Create** — Biological construction. Growing structures, producing organic materials (wood, fiber, cellulose, resin). The World Tree builds.
- **Feature** — Self-repair (Generative passive expression). Persistent biological function. Structural plant properties. The "living item" property.
- **Enhanced Trait** — Biological structural reinforcement at specialized application. Hardening tissue, reinforcing bone, densifying muscle fiber. Construction applied to performance.
- **Environment** — At high Stage, growth altering terrain. Living walls, root barriers, canopy creation. The forest asserting itself.

**Hard Exclusions:** Damage (direct — the World Tree builds, it does not destroy), Teleport, Luck Control, Illusion, Mind Reading, Nullify, Weaken.

**Formation Stage Shift:**
- Stage 1–2: Basic healing. Wound closure. Minor tissue repair. Passive self-repair for living items. The poultice.
- Stage 3: Strong regeneration. Limb regrowth over time. Biological armor formation. Structural reinforcement. The surgeon's compound.
- Stage 4–5: Complete biological reconstruction from minimal surviving tissue. Living terrain alteration. The World Tree's full expression — what was destroyed grows back.

**Median Expression:** Healing. A V-01 compound installed without further specification repairs biological damage.
