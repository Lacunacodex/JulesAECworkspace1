## VERDANT FONT

### V-04 — Overgrowth
**ADD to Natural Effects:**

### V-05 — The Hunt
**ADD to Natural Effects:**
- **Swimming** — Predatory aquatic pursuit. The shark, the orca, the crocodile. Metabolic acceleration applied to aquatic movement.

### V-08 — Chimera
**ADD to Natural Effects:**
- **Swimming** — Adaptive aquatic form. The Chimera grows fins, gills, or streamlined body plan in response to aquatic environment. Reactive evolution for water.
- **Elongation** — Adaptive reach modification. Growing longer limbs or appendages as tactical response to reach requirements.
- **Variable** — Reactive biological Variable. The threat determines the adaptation. The organism produces whatever biological effect counters what it's currently facing. Controlled by the environment, not the organism.

### V-13 — Cancer
**ADD to Natural Effects:**
- **Variable** — Uncontrolled and dangerous Variable. Random biological output with no direction. You might get a useful mutation or a tumor. Variable at maximum risk with zero control.

### V-16 — Mutagen
**ADD to Natural Effects:**
- **Variable** — With Forge Calibrated mediation (F-16), this becomes DIRECTED Variable for biological effects. The practitioner selects which output manifests. The expensive, precision-controlled version. Without Forge: random Variable from the Viral profile.

## PALIMPSEST

### P-01 — The Loop
**ADD to Natural Effects:**
- **Quickness** — Temporal compression through repetition. The task has already been done — the Loop resolves it. Routine processes complete because the Loop has predetermined their completion.

### P-05 — Dream
**ADD to Natural Effects:**
- **Quickness** — Narrative compression. "This boring part is over." The Dream skips the uninteresting duration because the story doesn't require it. Temporal compression through narrative logic.
- **Variable** — Narrative logic Variable. The story determines which effect manifests. Controlled by dramatic appropriateness — the GM decides what the narrative requires. Not random, not player-controlled — story-shaped.

### P-07 — Fey Logic
**ADD to Natural Effects:**
- **Variable** — Conviction Variable. Genuine belief determines the output. The practitioner's conviction about what SHOULD happen influences which effect manifests. Unreliable in the opposite direction from Casino — biased by the heart rather than random.

### P-09 — Casino
**ADD to Natural Effects:**
- **Quickness** — Probability favoring completion. The Palimpsest's contested space resolves toward "this task is already done" — accelerating outcome resolution for routine processes.

*Note: Casino already has Variable listed in the existing profile.*

### P-12 — Chaos
**ADD to Natural Effects:**
- **Variable** — Anti-determination Variable. Like Casino but actively hostile to consistency. Each manifestation contradicts the previous one. The universe refusing to repeat itself.
