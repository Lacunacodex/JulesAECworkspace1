# ÆTHERIUM CODEX — Talent Tree Clock Mapping
## The Two-Ring Compass: Mundane (Outer) and Expressed (Inner)

*Working Document — Preliminary Placement*

---

## STRUCTURE

**Outer Ring:** Mundane talent trees. Pillar-expressed only. No planar prerequisite. Available to anyone with the skill ranks and Crux talent.

**Inner Ring:** Expressed talent trees. Pillar-expressed AND planar. Requires isotope investment in addition to skill/Crux prerequisites. The institution's planar character determines which expressed trees are available.

**Cardinal Positions (Aligned):** Institutional and territorial planar match. Deepest inner ring access. Four aligned positions: 16 (Synod), 10 (Font), 12 (Borderlands), 14 (Palimpsest).

**Institutional Positions (Non-Aligned):** Institution has planar character but territory doesn't accumulate it. Inner ring access through institutional training rather than environmental exposure. Positions: 02, 04, 06, 08.

**Buffer Positions (Transitional):** Blend neighboring institutions. Limited or blended inner ring access. Outer ring draws from both neighbors. Positions: 01, 03, 05, 07, 09, 11, 13, 15.

## POSITION 16 — MORTIS AEVUM COLLEGIUM
**Planar:** Synod (Osseous/Archival dominant) | **Pillar:** Metaphysical
**Aligned:** Territorial + Institutional = S-03 Ossuary

### Outer Ring (Mundane)
- **Chirurgical Practice** (Medicine) — Surgical technique, autopsy, anatomical knowledge. The physician's manual skill without magical augmentation.
- **Archival Science** (Lore) — Record management, historical research, document authentication. Reading the written record through scholarship, not psychometry.
- **Administrative Authority** (Influence) — Bureaucratic navigation, institutional procedure, command within hierarchical structures. The talent of making systems work for you.
- **Death Rites** (Praxis) — Funerary procedure, soul-cycle protocol, respectful handling of remains. The rituals performed by non-magical practitioners.

### Inner Ring (Expressed — requires Synod isotope investment)
- **Entropic Strike** (Athletics + S-04 Necropolis / S-14 Decay) — Combat discipline applying accelerated aging through contact. Weapons age what they strike. Higher tiers target specific materials or tissues.
- **Ossuary Reading** (Artifice + S-02 Ossuary) — Psychometric analysis through Synod expression. Reading the death-record embedded in physical remains.
- **Spectral Discipline** (Metaphysics + S-09 Wraith) — Projection of spectral presence. Insubstantial movement, emotional imposition, identity persistence techniques.
- **Oblivion Guard** (Metaphysics + S-13 Oblivion) — Nullification combat. Process arrest applied defensively — suppressing incoming expressions, creating dead zones, counter-magic through entropy.
- **Preservation Arts** (Artifice + S-01 Grave / S-05 Record) — Stabilization and permanence. Locking condition tracks, sealing records, imposing Settled heritage.
