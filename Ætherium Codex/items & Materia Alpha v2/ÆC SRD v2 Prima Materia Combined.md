## FOUNDATIONAL PRINCIPLES

### The Inseparability Principle

An item's alchemical specification IS its mechanical specification. The dual periodic table entry for a material IS its stat block. A designer does not decide that a steel longsword deals a certain damage tier — the real periodic table specifies that steel's tensile strength places it there. An alchemist does not decide that obsidian has a Borderlands heritage — the material's volcanic origin determines its planar passport.

The system reads the world. It does not impose upon it.

### The Universal System Principle

The same framework — spell sentence grammar, isotope profiles, interaction rules, hazard classifications — operates identically at every scale. A chemical reaction in a crucible, a mutation expressing in a blade, a practitioner composing a rote, a planar bleed zone forming across a region — all are governed by the same mechanics. The scale changes. The system does not.

### The Standards Commitment

Material properties derive from real-world measurements. Hardness from the Mohs scale. Tensile strength from measured MPa. Hazard classifications from NFPA 704 and GHS standards. Published real-world data can be plugged directly into the system because the system was built to conform to those standards, not approximate them.

## THE FIVE PLANES

Five planar engines underlie all magical and material reality. Each is a complex system governing a domain of existence. None reduce to a single concept.

### The Borderlands — The Engine of Matter

The Borderlands governs physical substance and its transformations — elemental interaction, phase transition, material properties, environmental forces, and the behavior of matter under pressure, temperature, and chemical reaction. It encompasses fire, water, earth, air, and every state that emerges from their combinations. The Borderlands IS the physical environment.

Phase Wheel: Earth (Solid/Static) / Water (Liquid/Cycling) / Air (Gas/Projecting) / Fire (Plasma/Absorbing)
Diagonal Pairs: Earth ↔ Air / Water ↔ Fire

### The Verdant Font — The Engine of Biological Process

The Verdant Font governs biological systems in their full complexity: growth, adaptation, consumption, propagation, mutation, symbiosis, predation, parasitism, disease, immune response, and ecological interaction. It is NOT "life" in any simplified sense — it includes the fungal network consuming a dead tree, the virus hijacking a host cell, the predator consuming prey, and the cancer growing without regulation.

Phase Wheel: Flora (Solid/Static) / Fauna (Liquid/Cycling) / Myco (Gas/Projecting) / Viral (Plasma/Absorbing)
Diagonal Pairs: Flora ↔ Myco / Fauna ↔ Viral

### The Synod of Endings — The Engine of Completion

The Synod governs endings, preservation, permanence, information storage, identity persistence, and the process by which all systems reach their most stable final state. It is NOT "death" — it is the administrative engine that processes what death produces. The Synod absorbed the functions of both the Silent Athenaeum (forbidden knowledge and preserved secrets) and the Concordance (interplanar diplomacy and transit), making it the Plane of Record, Judgment, and Interplanar Transit. Its domain includes the physical remains after biological process ceases, the archival record of what existed, the spectral persistence of identity beyond physical function, and the entropic void that claims what cannot be preserved. The Synod operates as an infinite bureaucracy — the Triad of Administration (Vestus managing data, Nomos managing process, Moros managing physicality) runs the plane as a processing engine for the cosmos. The Synod is patient. It asserts that everything will eventually reach its most stable state. It is always correct.

Phase Wheel: Osseous (Solid/Static) / Archival (Liquid/Cycling) / Spectral (Gas/Projecting) / Void (Plasma/Absorbing)
Diagonal Pairs: Osseous ↔ Spectral / Archival ↔ Void

### The Hidden Forge — The Engine of Technology

The Hidden Forge governs the processes by which incompatible things are made to work together — mediation, catalysis, engineering, and deliberate problem-solving through applied technique. The Forge has no native products and no inherent expression. It is not a reagent — it is the engineer holding the crucible in the right position. The Forge has no internal antithetical states because making incompatible things compatible is its fundamental nature. All sixteen of its internal isotope combinations are natural. The Forge is the universal mediator: the only plane that can interface with all others without being captured by any of them.

Phase Wheel: Pneuma (Gas/Projecting) / Volt (Plasma/Absorbing) / Chem (Liquid/Cycling) / Crystal (Solid/Static)
Diagonal Pairs: None — all combinations are natural within the Forge

### The Palimpsest — The Engine of Superposition

The Palimpsest governs probability, narrative determination, and the space where choices exist before they are made — fate, luck, historical echo, temporal perception, and the process by which contested outcomes become resolved. The Palimpsest produces no physical effects on its own. Every physically impactful Palimpsest expression requires a secondary plane to give the probability a body. What the Palimpsest determines is WHICH of the possible outcomes actually occurs. Mortals attribute the Palimpsest as the source of learned magic, but it is more accurately the filter through which the deeper, unnamed source of raw fate is accessed — a filter mortals require because direct contact with unfiltered probability would be overwhelming.

Phase Wheel: Echo/Resolved (Solid/Static) / Veil/Handed (Liquid/Cycling) / Probability/Contested (Gas/Projecting) / Glitch/Suspended (Plasma/Absorbing)
Diagonal Pairs: Echo ↔ Probability (Resolved ↔ Contested) / Veil ↔ Glitch (Handed ↔ Suspended)

## PLANAR INTERACTIONS

### Formation Types

When planar expressions combine, the formation type determines stability and crafting requirements.

**◆ Natural Combinations** form without external catalyst under appropriate conditions. Adjacent expressions on the phase wheel cooperate or coexist. All Hidden Forge internal combinations are natural.

**⚙ Catalyzed Diagonal Combinations** are competing states within the same plane. Fire and Water, Earth and Air — these annihilate toward one or the other without sustained energy input. In nature, the environment provides that input (steam exists at geysers). In crafting, Forge mediation provides it. The ⚙ designation tells the alchemist: this compound needs ongoing stabilization.

**◆⚙ Emulsion Combinations** force expressions from antithetical planes together through a Hidden Forge catalyst. The two antithetical pairs are Verdant Font ↔ Synod (the Biotic Paradox) and Borderlands ↔ Palimpsest (the Reality Paradox). Emulsions require three-domain knowledge: both antithetical planes plus the Forge. The catalyst type (Crystal, Chemical, Pneuma, or Volt) determines how the antithetical tension is maintained.

### Behavioral Outcomes

When expressions interact, the result falls into one of four categories:

**Cooperative** — the expressions reinforce each other. The combination amplifies what both already do. Produces stronger output with minimal instability.

**Competing** — the expressions conflict. Each pushes toward a different outcome. The combination requires negotiation — one may dominate, or both may persist in tension.

**Cancellation** — the expressions annihilate. Without mediation, one destroys the other or both are consumed. Antithetical expressions without Forge mediation produce cancellation.

**Emergent** — the combination produces something neither expression would produce alone. The most dramatic and least predictable outcome. Emergent results are why alchemy is simultaneously the most valuable and most dangerous discipline.

## THE ISOTOPE SYSTEM

### What Isotopes Are

Each plane contains sixteen isotope states — four pure expressions and twelve compounds formed from pairs of the four phase elements. These isotopes are the specific planar expressions that materials embody, that practitioners learn to work with, and that items manifest as mutations or installed effects.

Each isotope profile specifies which Effects it can naturally produce, which it categorically excludes, how its expression scales with Formation Stage, and what its default behavior is. A material's isotope identity determines what it CAN do — not through arbitrary assignment but because that combination of phase elements produces those specific physical or metaphysical behaviors.

### Learning Isotopes

A practitioner learns individual isotopes as discrete talents, each ranked 1-10. Pure elements (the four cardinal expressions of each plane) are prerequisite foundations — a practitioner must understand constituent pures before working with compound isotopes. Catalyzed diagonal isotopes require additional Forge knowledge. Emulsion isotopes require knowledge from both antithetical planes plus the Forge.

### Interaction Profiles

Each isotope inherits a behavioral profile from its dominant phase:

| Phase | Profile | Behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Solid | Static | Persists without change. Holds position. Resists transformation. |
| Liquid | Cycling | Transforms what passes through it. Processes, converts, redistributes. |
| Gas | Projecting | Pushes outward. Disperses, spreads, broadcasts. |
| Plasma | Absorbing | Draws inward. Consumes, converts to fuel, self-sustains through consumption. |

Compound isotopes carry a dominant profile from their primary phase and a behavioral character from their recessive phase.

## FORMATION STAGE

Formation Stage (1-10) rates the intensity at which a material's planar expression has crystallized. It simultaneously represents quality, purity, and the depth of planar adaptation the material has undergone.

### The Three Bands

Formation Stage bands align with NFPA 704 hazard severity and proficiency thresholds:

| Band | Formation Stage | NFPA Equivalent | Expression Level | Access |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Minor | 1-4 | 0-1 | Cosmetic to mild. Detectable but not dangerous. Safe with basic precautions. | Novice |
| Moderate | 5-7 | 2-3 | Combat-relevant. Mechanically significant. Requires professional handling. | Journeyman |
| Intense | 8-10 | 3-4 | Powerful, potentially area-affecting. Dangerous without expert containment. | Master |

### The Bottlenecking Principle

In any combined state, the lowest-quality component limits the overall expression. A Formation Stage 8 mutation in a Formation Stage 4 material can only express at the substrate's capacity. The mutation's potential is 8; its actual output is capped at 4. The material is the bottleneck.

### Heritage States

Every material's planar expression exists in one of three heritage states. These do not modify target numbers — they determine which operations are applicable.

**Active:** The process is ongoing. The expression is still changing, still responsive to input. Lava is Active — it's still flowing, still cooling, still reacting with its environment. Active materials accept new influences more readily.

**Arrested:** The process was interrupted. The expression carries tension from the interruption — it was going somewhere and was stopped mid-journey. Tempered steel is Arrested — the quench froze the crystalline structure in a metastable state that carries the energy of the interrupted cooling process. Arrested materials carry properties that neither the starting nor the ending state would have.

**Settled:** The process reached completion. The Synod has claimed it. The expression is at its most stable final state and resists change.Ite is Settled — geological time has driven it to equilibrium. Settled materials are the most stable and the most resistant to modification.

## THE FOUR-LAYER ITEM MODEL

Every item has up to four distinct layers. These are separate in origin, function, and mechanical behavior.

### Layer 1: Base Material

The physical substance. Its properties derive from the real periodic table — physical stats on 1-10 scales, material flags, hazard ratings. The base material also carries an alchemical passport identifying its planar heritage and compatibility. The base material's Tier (derived from its physical properties as potentially modified by mutations) determines the item's architectural capacity — how many slots it has and what EML ceiling it can support.

### Layer 2: Expressions (Mutations and Enchantments)

Planar influences combined into or imposed upon the substance. Mutations occur through natural exposure or environmental process. Enchantments are deliberately imposed by a practitioner. Both are mechanically identical — the distinction is origin, not mechanism. Expressions generate their own charges through the drip recharge system and count as Active Expressions toward the item's EML.

### Layer 3: Attachments

Sub-items carrying rotes, installed in the parent item's slots. Each attachment is a complete item with its own material reading, its own charge pool, and its own condition tracks. Attachments are portable between compatible housings. They contribute their material properties to any spell routed through them.

### Layer 4: Insets

Spell sentence modifiers installed in slots. Each inset corresponds to an Additive or Subtractive from the spell sentence system, made into a physical component. Insets modify rotes or channeled spells that pass through their position in the routing architecture. They generate charges as a secondary function of their material.

The **battery** is not a separate layer — the base material generates its own charges naturally. A dedicated battery component can be installed in a regular slot as a trade-off (more power budget, one fewer capability slot).

## MASTERY AND SCALE

### The 1-10 Mortal Scale

Mastery is rated 1-10 for mortal beings and items. Bands align with proficiency thresholds:

| Band | Range | Character | Crafting Access |
|---|---|---|---|
| Novice | 1-4 | Learning, developing | Formation Stage 1-4 materials |
| Journeyman | 5-7 | Professional, trained | Formation Stage 5-7 materials |
| Master | 8-10 | Expert, peak mortal capability | Formation Stage 8-10 materials |

### Item EML

An item's Effective Mastery Level reflects its current magical expression:

**EML = Highest Active Expression + floor((Total Active Expressions − 1) ÷ 4)**

Active Expressions include the item's own mutations/enchantments AND each attachment's rote. Insets, batteries, and Forge Links are not Active Expressions.

Material Tier caps EML — a Tier 6 material cannot support EML above 6 regardless of installed expressions.

### Material Properties and the Spell Sentence

An item's physical properties feed directly into the spell sentence parameters for any expression it carries or any spell channeled through it. The material is not neutral housing — it is an active participant in every magical expression it supports.

### PREAMBLE

Aíon did not want portals blinking open and closed. A portal is a rupture — raw planar energy flooding into an unprepared environment, destroying everything it contacts. Instead, Aíon caused planar boundaries to bleed. The bleed is gradual, sustained, and most importantly — it gives the affected area time to adapt. Trees near a Borderlands fire bleed develop fire-resistant bark over generations. Animals in a Verdant Font bleed zone develop accelerated healing. Soil in a Synod bleed zone mineralizes and fossilizes.

Mutations are not damage. They are adaptation. The Planar Strain system tracks the pressure that drives that adaptation, and the Mutation Economy tracks the cost of carrying the resulting power.

These are two connected but distinct systems:

**Planar Strain** governs WHEN a mutation expresses and WHAT it is. It operates before the mutation manifests.

**The Mutation Economy** governs the XP cost of having the mutation and the consequences of carrying more adaptation than your development can support. It operates after the mutation manifests.

The handoff point is the moment of expression — Strain builds until adaptation triggers, at which point a Nascent mutation manifests and its cost enters the debt ledger.

## PART I: PLANAR STRAIN — THE ADAPTATION PRESSURE SYSTEM

### What Strain Tracks

Strain measures how much adaptation pressure a specific planar expression is applying to a subject. Every planar exposure — ambient bleed zones, alchemical compounds, direct contact with expressed materials, magical effects — applies pressure that the subject's biology or material structure must negotiate.

Strain is tracked **per isotope.** A character exposed to B-01 Inferno and V-13 Cancer simultaneously carries two separate Strain counters because those are two different adaptations trending toward two different mutations. This is consistent with how real hazard exposure is tracked separately per substance.

### How Strain Accumulates

Each exposure event generates **Adaptation Pressure** — a value representing how intensely the incoming planar expression is pushing the subject toward a new state.

**Adaptation Pressure = Source Formation Stage × 2**

This value is then modified by the **interaction type** between the incoming expression and the subject's current state:

| Interaction Type | Pressure Modifier | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Cooperative** | ×0.25 | The expression aligns with the subject's existing state. The body accepts it. Minimal adaptation needed. Healing potions, compatible enhancement, expression reinforcing what's already there. |
| **Competing** | ×1.0 | The expression conflicts with the subject's current state. The body must negotiate. Standard adaptation pressure. Incompatible expressions, forced exposure, occupational chemical contact. |
| **Cancellation** | ×1.5 | The expressions fight. The subject is caught between. Higher stress, higher adaptation demand. Antithetical exposure without Forge mediation. |
| **Emergent** | ×2.0 | Something new is being created. The subject has no template for this. Maximum adaptation demand. Emulsion exposure, unprecedented combinations. |

### Defense Against Strain

The subject defends using the **Metaphysical pillar.** Planar adaptation is a Metaphysical phenomenon — it changes what the subject IS at the planar level. The Metaphysical defense stats (Pattern, Dodge, Deflect, Soak, Threshold) apply.

The modified Adaptation Pressure acts as an "attack" against the Metaphysical defense. If the pressure penetrates the defense, the penetrating amount is added to the Strain counter for that specific isotope.

If the defense fully absorbs the pressure, no Strain accumulates from that exposure event. The subject's current planar identity successfully resisted the incoming influence.

### The Adaptation Threshold

When accumulated Strain on a specific isotope exceeds **(Integrity × 5)**, a Nascent Mutation manifests.

- High Integrity characters resist longer. More pressure must accumulate before expression triggers. But when it does, the higher accumulated Strain produces a more powerful mutation — they adapted slowly but thoroughly.
- Low Integrity characters mutate faster from less exposure. The mutations are proportionally weaker because less Strain had accumulated at the point of expression. They adapted quickly but shallowly.

At the moment of expression:
1. The Strain counter converts to a Nascent Mutation
2. The mutation's nature is determined by the source isotope's profile
3. The mutation's power level is built through the spell sentence system — higher accumulated Strain at expression produces higher ESL
4. The mutation's ESL determines its XP cost, which enters the Mutation Debt ledger
5. The Strain counter for that isotope resets to zero

### Exposure Types

**Acute Exposure (single event):**
One contact, one check. Direct touch of a mutated material, being hit by a planar expression in combat, drinking an alchemical compound. The full Adaptation Pressure is applied once.

**Chronic Exposure (sustained contact):**
Repeated or continuous exposure over time. Living in a bleed zone, working daily with hazardous materials, ongoing proximity to a strong planar source. Each exposure interval (per day for occupational, per week for environmental) applies Adaptation Pressure separately. Strain accumulates across intervals until the threshold is crossed.

**Deliberate Exposure (intentional mutation):**
The subject (or their alchemist) is deliberately seeking mutation. The Witcher potion, the Wildpath Warden's bio-management discipline. The exposure intensity and isotope are chosen by the practitioner. A Forge Calibrated component (F-16 Forge Mutagen) determines which specific mutation pathway expresses from the source isotope's profile, turning random adaptation into directed evolution.

### Interaction Type Determination

How to determine which interaction type applies:

**Cooperative:** The incoming expression reinforces or aligns with the subject's existing biology or planar profile. Healing a biological creature with V-01 World Tree is cooperative — the expression does what healthy biology already does. Enhancing a fire-adapted creature with B-01 Inferno is cooperative — more of what it already is.

**Competing:** The incoming expression conflicts with the subject's current state but isn't antithetical. Mercury exposure to a healthy human is competing — mercury's Borderlands mineral expression pushes toward mineralization, which conflicts with biological function but isn't cosmologically opposed to it. Most occupational and environmental exposures are competing.

**Cancellation:** The incoming expression is antithetical to an existing expression the subject carries. A Synod expression contacting a Font-mutated creature. A Borderlands expression contacting a Palimpsest-mutated material. The two expressions fight rather than negotiating. Without Forge mediation, one may destroy the other.

**Emergent:** The combination of incoming and existing expressions produces something neither would produce alone. This typically occurs with emulsion-bearing compounds or when multiple strong planar expressions interact within the same subject simultaneously. The result is unpredictable — the subject has no template for what they're becoming.

## PART II: PRACTICAL STRAIN SCENARIOS

### Standard Adventuring Potions (Safe)

A healing potion (V-01, FS 3, cooperative with healthy biology):
- Adaptation Pressure: 3 × 2 = 6
- Cooperative modifier: 6 × 0.25 = 1.5, round to 2
- Against standard Metaphysical defenses, this doesn't penetrate
- Result: the potion heals and passes through. No Strain. Safe for routine use.

### Professional Enhancement Compounds (Monitor)

A Hunt enhancement elixir (V-05, FS 5, cooperative with predatory biology):
- Adaptation Pressure: 5 × 2 = 10
- Cooperative modifier: 10 × 0.25 = 2.5, round to 3
- Single use: probably doesn't penetrate. Safe periodically.
- Daily use for months: 3 Strain per day accumulating. A scout relying on these daily eventually develops predatory biological traits. They adapted to their own medicine.

### Occupational Chemical Exposure (Manage)

An alchemist working unprotected with mercury (Borderlands mineral, FS 4, competing with biology):
- Adaptation Pressure: 4 × 2 = 8
- Competing modifier: 8 × 1.0 = 8
- Per day of unprotected exposure, 8 pressure against Metaphysical defense
- WITH proper protection (reducing effective FS reaching the body): lower daily pressure
- WITHOUT protection: Strain accumulates steadily. Borderlands mineral mutations develop — nervous system mineralization. This IS mercury poisoning.

### Deliberate Mutation Compound (Intentional)

A Witcher mutation potion (V-16 + F-16 Forge Mutagen, FS 7, competing with current template):
- Adaptation Pressure: 7 × 2 = 14
- Competing modifier: 14 × 1.0 = 14
- Penetrates most defenses significantly. Large single-dose Strain accumulation.
- Probably exceeds the Integrity × 5 threshold immediately, triggering Nascent expression.
- The F-16 Forge Calibrated component selects WHICH mutation pathway expresses — directed evolution rather than random adaptation.

### Emulsion Artifact Exposure (Extreme)

Contact with a Biotic Paradox emulsion artifact (Font + Synod, FS 6, emergent):
- Adaptation Pressure: 6 × 2 = 12
- Emergent modifier: 12 × 2.0 = 24
- Blows through almost any Metaphysical defense
- Massive immediate Strain. Likely triggers Nascent expression of something unprecedented.
- This is why emulsion artifacts are the most regulated items in the setting.

## PART III: THE MUTATION ECONOMY — THE XP DEBT SYSTEM

### Front-Loaded Power

Mutations give you power NOW. The ability is immediately available from the moment of Nascent expression. But the power has a cost, and the cost must be paid over time through XP investment.

### Mutation Cost

A mutation's XP cost is determined by building it through the spell sentence system. The mutation IS a complete spell sentence in a specific fixed configuration (a rote) — with an Effect, an Isotope, target geometry, range, duration, and any additives. The ESL of this rote determines the base XP cost.

The mutation also carries detriments if the character is in the Stressed zone. Each detriment costs the same XP as the positive side of the mutation.

### The Debt Ledger

When a Nascent mutation manifests, its total XP cost enters the character's **mutation debt ledger.** The power is immediately available. The debt remains until paid.

### The Two Thresholds

The thresholds are calculated from the character's **total lifetime XP** — including the equivalent XP value of creation choices (starting dots, backgrounds, etc.).

| Zone | Debt Range | Status | Consequences |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Safe** | Debt ≤ Lifetime XP × 2 | Clean adaptation | No detriments. The character's growth can absorb this level of change. The mutation expresses its benefits only. |
| **Stressed** | Lifetime XP × 2 < Debt ≤ Lifetime XP × 8 | Strained adaptation | Detriments manifest alongside benefits. The character is carrying more adaptation than their development cleanly supports. Each mutation in this zone expresses both positive effects AND negative side effects. |
| **Overwhelmed** | Debt > Lifetime XP × 8 | Catastrophic adaptation | The character's identity is losing coherence. Mutations are expressing uncontrollably. This is the crisis state that produces monstrous transformation. |

### Automatic Repayment

When mutation debt exceeds the ×2 Safe threshold:

**25% of all XP gained is automatically deducted and applied to mutation debt.** The character's growth is being consumed by adaptation pressure. This isn't optional — the body is directing resources toward integrating the change.

**Minimum floor:** There is a guaranteed minimum XP the player receives per session regardless of mutation debt. The player always has some agency over their character's development. The adaptation tax cannot consume everything.

### Natural Threshold Growth

Because the thresholds are based on **current lifetime XP**, simply gaining XP over time moves the ×2 and ×8 lines upward. A mutation that pushed you into the Stressed zone at Mastery 3 might fall into the Safe zone by Mastery 5 — not because you paid anything toward it, but because your total development grew enough that the mutation is proportionally smaller relative to who you are now.

You grew around it. The fire adaptation that was destabilizing at level 3 is just part of you at level 6.

### Paying Down Debt

A character can voluntarily invest XP into mutation debt at any time. This accelerates the payoff process beyond the 25% automatic deduction.

**Paying off the positive side (full debt clearance) = Calcification.** The mutation becomes permanent baseline. It costs nothing ongoing. It's just what you are. The Synod has Settled it. The Heritage State shifts from Active to Settled.

**Paying off the positive side does NOT remove the mutation.** The power stays. The obligation goes.

### Detriments

Each detriment that manifests in the Stressed zone has its own XP cost equal to the positive side of the mutation. Detriments are tracked separately in the debt ledger.

**Paying off a detriment ELIMINATES it.** Unlike the positive side (which stays when paid), the negative side disappears when its debt is cleared. You outgrew the problem. The fire adaptation no longer burns you from the inside because your biology learned to handle it.

Detriments can be bought down independently from the positive side's debt. A character might choose to pay off their detriment first (eliminating the downside) while still carrying debt on the positive side (the power remains, the obligation remains, but it no longer hurts).

### What Detriments Look Like

Detriments are the negative expressions of the same isotope that produced the positive mutation. They are mechanically built through the same spell sentence system as the positive side, using effects from the isotope's natural profile that work against the character.

A B-01 Inferno mutation in the Stressed zone might produce:
- **Positive:** Fire damage on contact, heat immunity, light production
- **Detriment:** Vulnerability to cold (the fire adaptation made the character cold-sensitive), uncontrolled combustion under stress (the fire expresses when the character doesn't want it to), or chronic tissue dehydration (the internal heat damages their own biology)

The detriment's nature is determined by the isotope profile and the character's specific situation. The storyteller selects detriments that are narratively meaningful — consequences that create story, not just penalties that punish.

---

## PART IV: THE FULL LIFECYCLE

### Stage 1: Exposure

The character encounters a planar expression — ambient bleed zone, alchemical compound, magical effect, direct material contact. Adaptation Pressure is calculated from the source's Formation Stage and the interaction type.

### Stage 2: Defense

The Adaptation Pressure "attacks" the character's Metaphysical defense. If it penetrates, the penetrating amount is added to the Strain counter for that specific isotope.

### Stage 3: Accumulation

Strain accumulates across exposure events. A single intense exposure might cross the threshold immediately. Chronic low-level exposure builds over weeks or months. The Alchemist path's chronic exposure management talent monitors these counters.

### Stage 4: Expression (Nascent)

When Strain exceeds (Integrity × 5), a Nascent Mutation manifests. The mutation's nature comes from the source isotope profile. Its power level is determined by the accumulated Strain, built through the spell sentence system. Its XP cost enters the debt ledger.

The character has the power immediately. The debt clock starts.

### Stage 5: Integration or Struggle

If total mutation debt is in the Safe zone (≤ Lifetime XP × 2): clean integration. Benefits only. The character adapts smoothly.

If total mutation debt is in the Stressed zone (> ×2, ≤ ×8): detriments manifest. The character has the power but it's causing problems. The 25% auto-repayment begins. The character is actively struggling with the adaptation.

### Stage 6: Growth

Over time, through automatic repayment, voluntary investment, and natural threshold growth from gaining XP, the debt shrinks relative to the character's total development.

Detriments can be bought off individually — each one eliminated is a problem solved.

### Stage 7: Calcification (Settled)

When all debt on a specific mutation is cleared — both positive and negative sides — the mutation Calcifies. Heritage State shifts to Settled. The mutation is permanent baseline. It costs nothing. It's who you are.

The Synod has claimed it. The adaptation is complete. The record is Settled.

---

## PART V: INHIBITORY TREATMENT

Synod Inhibitory expression (the anti-proliferative compounds from the Synod's functional repertoire) can arrest the adaptation process at any stage.

### Arresting Strain Accumulation

Inhibitory treatment applied during the accumulation phase reduces the rate of Strain buildup. The Inhibitory compound's Formation Stage reduces the effective Adaptation Pressure per exposure event. A FS 4 Inhibitory treatment reduces incoming pressure by 8 (FS 4 × 2), potentially preventing any Strain accumulation from low-intensity sources.

This is the alchemist's protective equipment expressed through the Synod — PPE as anti-adaptation chemistry.

### Arresting Nascent Mutation

Inhibitory treatment applied to a Nascent mutation shifts its Heritage State from Active to Arrested. The mutation persists at whatever level it reached but stops developing. Detriments that have manifested persist but don't worsen. The adaptation is frozen mid-process, carrying tension.

The mutation debt remains in the ledger. The 25% auto-repayment continues. The character still needs to pay off the obligation — but the mutation isn't getting worse.

Removing the Inhibitory treatment lets the process resume from where it was arrested. If the character is still exposed to the source isotope, Strain begins accumulating again from the current level.

### Inhibitory Treatment Cannot Reverse Calcification

Once a mutation has Calcified (Settled Heritage State, debt fully cleared), Inhibitory treatment has no effect. The adaptation is complete. The Synod has Settled it. Reversal would require Decomposition (alchemical operation to strip the expression) or Displacement (alchemical operation to replace it with a different expression), both of which are separate, significant procedures.

---

## PART VI: ITEMS AND STRAIN

Items experience planar adaptation through the same system but with different parameters.

Items do not have XP. They do not have a debt ledger. Their adaptation is tracked through Formation Stage progression and the condition track system.

When an item accumulates Strain beyond its material's current capacity (determined by base material Tier rather than Integrity), it manifests a mutation. The mutation's nature comes from the source isotope. Its power level is constrained by the material's Formation Stage (the bottlenecking principle). The mutation becomes part of the item's combined state.

Item mutations don't have "detriments" in the XP sense — they have the crafting mutation system (benefits and flaws from the Item Creation section). A mutation that manifests in an item through environmental exposure rather than deliberate crafting follows the same benefit/flaw logic but without the crafter's ability to manage the outcome.

This is why naturally mutated materials are valued — the mutation occurred through genuine adaptation, producing expressions that are organically integrated with the material's nature. And why they're unpredictable — nobody controlled the conditions under which the adaptation occurred.

## Item Creation — The Crafting System

## PREAMBLE

Item creation is the intersection of physical fabrication and alchemical manipulation. A crafter shapes matter and directs planar expressions simultaneously. Every crafting operation involves two categories of work: physical operations that change material form, and alchemical operations that change material composition. Both happen within the same weekly workflow, abstracted into a single Artifice check.

---

## PART I: THE TWO CATEGORIES OF OPERATION

### Physical Operations

Physical operations change form without changing composition. The iron is still iron after forging.

| Operation | What It Does | Common Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| **Shaping** | Forming through deformation — forging, hammering, pressing | Temperature, Mechanical Force, Geometry |
| **Cutting** | Removing material — carving, sawing, chiseling | Mechanical Force, Geometry |
| **Grinding** | Removing through abrasion — sharpening, polishing | Geometry, Mechanical Force |
| **Joining** | Connecting pieces — welding, riveting, binding | Temperature, Geometry, Timing |
| **Finishing** | Surface treatment — oiling, lacquering, burnishing | Medium, Timing, Moisture |
| **Assembly** | Combining sub-components — mounting, setting, stringing | Geometry, Sequence |

### Alchemical Operations

Alchemical operations change composition — chemical structure, planar expressions, or both.

| Operation | What It Does | Common Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| **Synthesis** | Combining substances or expressions into a new combined state | Temperature, Atmosphere, Reagent Ratio, Reagent Compatibility (both axes), Timing, Containment |
| **Decomposition** | Breaking a combined state into simpler constituents | Temperature, Pressure, Medium, Reagent Ratio, Collection Medium, Stabilization, Timing |
| **Displacement** | Replacing one constituent with another | Reagent Ratio, Reagent Compatibility (both axes), Planar Balance, Collection Medium, Stabilization |

### The Dual-Axis Reagent Matching Principle

Every alchemical operation requires reagents satisfying two requirements simultaneously:

**Physical axis:** The reagent must physically survive the processing conditions.

**Alchemical axis:** The reagent must carry the correct planar profile to interact with the target expression.

The intersection of valid physical carriers and valid alchemical profiles determines what reagents are available for any given operation.

## PART III: THE CRAFTING CHECK

### The Roll

**Artifice Check = d20 + (Artifice Rank × 2) + Relevant Attribute + Modifiers**

Relevant attribute: Intellect (alchemical), Finesse (precision physical), Wits (reactive/volatile), Might (force operations).

### Modifier Types

Same type does not stack (take highest). Different types always stack.

| Type | Stacks? | Represents | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Competence** | No | Skill, training | Specialized tradition, focused study |
| **Enhancement** | No | Magical amplification | Enchanted tools, attribute buffs. Subject to +2 temp / +4 combined caps |
| **Alchemical** | No | Applied preparations | Tinctures on tools, treated surfaces |
| **Infrastructure** | No | Right tools for the job | Workshop quality, specialized equipment |
| **Insight** | No | Perfect information | Analyzer readings, divination |
| **Collaborative** | Special | Another practitioner's expertise | See collaborative rules |
| **Circumstance** | Yes | Situational conditions | Favorable environment, good lighting. Multiple distinct circumstances stack |
| **Luck** | Yes | Probability manipulation | Multiple distinct Luck sources stack |

### Hazard Penalties

Material hazard ratings impose penalties unless mitigated. These are not modifier types — they are measured material properties from the NFPA/GHS classification.

**Health Hazard (1-10):** Biological danger to the practitioner. Penalty applied to the roll representing impaired ability to work freely while managing exposure risk. Mitigated by protective measures (sealed environments, alchemical treatments, magical shielding). Full protection eliminates the penalty. NOT mitigated by crafting skill — a master alchemist working with mercury is still endangered by mercury.

Chronic exposure to materials with Health Hazard ratings while unprotected is tracked through the **Planar Strain system.** The material's planar expression applies Adaptation Pressure per the Strain mechanic. Accumulated Strain may produce mutations. See the Planar Strain & Mutation Economy section.

**Physical Hazard (1-10):** Flammability and thermal energy release. Penalty applied to the roll. Mitigated by appropriate containment infrastructure.

**Instability (1-10):** Tendency toward violent decomposition or detonation. Penalty applied to the roll. Mitigated by containment and stabilization.

**The Oxidizer Rule:** A material flagged as an Oxidizer increases the effective Physical Hazard rating of adjacent flammable materials by the oxidizer's own Instability rating. The oxidizer itself may not be flammable, but it makes everything around it burn more readily. Storage and workspace organization must account for this — keeping oxidizers separate from flammables is basic alchemist safety.

### Collaborative Crafting

Multiple practitioners fill distinct roles managing specific conditions.

| Operation Complexity | Max Assistants |
|---|---|
| 1-2 conditions | 0 |
| 3-4 conditions | 1 |
| 5-6 conditions | 2 |
| 7+ conditions | 3 |

Each assistant checks against the same TN using their relevant skill and attribute. On success: **Collaborative** modifier to primary crafter equal to half their Artifice Rank (minimum +1). On failure: no contribution. On critical failure: −2 penalty to primary crafter. Different assistants' Collaborative modifiers stack (distinct roles).

## PART IV: THE FIVE OUTCOME STATES

| Result | Outcome | PV Progress | Event |
|---|---|---|---|
| TN+10 or higher, OR natural 20 | **Critical Success** | Double progress | Major benefit, no flaw |
| TN to TN+9 | **Success** | Normal progress | Minor benefit, no flaw |
| TN−1 to TN−4 | **Success with Flaw** | Half progress | Minor benefit + minor flaw |
| TN−5 to TN−9 | **Failure** | No progress | Minor flaw, no benefit |
| TN−10 or lower, OR natural 1 | **Critical Failure** | Lose progress | Major flaw, no benefit |

**Normal Progress per week = (Artifice Rank × 10) + (Relevant Attribute × 5)**

### Outcome Options

**Critical Success:** Accept as-is (major benefit), OR reckless overcharge (take major flaw, gain additional major benefit), OR minor push (take minor flaw, gain additional minor benefit). One voluntary addition maximum.

**Success:** Accept as-is (minor benefit), OR overclock (take minor flaw, minor benefit upgrades to major).

**Success with Flaw:** Accept as-is (minor benefit + minor flaw), OR escalate (both upgrade to major), OR sublimate (cancel both — net zero, clean week).

**Failure:** Full buyoff (1 extra week OR 1 minor success + 12% acquisition cost), OR accept minor flaw.

**Critical Failure:** Full buyoff (2 extra weeks OR 2 minor successes OR 1 major success + 25% cost), OR downgrade to minor (1 extra week + 12% cost), OR accept major flaw.

### Buyoff Rules

All flaws are buyable. No flaw is permanent unless the crafter chooses. Buyoff weeks carry no risk — no rolls, no new flaws.

| Flaw | Time | Material | Success Trade |
|---|---|---|---|
| Minor | 1 week | 12% acquisition cost | 1 minor success from any future roll |
| Major | 2 weeks | 25% acquisition cost | 2 minor or 1 major success from future rolls |

Non-consecutive application permitted. Time-based and success-based buyoff can be mixed. The crafting ledger remains open until declared complete.

# THE ÆTHERIUM CODEX — System Reference Document
## The Housing Architecture — Staves, Attachments & Insets

*Alpha SRD — Second Draft*

---

## PREAMBLE

A staff is not a weapon that casts spells. It is a platform — an architectural housing built from a material whose quality determines how many systems it can support, how powerful those systems can be, and how they route to produce their effects. A wand is the same architecture at smaller scale with a lower ceiling. The distinction is material quality, not purpose. Everything described here applies to any item with slots — swords, armor, rings, staves, wands. The architecture is universal.

---

## PART I: MATERIAL GRADE AND CAPACITY

Material Tier — derived from physical properties as modified by mutations — determines architectural capacity. Tier equals slots. Tier caps maximum EML.

**Wand-Grade (Tier 1-4):** Limited slots, low EML ceiling. Single-purpose tools, consumer-grade equipment, focused devices. The material cannot support complex architecture regardless of effort.

**Staff-Grade (Tier 5-10):** Sufficient capacity for complex multi-system architectures. The platform grows with the wielder as slots are filled over a career.

| Tier | Grade | Slots | EML Ceiling |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Wand | 1 | 1 |
| 2 | Wand | 2 | 2 |
| 3 | Wand | 3 | 3 |
| 4 | Wand | 4 | 4 |
| 5 | Staff | 5 | 5 |
| 6 | Staff | 6 | 6 |
| 7 | Staff | 7 | 7 |
| 8 | Staff | 8 | 8 |
| 9 | Staff | 9 | 9 |
| 10 | Staff | 10 | 10 |

**Ceiling Versus Current Expression:** A Tier 8 staff with one EML 2 rote is currently EML 2 sitting in a Tier 8 housing with seven empty slots. Material quality is an investment in future potential.

---

## PART II: INTERNAL EXPRESSIONS

A housing's base material — through its mutations and enchantments — can possess its own internal rotes. These belong to the material itself.

Internal rotes:
- Originate from base material mutations/enchantments
- Use the item's own EML when activated
- Generate charges through the drip recharge system
- Are permanent features — removal requires alchemical decomposition
- Count as Active Expressions toward EML
- Route through internal architecture and can be modified by insets they pass through

All housings are inherently hybrid — every item with a material Tier can carry internal rotes AND accept attachments AND channel spells through it. These are not separate configurations but simultaneous capabilities of the same architecture.

---

## PART III: SLOT COMPONENTS

Every slot on a housing can hold one of four component types:

### Attachments

A sub-item carrying one rote, installed in a slot. Each attachment is a complete item with its own full material reading:
- Base material with physical properties (Hardness, Tensile, Density, Conductivity, Elasticity on 1-10 scales)
- Heritage State, Formation Stage, isotope identity
- Own four-pillar condition track (Physical/Mental/Social/Metaphysical)
- Own charge pool generated from own material through the drip system

**Portability:** Attachments can be removed from one housing and installed in another, provided the destination has an empty slot and the attachment is compatible. Removal and installation are separate Artifice checks performed by an Enchanter.

**Material Contribution:** When a spell routes through an attachment, the attachment's material properties modify the spell alongside the parent housing's properties. A copper attachment in a heartwood staff contributes copper's conductivity to spells routed through it.

**Rote:** Each attachment carries one rote — a complete spell sentence in fixed configuration. Changing the rote requires decomposition and recomposition. When activated, uses item EML.

### Insets

A spell sentence modifier installed in a slot. Does not carry its own rote. Each inset corresponds to one or more Additives or Subtractives from the spell sentence system, made into a physical component.

**What Insets Modify:** Any rote or channeled spell that passes through the inset's position in the routing architecture. A Chain inset adds Chain targeting. A Range inset extends reach. The modification applies automatically to everything routed through that position.

**Charge Generation:** Insets generate charges as a secondary function of their material. These can be linked to other components via Forge Links to supplement charge-hungry rotes elsewhere.

**Material Properties:** The inset's material shapes HOW the modification expresses. A Chain inset in copper performs differently from a Chain inset in ceramic.

**Stacking:** Same-type insets in the same routing path do not stack (take highest). Different-type insets in the same path do stack. Insets in different routing paths only affect expressions passing through their specific path.

### Batteries

A dedicated power storage component occupying a regular slot. The base material generates its own charges naturally, so a battery is a trade-off — more power budget at the cost of one fewer capability slot. The battery's material determines its recharge rate through the drip system.

### Forge Links

A Forge Interfacial component connecting two or more slots to share charge pools. The shared pool is capped by the lower expression of the linked components. Linking doesn't enhance — it shares. Useful when one component generates surplus charges and another runs a deficit.

---

## PART IV: THE EML CALCULATION

**EML = Highest Active Expression + floor((Total Active Expressions − 1) ÷ 4)**

**Active Expressions include:**
- The housing's own internal rotes (from mutations/enchantments)
- Each attachment's rote

**Active Expressions do NOT include:**
- Insets (modify, don't produce independent effects)
- Batteries (store, don't produce effects)
- Forge Links (connect, don't produce effects)

**Example:** A Tier 8 staff with 2 internal rotes (CML 4, CML 3), 3 attachments (CML 4, CML 3, CML 2), 2 insets, 1 Forge Link:
- Active Expressions: 5 (2 internal + 3 attachments)
- Highest CML: 4
- EML = 4 + floor((5−1) ÷ 4) = 4 + 1 = **EML 5**

Fits within Tier 8 ceiling. Three more slots available. Room to grow.

---

## PART V: THE ROUTING ARCHITECTURE

When a spell is activated or channeled through a housing, it follows a physical path. The spell is modified ONLY by what it passes through — base material, attachments in the path, insets in the path. Components outside the routing path have no effect.

The Forge Channeled lane builds the channels. The Forge Interfacial lane builds the junctions and gates. Routing is a deliberate, permanent crafting decision.

### Linear Routing

Everything in sequence. Spell enters at one end, passes through all components, exits. All modifiers stack. Maximum modification, zero selectivity. Simplest architecture — novice crafters default to it.

### Parallel Routing

Independent channels. Spell enters base material, wielder directs to one channel. Only that channel's modifiers apply. Maximum selectivity, each path less modified. Architecture for multi-purpose items.

### Selective Routing

Junction points with Forge Interfacial switches. Wielder directs flow between paths, combines paths, or bypasses components. Tactical options — fire channel alone, ice channel alone, or both through a shared modifier. Most complex architecture, requires Journeyman+ Forge work.

### Routing and Internal Rotes

When the housing activates its own internal rote, the expression originates in the base material and follows the architecture. If the channel passes through an inset, the inset modifies the internal rote. Strategic inset placement enhances innate abilities.

### Routing and Channeled Spells

When a caster channels their own spell, the spell enters the base material and follows the designated path. The base material's physical properties modify spell parameters through the property-to-spell-sentence mapping. Insets and attachments in the path further modify. The caster uses their own stats (Mastery, Effect Tier, Isotope Tier). The housing adds its material contributions on top.

---

## PART VI: CHARGE POOLS AND POWER

### Self-Generation

Every rote-bearing component generates its own charges through the drip system. The housing's base material powers its internal rotes. Each attachment powers its own rote from its own material. Generation rate determined by Formation Stage and interaction profile. Pools are isolated by default.

### Forge Links

Bridge two or more pools. Shared pool capped by lower expression. Surplus feeds deficit through the link.

### Channeled Spell Costs

Channeling through the housing uses the caster's own ÆP. The housing's charges are not consumed — the caster provides energy, the housing provides modification. Housing charges are only consumed when internal rotes or attachment rotes are triggered.

---

## PART VII: ACTIVATION AND ACTION ECONOMY

**Triggering an item rote:** Standard action. Uses item EML. Wielder does not need to be a practitioner — the item does all the work.

**Channeling through the item:** Whatever action the spell normally requires. Material modifications apply automatically as part of casting. No additional action cost.

**Multiple activations:** Each rote is independent. Activating one doesn't prevent activating another next round. The action economy limits throughput — one standard action per round, multiple tactical options available.

---

## PART VIII: INSTALLATION AND REMOVAL

### Installation

Requires empty slot, component to install, appropriate tools, Enchanter-path Artifice skill. Artifice check vs TN (10 + component CML + item's current Instability rating). Time: 1 day per CML minimum.

**Success:** Component seated and integrated. EML recalculated if Active Expression added.
**Failure:** Component damaged. Failure by 5+: slot itself damaged, requires repair before accepting new component.

### Removal

Same practitioner skill and TN. 

**Success:** Component extracted intact. Slot cleared.
**Failure:** Component damaged or destroyed. Failure by 5+: slot damaged.

### Compatibility

An Analyzer/Appraiser can assess compatibility before installation — identifying competing combinations, antithetical conflicts, and Metaphysical condition track risks before the attempt.

### Nested Architecture Limits

Two levels only — housing and components. Attachments cannot hold further nested components. Prevents infinite complexity.

### Condition Track Independence

Each component maintains its own condition track independently. The housing can be Pristine while an attachment is Worn. Damage to one doesn't automatically damage others (unless specifically targeted or distributed proportionally). A component at Broken or Destroyed ceases functioning — its rote, modifications, and charge generation stop until repaired or replaced.

---

## PART IX: ITEMS AS CAREER PLATFORMS

The housing architecture is designed for long-term growth.

**Early Career:** Staff-grade material acquired. One or two rotes installed. Most slots empty. EML low. The material represents potential far exceeding current expression.

**Mid Career:** Attachments added as capabilities develop. Insets installed. Routing becomes more complex. EML climbs. The item reflects the wielder's growing expertise.

**Mastery:** Most slots occupied. Sophisticated selective routing. Forge Links managing charge distribution. Internal rotes deepened through Legacy. EML approaching Tier ceiling. The item has a reputation, a history, a legend — its Social condition track reflects decades of significant use.

## Legacy & Ascension — The Palimpsest History

*Alpha SRD — Second Draft*

---

## PREAMBLE

Every permanent item accumulates history. The Palimpsest records what happens to an item the same way it records what happens to any entity with continuity of experience. This record progressively narrows the item's probability space from wide-open potential toward specific, crystallized identity.

A freshly forged sword is Contested — it could become anything. A sword carried through war, broken and reforged, wielded by three generations is Resolved — it has become something specific. The Palimpsest made that determination based on what actually happened.

This system applies only to permanent or reusable items. Consumables do not accumulate Ascension Points or develop Ascension Traits.

---

## PART I: ASCENSION POINTS

AP is the currency of experiential history. It accumulates through meaningful use — not prescribed actions, but genuine participation in significant events.

### How AP Is Distributed — The Milestone System

At the completion of a major story arc, the GM initiates the AP distribution process.

**Step 1: Set the Pool and Tags.** The GM determines a base AP pool (50-100 AP for the milestone) and selects relevant planar/isotope tags representing the arc's dominant themes. A campaign arc centered on fire elementals tags Borderlands fire expressions. A negotiation arc tags Social/Influence expressions. Multiple tags can apply.

**Step 2: Base Distribution.** The base AP pool is divided among items that were meaningfully used during the arc.

- Equipped items matching the GM's tags: **1.25×** base AP
- All other equipped items: **1.0×** base AP
- Bonded but stowed items: **0.75×** base AP

**Step 3: Player Boost.** Each player selects **(Mastery + 1)** items from their bonded or equipped gear. Selected items receive boosted AP:

- Non-matching boosted items: **1.5×** base AP
- Matching boosted items: **2.0×** base AP

This ensures story-relevant items are naturally favored by the GM's tag selection while players retain agency to spotlight the items they care about most. Even stowed gear grows, albeit slowly, so backup items can develop over long campaigns.

**Step 4: Record in Escrow.** All awarded AP enters the item's Ascension Point escrow. When escrow reaches the threshold for the item's current EML band, a new Ascension Slot unlocks.

### The AP Pattern

AP accumulates as a PATTERN, not just a number. The Palimpsest records what KIND of experience the item has had. A sword used defensively builds a defensive pattern. One used aggressively builds an offensive pattern. The pattern determines what Ascension Traits become available when thresholds are crossed.

---

## PART II: ASCENSION THRESHOLDS

AP thresholds escalate with item EML. Each time a slot unlocks, the escrow resets and the next threshold is higher.

| Item EML Band | AP Threshold Per Slot | Escrow Resets After Unlock |
|---|---|---|
| 1-3 | 50 AP | Yes |
| 4-5 | 100 AP | Yes |
| 6-7 | 150 AP | Yes |
| 8-9 | 200 AP | Yes |
| 10+ | 250 AP | Yes |

**Progression Example:** An item starting at EML 2 needs 50 AP for its first Ascension Slot. Escrow resets. If the item is now EML 3, the next slot costs another 50 AP. If it reaches EML 4, the next costs 100 AP. The cost escalates as the item grows — legendary development requires legendary investment.

---

## PART III: THE PROBABILITY NARROWING

The item's Palimpsest state progresses through four stages as AP accumulates.

### Unattuned (Pre-First Threshold)

**Palimpsest State: Contested.** All outcomes equally probable. No experiential bias. The item functions at base specification.

### Awakened (First Slot Unlocked)

**Palimpsest State: Contested narrowing.** The AP pattern constrains probability. Certain trait categories more likely. The item can hold one Ascension Trait. An Analyzer can read trends in the Palimpsest state. The item's Social condition track begins developing — it has a story.

### Mastery (Second Slot Unlocked)

**Palimpsest State: Resolved.** Probability crystallized. The item can hold two Ascension Traits. Its identity is defined. The Synod Sepulchral considers the item's record worth preserving. Named items typically reach this stage.

### Pinnacle (Third Slot Unlocked)

**Palimpsest State: Resolved (Synod Sepulchral seal).** Three Ascension Traits. The item has a soul — its experiential archive is a persistent entity in the Synod's administrative system. Destroying a Pinnacle item is a significant narrative event. The Bureaucracy of the Pinnacle maintains the record.

---

## PART IV: ASCENSION TRAITS

When an item crosses a threshold, it unlocks capacity for a new trait. The trait emerges from the AP pattern.

### Determining Traits

1. **Read the pattern.** What has the item been doing? What capabilities relied on? What situations survived?
2. **Identify the trend.** Defensive pattern → Protection, Soak, reactive traits. Aggressive pattern → Damage, offensive riders. Utility pattern → Feature, skill bonuses.
3. **Select a consistent trait.** Must feel like natural outgrowth of experience.
4. **Express through existing isotope profile.** Must be mechanically consistent with the item's planar expressions.

### What Traits Are NOT

Not quest rewards. Not unlockable achievements. Not prescribed milestones. The system avoids "complete X to unlock Y." A player cannot engineer a specific trait through planned use — the Palimpsest reads the TOTALITY of experience including unplanned events. The storyteller is the Palimpsest's voice.

---

## PART V: ABERRATIONS

An Aberration occurs when a proposed trait conflicts with the item's established identity.

### Expression Conflict

Trait contradicts primary planar expression. AP refunded. A Suspended echo remains — visible to Analyzers as what the item almost became.

### Pattern Conflict

Trait contradicts AP history. AP refunded. No damage. The item hasn't built the experiential foundation. Change use pattern and reattempt.

### Resolved Conflict

Trait contradicts an existing Resolved trait. AP NOT refunded. Minor Flaw added as the unresolved tension manifests physically. Only exceptional intervention (Palimpsest probability manipulation, emulsion, divine action) can reopen the Resolved state.

---

## PART VI: ACCIDENTAL DISCOVERY

Sometimes items change unexpectedly when planar pressures force a breakthrough.

### Trigger Conditions (ALL required)

1. Item used meaningfully in at least 3 sessions since last evolution
2. At least 50% of AP needed for next threshold accumulated
3. Catalyst event: planar breach, extreme emotional resonance, proximity to Pinnacle item or legendary location, OR Critical Success on Artifice check during active use

### Resolution Through Interaction Outcomes

The catalyst event IS a planar interaction. The outcome follows the four behavioral categories:

**Cooperative** — catalyst reinforces existing expressions. Evolution deepens what's already there. Resolution: stabilize amplified output.

**Competing** — catalyst conflicts with item's nature. Two expressions wrestle for dominance. Resolution: determine which side wins or whether they coexist in tension.

**Cancellation** — catalyst threatens to neutralize an existing expression. Evolution strips something away. Resolution: prevent loss or accept the change.

**Emergent** — catalyst combines with item's nature to produce something neither had alone. Most dramatic, least predictable. Resolution: understand and integrate the new property.

### Resolution as Quest Hook

The discovery creates an unstable state requiring resolution through play:
- Erratic behavior requiring stabilization
- New presence or awareness demanding acknowledgment
- Latent mutation destabilizing existing expressions
- Appetite or dependency requiring satisfaction

Unresolved discoveries cause progressive condition track degradation and unpredictable behavior.

### Resolved Discovery

The item unlocks a new Ascension Trait immediately (no AP threshold required). The trait reflects both the catalyst and the resolution.

---

## PART VII: LEGACY AND THE CONDITION TRACKS

### Physical Condition

Every scratch, repair, and reforging is Palimpsest record. An item reforged from destroyed fragments carries the predecessor's history — the Sepulchral archive persists through physical reconstruction.

### Mental Condition

Deepens as AP accumulates — richer to analyze, more complex to read. A Pinnacle item's Mental condition is a deep archive. Damaged Mental condition from information attacks affects future Ascension Trait development — imprecise probability narrowing.

### Social Condition

The item's legend. Grows through Legacy as the item participates in witnessed events. Provides Influence bonuses when displayed. Can be cultivated (bardic performance, institutional recognition) or degraded (obscurity, suppression, hiding).

### Metaphysical Condition

Stability of planar expressions. As Legacy deepens, becomes more robust (Resolved state resists disruption) AND more consequential (damage can unravel traits, destabilize the Palimpsest record). A Pinnacle item with Destroyed Metaphysical has lost everything — but the Synod Sepulchral archive maintains a copy. Recovery requires navigating the Bureaucracy of the Pinnacle.

---

## PART VIII: DEEPENING EXPRESSIONS

Existing expressions can intensify through Legacy without adding new capabilities.

### Mutation Deepening

A mutation at CML 3 may deepen to CML 4-5 as experiential depth grows. Formation Stage of the material still caps expression — FS 5 cannot support CML 6. Within the cap, power increases through sustained use. Deepening happens gradually, typically at Ascension thresholds or during Accidental Discovery.

### Rote Deepening

Loaded rotes similarly intensify. Same spell sentence, greater power scaling. The wielder feels the difference — same rote, harder impact, longer reach, greater persistence. Not new tricks — existing tricks becoming more potent.

### The EML Growth Curve

Item EML grows as Active Expressions deepen. Bounded by material Tier ceiling. A Tier 6 staff grows from EML 2 (fresh, one weak rote) to EML 6 (fully matured, deepened expressions at maximum capacity) but never to 7. The crafter's material choice at creation determines long-term ceiling.

### PREAMBLE

The five Artifice Paths represent distinct disciplines within the crafting tradition. Each path specializes in a different relationship with materials, expressions, and the crafting process. A practitioner can invest in multiple paths, but each path's talents require progressive investment — Novice before Journeyman, Journeyman before Master.

All paths use the Artifice skill as their primary skill, combined with relevant attributes that vary by operation type. The Artifice check formula is universal:

**Artifice Check = d20 + (Artifice Rank × 2) + Relevant Attribute + Modifiers**

Path tiers align with the system's proficiency bands:
- **Novice:** Artifice Rank 1+ (Formation Stage 1-4 materials)
- **Journeyman:** Artifice Rank 5+ (Formation Stage 5-7 materials)
- **Master:** Artifice Rank 8+ (Formation Stage 8-10 materials)

## PATH 1: THE MUNDANE ARTISAN

**Discipline:** Physical operations — shaping, cutting, grinding, joining, finishing, assembly.
**Primary Attributes:** Might (force operations), Finesse (precision operations), Wits (reactive decision-making)
**Domain:** The Artisan works with the physical properties of materials. Their expertise is the real periodic table — hardness, tensile strength, density, elasticity. They transform raw material into functional objects through physical technique.

### Novice: Masterwork Foundation

**Prerequisite:** Artifice Rank 1+
**Effect:** You can produce Masterwork-quality items (×1.0 quality multiplier). You understand the relationship between material physical properties and item function — hardness determining Soak contribution, tensile strength determining damage tier, density determining weight class.

**Mechanical Benefits:**
- Unlock Masterwork item creation
- When performing physical operations (Shaping, Cutting, Grinding, Joining, Finishing, Assembly), you gain a **Competence** modifier of +1 to your Artifice check
- You can read a material's physical properties through examination (10 minutes of handling): Hardness, Tensile Strength, Density on the 1-10 scales. No Artifice check required for Formation Stage 1-4 materials
- You can identify the Heritage State (Active, Arrested, Settled) of materials you work with

### Journeyman: Efficient Production

**Prerequisite:** Artifice Rank 5+, Masterwork Foundation
**Effect:** Your physical technique has matured. You work faster, waste less material, and can handle more demanding physical conditions.

**Mechanical Benefits:**
- **Competence** modifier increases to +2 on physical operation Artifice checks
- Your weekly Progress Value output on physical operations increases by 50% (multiply normal PV by 1.5)
- You can work with materials up to Formation Stage 7 without penalty. Materials at Stage 5-7 that would normally impose Physical Hazard penalties from their working conditions (heat, force, pressure) have those penalties reduced by your Finesse score
- You can perform Heritage State transitions through physical operations — specifically, creating Arrested states through controlled thermal processing (tempering, quenching, annealing). This requires understanding the specific temperature windows and timing, documented in the 19 crafting conditions
- You can identify Conductivity and Elasticity ratings in addition to the Novice-tier properties

### Master: Signature Creation

**Prerequisite:** Artifice Rank 8+, Efficient Production
**Effect:** You have achieved the peak of physical craftsmanship. Your items carry a signature quality that reflects your personal technique — a non-magical property that emerges from perfect physical execution.

**Mechanical Benefits:**
- **Competence** modifier increases to +3 on physical operation Artifice checks
- Your weekly PV output on physical operations doubles (×2.0)
- You can work with materials at any Formation Stage without penalty. Physical Hazard penalties from working conditions are reduced by (Finesse + Might) combined
- **Signature Property:** Each Masterwork item you complete can receive one non-magical property from the following list. The property must be consistent with the item's physical form and material:
  - **Keen:** Edge geometry optimized. Critical threat range extended by 2.
  - **Reinforced:** Structural density maximized. Item's Physical condition track gains +2 resistance against downward movement.
  - **Balanced:** Weight distribution perfected. Wielder gains +1 **Infrastructure** modifier to attack checks with this item.
  - **Lightweight:** Material removed without compromising integrity. Item weighs one category less than its material Density would indicate.
  - **Resonant:** Acoustic properties tuned. The item's material vibration frequency is harmonized, making it a superior conduit for channeling. Spells channeled through this item gain +1 **Infrastructure** modifier to their effect.

## PATH 2: THE APPRAISER

**Discipline:** Reading, analysis, valuation, and decomposition of items, materials, and expressions.
**Primary Attributes:** Intellect (technical analysis), Wits (rapid assessment), Acuity (detail perception)
**Primary Skills:** Artifice (technical analysis), Valuation (social/economic assessment)
**Domain:** The Appraiser reads the world the way the system reads it — through the dual periodic table, the four-pillar condition tracks, the inverted spell sentence formula, and the NFPA/GHS hazard classifications. They are the practitioner who decomposes observed effects into constituent variables.

### Novice: Material Reading

**Prerequisite:** Artifice Rank 1+
**Effect:** You can perform the Two-Table Read — examining a material and determining both its real periodic table properties and its alchemical passport simultaneously.

**Mechanical Benefits:**
- **Two-Table Read (10 minutes):** Determine a material's full physical property profile (Hardness, Tensile Strength, Density, Conductivity, Elasticity on 1-10 scales), Heritage State, and Formation Stage for materials up to Stage 4. No Artifice check required at this tier
- **Hazard Assessment:** Determine a material's three NFPA-equivalent hazard ratings (Health Hazard, Physical Hazard, Instability) and identify any GHS flags (Mutagenic, Carcinogenic, Corrosive, Water-Reactive, Oxidizer, Pyrophoric, etc.). This is occupational safety — the Appraiser knows what precautions are needed before anyone touches the material
- **Basic Item Read (10 minutes):** Determine an item's current EML, number of slots (empty and occupied), and overall condition track states across all four pillars. Artifice check vs TN (10 + item EML) for items up to EML 4
- **Market Valuation:** Using the Valuation skill, determine the market price of any item with EML 4 or below. Valuation check vs TN 12 for common items, TN 15 for uncommon

### Journeyman: Component Analysis

**Prerequisite:** Artifice Rank 5+, Material Reading
**Effect:** You can decompose items and expressions into their constituent variables — the inverted formula applied in practice.

**Mechanical Benefits:**
- **Two-Table Read** now extends to Formation Stage 7 materials without check. Stage 8+ requires Artifice check vs TN (10 + Formation Stage)
- **Expression Decomposition (1 hour):** Examine a mutation, enchantment, or active expression and decompose it into its spell sentence components — identify the Effect and its level, the Isotope and its level, the construction geometry (target/range/duration), and any active additives. Artifice check vs TN (10 + expression EML). This IS the inverted formula in practice
- **Interaction Assessment:** When examining a combined state (alloy, compound, multi-expression item), determine the interaction type between constituent expressions — cooperative, competing, cancellation, or emergent. Identify which expressions are bottlenecking which
- **Substrate Contribution Analysis:** Determine how the material's physical properties are modifying any expressions it carries — which properties are feeding which spell sentence parameters, where amplification is occurring, where distortion is occurring
- **Provenance Reading:** Using combined Artifice + Valuation, determine the crafting origin, approximate age, modification history, and institutional certification level of an item. This reads the item's Mental condition track — contested Mental checks against the item progressively reveal its history
- **Advanced Market Valuation:** Valuation skill extends to items up to EML 8. You can assess "soft power" value — what specific collectors, institutions, or factions would pay beyond market rate

### Master: Full Spectrum Analysis

**Prerequisite:** Artifice Rank 8+, Component Analysis
**Effect:** You can read any item, material, or expression at any Formation Stage or EML with full decomposition capability.

**Mechanical Benefits:**
- **Two-Table Read** at any Formation Stage without check. Instantaneous for materials you've examined before
- **Psychometric Reading (10 minutes, physical contact required):** Read the item's Palimpsest record — the Legacy history. Artifice check vs TN (10 + item EML × 2). On success, perceive the item's AP pattern, Ascension Trait trends, significant experiential events, previous wielders, and emotional resonance. This reads the item's full four-pillar state simultaneously
- **Predictive Analysis:** Based on full decomposition, you can predict how an item or material will behave under specified conditions — what interaction outcome will occur when two specific expressions combine, what Formation Stage shift will occur under specific environmental pressure, what hazard profile will emerge from a proposed alchemical operation. This doesn't guarantee outcomes but provides **Insight** modifier of +2 to any subsequent operation performed based on your analysis
- **Aberration Detection:** You can identify Ascension Trait aberrations (expression conflict, pattern conflict, resolved conflict) before they manifest, potentially preventing failed trait development
- **Unlimited Valuation:** Any item regardless of EML or Formation Stage. You can assess historical significance, institutional value, and identify hidden properties or alternate uses not immediately apparent

- **Displacement:** You can swap one constituent expression for another within a combined state. This is how mutations are replaced, how one alchemical expression is substituted for a different one

- **Interaction Prediction:** Before performing an operation, you can predict the interaction outcome (cooperative, competing, cancellation, emergent) with an Artifice check vs TN 15. Success provides an **Insight** modifier of +1 to the subsequent operation check
- **Elixir Production:** Create multi-effect alchemical compounds combining up to (Intellect + 1) compatible effects in a single compound. Extended shelf life (2 years). Duration of effects doubled compared to basic potions
- **Chronic Exposure Management:** You understand the Strain → Nascent → Calcification progression for chronic material exposure. You can identify when a practitioner (including yourself) is accumulating Strain and recommend appropriate protective measures or Inhibitory treatment

### Master: Essence Alchemist

**Prerequisite:** Artifice Rank 8+, Process Alchemist
**Effect:** You can work with emulsion combinations (antithetical planes forced together through Forge catalyst) and perform the most dangerous and precise alchemical operations.

**Mechanical Benefits:**
- **Competence** modifier increases to +3 on alchemical operation Artifice checks
- **Emulsion Synthesis:** You can produce emulsion combinations — the Biotic Paradox (Font + Synod) and the Reality Paradox (Borderlands + Palimpsest) — using the four Forge catalyst types (Crystal, Chemical, Pneuma, Volt). This requires three-domain knowledge: both antithetical planes plus the Forge
- **Catalyst Selection:** You understand the four catalyst binding mechanisms and can select the appropriate catalyst type for a desired emulsion behavior — Crystal for geometric containment, Chemical for cycling conversion, Pneuma for pressure boundary, Volt for electrical threshold bridge. Each produces a different product from the same antithetical pair
- **Essence Distillation:** You can extract pure planar essence from materials, creatures, or environmental sources. The distillate is a concentrated expression that can be used as a high-potency reagent. Artifice check vs TN (10 + source Formation Stage × 2). Time: 1 hour per Formation Stage
- **Permanent Alchemical Compounds:** You can create non-consumable alchemical items — compounds that recharge through the drip system rather than being expended on use. Cost: 10× standard PV. Time: significant (1 month+ for complex compounds)
- **Combined State Reading:** You can read the combined state of any material or compound — assessing the full interaction profile of all constituent expressions simultaneously, predicting behavior under specified conditions, and identifying the precise intervention needed to shift the state in a desired direction

## PATH 5: THE ENCHANTER

**Discipline:** Component installation, removal, routing architecture, and magical infrastructure management.
**Primary Attributes:** Intellect (architectural design), Dominion (magical interface), Finesse (precision installation)
**Domain:** The Enchanter is the architect of magical items. They design routing configurations, install and remove attachments and insets, manage Forge mediation between components, and build the infrastructure that makes multi-system magical items function. Every step of the Enchanter's work is a Forge operation.

### Novice: The Artificer's Eye

**Prerequisite:** Artifice Rank 1+
**Effect:** You can perceive the slot architecture of items and perform basic component installation and removal.

**Mechanical Benefits:**
- **Slot Perception (instant, no check):** Examine any item and identify the number of slots, which are empty and which occupied, and the general nature of each occupant (attachment, inset, battery, Forge link)
- **Component Installation:** Install an attachment or inset into an empty slot. Artifice check vs TN (10 + component CML + item's current Instability rating). Installation time: 1 day per CML (minimum 1 day). On success, the component is seated and integrated into the routing. On failure, the component is damaged. On failure by 5+, the slot itself is damaged
- **Component Removal:** Remove an attachment or inset from an occupied slot. Same TN as installation. On success, the component is extracted intact and the slot is cleared. On failure, the component is damaged or destroyed. On failure by 5+, the slot is damaged
- **EML Limit:** You can install or remove components on items whose EML does not exceed your Artifice Rank
- **Routing Recognition:** You can identify the routing configuration of an item (Linear, Parallel, or Selective) and trace which components are in which routing path

### Journeyman: Architecture Design

**Prerequisite:** Artifice Rank 5+, The Artificer's Eye
**Effect:** You can design and modify routing architecture, install Forge links between components, and manage Volatility during complex installations.

**Mechanical Benefits:**
- **EML Expansion:** You can install or remove components on items with EML up to (Artifice Rank + 1)
- **Routing Design:** You can design and implement Linear, Parallel, and Selective routing configurations in items you are constructing or modifying. Designing routing requires Artifice check vs TN (12 + number of active paths). Selective routing requires Forge Interfacial compounds at junction points
- **Forge Link Installation:** You can install Forge Link components connecting two or more slots to share charge pools. The shared pool is capped by the lower expression. Artifice check vs TN (12 + higher CML of linked components)
- **Volatility Management:** When installing components, you can apply stabilization measures that reduce the installation TN by your Intellect score. This represents understanding of how to manage Instability during the integration process
- **Compatibility Assessment:** Before installation, you can determine whether a proposed component is compatible with the housing's existing architecture. This identifies potential competing combinations, antithetical expression conflicts, and Metaphysical condition track risks BEFORE the installation attempt
- **Attachment Portability:** You can safely transfer attachments between compatible housings. The transfer requires two checks — removal from source and installation in destination — but your Journeyman training reduces the risk of damage during transit

### Master: Forge Architect

**Prerequisite:** Artifice Rank 8+, Architecture Design
**Effect:** You can build the most complex multi-system magical architectures, work with emulsion-bearing components, and design items intended to grow through the Legacy system.

**Mechanical Benefits:**
- **EML Unlimited:** You can install or remove components on items of any EML
- **Selective Routing Mastery:** You can design routing configurations with multiple junction points, conditional paths (routes that activate only under specified conditions), and feedback loops (output from one path feeding input to another). This is the architecture of legendary staves and multi-century heirloom items
- **Emulsion Component Installation:** You can install components bearing emulsion expressions (Biotic Paradox or Reality Paradox). This requires understanding of the Forge catalyst maintaining the emulsion and the specific instability mode of the catalyst type. Installation TN includes the emulsion's inherent Instability
- **Legacy Architecture:** You can design an item's slot architecture specifically to accommodate future growth through the Legacy system — leaving slots empty in positions that will be most useful as the item develops Ascension Traits. This is predictive design: building the platform for what the item will become, not just what it currently is
- **Catastrophic Recovery:** If a component installation or removal fails catastrophically (failure by 10+), you can attempt an emergency stabilization — Artifice check vs TN 20 to prevent cascade failure from damaging adjacent components. On success, the damage is contained to the failed component only
- **Architectural Consultation:** Your understanding of item architecture provides an **Insight** modifier of +2 to any other practitioner's Artifice check when you are advising on their installation, removal, or routing design. This stacks with Collaborative modifiers from the collaborative crafting system

## PATH INTERACTIONS

The five paths are designed to complement each other. A single practitioner rarely masters all five — specialization produces deeper expertise while breadth produces versatility.

**Common Combinations:**

**Artisan + Enchanter:** The smith who also installs components. Builds the housing AND populates it. The most common two-path combination for independent crafters.

**Appraiser + Alchemist:** The analyst who also processes. Reads what a material IS and then performs the operations to change it. The research alchemist.

**Alchemist + Enchanter:** The compound specialist who also installs. Creates the alchemical treatments AND integrates them into items. The production enchanter.

**Scrivener + Appraiser:** The documentarian who also analyzes. Reads what a process does AND records it for reproduction. The institutional researcher.

**Artisan + Alchemist:** The physical operator who also does chemical work. Handles both physical conditions (temperature, force, geometry) and alchemical conditions (reagent ratio, planar balance, catalyst maintenance) in the same project. The broadest single-practitioner crafting capability.

**The Full Workshop:** An institutional crafting operation typically employs all five paths — Artisans handling physical operations, Alchemists handling chemical operations, Enchanters handling component installation, Appraisers performing quality control and material certification, and Scriveners documenting everything as schematics for institutional reproduction.

### CP-01 — Animism
**Dominant:** Borderlands | **Recessive:** Verdant Font

Elements with biological imperatives. Fire that hunts prey. Water that bleeds when cut. Stone that grows toward light. The element is still itself — it just moves with purpose. Not alive in the Font sense — it does not metabolize or reproduce. But it acts as if it wants something.

**Emission:** Purposeful elemental field. Elemental properties express with intention rather than random physical behavior.
**Material Use:** Ideal for weapons whose elemental properties need to be directed. An Animism-expression flame weapon will pursue the target rather than simply burning on contact.

### CP-02 — Adaptive Flesh
**Dominant:** Verdant Font | **Recessive:** Borderlands

Biology rapidly evolving elemental traits to survive environmental pressure. Carapaces resistant to local elements. Metabolisms running on volcanic heat or electrical current. Biological material that has absorbed elemental character.

**Emission:** Adaptive resistance field. Material near this source adapts its resistances to match the most recent damage type encountered. Slow (10-minute tick) but persistent.
**Material Use:** Armor made from Adaptive Flesh gradually develops resistance to the damage types it encounters. Over an adventure, becomes a customized defensive platform.

### CP-03 — Fossilization
**Dominant:** Borderlands | **Recessive:** Synod of Endings

Elements subjected to absolute stasis. Lightning frozen mid-strike as glass branches. A waterfall hanging motionless. Physics preserved at the cost of all function. Enormous potential, zero current expression.

**Emission:** Stasis field. Movement near this material becomes slightly more effortful — not paralysis, but the sense of moving through something that prefers stillness.
**Material Use:** Extremely dense structural material. Item condition tracks frozen at current state when Fossilization expression is fully active. Prevents degradation permanently — but cannot recover from existing damage either.

### CP-04 — Entropic Weather
**Dominant:** Synod of Endings | **Recessive:** Borderlands

Decay delivered through elemental force. Rain that accelerates rot on contact. Wind that carries age. Earth that crumbles structures back to component minerals. Weather phenomena infused with the Synod's entropic character.

**Emission:** Entropic weather field. Structures and items in range experience gradual condition track degradation as if exposed to rapid weathering. Organic materials particularly vulnerable.
**Material Use:** Weapons carry age into their strikes — Sunder maneuvers against struck items trigger condition track checks.

### CP-05 — The Living Myth
**Dominant:** Verdant Font | **Recessive:** Palimpsest

Organisms shaped by narrative logic rather than natural selection. Creatures evolving toward what stories say they should be. Flora producing the herb specifically needed by whoever enters. Biology that responded to narrative expectation rather than survival pressure.

**Emission:** Narrative biology field. Living things near this material evolve slightly toward dramatic necessity — a predator becomes more predatory when the story requires it.
**Material Use:** Items have a quality of being exactly what's needed. Once per scene, item reveals a property appropriate to the current situation that wasn't known to exist.

### CP-06 — The Somatic Prophecy
**Dominant:** Palimpsest | **Recessive:** Verdant Font

Fate expressed through biological form. Bodies that physically manifest their destiny before it occurs. Wounds appearing before the battle that causes them. Biology as prophetic text.

**Emission:** Prophetic tissue field. Creatures near this material begin showing minor physical signs of their near future — not damaging, but legible to the trained eye.
**Material Use:** Armor shows the location of the next significant hit before it lands — provides +2 Dodge Defense as the body has already begun moving away.

### CP-07 — The Inevitable Record
**Dominant:** Synod of Endings | **Recessive:** Palimpsest

Death given narrative structure. Every death becomes a story that was always going to be told exactly this way. History as unbreakable script. The past constraining what can happen next.

**Emission:** Destiny record field. Events near this material feel inevitable in retrospect. The Chronosynclastic Institute's theoretical framework for fate as a cross-planar principle made local.
**Material Use:** Items produce effects that seem inevitable in retrospect — deal exactly the damage needed for a specific condition track movement, arrive at the exact moment needed, fail only when the story demands it.

### CP-08 — The Forgotten Future
**Dominant:** Palimpsest | **Recessive:** Synod of Endings

Probability subjected to entropy. Possible futures calcifying before they can occur. Luck running out permanently — not bad luck but the exhaustion of all potential until only one path remains.

**Emission:** Exhausted possibility field. Probability distributions flatten to single outcomes — rolls tend toward average, variance disappears, the unexpected becomes impossible.
**Material Use:** The most reliable items possible — always perform at exactly stated function, never critically fail, never critically succeed. For operations where reliability matters more than excellence.

### CP-09 — The Elemental Engine
**Dominant:** Hidden Forge | **Recessive:** Borderlands

**Emission:** Raw power field. Far more energy output than a refined Forge equivalent — and far less predictably.

**Dominant:** Borderlands | **Recessive:** Hidden Forge

Natural elemental formations behaving as machines without being built. Crystal geodes processing information. Magma flows running as industrial pipelines. Geography as unintentional engineering.

Organisms whose biological systems function with mechanical precision and can be read as engineering. Hearts as pumps with measurable tolerances. The theoretical basis for soul-drives.

Technology held together by the memory of function rather than active maintenance. Machines that work because they have always worked — entropic weight of operational history maintaining them past component failure.

**Emission:** Operational momentum field. Technologies are harder to break — item condition tracks resist moving down because the history of function resists the discontinuity of failure.

Fate expressed through technological behavior. Machines operating according to narrative logic rather than engineering specifications. A weapon that always misfires at the dramatically appropriate moment.

**Emission:** Narrative mechanism field. Technologies make decisions appropriate to narrative context rather than random mechanical probability.
**Material Use:** The most unreliable and most dramatically appropriate items in existence. Succeed at critical moments, fail at others — not randomly, but according to narrative logic the GM interprets. For players who trust their GM absolutely.

### E-01 — Amber Stasis

Life frozen at its moment of greatest vitality. Conscious but immobile. Communicating through psionic resonance inside crystal structures. The most life possible, held at exact peak.

**Emission:** Peak preservation field. Biological processes preserved at current state — neither advancing nor regressing. Damage does not worsen. Healing does not improve. Perfect stasis.
**Material Use:** Item condition tracks frozen permanently at Worn (never below). Living armor stops wielder's wound progression during combat. The most powerful wound stabilizer in the system.

**Catalyst:** Chem (Interfacial Lane) | **Pair:** Verdant Font + Synod of Endings

Fermentation. Decay that fuels growth. Cities of bloated flesh constantly dying and regenerating. Life and death as a single continuous chemical process rather than sequential states.

**Binding:** Chem's fluid nature is the medium letting Font growth and Synod decay cycle through each other. Death becomes fuel for the next cycle.
**Emission:** Cycle acceleration field. The life-death transition is rapid — organic things complete their cycle faster, but the cycle is unbroken. Quick death, quick renewal.
**Material Use:** Biological battery. Drain HP from defeated enemies to recharge (1 HP drained = 2 charges). Gruesome but self-sustaining in combat.
**Instability Mode:** Chemical cycling overwhelmed when one expression feeds too much — Font floods the medium or Synod permanence overwhelms vitality.

### E-03 — The Iron Lung
**Catalyst:** Pneuma (Channeled Lane) | **Pair:** Verdant Font + Synod of Endings

Life preserved in a dead world by machinery. Sealed environments maintained by pressure systems. The technology is the reason life still exists — without it, the Synod wins.

**Binding:** Pneuma pressure creates a gasket between living interior and dead exterior. The machinery maintains the boundary.
**Emission:** Life support field. Near-lethal conditions become merely dangerous. Lethal conditions become near-lethal.
**Material Use:** Environmental suit material. Complete environmental protection — vacuum, poison atmosphere, deep water, extreme temperature. Maintains livable conditions regardless of environment.
**Instability Mode:** Pressure loss. Seals fail, boundary collapses, the Synod reaches the living interior.

### E-04 — The Galvanic
**Catalyst:** Volt (Propagated Lane) | **Pair:** Verdant Font + Synod of Endings

Reanimation. Life forced into dead matter by electrical charge. The body is dead (Synod). The charge is alive (Font). The Volt holds both true simultaneously.

**Binding:** Electricity is neither alive nor dead, providing the third term letting Font and Synod occupy the same tissue.
**Emission:** Threshold field. Exists precisely at the line between life and death, making that line easier to cross in either direction.
**Material Use:** Foundational material for soul-drive construction. Can interact directly with souls as both data and energy — the soul is both operator and fuel. Deepguard's most carefully controlled material.
**Instability Mode:** Bridge overload — both expressions push maximum simultaneously. Discharge rather than conversion. Explosive failure.

**Material Use:** The only material that can permanently change another material's planar identity at the same tier. Requires Artifice check and 24 hours. The cosmological basis for the alchemical system.

# THE ÆTHERIUM CODEX — System Reference Document
## Section: The Spell Sentence System — The Grammar of Magic

*Alpha SRD Draft — Working Document*

---

### PREAMBLE

Magic is not a static list of incantations to be memorized; it is a living language. The so-called "spells" taught in academies and whispered in covens are merely common phrases and established prose — recipes proven to be effective and stable. The true master does not merely recite poetry; they compose it. They understand the fundamental grammar of reality and can shape it to their will.

This grammar — the Spell Sentence System — is the universal framework governing all magical expressions in the Ætherium Codex. It applies equally to casters composing rotes, items expressing mutations, alchemists building compounds, and the analysis of ambient planar phenomena. The same system operates at every scale.

---

## PART I: THE PRIMA ÆTHER

The source of all magical power in Tessera is the Prima Æther — a pervasive, fundamental force that operates with its own turbulent weather patterns. This magical climate results from the constant interplay between two fundamentally different and incompatible magical systems forcibly merged during the Ancient Magical Calamity. These two systems are not good or evil; they are simply different laws of reality, personified by the dual gods of magic, Kanon and Anima.

**The Primal Current (Anima's Law):** The world's own native magic. Raw, chaotic, life-giving energy generated by the planet, its ecosystems, and its magical materials. Akin to alternating current — wild, cyclical, intuitive. Practitioners of Innate Magic (Sorcerers, Psions, Sōmaturges) have a deep personal connection to this current.

**The Structured Current (Kanon's Law):** An imposed lattice of magical law layered on top of the Primal Current. Orderly, systematic, predictable. Akin to direct current — rigid, structured, reliable. Practitioners of Studied Magic (Wizards, Clerics) specialize in the complex rules necessary to tap into this grid.

---

## PART II: THE SPELL SENTENCE

All magical expressions can be deconstructed into a foundational "spell sentence." By combining fundamental components, a practitioner gives form and function to the raw Prima Æther.

### The Components

| Component | Grammatical Role | Function |
|---|---|---|
| **Effect** | Verb | The core action — what the expression fundamentally does |
| **Isotope** | Adjective | The planar character — what nature or quality the expression carries |
| **Target** | Noun | Who or what the expression affects — its spatial geometry |
| **Range** | Preposition | How far the expression reaches |
| **Duration** | Adverb | How long the expression persists |
| **Additives** | Augmentations | Enhancements that make the expression more complex or powerful |
| **Subtractives (Anchors)** | Limitations | Restrictions that make the expression easier to control or less costly |

### The Rote

A rote is a complete spell sentence frozen in a specific configuration — a stable, repeatable formula. Once composed, a rote produces the same expression every time. Different rotes can share the same Effect and Isotope but differ in geometry, duration, and additives. Burning hands, fire bolt, and wall of fire are all Damage + a Borderlands fire isotope, composed with different Construction Pool geometry and different additives.

A mutation in an item is a rote that the material expresses as a consequence of its planar nature. The Cancer blade's shard-flaking is a rote — Damage + V-13 Cancer, with specific target geometry, range, and seeding behavior — but the blade is not "casting" any more than it is "intentionally" flaking. The system measures what the expression actually does using the same framework regardless of whether the source is a caster's will or a material's nature.

### Secondary Subtypes (Thematic Descriptors)

Secondary subtypes are the divine values and philosophical alignments (Sovereignty, Predation, Nurture, Expression, Duty, etc.) that describe HOW an expression manifests aesthetically and narratively. A fire damage rote cast through the Sovereignty philosophy looks and feels different from the same rote cast through the Predation philosophy — different aesthetics, different narrative texture. Same damage, same geometry, same isotope. Secondary subtypes are important for setting, characterization, and divine alignment but do not alter the mechanical calculations.

---

## PART III: THE THREE TIERS OF PROFICIENCY

A practitioner's magical ability is understood through three distinct, independent tiers of proficiency, each rated on a 1-10 scale for mortal beings.

### A. Overall Mastery

The measure of total potential — raw power, force of will, and connection to magical energies. Governs the absolute ceiling of what a practitioner can accomplish, acting as a hard cap for the other two tiers. Also determines the maximum ÆP a caster can spend on a single rote.

### B. Effect Tier

Deep understanding and discipline within a particular Effect (Damage, Affliction, Create, etc.). Rated 1-10 per Effect. A practitioner can be a master of Damage and a novice of Create. Effect Tier determines raw power (Base Effect Rank) and contributes to pool size.

### C. Isotope Tier

Fine control and specialization over a specific isotope (B-01 Inferno, V-13 Cancer, S-09 Wraith, etc.). Rated 1-10 per learned isotope. Each isotope is a separate talent investment. A high Isotope Tier allows more complex and efficient expressions of that particular planar character. Isotope Tier also determines accuracy for targeted expressions.

### The Interlocking Cap

A practitioner cannot apply more precision than the power they are channeling.

**Effective Spell Level (ESL):** The final power level of any expression is capped by the LESSER of the relevant Effect Tier and Isotope Tier. A practitioner with Damage 7 and B-01 Inferno 5 can produce fire damage at a maximum ESL of 5.

**Accuracy Cap:** The attack bonus for a targeted expression equals the practitioner's Isotope Tier, but cannot exceed the ESL of the expression being produced.

---

## PART IV: THE TWO POOLS

A practitioner's ability to shape an expression — both in its application (geometry) and its complexity (augmentation) — is determined by two separate resource pools calculated from the same unified formula.

### The Unified Pool Formula

**Pool Size = (½ Effect Tier) + (½ Isotope Tier) + Overall Mastery + Integrity**

Both pools use this formula. They are the same size but spent on different things.

### A. The Construction Pool (Application)

The Construction Pool buys the expression's geometry — its Target shape, Range band, and Duration step. Points are spent from the Construction Menu (see Part IX).

**Base State (Universal Grammar):** Every expression starts with Individual / Touch / Instantaneous at 0 CP cost. You only pay CP to upgrade from this baseline. All practitioners inherently know all 0 CP components.

### B. The Complexity Pool (Augmentation)

The Complexity Pool buys Additives — enhancements that make the expression more powerful or versatile. The total cost of all Additives in a rote cannot exceed this budget. Subtractives can be taken to grant bonus points to this pool, representing a trade-off of limitation for greater complexity.

**Architect Bonus:** Studied casters receive a bonus to their Complexity Pool equal to ⌊Dominion ÷ 2⌋.

---

## PART V: THE INVERTED FORMULA — READING EXPRESSIONS IN ITEMS AND ENVIRONMENTS

For casters, the formula works inside out — known stats combine into pools that compose rotes. For items and environmental expressions, the formula works outside in — an observable effect is decomposed into its constituent variables.

### Observable Output

An item's mutation or an environmental planar bleed produces an observable total effect — the Cancer blade sheds shards at a certain rate with a certain intensity. This observable output is a composite value.

### Decomposition

An Analyzer examining the expression decomposes the composite into:

- **Effect and its level:** What the expression does and how strongly (e.g., Damage 4)
- **Isotope and its level:** What planar character it carries and how intensely (e.g., V-13 Cancer at level 5)
- **Substrate contribution:** How the material modifies the expression — bottlenecking, amplifying, or distorting
- **Interaction effects:** Modifications from other expressions in the same combined state

### Item Pool Calculation

For an item's mutation, the Construction Pool and Complexity Pool are derived from the expression's own properties:

**Item Pool = (½ Effect Level) + (½ Isotope Level) + EML + Integrity equivalent**

Where Effect Level and Isotope Level are intrinsic properties of the expression itself, and EML is the item's current Effective Mastery Level. The pools determine what geometry and complexity the mutation can support — its range, area, duration, and any additive effects.

### Extraction and Transplantation

When an expression is extracted (through alchemical decomposition), the distillate carries its Effect Level and Isotope Level as intrinsic properties. These values belong to the expression, not to the substrate.

When the extracted expression is introduced to a new substrate (through synthesis), the Effect and Isotope levels travel with it, but the new substrate may support or resist the expression differently. The new combined state must be assessed as a fresh reading — the same interaction rules (cooperative, competing, cancellation, emergent) apply.

When an expression is moved between planar contexts (displacing one isotope with another), the Effect level may be preserved but the mechanical output changes because different isotope profiles constrain which effects are natural, which require effort, and which are excluded.

## PART VII: ISOTOPES AS SUBTYPES

The primary subtypes of the spell sentence system are individual isotopes from the five planes. Each isotope is learned as a separate talent, ranked 1-10, representing the practitioner's investment in that specific planar expression.

### Learning Progression

Pure elements (the four cardinal expressions of each plane) are prerequisite foundations. A practitioner must understand constituent pures before working with compound isotopes. Catalyzed diagonal isotopes require additional understanding of Forge mediation. Emulsion isotopes require knowledge from two antithetical planes plus the Forge.

### Isotope Organization

Isotopes are organized by plane:

- **B-01 through B-16:** Borderlands (Matter)
- **V-01 through V-16:** Verdant Font (Biological Process)
- **S-01 through S-16:** Synod of Endings (Completion)
- **F-01 through F-16:** Hidden Forge (Technology)
- **P-01 through P-16:** Palimpsest (Superposition)
- **CP-01 through CP-16:** Cross-Planar Natural States
- **E-01 through E-08:** Emulsion States

Each isotope's profile (documented in the Isotope Reference section) specifies which Effects it can naturally produce, which it excludes, and how its expression scales with Formation Stage. This constrains what rotes can be composed using that isotope — B-10 Magma can produce Damage, Affliction, Environment, Protection, Create, and Weaken, but NOT Concealment, Healing, Flight, Speed, or Teleport.

### Caster Specialization

Because each isotope is a separate talent investment, practitioners naturally specialize. A practitioner who has invested deeply in B-01 Inferno and B-04 Scorch has a very different capability profile from one who has invested in B-05 Abyss and B-08 Hail — even though both are Borderlands practitioners. This distributed specialization ensures masters feel mechanically distinct from one another.

## PART VIII: CASTING PHILOSOPHIES

Every individual in Tessera possesses a spark of magical potential. The path to power is defined by the practitioner's psychology and philosophical approach. Most have a natural tendency toward one of two primary philosophies.

### A. The Studied Caster — The Architect

Power from meticulous study, perfect design, and theoretical understanding. Magic as science. Rotes are optimized blueprints.

**Recipe Optimization:** When an Architect creates a rote, their understanding allows exceptional efficiency. The rote gains a Final Rank reduction:
- ESL 1-4 rotes: −4 Final Rank
- ESL 5-7 rotes: −3 Final Rank
- ESL 8-10 rotes: −2 Final Rank

**Complexity Mastery:** Bonus to Complexity Pool equal to ⌊Dominion ÷ 2⌋.

**Intellect-Based Metamagic Efficiency:** When applying Metamagic Talents to an Architect rote, each Metamagic surcharge is reduced by ⌊Intellect ÷ 2⌋, to a minimum of +1 ÆP per Metamagic.

**Weakness — Tactical Rigidity:** Rotes are fixed once designed. Changing effects requires returning to research and designing a new rote.

### B. The Innate Caster — The Improviser

Power from intuitive, personal connection to the Prima Æther. Magic as art. Rotes are familiar melodies for improvisation.

**On-the-Fly Modification:** When casting, the Improviser can freely swap Additives or even the primary Isotope within a known rote, provided the new combination is valid and within Complexity limits.

**Anima-Based ÆP Reduction:** For Innate castings, per-rote Base ÆP is reduced by (Anima − 3), minimum 0. ÆP cost floors at 0 — casting can never generate ÆP.

**Weakness — Inefficient Design:** Innate rotes do not gain Final Rank reduction. Their foundation is less structurally efficient than Architect designs, especially at higher ESL and with heavy Metamagic.

### C. The Polymath

A practitioner can learn both philosophies, maintaining separate repertoires of optimized Architect rotes and flexible Improviser forms. Polymaths are the most versatile practitioners but require investment in both paths.

## PART XII: ÆP — THE MAGICAL RESOURCE

### Total ÆP Pool

**ÆP_max = (Dominion + Tₘ) × Mastery**

Where Dominion is the Metaphysical attribute governing active control of esoteric energies, Mastery is Overall Mastery rating, and Tₘ is the total number of magical/supernatural talents purchased.

ÆP is agnostic to casting philosophy. Architects and Improvisers use the same pool.

### Base ÆP Cost

**Base ÆP = (ESL × 2) − 1**

### Architect Metamagic Efficiency

When applying Metamagic to an Architect rote:
1. Calculate base ÆP from ESL
2. Calculate total Metamagic surcharge
3. Each individual Metamagic surcharge is reduced by ⌊Intellect ÷ 2⌋, minimum +1 ÆP per Metamagic
4. Final ÆP = Base ÆP + sum of reduced surcharges

### Improviser ÆP Reduction

When casting via Innate expression:
1. Calculate base ÆP from ESL
2. Anima Discount = max(0, Anima − 3)
3. Innate Base Cost = max(0, Base ÆP − Anima Discount)
4. Add full Metamagic surcharges (no Innate discount on Metamagic)
5. Final ÆP = Innate Base Cost + Metamagic surcharges

Casting can never generate ÆP. A 0-cost rote costs nothing; it does not refund.

### ESL and Final Rank

**Final Rank = (Base Effect Rank) + (Additive Costs) − (Subtractive Savings)**

The Final Rank determines the ESL on the 1-10 mortal scale (and beyond for Epic/Deific).

## PART XIII: THE BOOST SYSTEM

Practitioner tiers can be enhanced through magic, artifacts, or transformation. These boosts are governed by strict caps.

| Cap Type | Overall Mastery | Effect/Isotope Tier |
|---|---|---|
| Permanent Cap | +2 maximum | +3 maximum |
| Temporary Cap | +2 maximum | +3 maximum |
| Combined Cap (all sources) | +4 maximum | +6 maximum |

**Temporary boosts cannot break tiers.** A mortal at Mastery 9 with a +2 temporary boost caps at 10 (mortal peak). The boost cannot grant true Epic power.

**Permanent boosts CAN break tiers.** A permanent boost is a fundamental change to the practitioner's nature. Mastery 10 + permanent +1 = Mastery 11 (Epic tier). This is how ascension happens.

**The Prodigy Exception:** The Prodigy Aptitude is not a bonus — it is a fundamental quality of the soul. Its +1 for a specific focus stacks on top of all other boosts.

## PART XIV: THE DRIP RECHARGE SYSTEM

All recharge rates are expressed in terms of the base interval unit: the 10-minute period. The system scales continuously — no discrete rest categories.

### Time Units

| Unit | Duration | Intervals |
|---|---|---|
| Round | 6 seconds | — |
| Interval | 10 minutes | 1 |
| Hour | 60 minutes | 6 |
| Day | 24 hours | 144 |
| Week | 8 days (one per plane) | 1,152 |
| Fortnight | 16 days | 2,304 |

### Calculating Recharge Rate

**Period ÷ Frequency = Interval between charges**

When the interval resolves to 6 seconds or less, flip to per-round expression.

| Stated Rate | Calculation | Expressed As |
|---|---|---|
| 10× per minute | 60s ÷ 10 = 6s | 1 charge per round |
| 20× per minute | 60s ÷ 20 = 3s | 2 charges per round |
| 2× per 10 min | 10 min ÷ 2 = 5 min | 1 charge every 5 minutes |
| 1× per 10 min | 10 min ÷ 1 = 10 min | 1 charge per interval (baseline) |
| 1× per hour | 60 min ÷ 1 = 60 min | 1 charge every hour |
| 2× per day | 24 hr ÷ 2 = 12 hr | 1 charge every 12 hours |
| 1× per week | 8 days | 1 charge per week |

### Planar Influence on Recharge

Recharge rates are affected by ambient planar radiation, the carrier's own planar profile, and active planar pressure in the area. An item's battery is not immune to the planar environment. A Generative Font battery near a Synod Inhibitory field may fail to drip at all. Items with strong affinity to the dominant planar expression are less affected; items with antithetical participation are most vulnerable.

### Mutation Self-Generation

A mutation generates its own charges for its own effects at a drip rate determined by the mutation's Formation Stage and the isotope's interaction profile. This self-generation powers the mutation's innate effects exclusively — it cannot fuel installed inset components. Projecting profile mutations (Gas-derived) tend toward faster, wider-dispersing recharge. Static profile mutations (Solid-derived) tend toward slower, more stable recharge. Absorbing profile mutations (Plasma-derived) generate charges by consuming available fuel or ambient energy. Cycling profile mutations (Liquid-derived) generate charges through internal conversion processes.
