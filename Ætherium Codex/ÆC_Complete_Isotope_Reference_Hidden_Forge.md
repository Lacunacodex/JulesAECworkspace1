*Phase Wheel: Crystal (Solid) / Chem (Liquid) / Steam (Gas) / Volt (Plasma)*
*Profile derivation: Crystal=Static, Chem=Cycling, Steam=Projecting, Volt=Absorbing*
*Diagonal pairs: Crystal↔Steam (Solid↔Gas), Chem↔Volt (Liquid↔Plasma)*

**UNIQUE PROPERTY: ALL 16 Hidden Forge states are Natural (◆).** The Forge has no internal antithetical states. Making incompatible things work together is the Forge's fundamental nature — it mediates its OWN diagonals without requiring external catalysis. Every other plane's diagonals require Forge mediation. The Forge mediates itself.

**THE FORGE'S DUAL ROLE:** The Forge produces direct Effects (especially along the Volt axis) AND serves as the universal catalytic mediator that enables cross-planar combinations. No other plane fills both roles. The Forge version of any Effect is always the engineered/directed version — not natural expression but designed output. Forge Healing isn't biological repair; it's electrical threshold maintenance. Forge Flight isn't anti-gravity; it's propulsion engineering. Forge Illusion isn't false perception; it's hard-light construction.

### F-01 — The Spire (Solid Pure / Crystal)

**Phase:** Solid | **Type:** ◆ Natural | **Profile:** Static
*Psionic resonance. Singing stones. Geometry that processes and stores information passively. The world as crystal lattice — perfect information architecture in physical form. - **Communication** — Information architecture. Crystal lattice as data storage and transmission medium. The structural backbone of Deepguard relay networks. Not message-sending but infrastructure-providing.
- **Comprehend** — Pattern recognition. The Crystal identifies, categorizes, and organizes patterns in data, language, and magical structure. Translation and decryption through geometric analysis rather than linguistic knowledge.
- **Feature** — Passive information processing. Items near Spire materials become organized and categorized. The workspace becomes more efficient. Operate checks gain advantage.

- **Enhanced Trait (mental)** — At specialized application, the Crystal processing field enhancing cognitive efficiency. Not making the user smarter — making their existing thinking more organized, faster, and less prone to error.
- **Nullify** — At specialized application, the rigid geometric structure disrupting chaotic or disorganized magical effects. Order imposed on disorder. The Crystal lattice rejects noise.

**Hard Exclusions:** Healing (biological), Damage (direct — the Spire processes, it doesn't strike), Growth, Speed (physical), Luck Control, Affliction (the Spire organizes — it doesn't impose conditions), Move Object.

**Formation Stage Shift:**
- Stage 1–2: Minor information processing. Operate checks advantage. Simple message storage. The Crystal hums near magical sources.
- Stage 3: Active computational field. Complex calculations performed as minor action rather than study time. Lore and Discovery checks enhanced. Deepguard relay network compatible.
- Stage 4–5: Full processing node. All information within the relay network accessible. Real-time analytical support. The item thinks alongside the wielder.

**Median Expression:** Senses (information processing). The Spire organizes and clarifies information in its environment.

**Phase:** Plasma dominant, Solid recessive | **Type:** ◆ Natural | **Profile:** Absorbing primary, Static character
*Electricity meeting crystalline resonance. Neon lights. Holograms. Hard-light constructs. Laser systems. The Volt's energy given Crystal's geometric permanence — electricity that holds its shape. Absorbing (Volt concentrating electrical energy) with Static (Crystal giving that energy permanent geometric form). The result: persistent, shaped light with physical interaction properties.*

**Natural Effects:**
- **Illusion** — THE Forge's illusion expression. Hard-light projection. Not false perception (that's Palimpsest) — actual light-constructs with geometric structure and physical interaction properties. Visually perfect, structurally real. Indistinguishable from reality to unaided senses.
- **Create** — Hard-light construction. Barriers, shields, furniture, tools, structures made of persistent shaped electrical light. Physical properties (Soak, HP). Shaped to specification. Duration limited by power.
- **Feature** — Hard-light projection on demand. Geometric light constructs shaped by will. The versatile crafting material that's made of light. Torch that can be molded into any shape or tool.
- **Senses** — At specialized application, holographic display. Information presented as three-dimensional visual construct. The Heads-Up Display. Tactical overlay.
- **Communication** — At specialized application, holographic communication. Visual messages shaped in light. Remote visual presence. The video call as magical technology.
- **Protection** — At specialized application, hard-light barriers providing physical defense. The energy shield. Stage 3: 10-ft cube constructs with Soak 2 and HP 20. Shapeable as armor, barriers, or terrain.
- **Concealment** — At specialized application, hard-light projection disguising appearance. The technological disguise. Detectable as energy construct by analysis but visually perfect to casual observation.

**Hard Exclusions:** Healing, Luck Control, Growth, Teleport, Speed (Cyberpunk creates, it doesn't accelerate), Affliction (hard-light is constructive, not condition-imposing), Damage (direct — hard-light has physical properties but isn't optimized for destruction. Use F-04 Lens for directed energy damage).

**Formation Stage Shift:**
- Stage 1–2: Minor hard-light. Geometric light construct on demand. Functions as torch. Can be shaped into simple blocking forms (shield, wall segment) deflecting thrown objects.
- Stage 3: Full holographic interface. Visually perfect illusion within 10 ft. Hard-light structures up to 10-ft cube with material properties. Duration: one hour per battery charge.
- Stage 4–5: Persistent hard-light architecture. Structures of significant size and complexity. Near-permanent duration. The holographic city as physical reality.

**Median Expression:** Illusion (hard-light) + Create (light constructs). Cyberpunk makes things out of shaped light that function as real.

### F-15 — Clockwork (Plasma + Gas / Volt + Steam)

**Phase:** Plasma dominant, Gas recessive | **Type:** ◆ Natural | **Profile:** Absorbing primary, Projecting character
*Electricity driving pressure mechanics with supreme precision. Precision droids. Automatons wound by lightning keys. The Volt provides the motive energy; the Steam transmits it through pressure mechanics. The result: mechanical action with perfect timing and zero tolerance. The Forge's most philosophically significant isotope — Clockwork approaches probability not through magic but through engineering.*

**Natural Effects:**
- **Quickness** — THE precision timing expression. Extremely regular, high-frequency timing pulse. Mechanical systems in proximity become more precise. Initiative bonus equal to item EML. Cosmological accuracy in timekeeping.
- **Luck Control** — Mechanical determinism. Not fate manipulation — CALCULATION. The Clockwork computes outcomes by running physics forward with perfect precision. The wielder can declare one action per encounter as "inevitable" because the Clockwork has already calculated the exact sequence of physical events needed. The result is predetermined not by magic but by engineering. The Chronosynclastic Institute's philosophical foundation: probability is just insufficiently computed physics.
- **Feature** — Perfect timing accuracy. Programmable routine — up to three sequential actions encoded and triggered on command, executing with zero deviation regardless of external interference.
- **Senses** — At specialized application, predictive analysis. The Clockwork computing future physical states from current conditions. Not prophecy (P-10 sees what WILL happen through fate) — Clockwork calculates what SHOULD happen through physics. When both agree, certainty approaches absolute.
- **Enhanced Trait** — At specialized application, the Clockwork timing providing optimal action timing. Every movement at the mechanically ideal moment. Maximum efficiency without enhanced force.
- **Nullify** — At specialized application, disrupting timing-dependent processes by imposing the Clockwork's own timing cycle. Overriding enemy action sequences. Forcing mechanical rhythm onto organic or chaotic systems.

**Hard Exclusions:** Healing, Growth, Illusion, Concealment, Teleport, Damage (direct — Clockwork is precision, not force), Create (physical), Move Object, Affliction (Clockwork doesn't impose conditions — it calculates outcomes).

**Formation Stage Shift:**
- Stage 1–2: Perfect timepiece. Initiative bonus. Programmable routine with single encoded action. Minor timing optimization.
- Stage 3: Active determinism. Three-action programmable routine. One "inevitable" declaration per encounter. Predictive analysis available.
- Stage 4–5: Fate mechanism. The Clockwork's computational model is sophisticated enough that the distinction between "calculated" and "fated" becomes academic. The item knows what happens next because it has computed every variable.

**Median Expression:** Quickness (precision timing) + Luck Control (mechanical determinism). Clockwork calculates the future and makes it happen.

### F-16 — Battery (Plasma + Liquid / Volt + Chem)

**Phase:** Plasma dominant, Liquid recessive | **Type:** ◆ Natural (Forge self-mediates diagonals) | **Profile:** Absorbing primary, Cycling character
*Electrical plasma forced into fluid containment. Acid-based energy storage. Contained, dangerous, essential. The Forge's diagonal between Volt (Absorbing, concentrating energy to destructive levels) and Chem (Cycling, converting between states through fluid process) — held naturally because the Forge IS the mediator. The result: enormous energy stored in stable but dangerous chemical-electrical medium. The foundation of all Powered items.*

**Natural Effects:**
- **Feature** — THE energy storage expression. Item battery capacity doubled. Recharge rate doubled. Passive recharge of nearby compatible items. The fundamental power source for all Powered items in the system. Without Battery expression, there is no Kan-tech civilization.
- **Enhanced Trait** — At specialized application, power amplification. The Battery providing surplus energy to connected systems, enabling them to operate at higher output than their own capacity allows. The booster pack.
- **Damage** — At specialized application, battery discharge as weapon. Concentrated release of stored electrical+chemical energy. Powerful single-shot but depletes the source. The capacitor dump.
- **Affliction** — Chemical corrosion as persistent side effect. The Battery's chemical component corrodes organic materials on prolonged contact. Acrid smell. The cost of portable power storage.
- **Create** — At specialized application, producing power cells. Manufacturing portable energy storage units. The Battery making batteries. Recursive infrastructure.
- **Feature (critical risk)** — Explosive failure. If fully depleted in a single encounter, the chemical-electrical tension the Forge holds in balance can catastrophically release. Vigor check for wielder or take fire+acid damage from catastrophic decompression. This isn't a flaw — it's an inherent property. Batteries are powerful because they're dangerous.

**Hard Exclusions:** Healing, Luck Control, Illusion, Teleport, Growth, Concealment, Senses (the Battery stores energy, it doesn't perceive), Communication (the Battery powers things, it doesn't transmit).

**Formation Stage Shift:**
- Stage 1–2: High-capacity storage. Battery capacity doubled. Recharge doubled. Chemical corrosion on prolonged organic contact. Acrid smell.
- Stage 3: Power amplifier. Adds own capacity to any connected item's pool through proximity. Contact corrosion no longer required for connection — proximity within 5 ft suffices.
- Stage 4–5: Independent power station. Up to five connected items simultaneously. 200-charge pool. Explosive failure risk if fully depleted in single encounter. The portable reactor.

**Median Expression:** Feature (energy storage). Battery stores power and makes it available.

## HIDDEN FORGE SUMMARY — Effect Coverage

**THE FORGE IS CATEGORICALLY DIFFERENT.** Every other plane produces Effects that express its nature directly. The Forge produces Effects AND mediates other planes' Effects. This dual role means the Forge's Effect coverage appears wider than any other plane — but the Forge's version of each Effect is always the ENGINEERED version, achieved through process rather than natural expression.

**Effects the Forge EXCELS at:** Feature (the Forge's broadest domain — reliability, efficiency, infrastructure, timing, power storage, sustained operation, force multiplication), Senses (precision observation, data storage, information processing, electrical detection — the Crystal axis), Communication (data transmission, relay networks, infrastructure networking — Crystal+Steam systems), Enhanced Trait (through augmentation rather than natural enhancement — Serum implants, Hydraulic force multiplication, Steampunk overclocking, Diesel endurance), Damage (directed energy — Lens precision, Grid electrical, Steampunk dual-element), Quickness (process acceleration and precision timing — Engine reliability, Clockwork determinism)

**Effects the Forge CAN produce (engineered versions):** Healing (three distinct mechanisms — Galvanism threshold maintenance, Vat pharmaceutical correction, Serum diagnostic repair, Diesel biological maintenance, Mana-Punk life support — NONE of which are biological acceleration), Flight (Aether-Tech propulsion — engineering, not magic), Illusion (Cyberpunk hard-light — construction, not perception), Transform (Vat chemical conversion — chemistry, not transmutation), Create (Vat compounds, Cyberpunk hard-light, Engine mass production — manufactured, not conjured), Luck Control (Clockwork mechanical determinism — calculation, not fate manipulation), Morph (Serum template installation — surgery, not shapeshifting), Immortality (Galvanism reanimation — threshold maintenance, not soul management), Protection (Engine reliable defense, Hydraulic pressure-balanced armor, Cyberpunk energy shields, Aether-Tech deflection), Nullify (Lens focused disruption, Grid electrical overload, Spire order-imposition, Data corruption, Mana-Punk disconnection)

**Effects the Forge CANNOT produce:** Growth (the Forge doesn't change scale — it changes efficiency), Regeneration (sustained biological repair is Font territory — the Forge can maintain thresholds but not rebuild tissue over time), Panacea (broad-spectrum biological cure requires Font Generative expression), Teleport (the Forge can build vehicles but not fold space), Dimensional Travel (planar transit requires Palimpsest mechanism), Time Travel (temporal manipulation is beyond engineering), Affliction (biological conditions — the Forge doesn't sicken, it disrupts systems), Concealment (most Forge expressions are loud, visible, and detectable — industrial noise, electrical signature, mechanical rhythm)

**THE FORGE'S UNIQUE CONTRIBUTION:** The Forge is the only plane that appears in EVERY cross-planar combination as the mediating catalyst. Without the Forge, the antithetical combinations (Borderlands↔Palimpsest, Verdant Font↔Synod) produce Forbidden or Paradox states rather than functional materials. The Forge's Effect list is less important than its CATALYTIC FUNCTION — it makes everything else work together.
